# Independent pre-outcome design review

This review reads no evaluation geography and runs no new position search. The
three report-local prototypes are explanatory experiments, conditional on the
published RF graph, native identities, aliases and masks. The complete available
DS24/26/27/28 fast roster must retain unsupported inputs, numerical failures,
time caps and unchanged fallbacks. Neither raw RF improvement nor a covariance
guard proves better localization.

A retains the ALGO07 objective and changes only its numerical solution schedule.
Half damping has the same unclipped phase fixed points. A central-difference
position Jacobian can repair the observed discrepancy between a solver success
flag and a nonzero reproducible gradient. The independent central stationarity
check must remain part of endpoint qualification, alongside raw phase closure,
inner precision convergence, the full native observation/owner union and gates.
An objective comparison across changed phase references is not an exact
nonlinear likelihood nondegradation result. If used as a conservative policy
gate, its approximation must be explicit; a comparison of positions under one
common final reference is a coherent narrower position-only alternative.

B keeps the same three supported physical orbit directions and their zero
Gaussian prior mean. Common-epoch position/velocity TLE disagreements are
phase-aligned using orbital states alone, projected into those directions and
converted to a shrunk uncentered second moment. The declared baseline variance
floor and SD4 ceiling are engineering choices, fixed before outcomes. No RF
residual or position score sets that covariance. Causal eligibility requires
both element epoch and archived availability before the scan cutoff, actual
element-byte epoch matching, separated historical epochs and a minimum span.
Unrepresented state discrepancy is reported and can trigger fallback; adding
unmodeled directions is outside this prototype.

The mode-bank orientation is correct: if the old frequency design is `F` and
the desired coefficient covariance is `C=L L.T`, transformed columns are `F L`
with standard normal coefficients. Integration retains the resulting Gaussian
covariance determinant. These are correlated TLE disagreements, not independent
orbit errors, so epoch count must not increase claimed precision. They can
share a bias and underrepresent true error. Their relation to existing
previous-TLE-derived mode scales also precludes treating them as independent
new information. The replacement/shrinkage interpretation is appropriate.

C uses only other-family curved observations and jointly removes the union of
all nuisance directions at the saved control/candidate positions. It never
freezes hardware learned using the target. Each family-deletion counterfactual
recalibrates its retained data. This is a conservative necessary-support
diagnostic with engineering cutoffs, not a test with a calibrated error rate.
It may remove real position information and abstain even on a beneficial B
model. It also cannot remove every shared unmodeled hardware/orbit error, so a
pass does not establish identity uniqueness or position correctness. Its
nominal zero-reference phase geometry, fixed150 white noise and curved-only
scope must be disclosed. The prior sensitivity is separate and cannot rescue a
projection rejection.

Both c=0 and fitted-c must retain matched observations, identities, priors
except the declared target covariance intervention, starts, solver budgets and
RF policies. Require paired-arm numerical qualifications for deployment-like
selection; report the individual arm outcomes even when the pair abstains.
Store normalized score components separately from frequency residuals and
eventual position error. Root alone may release geography after sealed RF
decisions; no case/NORAD-specific fallback or threshold may be tuned from it.

Read-only implementation comments sent before the cohort run include checking
actual TLE satellite numbers against the requested family and requiring the
bounded orbital phase-alignment iteration to converge, rather than silently
using its final state. Final-source and receipt checks follow after freeze.
