# Saved results and scientific replay

This report contains the new sources, protocols, preparation snapshots,
synthetic observation NPZ files and truths, saved fit/checkpoint outputs,
identity screens/scores, audit receipts, tables and figures. The
`publication_manifest.json` records their exact bytes and SHA256 hashes,
excluding itself. No IQ, credentials, original scan banks or complete runtime
capsule is republished. The operational baseline remains unchanged.

The synthetic observations are actual saved CFO arrays in16 NPZ files, unlike
the prior control's archived generating draws alone. Their labels, aliases,
native IDs and times accompany the values. The reused eight noisy controls
and their16 old fitted outputs remain in the immutable
[fast-identifiability report](https://github.com/misko/leo-tracker-reduxredux/blob/dad19ba5ef74744d8234ccb4d461bac8444663aa/reports/2026_10_25_ds24_fast_identifiability/README.md)
and its documented input assets; they are not refitted or duplicated here.

For an inexpensive saved-result audit, inspect `audit/synthetic_terminal_audit.json`
and `audit/identity_terminal_audit.json`. Their accompanying check scripts
recompute saved norms, density differences and hash checks without propagation
or fitting. They preserve the original absolute research paths: executing them
requires restoring those paths and their referenced files. Scientific seals
bind exact archived outputs, including intermediate rounds. Superseded
prelaunch source/test fixtures are provenance, not additional scientific runs.
The initial filename-only exposure probe is explicitly superseded by
`audit/exposure_inventory02.json`; see `audit/EXPOSURE.md`.

Scientific reconstruction additionally requires the original DS24 fast-scan
inputs for0010–0013, stored in the shared `ds25-20261024` research directory:
graph/native GLRT records, physical/ephemeris banks,
selected causal TLEs and history, masks/powers, radio calibration and complete
accepted-ID ledgers. The source contracts and hashes are recorded in the branch
protocols and independent preflight receipts. Both branches import the earlier
recovery, uncertainty/quadrature, transfer and repository research modules at
their saved locations. Those exact runtime/source dependencies are not all
contained in this compact report. In particular, a published input hash is not
the corresponding input bytes or proof of a clean full-input restoration.

The execution environment used the repository `.venv/bin/python`, NumPy/SciPy,
Matplotlib for figures, `PYTHONPATH=/tmp/algo03-feasible-deps:src:tools` plus the
referenced research adapter directories, and one BLAS/OMP/MKL thread per worker.
Sources deliberately retain their frozen imports and path defaults. They refuse
to overwrite scientific artifacts. Any later rerun needs a fresh output location
and an explicit new protocol/budget; merely rerunning a report script in place
may fail its preservation guards.

This publication verifies the new file bytes, current seals and Markdown links.
It does not claim a new clean full-input numerical replay. The independent
audits use saved artifacts; the branch-owned numerical checks are separately
reported. No future TLE, real geographic selection, new RF processing or new
position fitting was performed for publication.
