# Closest-approach proposals and coherent orbit selection

Use DS26-0036 and DS29-0082 as the two development recordings. Replay complete native GLRT archives at their original times with the hash-bound causal TLE catalogue. The global worker receives no tile. The matched-tile worker receives the original public 25 km tile. Neither receives an evaluated coordinate, learned case offset, previous track, prepared orbit prediction or satellite assignment.

Build the same independent triplet tracks as the rate-triplet receiver. Fit a constant-offset straight-pass Doppler curve to each seven-sample track shape using a nominal 7.45 km/s speed. Limit the fit to two initializations, 32 evaluations each, a 40–240 s pass scale and a closest approach within 55 s of the observed track. Require a centered shape residual of at most 270 Hz and a closest-approach Doppler rate magnitude between 950 and 6,500 Hz/s. Use at most eight fitted pass times spaced by at least 8 s, chosen by estimated fit RMS.

Propagate each causal satellite to the pass time and a symmetric ±0.75 s stencil. For each pass time, intersect the zero range-rate plane with the Earth sphere. On either side of the satellite ground track, solve the range acceleration constraint by 18 bracketed bisections over a 25° angular arc. Require elevation above 5° and at most 1,050 Hz centered residual against the pass's seven measured Doppler samples. Vote once per pass per occupied cell of the existing 1,536-point equal-area sphere. Keep at most ten cell representatives separated by 450 km. Refine them over 5 × 5 meshes at fixed steps of 60, 15 and 3.5 km, then rank the resulting geographic basins by joint carrier consistency. A global case with fewer than two distinct fitted pass times with geometric proposals withholds.

For the supplied tile, evaluate an approximately 3 km mesh and refine the three best points to 1 km and 0.3 km spacing. For orbit selection in both modes, offer at most six orbits per track with centered Doppler RMS at most 700 Hz. Vote for separate circular CFO intercepts by receiver and half-band using 230 Hz shape and 1,800 Hz intercept scales. Keep each track only if it agrees within 3,500 Hz of its group intercept; downweight repeated labels in the site score. Freeze chosen orbit identities, CFO integers and observations before both calibration fits. Use the rate-triplet robust position fit, including the same evaluation budgets, priors and difference of one common RF coefficient between the zero and fitted arms. Require four satellite identities, multiple channels, a median centered shape RMS at most 300 Hz and conditional joint frequency RMS at most 1,200 Hz. In global mode require a 1.5-unit score margin over the next retained basin more than 250 km away.

Do the planted closest-approach and null tests before scoring the roof pair. If available, test one fresh generated scene from the independent causal-orbit simulation after locking implementation hashes. Keep analytical and generated diagnostics separate from the two archive recordings. Report forced and returned positions, frequency, runtime and failure status by mode and calibration. Use isolated workers with one numerical thread, a 120 s case deadline and at most four pinned CPUs. Do not collect RF.
