## What changed by recording

Stopping v5 after the cavity fit produced all 135 fixes and kept the original median and P95 on both datasets, in both calibration settings. With fitted calibration, 131 recordings had error within 5% of full no-repair v5 or better, and 133 had error within 10% or better. The two larger regressions were DS26-0066 (1.218 to 2.370 km) and DS26-0287 (0.440 to 0.689 km). The median post-GLRT solver time across six paired host replays fell from 266.62 to 135.88 seconds. [Cavity chart](roof/fitted_c/v5_stop_cavity.png) · [Paired accuracy counts](roof/paired-v5.csv) · [Timing and calibration evidence](../2026_11_19_receiver_simplification/README.md)

Three detector studies now have downstream position measurements. The wideband pilot and sliding-tone changes replayed two and eight scheduled visits per recording, respectively, with capture-era inputs for the other visits. Both published both recordings. The late-pilot experiment uses ordinary native detections as its matched control. Each chart labels the capture and processing boundary. [Wideband pilot](native-geometry/README.md) · [Sliding-tone pilot grid](spectral-grid/README.md) · [Late-pilot confirmation](pilot-confirmation/README.md)

The incremental GLRT reader preserved the tracks and both positions on its two test recordings. Preparing the input used less peak memory in both cases. Whole-worker memory and time were mixed. The pass-geometry receiver published one of the two supplied-tile fixes and withheld both global fixes. Its fitted error on the published roof recording was 3.224 km. The single fresh simulated location and its shuffle are reported separately. [Incremental reader](stream-prepare/README.md) · [Pass geometry](pass-geometry/README.md)
