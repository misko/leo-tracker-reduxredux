# Association join and split-exposure addendum

The association/model-row join is exact. The 10,369 keys formed by session ID, track ID, and observation ID equal the 10,369 association rows with the pre-existing frequency training=false mask. There are no duplicate keys or differences in UTC timestamp, the ordered three candidate IDs, their weights, or their training RMS values.

All 599 tracks keep the same three candidate IDs and normalized weights throughout their rows. Candidate azimuth is recorded in degrees from 0.0025 through 359.9841 and elevation in degrees from 16.0729 through 89.6973. Every source point carries group ID, start/end sample, support-center UTC, and stream ID; association observation, support center, and propagation timestamps are equal. This supports an exact conditional temporal feature join without regenerating LOS.

For a matched-margin ratio endpoint, deduplicate by the existing physical_pair_key, which is the sorted pair of complete session/visit/probe/receiver/candidate-rank anchor keys. Keep RX0 if a reciprocal RX0/RX1 pair produces two rows, then break ties by lexical anchor key. Of 6,285 matched rows, this retains 6,043 physical candidate pairs over 485 tracks and all 10 recordings, removing 242 reciprocal rows. All retained rows have a finite ratio.

The proposed new seeded 6/4 split is not untouched validation when run from these inputs. The current CFO compatibility bias was learned from the old six calibration recordings. The existing persistent graph tracks, source links, and conditional rows were built before the new split from detection-gated whole-recording evidence. The old per-observation frequency mask prevents reception outcomes from selecting its candidate weights, but does not implement the new whole-recording split, and the LOS branch uses the known roof position. A new seed can support an exposed conditional feasibility comparison only unless association/compatibility inputs are rebuilt and frozen using its training recordings before its evaluation outcomes are inspected.
