# Dual-RX beam-crossing input audit

The frozen roof10 cache is usable for a **conditional** temporal feasibility test. It has ten complete, pose-bound recordings: six chronological calibration sessions and four holdout sessions. The cache contains 22,155 complete visits and 44,310 simultaneous receiver probes. Every cache hash matches its frozen inventory, every input/timing receipt is qualified, and both receiver probes agree on visit, probe, lane, and device start counter. Each retained probe has 20 ms support at offset 0.

The reusable input contract is [evaluation_inventory.json](../../2026_09_27_roof_direction_subset/evaluation_inventory.json), bound to [evaluation_manifest.json](../../2026_09_27_roof_direction_subset/evaluation_manifest.json). Load only the listed local `TrackingInput` caches; their source provenance is already bound through the read-only `ScannerTrackingInputStore` and `AdaptiveHopIqStore` ports. The fields required by a conditional model are receiver identity, UTC/counter timing, lane, positive `fractional_margin`, and the frozen candidate CFO/epoch compatibility rule.

Existing compatibility accounting supplies 8,929 same-probe `both_compatible` windows under the fixed 2.2 µs/10 kHz rule and calibration-only 2,380.14 Hz receiver CFO bias. The prepared `model_rows.json` has 10,369 detection-selected anchor rows over 599 tracks, including receiver identity, observation UTC, margin, candidate-direction provenance, and a physical-pair key. The independent calibration temporal diagnostic confirms 6,034 consecutive within-track pairs over 344 calibration tracks, with 0.120–13.541 s gaps. These are enough to make a bounded crossing model auditable.

They do **not** support SOL's unconditional four-class target outcome. A raw no-detection receiver pair is a complete opportunity, but it has no target label. Existing tracks begin at detections, so neither-receiver windows cannot be assigned to a target without a new training-only frequency/CFO association and forecasting contract. The same issue makes an RX0-only or RX1-only label conditional on its detected anchor, rather than a target-level observation. Do not turn those raw opportunities into `neither` observations or call them full DS7/new-blind validation.

The nominal receiver geometry remains a provisional software-to-physical mapping: RX0 is documented as the west-facing physical RX1 and RX1 as east-facing physical RX2, with nominal ±10° outward tilt and 0.08 m separation. RF phase centers, directed baseline, elevation, world tilt, and survey-grade position are absent. A conditional crossing result can test consistency under that mapping; it cannot calibrate a physical beam or establish location improvement.
