# Root's pre-outcome strong-screen binding

Before timing outcomes, root adopted the well-timed strong cohort as the primary
frequency/availability screen:48independent trials at±0.5sample timing error,
at least90%**admitted and within500Hz**. The±3.5sample cohort is a separate
timing-mismatch stress test with every result retained. No admitted>1kHzerror
is allowed across any strong cohort. This explicitly couples the primary
well-timed accuracy screen to admission availability; the earlier protocol's
complete-frequency count is also reported but is not a substitute.

Both edges, large donor errors and half-bin-origin cases remain. There is no
post-outcome change to score, waveform, forecasts, gates, primary method or
held-validation policy. No timing control outcomes have been opened here.
