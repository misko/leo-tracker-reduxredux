## What these directions must not repeat

Sample-clock time/frequency coupling, causal cross-family orbit forecasts,
Student/coplanar covariance and neighboring-family corrections already have
mixed or negative controls. Lower-noise CFO remeasurement also failed to supply
a qualified accuracy gain. Existing same-family cross-RX differences cancel
useful Doppler and cannot be counted as another independent likelihood term
alongside their constituent CFOs. The 40-degree feed separation is not a
calibrated bearing; every reviewed fast scan is 10 MS/s, so sample-rate causality
remains untested. Generic multistart, blanket prior narrowing, repeated 60108
owner comparisons and unqualified prefix acceptance should remain closed.

Independent observations can test identity without improving geometry, and
different geometry can be useless if its identity is unsupported. Track these
two requirements separately. Preserve matched c=0/fitted-c inputs, candidate
sets, priors and budgets. Any future geographic evaluation must follow frozen
RF decisions and distinguish exposed development scans from a genuinely
exposure-audited untouched block. The old eight later scans are now exposed.
Promotion still needs the user's broader matched DS23/DS24 evidence and adverse
tails/failures, not success on these local examples.
