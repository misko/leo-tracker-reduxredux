of archived alternative peaks against the existing CH5-only prediction. Use
both saved c arms, with no new orbit calculation, parent update, peak choice
or source fit. CH3 and CH4 retain every original point and all stored peaks.

not a receiver term learned from the witness. Verify native IDs and timestamps.

witness prediction support; never extrapolate across its ends. No score or
numerical association criterion is computed from this interpolation.

the selected source residual range plus a fixed 3 kHz margin. All candidate
records remain in the original CSV; view limits do not change input membership.

zero, not calibrated 95% coverage. Other aliases are not selected from witness
responses. Alternative records retain gate/pass and winning-probe provenance;
multiple candidate records may also describe nearly duplicate peaks.

identity, join fault or independent corroboration. Preserve the original trace,
