# Independent assessment and next decisions

This assessment uses saved reports only. It introduces no propagation, fitting,
GPS selection or new scientific branch. The current all-100-point comparison
is a conditional source-coherence experiment, not a localization result.
The subsequent [terminal audit](terminal.json) passes all 67 checks. Both
normalized split-class comparisons have separated global evidence bounds;
this new result localizes predictive compatibility but authenticates no identity.

The strongest measured bottleneck is weak separation of position from orbital
timing and shape. The [matched information diagnostic](../../ds24-bottlenecks-20261024/information/REPORT.md)
gives median curved-only weak-direction scale 534 m, versus 110 m with phase
known and 56 m with phase and shape known; the strongest phase correlation has
median absolute value 0.935. These are conditional model diagnostics, not
achieved errors, calibrated confidence or an irreducible physical floor.
Holding all nuisance coordinates known is an unavailable-information oracle.

The evidence does not identify a single remaining cause. Thirty continuous
restarts within 2.06 cm concern fixed identities, not global discrete search.
The 10 MS/s fast cohort cannot isolate sample-rate causality. Remeasurement and
receiver corrections can improve frequency fit without improving position.
Known-label simulations and same-site deployment results do not establish
identity reliability on winner-selected real observations or new-site accuracy.

Several attractive general fixes have already received direct negative or
mixed tests. The [active-parent uncertainty comparison](../../ds24-parent-orbit-uncertainty-r01/evaluation/FINDINGS.md)
completed 16 fits but qualified none of four research pairs; diagnostic raw
means worsened by 8.412/9.948 m in c0/fitted-c, and operational outputs remained
the reference. The [active-parent dynamic-mode control](../../ds24-ch5-prediction-r01/parent_coupling/summary.json)
has maximum local movement proxy 0.571 m across two families and 20 probes.
It closes that frozen-Gamma local mechanism, not every nonlinear hierarchy.
The [later RX1 panel](../../ds24-phase-shape-validation-r01/validation/report/FINDINGS.md)
retains only 2.744/15.444 m mean gains with worse P95 in both arms, despite
stronger development gains. Repeating broad covariance, receiver drift,
multistart, orphan or soft-identity variants needs a different falsifiable
mechanism, rather than another flexible fit.

Three next decisions have the clearest experimental separation:

1. **Use this specific coherence falsification, then stop broad splitting.** Compare whole
   physical, whole polynomial, normalized physical-portion mixture and
   piecewise-polynomial control on all 100 observations. A split preference
   matters only beyond the piecewise-polynomial gain and declared cut prior.
   If full-domain evidence bounds overlap or the budget ends, retain that
   limitation rather than extend it. Even a resolved split preference locates
   incompatibility; it does not prove graph switching. The [native trace](../ownership/FINDINGS.md)
   establishes no second coherent CH4 continuation near 250 s, and the original
   source was already structure-flagged. A two-physical-source implementation
   should require such independent continuation evidence first. The [duration
   check](../eligibility/FINDINGS.md) also shows the middle prefix is too short
   for the historical 20 s curved-calibration prerequisite; a good prediction
   cannot waive it.

2. **Use a small confusable, exactly matched-law blind-versus-oracle control.**
   The [previous nomination pilot](../../ds24-nomination-information-r01/nomination/FINDINGS.md)
   already ranks truth first in all eight ordinary screens and both exact
   arms. Repeating easy random physical draws is not new evidence. That pilot
   generated under the full-parent conditional but scored after a
   response-dependent cavity, and its broad polynomial nulls were extreme
   mismatches. A useful new control fixes one common generation/scoring
   background and identical candidate priors, aliases, visibility event and
   noise, selects confusable geometry before CFO, and passes only observations
   to ordinary catalogue nomination. Retain missed truth without backfill.
   Compare shortlist loss against an oracle-label diagnostic on the same
   realization; a noisy incorrect winner alone is not an implementation bug.
   Stop after two layouts and a small frozen set if there is no specific
   proposal-versus-scoring discrepancy. This is conditional falsification, not
   a calibrated false-match rate. Fresh orbit priors for an active background
   label require an explicitly exogenous-background toy law, not a claim to
   reproduce shared-family conditioning. Position fits need a separately
   adequate observation panel and unchanged source qualification; a curve-only
   identity toy cannot promise an end-to-end localization diagnosis.

3. **Test additional independent information, after identity competition.**
   The [common-pool allocation study](../../ds24-nomination-information-r01/information/FINDINGS.md)
   selects different information/count winners in three of four scans, but
   predicts only approximately 5–21 m weak-direction SD reductions on
   0010–0012. The larger 0013 reduction relies on unqualified 60108. Compare
   one information-selected complete owner with one length-selected owner
   under the same causal nomination/null competition and compute budget;
   decline a position experiment if identity remains ambiguous or information
   is negligible. Multiple channels of one satellite share orbit uncertainty.
   A different satellite direction and a second native owner address different
   questions. In parallel, a bounded existing-metadata check can establish
   whether calibrated timing, power/pattern or pose supplies a genuinely new
   observable. Nominal feed separation supplies no measured bearing; missing
   calibration is a stop result, not authorization for collection or pooling.

The current root [review](../REVIEW.md) maintains these distinctions. There is
no present evidence to declare noise, wrong association or continuous local
minima the sole limiting cause. Every eventual position comparison must keep
matched c arms, observations, priors and search budgets, seal RF decisions
before geography, and retain exact reference fallbacks and adverse tails.
