## Distance from previous development areas

The simulator moves the receiver by up to two degrees in each coordinate after it draws an area's center. In a disposable replay with integer seed 45, the first center was 1,655 km from the development center at 12° N, 105° E. An allowed receiver coordinate in that box was only 1,385 km away. The earlier check could certify this receiver under the 1,600 km separation rule. The scorer now measures the two revealed receivers against every registered development center and against one another. It also checks the original seed, geographic boxes and pilot edges. The centers approximate previous development locations, including the city-level Austin reference. [Geographic checks](../../../tools/pipeline_eval/qualification/holdout_geography.py) · [Regression](../../../tests/pipeline_eval/test_holdout_geography.py)

New random seeds reserve enough distance for any receiver in either box before synthesizing signals. Moving two degrees per coordinate can travel at most 315 km on the scorer's sphere. The seed draw reserves that margin once relative to a fixed development center and twice between moving receivers. Of the 256 disposable integer seeds from 0 through 255, the earlier selection admitted 250. For 73 of those 250, at least one pair of allowed box corners violated the separation rule. The conservative draw admitted 162 and none had a violating corner pair. These are seed counts, not accuracy estimates. Explicit historical seeds still reproduce the original waveforms. The audit used no reserved seed or recording. [Waveform draw](../../../tools/pipeline_eval/qualification/waveform_site_model.py) · [Scoring protocol](PROTOCOL.md)

## Geographic inputs along the pipeline

The search is two-dimensional. The orbital screen and phase projection use zero receiver height above the reference ellipsoid. At three geographic control points, increasing height by 1 km changed median predicted Doppler by 115–119 Hz. Horizontal position accuracy at elevated physical sites remains unverified. [Height sensitivity and saved orbital evidence](height-sensitivity.md)

We independently exercised a geographic symmetry of the optimized Doppler projection. We reflected the synthetic receiver and all satellite states across the equator and rotated them 90° in longitude. Relative range rate and elevation must stay the same on the reference ellipsoid. The cached projection preserved Doppler within 0.1 microhertz absolute plus a relative tolerance of one part in a trillion, and elevation within 1e−10 degrees, for satellites above and below the horizon. All eight component tests passed. This tests the projection in isolation. The current public input contract accepts local squares that stay clear of the poles and the longitude seam. [Symmetry test](../../../tests/pipeline_eval/test_algo08_cavity_projection.py) · [Accepted region domain](../../../src/leo/contracts/algo08_region.py)

The active `shared-tables` candidate takes its search square and orbit screening center from the required runtime offer. The audit found a qualification gap after inference. An otherwise valid terminal digest could carry a different screening center from the assigned offer, and the verifier would accept the terminal's arms. The verifier now checks the terminal's full region against the invocation receipt. This changes evidence verification only.
