# Northern near-edge regression comes from the collapsed solver

The guarded Q15 receiver produces the same solver measurements as the original guarded receiver on this public northern recording. Replaying the combined build's exact saved Q15 detections through guarded `shared-tables` reproduced the original positions, solver RF RMS, 302 owner mappings per arm, full numerical grid and optimizer path. The switch to `collapsed-repair` accounts for the observed **70 m fixed-zero** and **72 m fitted** increases in horizontal error. This comparison isolates the solver mode as a whole. The publication receipts do not identify which inner collapsed or repair branch generated the selected pair.
