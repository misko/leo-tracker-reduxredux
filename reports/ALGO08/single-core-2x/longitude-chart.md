# Longitude handling in ALGO08v2 discovery and the terminal guard

The input contract accepts a local search square wholly on one side of the antimeridian and allows its independent screening center on the other side. Discovery computes nominal orbit projections directly in the search frame. For satellites with nonzero phase hypotheses, `SearchRegion.native_point` converts the anchor to the screening frame and `ScanLikelihood.latlon` converts it back before the shifted projection. The sparse candidate projection uses this same inverse. The checked-in and released versions of both coordinate classes matched byte for byte in this audit. [Region contract](../../../src/leo/contracts/algo08_region.py) · [Search frame](../../../src/leo/analysis/localization_v3/region.py) · [Scan frame](../../../src/leo/analysis/localization_v3/scan.py) · [Sparse projection](../../../tools/pipeline_eval/variants/algo08_sparse_rank_projection.py)

At 32° N, 25 km squares centered at ±179.6° with screens at ∓179.8° gave native center eastings of ±33,929.054 km. The conversion back to geographic coordinates almost canceled the large offset. Across the 25 anchors of each square, the largest difference from direct compact projection was 3.51 × 10⁻⁹ Hz in Doppler, 6.54 × 10⁻¹³ degrees in elevation, and 4.19 × 10⁻¹¹ Hz/s in the phase derivative. The prototype returned center eastings of ∓56.643 km, with corresponding maxima of 1.27 × 10⁻⁹ Hz, 3.13 × 10⁻¹³ degrees, and 1.55 × 10⁻¹¹ Hz/s. These differences are measured against direct projection of the same synthetic orbit states and phases. [Compact projector](../../../tools/pipeline_eval/variants/orbit_projector.py)

The large offset can cause a hard failure at a legal search face. At 2° N, a 25 km square centered at ±179.88764265806918° with an opposite screen at ∓179.85° has its far face at ±179.99999999999997°. The native round trip produced ±180.00000000000003°. Both the compact and stock projectors explicitly check this range. With the released compact projector, the original discovery ranking raised `geodetic coordinate outside domain` on both mirrored fixtures. With the prototype it ranked one candidate in each fixture. The separately measured corner nomination retained the synthetic orbit, recovered a 0.755379 s phase from a 0.75 s injection, and produced 3.240 Hz RMS in both signs. The fixtures use 61 synthetic frequency samples and orbit states with one active candidate. The ranking exercised `v5_core.ranked_initial_nodes`, its `discovery.explore` calls, dense association reuse, sparse shifted projection and the compact numerical bindings. The terminal guard has a separate seam failure. It samples exterior positions around all four faces, so a legal square can have probes whose longitude needs wrapping regardless of the screening center. The unwrapped probe code aborts the entire diagnostic as soon as one longitude leaves its numeric range. `inspect_boundary` records the diagnostic error and leaves the selected decision in place. On both synthetic squares above, the prototype let the existing scorer evaluate 48 inside and 48 exterior points, including probes on the opposite side of the antimeridian. Both receivers picked the injected exterior point at ±62.5 km east and −5 km north, 50 km beyond the face, and the terminal decision was to withhold. The gain per receiver was 1.0715 for the negative-longitude square and 1.3208 for the positive-longitude square on the guard's normalized score. The fixture substitutes deterministic ECEF states at the propagation boundary and exercises the real horizon filter, phase projection, orbit-mixture score, and terminal decision. The prototype uses the shortest signed longitude difference for the native screen-frame offset and takes an equivalent longitude only when a chart conversion leaves the accepted range. The ordinary-region test compares chart coordinates byte for byte over the full 25-point grid and checks all 96 guard probes for equality. The public region contract, frozen candidate bundle, and deployment were left as published. The audit ran on synthetic states and frequencies without capture, archive, waveform holdout, or publication replay.
