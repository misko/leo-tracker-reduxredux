# Prespecified Doppler revision direction test

The earlier DSX01 experiment included a one-column `old_new` orbital nuisance per supported satellite. It aligned the preceding state trajectory by its along-orbit phase and projected a perturbation from the state difference. The current ALGO08 marginal likelihood uses three physical element modes. The public mirrored-face study used nominal causal trajectories, exact later-orbit controls, whole-satellite weights from historical disagreement, and a common yaw test. This comparison isolates one exact retarded latest-minus-previous Doppler direction per NORAD on those already fixed faces. [Earlier DSX01 arm](../../../tools/prototype_dsx01_orbit_marginal.py) · [State-space modes](../../../src/leo/analysis/localization_v3/orbit_modes.py) · [Current marginal design](../../../src/leo/analysis/localization_v3/marginal.py) · [Whole-satellite weights](mirrored-orbit-weight.md) · [Yaw test](mirrored-yaw.md)

Use the existing northern and southern causal curve lock and all its tracks, satellite owners, frequencies, observation epochs and A/B coordinates. Build the historical revision column for each satellite from its latest-minus-previous retarded Doppler at the common midpoint. Use the same preceding element selection and single-receiver stereo differential RMS as the earlier weight lock. Restrict each column to observations owned by that satellite. Keep the nominal A and B templates, point weights and ordinary nuisance design fixed. Remove the same unpenalized track offsets, two receiver slopes and per-NORAD causal phase tangents from the observations, templates and revision columns. A track intercept absorbs the constant fitted RF calibration term, so fixed-zero and fitted `c` have identical scores in this diagnostic. [Causal prediction lock](measurements/mirrored-causal-prediction.json.gz) · [Historical revision and stereo lock](measurements/mirrored-orbit-weights.json)

Use independent zero-mean unit-variance revision coefficients and the measured stereo noise RMS `σ` at each site. A coefficient of magnitude one represents one observed historical revision. For residual `r` and projected design `Z`, compare the ordinary squared loss with `min_a ||r − Za||² + σ² ||a||²`. This is the Gaussian covariance score with covariance `σ²I + ZZᵀ` on the projected space, up to its shared normalization and scale. Use the same covariance and mean construction for A and B. The frozen recipe uses no fitted multiplier, stage-specific gate, satellite mass or vote threshold. [Stereo scale definition](mirrored-ephemeris.md)

Lock the revision columns and saved observed frequencies before evaluating the later orbit. Score each noiseless later-orbit face against both causal templates as a diagnostic. Also score the observed Graph frequencies after one midpoint-based alias lift, conditional on the nominal A and B independently choosing the same lifts on every row. Keep the measured waveform readout separate from known A/B oracle classes. Report the penalized score and fitted frequency RMS separately. Report retained information on the nominal face direction as the ratio of its new and ordinary quadratic forms. Drop each NORAD in turn and recompute the same nuisance projection and scoring without changing the stereo scale or unit prior; report the class counts and range of A/B loss differences. The leave-one-orbit-out check measures influence in the fixed waveform. It does not retrain the historical model or add independent geographies.

Advance the model only if both noiseless faces are separated at both sites and their preferences persist under every single-orbit omission. The two public simulations share one later generator revision. Even a successful oracle test cannot support a publication decision on observed frequencies from this comparison. No new waveform, raw RF, reserved site, or full solver enters the study.
