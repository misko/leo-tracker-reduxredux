## Neither forecast recovered the other face

The latest nominal TLE, signed phase mean and rolling state extrapolation all chose A under both noiseless counterfactuals at both sites. Both corrections increased the midpoint orbit mismatch. The nuisance projection removes the local linear phase direction. An exact finite phase shift can still change the score through higher-order geometry and a different phase derivative away from the midpoint. The full-state continuation extrapolates differences between two orbital solutions and can magnify an isolated revision. Neither result establishes the behavior of a new robust fit or orbit predictor.
