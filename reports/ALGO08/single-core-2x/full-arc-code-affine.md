The released waveform generator specifies one transmit phase per emitter and one physical ADC clock per capture. It computes the pilot epoch with that phase and retarded path delay, and the local receive period with radial velocity. Transmitter carrier offset, receiver LO drift and RF calibration enter the carrier term after the delay calculation. No independent per-emitter frame-rate perturbation is planted. The separate IQ carrier phase is randomized within each window and leaves the pilot envelope epoch intact. A fitted per-emitter delay rate therefore diagnoses a model discrepancy. It does not measure a free transmitter code clock in the simulator. Both directions produced the same measured curvature separation. The free-rate result profiles out roughly four-fifths of the previous held template separation. The remaining position signal is close to the measured carrier closure and well below the causal shape error.

For face A, the fitted emitter rates differ from the common-clock rate by −4.559 to +1.388 samples/s in the north and −3.935 to +0.974 in the south. An affine nuisance fit can absorb these differing trends even though the simulator uses one ADC clock. That fit uses the earlier robust circular routine, while the bounded common-rate fit maximizes training concentration. The held improvement therefore measures the effect of the complete affine diagnostic, with no exact decomposition of the difference into an orbit term and estimator term. The direct carrier comparison supplies independent support for the remaining relative shape discrepancy within the same waveform. The carrier's large integer-alias slopes are treated as a constant rate ambiguity. After subtracting the nearest alias for reporting only, the remaining fitted slopes are −0.390 to +1.326 and −0.780 to +0.400 samples/s. No corresponding alias choice enters the geometric scores.

Positive delay remains preferable to both reflected templates and the null in each site. The affine null RMS is 373.314 and 356.733 samples and the two reflected fits range from 744.853 to 744.909 and 712.948 to 712.951, respectively. The positive measured-carrier result remains 0.079 and 0.091 samples while its reflected control remains 745.220 and 713.619. These controls establish the physical shape sign much more clearly than the 200 m location contrast.

Both positive fits prefer B over A and choose the easternmost line point. The northern point is inside both original squares. The southern point is outside both. Both estimates reach the fixed search extent, making them unresolved under the preregistered rule. The leave-one-satellite-out A/B contrast ranges from −0.185 to +0.027 samples² in the north and −0.119 to +0.019 in the south. Neither site separates its two offers after the deletions. This leaves the full-arc code measurable and the specific causal boundary rule unsupported even after removing per-emitter trends.
