# Predeclared 200 m counterfactual noise experiment

Use the two released northern and southern simulations and the 200 m face positions A and B already frozen in the causal prediction lock. A and B lie on opposite sides of the face midway between the two shifted squares. Give the two synthetic alternatives equal prior mass. This measures local classification of two specified positions. The published containing offers and known positions play no role in assigning receiver noise or choosing a score. The earlier containing-offer owner maps, track selection and tangential coordinate are already planted-design aids. They make this a conditional experiment. [Published causal prediction lock](measurements/mirrored-causal-prediction.json.gz)

Recover only exact simultaneous receiver pairs with the same satellite, channel and time from the pinned public saved detections. Evaluate each position using the stereo average and a common nuisance space with the earlier track offsets, receiver clock slopes and per-satellite causal phase tangents. Average the corresponding design rows and remove their joint column space from every prediction. The separate fixed-RF track offsets absorb the fitted calibration column. Pairwise subtraction uses the difference of the same design rows. Use the wrapped receiver difference divided by two, with these difference nuisance terms removed, as a proxy for noise in the stereo average. This equality of variances requires equal-variance receiver errors without common noise. The proxy suppresses common RF, orbit, timing and waveform errors.

Score by the exact nearest-template squared-error rule in the shared projected space. Compare the fixed nominal capture-causal templates and the exact later generator orbit templates. Predictions and noise inputs must be locked before the scorer opens the later generator orbit. Generate synthetic data about each later-orbit face in turn. The oracle template pair uses the exact later orbit mean. The causal pair uses only the capture-causal mean. Report both true-position errors separately and their worst value.

Use these two prescribed receiver-noise controls with the same template comparisons.

1. Independent Gaussian errors at the measured stereo-proxy RMS on each paired epoch. Compute exact conditional class risks analytically, including the separation and signed noiseless margins. For the oracle and this noise model, the likelihood-ratio rule minimizes the balanced error.
2. Hold the signed noise trace within every satellite intact. Enumerate all independent whole-satellite sign flips of those frozen traces and compare the same squared-error rule, allowing half an error for ties. Report the resulting synthetic class risks and the score variation. This imposes no independent samples within a satellite. Independence and sign symmetry between satellites are assumptions of this control.

Evaluate the two noise models at their measured scales only. Report nominal and oracle confusion separately for the four endpoint/site combinations. Other orbit corrections, alternative owners and systematic errors are outside this experiment. Do not select a membership threshold, tune a noise multiplier, rerun a full solver or change the inference policy based on these outcomes. A/B positions are synthetic alternatives on these two waveforms. No operational claim about withheld valid fixes or calibrated confidence follows from the risks.
