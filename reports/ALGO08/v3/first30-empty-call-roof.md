# A perfect empty-lane detector screen falls short on the first 30 field pairs

Give the current successor a free oracle that skips the entire 8 ms native call on every lane that would return empty. Pricing those calls by the most favorable observed same-binary empty-to-positive cost ratio saves **154.524 s**, leaving **0.52174 paired median** and **0.55312 summed** elapsed ratios versus installed ALGO08v3. Both exceed the 0.5 gates. Even granting every empty 20 ms full-anchor call at the most favorable same-binary ratio gives **0.50762 / 0.53875**. This is a transfer of measured call costs to the observed population. The first-30 native clocks have aperture resolution only. ## What was actually paid

The sealed field export has 164,747 issued stereo visits, of which 14,486 were invalid and already did no native detector work. Both radios were admitted on each of the other 150,261 visits. Every eighth admitted visit of each original pilot-edge, channel and actual-RF stream used the original full anchor, resuming from the first visit in that stream. All 30 saved receivers reached the `unfiltered` EOF selection with no further detector or fixed-epoch remeasurement. The reducer checks the signed native digest, raw authority, aperture recipe, counts, exclusions and the independent first-30 match census. The callback clocks bracket each individual radio's native invocation. The full invocation can include an internal residual scan. Two radios therefore mean two native calls and a skipped stereo visit means two original calls only when both radios are empty. The short ABI returns at most one verified factor, so an empty packet entry under this unfiltered route corresponds to native output count zero. The original full ABI can return two verified factors. The full-anchor scenarios generously assume that an oracle proves the entire two-scan result empty and preserves the original cadence and future selector state. The observed successor used 1,696.681 s wall in total, 1,043.227 s in authenticated acquisition and 477.043 s in its native calls inside acquisition. Those process CPU counters may charge background prefetch activity while the native call is live. The per-call elapsed intervals are serialized on the foreground worker. Deleting one does not automatically delete its full observed elapsed time when part is overlapped or descheduled. In the table each saving is granted in full to the corresponding original process and every other interval is held at its measured cost.

All outcomes remain in the median, summed and per-case gates. No failure is replaced with a nominal fix time, and independent product verification stays charged where the actual field workers paid it. ## Through-exit savings with the original fixed costs

Each case's timed short and full spans are allocated using its own empty and positive counts. Donor sensitivities preserve those observed field totals and change the fraction allocated to empties, independently within each aperture. The stereo-only rows give credit only for the two empty calls on a both-empty visit. The direct donor estimate chooses the largest observed mean empty-to-positive ratio for each aperture to favor the oracle. The extremes assign every empty call the largest observed donor elapsed and every positive call the smallest, then use that ratio to allocate each measured field aperture. The extrema stress uses 1.517× for short and 0.986× for full. They are measured sample extrema and do not bound arbitrary field scheduling or every recording. The zero screening charge in all these models also favors the proposed change.

With all anchors paid, the summed gate requires saving **302.627 s** from the measured **328.265 s** short envelope, or **92.19%**. That would require the pooled 138,258 empty calls to average at least **2.189 ms** each and the 124,512 positives at most **0.206 ms** each, a minimum **10.630×** ratio. The donated clocks consistently show a ratio around 0.79 on these physical scopes. Whole-short elapsed includes positive calls and can clear both gates when removed completely. Thus aperture totals alone leave a mathematical possibility of extreme concentration, while available per-call evidence strongly rejects it. Removing only short process CPU and holding other wall fixed cannot clear the sum, even with all positives given away.

The generous dual-aperture oracle at the favorable observed mean rates leaves **108.044 s** over the half-total of **1,394.054 s**, or **7.19%** of its residual 1,502.098 s and **6.37%** of the measured successor wall. The paired median is also still above 0.5. Any case-weighted savings in the surviving positive detector, input traversal or inference would have to close that remaining gap. Even the donor-extrema stress leaves 22.946 s over half-total.

## Existing screens and measured acceptance

The frozen three-origin waveform screen retained every positive on its real 8 ms dense and held physical comparisons but skipped **0 of 92** empty short lanes. It cost 1.492 and 1.170 ms CPU on the two tested scopes. Per-member hindsight separation adds only 0/51 and 2/41 empties respectively. The two omissions depend on a cutoff chosen from the same member's outcomes. The older repeat-negative screen on 20/12 removed 12 of 41 empty lanes on its held member while losing 13 real selected factors. Its mixed full-recall hindsight cutoff skipped zero. The 12 ms cyclic-comb shape cut missed 3/21 and 4/34 physical positives and skipped only 2/11 and 0/2 empties. Those earlier apertures and ABIs give no direct current-8 ms cost or qualification. [Current 8 ms screen](three-origin-negative.md) · [Repeat-negative](repeat-negative.md) · [Cyclic comb](cyclic-presence.md)

Late-pilot contrast has the required current detector clocks but consumes an already paid short miss before triggering a possible additional 20 ms retry. It removed no initial calls. On the pre-enrolled held scopes, its nominal-lag statistic recovered fewer installed matches than displaced-lag, time or hash controls. The earlier 12 ms stereo mask reported an empty-lane oracle on one dense recording. The population, signed stereo pairing and cost weighting here use the current frozen receiver and all 30 outcomes. The archived evidence provides no deployable negative rule with measured nonzero exact savings on the current native route.
