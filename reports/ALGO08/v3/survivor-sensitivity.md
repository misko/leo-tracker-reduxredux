# A cold-reference sensitivity check misses the hard signed finish

The frozen check accepted the warm fit on held DS30-0402 even though it added **20.20 m at `c = 0` and 19.19 m at fitted `c`** to horizontal error versus named v3. It rejected the same-roster warm fit on held dense DS30-0008, where retaining that fit would have added **3.77 / 3.65 m** versus the current original finish and saved approximately **2.80 CPU s at signed refit**. The measured stress step was smaller in both 0402 arms than in their dense counterparts, so a single monotone movement threshold cannot separate these held results.
