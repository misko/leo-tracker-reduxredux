# Test of a local satellite-jackknife exterior veto

The previous fixed-owner profile, unbounded membership check and local curvature ellipse lost valid boundary fixes or missed 100 m omissions. Test whether a one-step influence calculation from the installed v3 signed fit supports a positive exterior finding while preserving valid fixes. The rule uses one independent group per NORAD and gives both calibration arms the same observations, original candidate set, nuisance model and computation. Repeat the local calculation independently for each arm. [Earlier exterior profile](../single-core-2x/exterior-profile.md) · [Earlier ellipse](../single-core-2x/mirrored-uncertainty.md)

Use the two already published v3 simulated development waveforms and matched public 25 km longitude-face offers. Within a waveform, keep the exact saved native observations and capture-time archive fixed and move the offer and its original screen center together by 200 m. Replay the installed v3 signed fit once for each offered square because its selected nuisance context is not persisted. Include the earlier wider-clearance containing offer at each site and use the archived installed-v3 northern far withholding as a control. Require both inside replays to reproduce their signed endpoints, RF RMS, owner digests and objective replay. Keep both solvers' ordinary grid budgets and compare owner maps across offers. The inference code receives only the signed likelihood, its endpoint and the offered square. Score positions against the known public references after collecting the inference results.

For each arm, let `J` be the selected negative-twice variational objective. Central differences at 25 m estimate gradient `g` and Hessian `H`. Use 50 m as a numerical check. Require both Hessians positive definite, both projected scales within 10%, the two unrestricted Newton estimates to agree within 0.1 fine-step likelihood standard deviations along each coordinate, and the fine-step displacement to stay within one eighth of the offered width. Check that directly evaluating `J` at the fine-step estimate agrees with its quadratic prediction within 10% of the larger of one objective unit and the predicted improvement. A failed check yields unresolved.

Delete all likelihood contributions from one NORAD at a time, including both receivers, all tracks and any predictive entries. Reprofile the original receiver and orbit nuisance terms on the remaining data with the same phase expansion and the same owners and aliases of retained observations. Verify that rebuilding with no deletions reproduces the full local stencil. Approximate each deleted position by `x − H⁻¹g₍₋sat₎` with the original endpoint `x` and full Hessian. Use the ordinary delete-one jackknife standard error on each horizontal coordinate. Require at least four satellite groups. The scale is the larger of that standard error and the corresponding marginal standard deviation from `2H⁻¹`. This is a sensitivity calculation conditional on the selected mode and common catalogue. Its use of a small-sample t multiplier does not establish coverage in the presence of a common orbit or receiver error.

For each arm, apply the t multiplier with `G−1` degrees of freedom and one-sided tail probability `0.05/8` to the signed distance from each face, allowing both tails across four faces. An arm certifies exterior if its upper clearance limit for a face is strictly below zero. It certifies inside if every lower clearance limit is strictly above zero. A paired veto requires both arms to certify exterior at the same face. Otherwise retain the installed decision and count unresolved outcomes separately from affirmative containment. Advance the rule only if it retains both near-edge containing fixes, withholds both 100 m omissions and preserves both centered controls and the archived far-withhold. The rule has no site-dependent thresholds. Report frequency RMS and horizontal error separately, plus owner counts, added gate CPU time, uncensored estimated clearance, likelihood and satellite-jackknife scale, and the decision for each arm.

Quantify the information scale by reporting 200 m divided by the face-normal likelihood standard deviation. As an optimistic conditional Gaussian comparison for two known positions 200 m apart on that axis, report the equal-prior error and highest power at 5% false-withhold rate. This comparison describes the local model with no orbit bias. The two recorded alternatives at each site share the same RF observations and differ in their offered square.
