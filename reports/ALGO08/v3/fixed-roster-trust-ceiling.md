The installed bounded secant already linearizes at the original exact residual, reuses that derivative across up to three radius retries, and carries a rank-one secant update through up to four successful moves. It evaluates the original robust residual on each trial and refreshes on poor agreement or to confirm a small step. Extending the lifetime of one local linearization or making coarse proposals over several radii can change future trial locations. To preserve the accepted decisions, each potentially accepted state still needs the full released loss and a convergence check. A proposal-only screen may safely avoid paying for a specific rejected point only when it can certify rejection under the same loss. The fixed owners and signed aliases do not fix the fitted nuisance, Gamma precisions or predictive covariance as the coordinates or phase reference move.
