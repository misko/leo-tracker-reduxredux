## Candidate handoff before the original final fit

The original `history_policy` produces a qualified `curved` pair and shared `keep` from `history_fit`. It uses the fixed-zero curved endpoint to select predictive `entries` and then calls `linear_refit(scan, node, keep, powers, means, model, arm, entries)`. The offered roster and its signed likelihood inputs are available at that call. A paired research hook could take each earlier curved endpoint and clip that arm's already computed posterior phases in the same family label order, retaining the pre-RF mean and weight model. Those values exist before the named final fit and require no survey or archived position. The hook would need to charge the original cold pair on a failed warm attempt, preserve `paired_policy`, and then run the original signed and cavity stages. [Named family policy](../../../src/leo/analysis/localization_v3/history_policy.py)

The complete publication roster is conditional on the later gates. The original `paired_policy` may reject the predictive roster after both linear fits, `signed_context` can adopt an adjusted phase fit, and the cavity stage can change family selection after seeing the chosen fit. Thus the earlier hook supplies an exact offered roster, while matching the final published roster and regional decision needs a separate equivalence check. The warm seeds used in this crossed diagnostic came from the already executed cumulative repair. The earlier named curved pair is a valid causal seed source for a future test, with unmeasured convergence and owner equivalence.
