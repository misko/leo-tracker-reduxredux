# What it will take to halve ALGO08v3 runtime

The current complete warm comparisons total **13.881 s for named ALGO08v3 and 10.644 s for the mixed 20/12 ms research receiver and solver**, or **1.30×**. The median recording took 79.9% of its v3 time. The largest downstream gain is **3.05× on saved detections** for one expensive solve. The raw and downstream experiments used different candidate configurations.

## Ranked next experiments

The first three fit together. Choose how to find timing from the waveform, reduce the cost of escalating an uncertain result, then check that the fast solver produces the position supported by those measurements. The other suggestions have narrower scope or a different deployment horizon.

## End-to-end budget and transfer

On 52 historical fixes with stage clocks, acquisition accounted for **67.1%**, solving **30.1%**, and publication through exit **2.8%** of elapsed time. On the 55-recording budget model, which charges the three unsplit processes in full, reducing acquisition by 55% and solving by 50% yields a median ratio of **0.480** and summed ratio of **0.497**. Reducing acquisition by 55% and solving by 60% yields **0.451 / 0.468**. These are elapsed-time scenarios. An implementation must save the waits as well as the compute needed to achieve those stage times. [Historical model and assumptions](second-doubling-frontier.md)

Prioritize one saved weak and one dense receiver prefix and one independent saved solver transfer. Advance the combination to a few complete original recordings only after it clears the paid component budgets. Freeze the algorithm before the geographic checks, use supplied containing and missing regions, and measure fix/withhold outcomes and horizontal errors under both calibrations. The current geographic safeguard missed both held 1.5 km omissions. DS30 alone cannot establish transfer to another roof. [Held geographic checks](local-exterior-descent.md)
