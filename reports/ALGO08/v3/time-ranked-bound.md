# Time-ordered refinement of cold satellite ranking

An adaptive time bound can screen the **fixed-state white association** without changing winners. It cannot remove the full-time rank inputs upstream or the coupled robust scores downstream. On the original dense and medium rank tapes, a hindsight screen that removed every unprotected row *after* the active catalogue groups would save at most **44.2 ms / 10.0 ms under the existing row-proportional cost model**, before its own work. The remaining original-height multiply also stays paid. The small estimated ceiling and the earlier negative real-epoch timing end this direction without a fresh numerical job. ## What an adaptive bound could prove

At one actual `ScanLikelihood.association` state, hold the orbit projection, RF, receiver calibration, times and track noise fixed. For a candidate residual `rᵢ = yᵢ − fᵢ`, use the centered squared energy `E(S) = Σ(rᵢ − mean(r over S))²` over the chronological observations opened so far. The constant alias and Gaussian bias both cancel under centering. Merging two disjoint subsets adds their two energies and the nonnegative between-means term `n₁n₂/(n₁+n₂) × (mean₁−mean₂)²`. Opening additional real observations cannot weaken the bound in real arithmetic. A refinement can prioritize long chronological intervals and reuse their exact count, mean and centered energy; it need not interpolate predicted Doppler or choose an alias from a subset. At fixed white scale `σ`, `E(S)/σ²` lower-bounds the full Gaussian cost, which has extra nonnegative bias prior and volume. Separately, `E(S)` lower-bounds the unnormalized native residual energy used by the 450 Hz RMS gate. The bias gate, independent original chronological noise checks, and full visibility still apply to survivors. For two candidates, the only useful order proof is the first candidate's outward-rounded upper cost below the second's outward-rounded lower cost. A complete independently evaluated visible candidate supplies the upper cost. A shortlist with low estimated scores supplies no upper bound by itself. [Original white profile](../../../src/leo/analysis/research/retained_track_likelihood.py) · [Original association and gates](../../../src/leo/analysis/localization_v3/scan.py)

Reject a candidate only after a proven invisible epoch, or after the padded lower bound beats the full visible incumbent and independently forces failure of the native RMS gate. The second condition keeps every potentially compatible competitor because `association` records the feasible count and later support rules can alter the eligible minimum. Preserve the original row-zero all-invisible fallback, both minima, original first-index ties and exact full-row alias rounding. A new binary64 implementation would require outward padding through all subtractions, means and updates or original replay. In particular, combining `E(S)` with the current grouped contrast uses their maximum unless the subspaces are proven orthogonal. Adding two overlapping lower bounds is unsound. For unresolved candidates the original full scorer still supplies signed aliases, noise diagnostics, residuals and order. Candidate-dependent geometry or unsupported numeric state must still run normally. For the optional correlated path, a contiguous chronological prefix of the original lower-triangular inverse Cholesky does give a partial whitened-energy sum. To exploit other intervals without reading all intervening rows would require a noise-aware projection, for example the inverse of the selected covariance marginal, along with its paid setup. The scalar white centered energy does not transfer to arbitrary colored intervals. At the later `NormalPosition` score, each finalist changes shared nuisance columns, robust rates and posterior covariance. Its actual objective includes normalization, elevation and prior terms. Fixed per-track white score intervals cannot certify that joint ranking or replace a final refit. ## Cost on existing rank tapes

The full DS30-0008 and paid DS30-0016 comparison gives the broad original white-association ceiling before grouped compaction. The stage consumed **1.865 / 0.026 CPU s**. Its existing real-observation contrasts retained all final decisions but moved complete ordered rank from **5.085 to 5.106 s** on dense and **0.123 to 0.128 s** on the weak packet. The independently archived four-observation North near-edge control also lost to exact visibility-first scoring, **2.113 vs 0.957 ms** for the original profile boundary. Neither nomination certificate uses a published fix. In the more direct full-state dense and medium comparison, geometry-selected actual times plus exact fallback added **0.561 / 0.195 CPU s** at four epochs. Even zero-cost correct exclusion in that test saved only **0.204 / 0.065 s**, before accounting for overlap with the newer exact groups. The earlier fixed 16-knot whole-solver approximation failed dense compatibility. Restoring complete final rows after its altered discovery did not repair it. ## Next rank operator with a measured cost

The original cold projection entry `orbit_projector.Projector.evaluate` took **0.569696 CPU s exclusive** across 20 calls in the dense mixed saved profile, **0.589295 s inclusive**. This is a substantial remaining named geometry leaf after removing catalogue rows and is independent of the temporal residual bound. The two clocks are separate witnesses. The 20-call profile also includes shifted calls, so 0.570 s is an upper bound on applicable work, before charging setup, rather than a corner-pair saving. The larger dense cold leaf, native robust posterior at 1.565 s exclusive, requires a separate optimization with its original nonlinear outputs intact. [Measured exclusive cold profile](measurements/cold-exact-budget-profile.json.gz) · [Exact centre-sharing attribution](blind-rank.md)

One site-neutral implementation worth isolating is **a native two-observer nominal stencil kernel** for the original independent corner projections. The released `project_orbit_grid` repeats orbit interpolation and rotation for nominal, `+step` and `−step` positions and velocities at every satellite/time cell for every observer. At the same immutable scan stencil, these three state pairs and their finite differences are observer-independent. After the best centre state is available, project one original pair of corners from that common state tile, preserving the per-observer operation order, exact elevation output and derivative. Keep original shifted calls and the four original corner exploration and tie order. This differs from memoizing complete requests, which have distinct site coordinates, and from the geographic expansion that reassociates floating operations. Prove complete f/e/h array parity under the stock C flags and charge fresh allocation and retention of the second full plane alongside the native call. A bounded second plane might offset the saved arithmetic or be inappropriate under the original memory budget. No such transfer or gain is measured here.
