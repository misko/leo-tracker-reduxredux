# Complete-packet tradeoffs for a cheaper receiver or solver

The measured cases support full-temporal 20/12 ms with the installed graph as the dense development control. It changed measurements and owners and improved both dense positions over named v3. The cheaper approximations with enough projected work to matter lost position accuracy or paid the gain back in the solver. A new saved 8 ms history check recovered the same 456 signed North lanes as 12 ms, but its measured short-primary advantage was only **0.343 CPU s**. Even a favorable transfer to dense leaves **2.332 s gross**, below the **3.490 s** acquisition saving required in the current frozen dense model with the rest held fixed. The evidence supports no new high-headroom route to take to an unused case. ## Signal-only aperture fallback

An 8 ms pass could choose history where both radios' original held cycles support it and otherwise buy 12 ms. The archived complete packets allow a pre-fit check without a new receiver pass. The released gate uses only measured masks and signed CFO chronology, with 20 ms anchors hidden from training and scored as held outcomes. On North, both apertures choose history. Moving from 12 to 8 ms changes the two log score gains from **+28.366/+26.345** to **+92.630/+83.174 nats**. Both project exactly the same 456 signed opportunities, with **456/456** rank-zero matches within two wrapped samples and 250 Hz. The 60 retained full anchors match exactly. Short CFO changes contribute **6.538 Hz RMS across the 456 shared factors**, with 22.814 Hz maximum. These measurements isolate an explanation for the earlier 8 ms unfiltered failure. The more selective route removes its excess admissions on this North fixture. It still lacks a measured position or withholding result at the changed signed frequencies. On weak DS30-0016 both apertures choose unfiltered, with **−15.273/−8.948** at 12 ms and **−25.287/−11.749** at 8 ms. The unfiltered 8 ms run is the one that improved both weak errors. History agreement on the held radio experiment measures detection predictability and cannot certify a correct location. [New signed opportunity replay](measurements/approximate-route-pareto.json) · [Existing held-cycle method](anchor-cycle-route.md)

| North | 2.200 → 1.857 s | 0.343 s | 15.58% |

The thresholds divide the observed direct short-primary saving by the measured cost of the full 12 ms short-primary retry. They grant every anchor, decoder and archive operation already shared, charge zero for scheduling, and omit the storage retention or reread and second fit needed for a post-fit retry. They assume equal average retry cost. A fixed complete-recording early choice would still need a physical evidence signal before buying both apertures. Selecting history cannot fill that role. Even under a zero-cost chooser between the two measured whole-aperture processes, weak's best would be 8.639 s and North's faster 12 ms aperture would be 13.633 s before the current guarded improvements. Neither reaches its named half-time baseline. The favorable **2.332 s** dense transfer comes from scaling the best observed **18.45%** short-primary reduction to the authentic complete **12.635 s** dense short-call pool, with no measured dense 8 ms detector packet or additional solver credit. This configuration is plausible as a local refinement of the North receiver. No held case trial was warranted as a high-headroom path.
