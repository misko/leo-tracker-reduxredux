# Neighbor-warm Gamma convergence on the genuine dense grid

A tighter warm convergence rule increased native posterior CPU from **105.476 to 127.842 ms** across 24 original arm-and-location calls on genuine short DS30-0008. All calls converged and returned a checked posterior factor. The warm scores stayed within **2.84×10⁻⁷** of a tighter cold reference. They exceeded the prespecified **1×10⁻⁵** difference from the archived publication objective at four sites in each calibration arm. Both the timing and numerical comparison stopped at the original 12 points per arm. [Frozen protocol](grid-neighbor-gamma-protocol.md) · [Per-call receipt](measurements/grid-neighbor-gamma.json.gz)

The four-point local sweeps saved two updates and 0.406 ms across both arms. The central and southern-face sweeps added 32 and 44 updates, respectively. Three fresh points per arm started from the original prior rates, and nine accepted both rate vectors from an adjacent point in the same sweep. Every mode retained the same ordering of the 12 sampled objectives and the same sampled minimizer in each arm. The score includes the original curved negative twice ELBO, complete reduced predictive integration and original elevation penalty. No change to a full-grid ordering, fix, or withholding decision was tested.

The installed `nb_run_posterior` ABI hard-initializes the noise and prior rates and uses a `1e-5` stopping threshold. It has no iterate input and returns final noise rates and prior precisions, alongside its checked triangular factor. The prototype adds optional noise and prior rate inputs, an exact final prior-rate copy, and a tolerance argument. The original robust operations and factor export still run for every point. The earlier warm and profiled Gamma trials changed ordinary fitting. The genuine-short Gamma transfer deliberately bypassed those changes during the grid. This check exercises the exported factor and real predictive objective. The tighter log-rate stop is an empirically checked tolerance on these points. It does not certify objective error on unsampled points.
