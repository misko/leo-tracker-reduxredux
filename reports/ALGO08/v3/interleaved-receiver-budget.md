# What can a receiver spend on the visits it skips?

Keep the original full anchor at ordinal zero in each eight-visit cycle, separately for each edge, tuning and RF. Measure the even-numbered visits on both radios. Withhold the original short callbacks on odd-numbered visits. For the first 30 and the next 16 original DS30 pairs, model the omitted time using the observed per-recording mean short-callback wall time. Keep every other interval at its measured time. Separately consider unchanged solver time and the previously published 1.4× whole-solver target. Include incomplete final cycles and all outcomes. Use the observed lane counts to bound the omitted count without reopening any raw capture.

Report summed and median paired wall speedups if all replacement work is free, followed by the maximum extra wall microseconds per omitted lane that satisfy both 2× gates. The replacement allowance includes any extra track building, second buffer access, frequency/timing search and independent verification. Candidate positions are held fixed in this timing calculation. A new receiver also needs measured coverage and position tests. [Prior two-stage budget](receiver-solver-targets.md)
