# Deferred nomination after the original second family screen

Use the observed, sealed `after_salvage` tape for DS30-0220 and the
preassigned DS30-0086 control only. Validate both against the stage
result and charted source before using them. Do not load survey or
installed owner rows during nomination. This is a read-only proposal
port and leaves the original rejected curves, masks, fit, and
publication untouched.

A family can enter only if its otherwise individually compatible
curves occupy exactly one surviving `(receiver, channel)` and the
original tape marks them rejected by singleton family closure. Inspect
only its rejected peers on different lanes at the same second screen
and with the same selected candidate identity. A peer qualifies when
its visibility, alias, profile RMS and bias, and original profile noise
all pass, while its conditional RMS passes and the conditional noise
fails only the original slope threshold. If the conditional failure is
slope-only and the original profile noise also fails, the tape is
insufficient unless an exact same-state record attributes that second
failure solely to slope. Verify gates with the released residual
thresholds at their existing values. Do not score, perturb, or rank
peers by residual magnitude.

Accept at most one unambiguous family and one distinct eligible peer
lane/support hypothesis. Count same-radio/different-channel and
different-radio peers separately. Leave multiple possible peers, missing
gate attribution, missing lane or owner fields, and ambiguous family
identity without a nomination. A deferred nominee must carry its exact
active observation IDs, current selected family and lane. The tape
does not identify the selected alias value, so use later authentic
chart evidence only to check prior-hypothesis identity and never to
relax nomination.

After freezing the nomination and unexplained peers, inspect the
already attempted salvage intervals, isolated target and alternatives,
and signed predictive labels on the same packet. Report overlap in
component and identity even when a specific ordered active interval
cannot be proved identical. A repeated original hypothesis, more than
one unresolved hypothesis, or lack of independent future evidence ends
this experiment. Only then inspect installed and charted terminal
owners. Count overlap, conflicts and other members on the closed
survivor and eligible peer footprint. Price the absolute joint and
direct coverage ceilings before discussing any possible position
effect. A target-coverage bound does not establish causal accuracy.

A novel and unique hypothesis would remain deferred until tested by
an independently reserved radio or time block with causal support,
original alias and noise gates, fixed geometry/calibration inputs in
both arms, and comparison against clutter and preexisting family
alternatives. This experiment runs no new fit, RF, decoder, stage
instrumentation, waveform, or orbit read. Do not change deployed
family gates.
