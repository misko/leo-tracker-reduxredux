# Fixed three-of-four original members

With every fourth full original member omitted from the saved detector packet, DS30-0086 published both calibration arms and reduced solver wall time from **11.333 to 8.030 s**. Horizontal errors fell from **371/382 m to 330/335 m** for `c0`/fitted `c` against the full 8 ms successor. Installed errors were **402/410 m**. The modeled end-to-end improvement reaches 2× on this recording only if an explicit alternate acquisition contract can skip the omitted payload reads and verification. Keeping the original authentication obligations gives a modeled 1.76× on 0086 and misses both 2× gates over the ten audited field pairs.

## Authentic schedule and retained support

Each segment independently omits the fourth full 64-visit member of each disjoint quartet. Short tails remain. The original admitted stream counter advances through omitted visits using signed index metadata. No omitted visit becomes a measured negative. Every retained lane keeps its original aperture, timestamp, radio and independent strongest verified factor, including literal measured empties. The new recipe fixes strongest-margin output before seeing any result. Historical unfiltered EOF decisions authenticate which saved packets contain the full strongest result; they supply no rule for choosing raw eligibility. All ten enrolled field packets reconstructed byte-for-byte to the original pre-held native digest, matched the field 20/8 ms sparse recipe and had zero refinement. A history-filtered final packet cannot recover missing pre-held candidates.

Two independent local index checks read the signed original index bodies and nothing from the waveform. DS30-0016 reproduced its full raw authority over 808 issued visits, all 713 admitted, and all 93 stereo anchors. The DS30-0049 six-member prefix reproduced its issued/admitted counts of 384/362 and 46 stereo anchors. The latter also matched its independent actually measured sparse-8 and full-anchor factor tape. Recounting only retained admissions would silently change 30 retained stereo apertures on 0016 and two in the 0049 prefix. Other field-case projections bind the signed accession descriptors to the previous original acquisition's actual hash-authentication witness and the immutable native receipt. Those cases did not have independent local index bodies for a new raw implementation.

These ratios divide candidate time by installed ALGO08v3 time. A ratio of 0.500 corresponds to 2×. The ten-case cost study includes DS30-0220. The separately sealed relocation pair DS30-0452 contributes to the eleven-pair field comparison and has no stage-cost estimate in this table.

The original raw contract requires encoded member SHA, full decoded extent and SHA, and every decoded window SHA. Current raw runs must pay those checks even where the alternate receiver omits detector work. The conservative projection preserves all reader, decode, byte authentication, and publication work. It credits only average measured native wall time at the omitted full/short duty and, where noted, a factor-linear solver sensitivity. On 0086 this gives 3.740 s of native savings and would need 9.461 s of solver savings to reach 2×. Substituting the observed 3.303 s saved solver difference gives **51.793 s** raw wall against the 91.270 s installed raw clock.

Skipping a signed member's payload would require a separately reviewed and independently identified omission contract. It must bind the submitted original inventory, the signed manifest and every index, original issued and valid/invalid chronology, omitted original ordinals, exact retained apertures and the explicitly skipped acquisition windows. Retained members still need all released encoded, decoded, and per-window verification. The publication must record which omitted payloads were authenticated only through their signed index and manifest commitments and were not read or freshly checked. A prior signed SHA commitment identifies the expected bytes. It does not attest that each unobserved payload still matches them. Under this alternate contract the generous 0086 acquisition credit is 10.651 s, compared with 9.089 s in the component CPU arithmetic. Using the same observed saved solver difference yields **44.882 s** modeled raw wall. Neither modeled raw number is a measured run.
