# Sparse 8 ms native costs on two detached physical prefixes

The registered sparse 8 ms build reproduced **every typed pre-held measurement** from the earlier generic 20/8 ms detector on both hash-verified physical prefixes. The one-in-eight installed 20 ms anchors, selected and unselected factors, signed CFO, epochs, order, empty probes, and original stream identity all matched. The short sparse call was **1.94% slower** on 0163 and **4.75% faster** on 0419 than the fresh generic comparison. The four receiver-model donors were **10.47–33.44% high** on 0163’s summed native CPU and **6.47% low to 14.71% high** on 0419. These clocks cover the timed native receiver calls on the detached visits.
