# Why an independent track bound cannot skip a cold anchor block

The proposed bound can relax the original robust rank score, but before nomination it gives the same lower bound to every centre-seed and corner block. The original rank re-scores each block's eventual assignment at one common centre with zero phase reference. A block-specific bound would need to constrain its **possible terminal assignments before doing the nomination work that produces them**. The existing source and saved receipts supply no such constraint. The same-pattern late alternative, certifying already constructed signatures against the exact top three, reaches at most **0.236 CPU s** on DS30-0138 even if all seven score constructions and evaluations were free. No new rank or solver ran. [Source and timing receipt](measurements/rank-block-0138.json)

## The three decisions use different scores

The installed rank spends four centre calibration seeds. Each seed explores with a shared receiver and satellite phase state, then runs two normal-prior state updates and re-nominations at the centre. The smallest centre `DiscoveryPosition` score elects the **single parent** for all four corner explorations. A centre seed can determine every corner even if it cannot enter the final top three. Its centre score uses the live seed's phase reference and nuisance state. The corner exploration uses that winner's shared calibration and changes its own satellite phases. Only after all of that does the original rank deduplicate exact `(component, candidate index, NORAD, signed alias)` signatures, reset to the common centre and zero phase reference and evaluate `NormalPosition` at fixed `c = 0`. The rank compares the squared residual without dividing by the number of points or adding the full ELBO normalization constant. Later nonlinear finalists have their own phase refreshes. [Installed ranking](/opt/leo-algo08/algo08v3/payload/tools/pipeline_eval/variants/v5_core.py:25) · [Centre discovery schedule](/opt/leo-algo08/bdb869682a2ea63af4b198a920b763a4c6a78c4c/src/leo/analysis/localization_v3/discovery.py:365) · [Final rank objective](/opt/leo-algo08/bdb869682a2ea63af4b198a920b763a4c6a78c4c/src/leo/analysis/localization_v3/position.py:63)

An incumbent for the centre-parent test must therefore be in the centre discovery objective, and the final third-place score can only certify the latter rank. Proposal scoring has per-track normalization, full-track visibility, top-four lists and relative weights against each track's minimum. A change to one track can change a shared coherent phase, the globally capped satellite list, the next calibration state, and thus another track's later nomination. The original nomination uses the minimum visible cost independently of its binary noise gate. When no row is visible, the original masked `argmin` can fall through to row zero. A practical pre-nomination block certificate must cover those cases, signed alias wrapping, phase motion and tied proposals. Keeping an incumbent for the eventual rank alone cannot verify these decisions. ## A valid relaxation and where it loses pruning power

In the final stock rank, all original curved groups stay in the model with fixed noise and unit power. For track \(g\), let \(E_g\) be its squared residual energy normalized by the fixed per-row noise at a candidate's fitted coefficient vector. The native score adds its weighted energy and the clipped Gamma rate penalty. Write its rate as \(r>0\), prior rate as \(\beta=2\), and shape as \(\alpha_g=2+n_g/2\) for \(n_g\) observations. Before clipping, the sum is

\[
 2\alpha_g\left[\log(r/\beta)+(\beta+E_g/2)/r-1\right].
\]

Relaxing the rate gives the minimum at \(r=\beta+E_g/2\), with value \(2\alpha_g\log(1+E_g/(2\beta))\). Clipping the original Gamma term can only increase the exact-real score. A proven lower bound on \(E_g\) can therefore enter its matching monotone expression. The fixed noise volume can be retained. Dropping positive coefficient, determinant and elevation penalties further relaxes the score. A whole-track white Gaussian energy cannot be added directly in place of the robust term. For example, the rate relaxation maps a provisional normalized energy of 10,000 and 100 observations to **813.742**. This example illustrates the softening and uses no measured orbit data. [Released Gamma update and score](/opt/leo-algo08/bdb869682a2ea63af4b198a920b763a4c6a78c4c/src/leo/analysis/localization_v3/hierarchical.py)

To make an independent track bound usable before nomination, one can let each track fit its own copy of the shared offsets, smooth basis, phase tangent, and any orbital-mode columns that could become supported. Independent copies and free constants also admit every legal signed alias. If a future certificate uses explicit energy projections, it must use a superset of the actual design and take a minimum over all labels reachable in that block. A missing mode or label set requires a weaker bound or ordinary evaluation. The stocked orbital-mode column set depends on which tracks across receivers and channels share a selected label. Treating a current provisional mode mask as fixed can understate the allowed fit and overstate the lower bound. [Shared design and support construction](/opt/leo-algo08/bdb869682a2ea63af4b198a920b763a4c6a78c4c/src/leo/analysis/localization_v3/marginal.py)

The only available pre-nomination label superset on 0138 is the same 1,527-label catalogue for every block. All blocks have the same 102 curved tracks, fixed per-row noise, final evaluation point and zero phase reference. Hence even the tightest independent relaxation using this common superset produces one common lower bound \(L\). For any three paid distinct incumbents, \(L\) is at most their lowest score and therefore cannot exceed their third score. It excludes zero blocks. A nontrivial future certificate must first prove a different reachable signature set for a block or preserve enough shared constraints in the original objective to distinguish it. An interval enclosure for the stock floating-point operations would still be required. Use strict separation from the third **exact distinct** incumbent, preserving first signature occurrence and stable tie order, and evaluate the original branch on equality or uncertainty. The analytical formula alone does not establish a production floating-point certificate.

## Saved falsifiers and the CPU actually reachable

The earlier independent three-epoch contrast kept all **165,312 dense** and **24,270 South** track–satellite slots. Under free receiver smooth coefficients every curve had a nonzero basis term in the contrast, allowing its independent data loss to reach zero. A fixed-state orbit-block bound did exclude most exact white-profile rows after a phase and anchor had already been materialized. It preserved all sixteen association requests and improved dense exact rank by **0.384 CPU s**. Its admissible box and incumbent certify local curve ranking and gates in that already-resolved state. They do not certify the evolving centre parent or a whole downstream anchor. The earlier time-moment shortlist with exact rescoring missed **all seven** of the full-observation dense rank signatures. Advancing there requires a valid centre-parent proof and a block-dependent reachable-signature bound in the actual final score.
