# Online budget for missing or disagreeing short detections

Development is DS30-0049. The reserved cases are DS30-0086 and DS30-0144, DS30-0170, DS30-0262 at frozen stage positions 10 and 12–14. The three additional cases come from the first available contiguous block after DS30-0220, which already had targeted receiver/solver crossover and second-screen study. No threshold is set from their installed results or final owner maps. All are independent captures in the same field campaign and site. Test only their declared authentic local replay packets. Read no waveform, ephemeris or survey. Keep all named cases with explicit barriers for any missing metadata.

Use the verified original candidate 20/8 packet whose unfiltered EOF policy left the original admitted chronology and factor fields intact. Decisions see the two actual candidate observations on the current stereo visit, plus the lane stream's own original history. A stream is edge, receiver, channel and actual RF. Store the last 12 admitted observations including empties. Form a straight signed-CFO forecast from the endpoints of the three most recent earlier positives within eight seconds. Require at least 0.4 s span, at most two seconds since the newest, signed slope at most 8,000 Hz/s, and middle-point residual at most 250 Hz. An empty short lane qualifies if this forecast exists or if its same-visit peer is positive and its own immediately preceding admitted opportunity was positive within two seconds. A positive short lane qualifies only if it departs from the forecast by more than 250 Hz plus twice the middle-point residual. This compares signed CFO within one radio and frequency stream. It does not require the two radios to see the same peak. Anchors contribute as ordinary historical evidence and never consume retry budget. No forecast, vote or token is updated from a hypothetical retry.

Earn one full callback credit at short opportunities 16, 32, and so on in each stream. For that credit take the first qualifying short lane from the earning opportunity to the one before the next credit. If no such lane appears, spend on the last opportunity before the next credit. Stop at EOF without spending a pending credit. This bounds each prefix by the earned count and limits waiting without keeping old waveform windows. The method decides about the current visit only. The primary equal-spend periodic comparator chooses the earning opportunity for each redeemed credit. Report the independent fixed-periodic schedule at all credit arrivals, with any extra unredeemed tail credits, alongside it. Report the uniform expected hits within each redeemed credit's observed opportunity span as an additional phase-independent description. The primary comparison changes which opportunity gets paid at a common callback count and the same stream allocation.

Before opening any registered installed packet for scoring, seal the candidate-only decisions and their native inputs. Freeze the same rule for the held cases after the development score. Classify installed positive plus mixed empty as missing. Classify two positives as disagreement if signed CFO exceeds 250 Hz or wrapped fractional epoch difference exceeds two samples using the native 40,000/3 sample period. This matches the previous joint restoration definition. An installed selected factor is an oracle availability witness under the original full receiver and its original chronology. A live revised receiver could return multiple candidates, select another factor, or withhold. Separate those unknowns from the upper bound on recovered installed factors under exact substitution. Check true installed owner counts on 0049 and 0086 if the archived rows pass their official digests. Where only totals survive, bound eligible and nominated owner counts using the universe of positive installed lanes.

Report precision, recall, and hit counts for both classes and combined at equal total spends, informative versus compulsory spends, exact and bounded owner coverage, and projected added wall and CPU from the same recording's measured mean full-anchor callback. Compare with the actual 2× wall deficit. The existing empty-lane rescue and selective-full-recheck results have different cost and input boundaries and are contextual comparators. The follow-up criterion is higher held combined hit rate than equal-spend periodic in each class separately and overall, with a majority of held recordings improved on combined hits. Do not recommend a raw trial on ambiguous or negative transfer. All position and true callback outcomes remain unmeasured.
