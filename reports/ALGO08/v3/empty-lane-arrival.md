## Signed measurements on the same hardware windows

On DS30-0086, the primary frequency-and-arrival window contained **65 of 323** originally missing measurements used by ALGO08v3. That includes **65 of the 84** whose frequency matched the prediction. Carrying the left neighbor's absolute phase forward matched one of those 84. Moving the interpolated phase by a quarter frame matched zero. Both calibration arms have the same verified owner IDs. This passes the three predeclared timing conditions. Querying every nomination still requires 1,342 checks on this case, and the full receiver had no selected factor on 1,139 of them. Across the three held recordings, the primary window retained **313/380** carrier hits. Those 313 comprise **14.9% of the 2,098 missing 20 ms winners** and **4.5% of the 7,007 queries**. On the same 0086 owner set, widening only the timing window to ±128 samples reached 79/84 carrier hits. The earlier ±2,250 Hz and ±6 sample setting covered 108/323 owners. On 0049 the primary setting covered 8/44 owners. The other two held publications retain digests and totals without the complete owner identities needed to score individual omissions. The test cannot establish the physical truth of a receiver rejection.

The median wait for the right donor was about 0.709 s on each recording. The original Graph also used later observations to establish membership. A live implementation would have to account for that delay and retain the necessary samples. Each query uses the unique signed bracket issued by the frozen carrier policy; multiple pieces and ambiguous neighbor aliases abstain. A raw test must measure actual pilot verification and its cost on all enrolled queries, including the large number on which the full detector also returned empty. These figures use saved selections and contain no new RF detection, timing benchmark, position fit or off-site result.
