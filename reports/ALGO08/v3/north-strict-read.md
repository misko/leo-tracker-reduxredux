## Verified acquisition and the attainable scale

All 19 source members and their signed indexes were already resident on tmpfs. Conversion reverified every original compound member and recovered all 1,920,000,000 original decoded bytes from the derivative before publishing its independent expected map digest. Inside the timed raw CLI, strict verification checked all 2,400 encoded blocks under those member roots. It decoded **913,920,000 bytes** and paid the same 304 true full and 2,096 true short native calls. It used every admitted packed member and resolved no original waveform windows. Ordinary installed v3 used its standard full read and full decoded verification. The complete packed encoded payload weighed 1,585,924,800 bytes against the original 1,579,423,424 bytes. The retained 13- and 14-bit simulation values gave a slight encoded expansion, so the benefit came from the acquisition implementation and reduced decode scope. The named child charged zero of either.
