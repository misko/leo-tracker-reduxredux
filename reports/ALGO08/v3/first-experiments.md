# Six tests toward a 2× faster ALGO08v3

**The tested changes do not yet halve ALGO08v3 end to end.** The measured gain that merits a physical acquisition trial comes from overlapping a verified compressed read with computation. A separate solver correction recovered two failed fixes. Two receiver experiments and two solver experiments establish where further work would miss the timing or accuracy target.

### What a viable successor must change

On the first 52 common successful v3 timings, a representative budget is about **2.5× faster acquisition plus 1.5× faster solving**, with publication at its present cost. The dense and cold budgets differ. Under cold conditions some of the acquisition gain can come from overlapping I/O. On warm fixes, v3 already averaged 83.54 CPU seconds in 85.72 elapsed seconds. [Measured stage budgets](single-core-target.md)

For the receiver, the next experiment should target *both* scans' dominant signal-processing work. Try a joint block representation from which the primary and signed-cancellation residual can each be scored, with exact verification and the full receiver on ambiguous blocks. On the dense saved prefix a 20 s projected saving requires about **1.26 ms per lane**. A design consuming 0.80 ms before fallback can afford full retries on only about **17%** of lanes. Screen new proposals against that arithmetic and factor recall on weak, dense and independent-site samples before a full replay.

For the solver, retain the full-region and held-family checks while sharing work across discovery, association recovery and both fitted and zero-calibration arms. Test the complete saved-packet solve on warm DS30-0008 and an independent region. The dense solve must move from about **57 s toward 24 s**. Continue to report frequency-fit changes separately from position error. Only an integrated raw-I/Q-to-artifacts comparison can establish either 2× timing gate.
