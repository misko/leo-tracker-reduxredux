## Which score the algorithm controls

The installed routine starts from its fixed prior rates and applies ordinary Gamma updates. It stops when the latest ordinary log-rate step is below `1e-5`, or when it reaches its original iteration limit, and exports the posterior at the returned rates. The profile takes the same initial updates, then can substitute a clipped log-rate L-BFGS step when the **curved** profiled objective falls sufficiently. A rejected accelerated step resumes the saved ordinary successor. The native conditioning tests still check the matrices at the visited rates. A small last ordinary step at the accelerated endpoint identifies neither the ordinary trajectory nor its chosen finite-stop rates. The early-refusal proof is limited to a matching native failure before any accelerated proposal. It does not govern accepted factors. ## Perfect conditioning and stationarity are insufficient

The released equations include a one-group scalar case with ten equal rows, unit likelihood power and observation noise, unit coefficient prior precision, \(\alpha=7\), \(\beta=2\), squared-design sum \(S=100\), and response squared norm \(Y=10,000\) proportional to that design. For \(C=\alpha S\) the ordinary rate recurrence is

\[
T(r)=\beta+\tfrac12\left[Y\left(\frac{r}{r+C}\right)^2+S\frac{r}{r+C}\right].
\]

This one-column posterior has reciprocal condition **1**. The recurrence has two attracting fixed points at **2.20648** and **3523.74972**, with an unstable fixed point between them. Both attracting points have absolute log-step residual below **7e−15**. The curved losses, after removing common normalization, are **105.5140** and **107.4000**. Adding one fixed, zero-valued predictive measurement with unit noise and unit design gives complete scores **204.5784** and **109.5040**. This demonstrates why perfect conditioning and even exact fixed-point stationarity cannot certify a model-independent agreement gate. The example establishes multiple fixed points. Which trajectories are taken on the failing South node remains unobserved. [Reproducible witness and tests](profiled_grid_fidelity.py)

## A sufficient condition and its cost

A location-independent certificate is possible with a verified **common log-rate radius**. For example, let \(F(\ell)=\log T(e^\ell)\). Prove an invariant domain containing the stock initialization and both trajectories, a uniform induced infinity-norm contraction \(q<1\), and a bound on each predecessor's ordinary residual \(\tau_s,\tau_p\). Then the returned-rate separation is at most \(q(\tau_s+\tau_p)/(1-q)\) in log units. A stock budget exit needs its own residual or a verified finite-step bound. Local contraction at the accelerated point does not prove an invariant domain or shared basin. The native condition proxy measures a Gaussian linear system.

The rate-map Jacobian exhibits the missing work. Write the ordinary updates as \(T_i=b_i+E[\psi_i]/2\), with \(\psi_i\) a fixed weighted squared-residual or prior-group quadratic and \(\lambda_j=a_j/r_j\). The Gaussian expectation gives

\[
(J_F)_{ij}=\frac{\lambda_j}{4T_i}\operatorname{Cov}(\psi_i,\psi_j).
\]

Even a point Jacobian would require cross-group posterior moments or a sufficiently sharp substitute. Proving an upper bound on the whole invariant domain is stronger. The sealed artifacts contain neither a trajectory enclosure nor such a contraction witness. The scalar example rules out a universal contraction for this supported model class.

Given a proven radius \(\epsilon\) against the **returned stock rates**, the fixed-design score can be bounded directly. In exact arithmetic, the two weighted precision matrices lie within factors \(e^{\pm\epsilon}\). Put \(Q\) for the profiled curved quadratic including coefficient penalty, and \(R^2\) for its separate predictive Gaussian quadratic. The candidate-centered shift in the prediction, measured in its covariance norm, is bounded by \(d=e^\epsilon(e^\epsilon-1)\sqrt Q\). The predictive quadratic then lies in \([e^{-\epsilon}(R-d)_+^2,\,e^\epsilon(R+d)^2]\). The predictive log determinant changes by at most \(m\epsilon\) for \(m\) the rank of the changed covariance subspace. The base measurement and bias covariance is fixed and the changing term is \(V H^{-1}V^\mathsf{T}\) on the same span.

For the curved negative twice ELBO, a separate absolute bound is \((e^\epsilon-1)Q+\epsilon[2p+2\sum_g\alpha_g+\sum_j\max(2a_0,d_j)]\), where \(p\) is coefficient width, \(a_0\) the common prior shape, and \(d_j\) the coefficient count of prior group \(j\). The terms bound the optimized quadratic, log determinants and Gamma penalties respectively. The Gamma derivative bound uses the original positive rate floors. Common normalization cancels. A complete certificate also needs matching layout and inputs, any additional score dependencies and finite-precision allowance. This study implements and tests the predictive part in isolation. It does not derive \(\epsilon\) from the stop rule or make a floating-point certification claim. Even if an actual \(10^{-5}\) radius were available, large predictive energies could still make its score allowance exceed **0.005**. The published total score includes other terms beyond its quadratic energy. [Bound implementation and controls](profiled_grid_fidelity.py)

## Decision

Before the first accelerated rate proposal, the pinned ordinary prefix is shared. A certified early native refusal can therefore enter the existing exact fallback without replay. A successful result before any proposal can also retain the ordinary result. Those cases avoid duplicate work but supply no accelerated iterations. After a rate proposal changes the trajectory, running the original recurrence is the available exact fallback absent a new certificate. Reusing setup and the common prefix would require a new port. The available receipts do not price that reusable fraction.

The matched physical DS30-0138 run saved **0.403 CPU s** in capture across 356 successful calls. Certification and paid replay would have to cost less than that total on that specific map. A policy running the complete ordinary capture again at each location would pay another measured **1.499 CPU s**. There is no measured eligible coverage for a cheaper selective guard under the frozen gate. The current blanket option therefore fails paired qualification. Its narrow measured speed gains remain valid on the tested passing cases. [Paid physical map](profiled-grid-export-0138-pair.md)
