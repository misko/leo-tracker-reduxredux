## Why the losing fit cannot simply be skipped

On causal restoration, the closest challenger was **275.709** score units behind after the two-step prefix, missing the guard's **277.221** requirement by **1.512**. Relaxing that margin enough to accept the early leader here would select branch 1. The original full refreshed score chose branch 2 by **775.316**. In hindsight reopening, branch 3 trailed the early leader by **520.336**, then won by **307.189**. Dropping each contender independently when its own prefix margin passes would discard that winner. Target's early leader stayed first, but the two losing branches exchanged order. The original full mixed guard completed only its leader; the unmeasured final challenger scores remain unknown. The older score-floor study already failed to certify exclusion. [Released prefix rules](cold_finalist_race.py) · [Previous lower-bound counterexample](cold-finalist-certificate.md)

The newly restored causal evidence changed the original shortlist membership. Its three ranked branches shared all 101 curated tracks and their signed aliases within that packet, with 7–19 satellite differences per pair. The chosen cold location moved **4.477 km** from full mixed. Its final publication shared **3,145 of 4,367** full owner IDs per arm, with **344** satellite and **31** role changes. Hindsight shared 4,190 with only 18 satellite changes and its cold location was 2.947 km from full mixed. The target and causal both selected their second-ranked branch, with distinct assignments.
