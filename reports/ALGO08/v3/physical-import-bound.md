A new preload opt-in could change the current fresh-process import guards. A scientific qualification would still need to show that both children start from immutable code-only state, authenticate their own recording, causal cutoff and offer, and reconstruct all mutable receiver, orbit, solver and figure state independently. The positive needs both calibration arms, ordered owners and complete numerical and image evidence. The tiny needs its exact signed withholding with zero position products. The current measurements provide neither worker's full scientific replay. The pair-level ceiling rules out closing the summed 2× gap by sharing alone, so neither was launched.
