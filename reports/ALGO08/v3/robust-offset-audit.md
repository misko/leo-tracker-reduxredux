# Sorted residual and moment reuse in the cold solver

The current DS30-0359 solver has no repeated Huber-style scalar offset sweep over a fixed signed residual vector. It already contracts the linearized rank into moments and computes the whitened Gaussian offset in one solve per candidate. The remaining native grouped-energy contraction has already been implemented and timed. It saved **0.284 CPU s** on the older DS30-0374 control, replayed **29.6%** of intermediate track energies under its numerical guard, and changed the endpoints and common-grid scores. The new 0359 receipt gives **0.0263 CPU s** as a gross ceiling for replacing every remaining exact *fine salvage* profile and its associated checks. The evidence supports stopping before another prototype or lease. ## Actual score inputs and decisions

- **Cold nomination and rank.** `v5_core.ranked_initial_nodes` builds full-observation seeds and regional alternatives, deduplicates assignments in original order, then scores each unique assignment through `NormalPosition.residual` at the center. `discovery.statistics` already contracts each exact catalogue projection into counts, residual means, centered sums of squares and time cross-products. `augment_phase_statistics`, `apply_phases` and `proposal_phases` add the derivative moments and evaluate a quadratic in the linearized phase. The discrete signed alias is rounded from the curve mean. The clipped term in `penalized_score` caps the winning score of an entire component after reduction. That cap does not induce observation-level Huber breakpoints. A different center or satellite projection changes the base vector and its statistics; changed phase/receiver calibration still uses the existing exact discovery contractions within their original model. [Discovery source](../../../src/leo/analysis/localization_v3/discovery.py) · [Phase score source](../../../src/leo/analysis/research/shared_orbit_phase_fit.py)
- **Signed per-curve Gaussian profile.** For `r = observed − predicted`, the original `profile` takes `a = rint(−mean(r)/PERIOD)`, `z = W(r + a·PERIOD)` and `v = W1`, then solves `b = (zᵀv)/(vᵀv + 1/σ_b²)`. The residual cost is the quadratic about that `b` plus log volume. There is one exact alias per candidate and support, no search over `b`. The algebraic scalar equivalent `zᵀz − (zᵀv)²/(vᵀv + 1/σ_b²)` is useful only after the exact response and whitening are known. It cannot reproduce original binary64 cancellation, ties at alias half-integers or candidate score ties automatically. Sorting residual values would discard timestamp association. Existing checks need ordered innovations to compute slope, consecutive steps and lag-one correlation. For instance, `(-100, 50, 50)` and `(50, -100, 50)` have the same sorted residuals, but their dot products with centered times `(-1, 0, 1)` are 150 and 0. Gaussian-only moment reuse cannot substitute for those checks. [Whitened curve profile](../../../src/leo/analysis/research/retained_track_likelihood.py) · [Chronological noise tests](../../../src/leo/analysis/research/polynomial_satellite_diagnostics.py)
- **Continuous refits.** Each secant query rebuilds the relevant two-coordinate physical prediction. `NormalPosition.calculate` subtracts the live RF-scaled intercept from the native observation vector and calls the grouped Normal/Gamma likelihood with the active design, mask and per-row noise. Fixed and fitted `c` use different design columns. An outer phase refresh changes orbital modes and the reference. Inside one fixed-design call, each iteration uses new posterior means, covariance and track/prior rate updates. The group energy and covariance trace drive the next rate; the stock stopping check also depends on those updates. A phase-, geometry- or arm-crossing residual summary therefore supplies neither the correct posterior nor its returned residual vector. The inner group Gram/RHS is already built once per query. [Released normal likelihood](../../../src/leo/analysis/localization_v3/position.py) · [Native update](../../../src/leo/analysis/localization_v4/native_bound.c)

## Earlier moment experiments and custody

The new proposal would overlap two different experiments. Time-moment discovery interpolated the geometry onto sparse time nodes and retained observation energy in an approximate quadratic. Exact rescoring did not restore the missing dense winner. The native [group-statistics implementation](../../../tools/pipeline_eval/variants/algo08_bound_statistics.h) already tried the real-valued identity `yᵀy − 2mᵀAᵀy + mᵀ(AᵀA)m`, with a per-track response shift, row fallback near cancellation and exact final residual readout. An unshifted whole-update guard replayed all 25,090 intermediate updates on the original 0374 input. The later shifted variant gained 0.284 CPU s against the reversed control and kept the same owners, with small endpoint and grid differences. Exact decisions also require tracking rounding through the rank/alias ties and the sequential `1e−5` Gamma stop, so the shifted version is not a numerical certificate for current fits. Profiled Gamma changes how the actual noise rates converge; it cannot establish savings for a sorted-offset primitive.
