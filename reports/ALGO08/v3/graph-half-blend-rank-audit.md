# Can the saved DS30-0049 rank certify the Graph half-blend?

The saved evidence cannot certify the ordered top three, their membership,
or even one invariant nomination. It does certify that the same seven
curves enter the ordinary producer. The 38 edits on those curves are small
enough to bound several fixed-state tests, but the operands that would turn
those bounds into actual margins are absent. The half-blend remains unscored.

## What the control measured

The original sparse control's healthy-history route entered the actual
successor's six-scout stage. Scout 1 beat scout 4 by 244.860155 Hz² on the
minimax statistic. The two paid centres and their corners yielded two
distinct signed nominees, so the distinct-three gate ran the ordinary
producer. That producer returned exactly three unique nominees from up to
four centre seeds and four corners. There was no fourth distinct ordinary
nominee to record. The nomination handoff retained the ordinary nodes and
two-refresh states in process memory. The durable witness has counts of
three nodes and three captured prefixes, with complete signatures only for
the two speculative nominees. The completed ordinary finalists' fixed-zero
loss differences of 44.510431 and 5.967539 were measured **after** two
position/phase refreshes. The separately recorded pause scores are already
inside the first fit. Neither reports the order margins at the nominal
centre before the fit. [Successor](combined_successor.py) · [Handoff](nomination_handoff.py) · [Saved control](measurements/accepted-confidence-0049.json.gz)

The frozen half-blend scopes the changed values to a copied scan inside
the cold rank call. The actual successor checks scan identity there.
Passing the copied scan skips its speculative scout and enters the exact
ordinary producer, which is the producer whose output the original
control used after declining the two-centre attempt. The direct question
for this frozen proposal is ordinary versus ordinary. A half-blend that
also reaches the scout would be a different experiment. Its six seed
vectors, their eligibility and ordering, and the distinct-three decision
could change. The archived minimax difference compares the originally
generated vectors only. [Frozen adapter](graph_half_blend.py) · [Scout](two_centre_seeds.py) · [Identity check](single_rank_refusal.py)

The ordinary producer builds its calibration seeds from slopes, shapes,
phases and visibility, chooses the lowest centre score as the parent of all
four corners, and deduplicates full signed assignments in first-seen order.
It then sorts using the zero-reference fixed-zero `NormalPosition` residual
at the offered centre. Thus a displaced seed or corner parent can introduce
a previously absent assignment. Even the full list of original centre
scores would need an upstream branch certificate to constrain that event.
The seven-curve relative overlaps identify some original differences. For
example, ordinary rank 2 differs from rank 1 on one curve and introduces one
otherwise absent catalogue label. They do not identify that curve or prove
that either nomination survives. The later accepted-confidence fingerprints
and the early assignment margins from 0008 and 0316 supply no missing 0049
centre or corner arrays. [Accepted-confidence scope](accepted-confidence-observer.md) · [Early margin scope](pre-finish-margin.md) · [Related nomination audit](center-corner-nomination-audit.md)

## Bounds from the saved measurement rows

I grouped the 399 signed lifted Graph rows by their original track and
applied the stored half-delta once in binary64, using zero on uninvolved
rows. All 15 curvature classifications agree before and after the edit
when recomputed using the released 20-second span and 250 Hz affine-removed
RMS gates. For the seven active tracks, the smallest edited RMS is 520.762 Hz
and the shortest span is 22.328 s. The largest active-track change is
122.988 Hz at one observation. The largest RMS norm of the perturbation
after removing its constant and slope is 27.071 Hz, which also bounds the
change in each active track's ordinary affine-removed shape RMS against
any *fixed* satellite prediction. [Bound Graph rows](measurements/track-cfo-denoise-0049-graph.json.gz)

For the actual per-curve white association profile, hold predicted CFO,
radio terms and phases fixed. The alias coordinate `−mean(y−prediction)`
moves by exactly `−mean(d)` for an edit vector `d`. Across the active
tracks `abs(mean(d))` is at most 7.448 Hz. Certifying a fixed alias requires
each original coordinate's distance from a half-period tie of
`1 / 4.4 μs`, with rounding slack. Those distances are absent.

Conditioned on that alias staying fixed, set
`b = sum(d)/(n + (150/1000)²)` Hz. The released 150 Hz white scale and
1000 Hz Gaussian bias prior give an exact change `b` in the bias and
`d−b` in the residual. The 450 Hz pre-screen RMS moves by at most
27.344 Hz, and the bias by at most 7.448 Hz. For profile cost `Q + C`,
where `C` is the unchanged log-volume plus bias normalization, the
triangle inequality bounds the change in `sqrt(Q)` by
`norm([(d−b)/150, b/1000])`, at most **1.277** here. These are rounded-up
real-arithmetic envelopes for the fixed-state screen. The original
candidate-wise bias, RMS, score, alias distances and chronological noise
tests were never saved. The joint Normal/Gamma rank objective also
re-estimates the posterior and precision, so this white-profile bound
cannot be applied to the stored refreshed losses.

Even in a fixed least-squares score, updating a stored squared norm
requires the signed cross term `2 r·d`. Residuals of equal norm along
`+d` and `−d` have the same original loss and opposite cross terms.
Here the centre/corner response vectors or perturbation cross terms
against the affine and phase bases are missing, as are their actual
seed, support, alias and deduplication traces. The durable catalogue and
projection digests authenticate inputs but do not expose those operands.

## Narrow discriminator

With one source-pinned prepared previous-bank scan, the exact comparison
ends at return from ordinary `ranked_initial_nodes`: invoke the same
producer on the raw and copied views, then compare the ordered first
three complete `(component, catalogue index, NORAD, integer alias)`
signatures. Preserve the authentic offer, time/phase-dependent geometry,
noise design, catalogue order, point/phase priors and arithmetic. Log the
emitted centre seeds, corner parent and corner origin, each pre-dedup
signature, then every distinct zero-reference centre score in binary64
and its original tie order. This also distinguishes a changed candidate
from a reorder of old candidates. Verifying the complete successor scout
as well would require running that same producer wrapper on both views
with proper scan ownership, logging its accepted branch or ordinary retry.

The old witness measured 0.170 CPU s for the speculative rank and
0.246 CPU s for ordinary rank, excluding other cold work. This is a scope
guide for a rank-only comparison, with deferred preparation charged
separately. The required operand is the prepared numerical scan with
causal position/velocity interpolation support, or a complete equivalent
checkpoint. The receipts do not serialize it. A rank return is sufficient
to answer the nomination question before the finalist fitter, geographic
truth, terminal decision or publication. This audit performed only source
and receipt reads and arithmetic over saved Graph deltas.
