## Why the existing proposal is a poor starting point

All eight dense and 19 weak post-cold optimizers carry a predictive contribution. None qualifies for the earlier temporal trust method, which required the ordinary curved likelihood with at least 256 active rows and no attached instance calculation. The broader observed bound above already gives every predictive-coupled nonfinal derivative call away for free. An interpolation that only reduces the nested projection would target **30.6 ms** dense. To reach even the broader ceiling, a new method must replace the derivative's nuisance, robust precision and predictive covariance work and still re-evaluate the full original objective at the proposed position.
