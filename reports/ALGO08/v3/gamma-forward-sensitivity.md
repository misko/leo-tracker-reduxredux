# Forward differentiation of the actual Gamma updates

Forward sensitivities reproduced the full residual derivatives of the released robust posterior on two real captured calibration arms. **The implementation was slower than the two original finite-difference queries in both arms, even with the whole original primal replay free.** The derivative alone cost **38.459/41.217 ms** CPU. The original queries cost **12.906/32.258 ms**. This tests the actual posterior recurrence on genuine saved operands under controlled input directions. Geographic, predictive and solver speedups remain unmeasured. [Frozen protocol](gamma-forward-sensitivity-protocol.md) · [Clocks and source receipt](measurements/gamma-forward-sensitivity.json.gz)

## Tangent and correctness boundary

For each direction the candidate differentiates every executed posterior. With current precision \(H\), nuisance mean \(m\), covariance \(Q=H^{-1}\), and right-hand side \(b\), it uses \(dm=Q(db-dH\,m)\) and \(dQ=-Q(dH)Q\). Each derivative includes both the changing input Gram/RHS and the contribution from the previous noise and phase-prior rate derivatives. The original grouped updates consume likelihood-weighted residual energy, covariance trace, and prior second moments. Their derivatives propagate from the actual successive rate values. It differentiates the final determinant and all returned residual terms, including the noise and prior Gamma penalties. This differs from a fixed-precision tangent, even though interpolation and observation selection are outside the sampled subproblem. [Source-pinned observer](gamma_forward_sensitivity.py)

The dense primal is byte-identical to an untouched dense control in both arms. The supported native primal's largest residual component difference from dense is **2.48 × 10⁻⁹**. Both arms retained their original route and **43/46** update counts at every sampled forward and symmetric step. The tangent differentiates the active branch of that executed sequence. The smallest observed distance to the original `1e-5` stop comparison was **1.02 × 10⁻⁶ / 3.14 × 10⁻⁷**. This finite set of checks does not certify nearby conditioning, support, clamp or iteration switches.
