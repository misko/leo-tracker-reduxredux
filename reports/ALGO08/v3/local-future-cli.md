# Local future repair in the full-short launcher

`full_short_run.py --local-future-repair` accepts the original authenticated 12 ms short receiver as live input or a saved packet from that receiver. It uses the installed graph and the same fixed-owner repair previously checked on the three containing offers. Both modes retain the original receiver recipe and sealed native factor packet. The optional flag attaches the verified research solver policy in `local-future-policy.json` and links its separate digest from `full-short-recipe.json`. Launches without the flag keep their existing defaults. [Launcher](full_short_run.py) · [Policy source and validation](local_future_policy.py) · [Dependency pins](local_future_pins.py)

The solver evaluates forward and reverse continuation once per authenticated native packet and reuses the original forward selection if preparation is retried. The pinned source for the repair is byte-identical to the earlier study. A saved-input parity check also reproduced all three forward packet digests and the complete ordered future rosters of **69 / 72 / 72** factors on physical, north and south. That check performed no new fits. The explicit `--causal-selection` mode is incompatible because it removes observations before the repair can inspect them. The repair remains scoped to the installed graph and original Gamma fit used in the frozen comparison.
