# Signed CFO and time support on shared DS30 satellite assignments

On DS30-0049, the archived tests show two separate ways that changing the receiver hurt the fix. Restoring full-length detections on short lanes that were empty recovered 324 m at `c = 0` and 377 m with fitted `c`. Substituting full-length measurements where the two detectors reported different short winners recovered 530 m and 262 m. Substituting the full measurements only where both winners already agreed recovered 275 m and 193 m. Each intervention started from the same sparse packet and left over 2 km of excess fitted error. These are measured separate effects and their sum is unmeasured. ## What changed within an assigned satellite

We joined both saved owner maps to actual signed hardware visits and grouped by NORAD, role, radio, edge, channel and actual RF, splitting at gaps over eight seconds. The time metric is one minus the sum of interval intersections divided by the sum of unions. It counts first-to-last owned time per group, including gaps within each interval. It can differ from both point count and the spread of the observed times. The groups approximate encounters and do not recover exact Graph piece identity. Results were identical across the two calibration arms because each selected the same rows and roles. Time mismatch and absolute owner-count change each rank one of the three recording pairs in the geographic order, while centered CFO RMS ranks two. Each assigns DS30-0086 more loss than its actual geographic outcome warrants. This sample gives no independent routing rule. The larger 0220 deterioration with charted inference includes an interaction with receiver input. On that same mixed packet swapping only the inference moves its errors by +819 / +825 m. For the held full packets, the same-solver archived owner rows reproduce the official installed observation-to-NORAD digest exactly. The installed output omits its own roles and ordering, so the within-role statistics come from the charted crossover on both detectors.

On DS30-0049, empty-lane restoration reduced the time mismatch from **16.5% to 9.5%**, regained 47 original observation/NORAD/role assignments, and reproduced the full detector tuple on 45 of those assignments. Shifted-winner substitution reduced the mismatch to **14.9%** and brought all its final common-owner short observations into the strict ±250 Hz and ±2 sample window. The ordinary result had 27 common-owner short disagreements outside this window, at 311 Hz signed-difference RMS. The matched replacement gave exactly zero raw paired CFO difference on the 184 now-owned strict matches and left the interval metric at 16.5%. Its fitted fix remained 2.427 km worse than installed. Dropping sparse-only factors changed the interval metric to 15.3% and left the strict CFO comparison exactly unchanged, yet improved both positions by 218/140 m. These observations separate membership and measurement changes and show why either scalar misses important differences. ## Anchor-based correction stopped after transfer

The test uses only the mixed packet's own nearby full 20 ms anchors to interpolate signed CFO at a target hardware counter. It moves the short estimate halfway toward the prediction when their signed difference is at most 250 Hz and both bracketing anchors are within eight seconds. It leaves the epoch, visit and membership unchanged. It gets the exact archived owner groups for this diagnostic, an optimistic input for a real receiver. Neither geography nor the installed packet selects the proposals. Installed CFO is used only for evaluation on strictly matched rows whose NORAD and role are unchanged. Group means are removed on the same before/after support. No orbital prediction or actual calibration residual enters these figures. [Reducer](owner_path_correction.py).

| DS30-0049 | 114 / 183 | 113.46 → 113.99 Hz | −0.94% | 6, 83.36 Hz |
| DS30-0086 | 732 / 1,396 | 105.89 → 105.39 Hz | +0.95% | 41, 70.36 Hz |
| DS30-0220 | 654 / 1,166 | 104.77 → 105.66 Hz | −1.70% | 37, 66.69 Hz |

The held cases clear the support gates, but neither approaches the predeclared 5% improvement. Grouping removes the constant within-group offset. A linear time term explains only 2.1%, 2.8%, and 3.9% of the remaining signed discrepancy energy, respectively. Masking an identical full/full anchor and predicting it induces substantial error even though both packets agree exactly at that anchor. Another 11, 103, and 171 proposed short corrections have no strict common-owner comparator. The assay leaves their physical error and all corrected geographic outcomes unmeasured. No actual correction was applied to an archived detector packet or publication.

Earlier work tested global aperture timing and nuisance-centered signed offsets, adjacent matched Graph temporal dependence, and loss of design leverage when masking uncertified owners. This study uses both final owner maps and tests interpolation from the mixed packet's own full measurements against reserved recordings. The matched and empty 0049 interventions support retaining genuine observations on missing or disagreeing lanes when independently corroborated. The limited recoveries and transfer errors rule out a general accuracy guarantee from this correction or the time-coverage metric. A causal retry would need independent selection evidence and a fresh qualification.
