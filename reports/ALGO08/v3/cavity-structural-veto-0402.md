## What a sound RF exclusion would still need

Both optimizers stay within the supplied ±12.5 km east/north box. Phase references are initialized or clipped to ±15 s, while final validity additionally requires the actual phase state within 15.000001 s and phase closure within 0.01 s. Receiver offsets, drift, smooth time coefficients, fitted RF response, the three supported orbital mode amplitudes and per-track intercepts instead enter a Gaussian or robust posterior. The stated scales are priors. The released code does not set finite hard trust boxes on those coefficients. The fitted arm has no separate incumbent objective limit under the final equal-prior adoption rule. Deriving a finite coefficient bound from the fixed-zero incumbent loss would require a certified lower floor for the full reoptimized objective, including robust prior and noise terms, covariance volume, the predictive contribution and the unused-coordinate correction. An assumed three-sigma range or an observed incumbent coefficient range cannot supply it. [Released nuisance design](/opt/leo-algo08/bdb869682a2ea63af4b198a920b763a4c6a78c4c/src/leo/analysis/localization_v3/marginal.py:96) · [Equal-prior policy](/opt/leo-algo08/bdb869682a2ea63af4b198a920b763a4c6a78c4c/src/leo/analysis/localization_v3/family_cavity.py:37)

A same-track contrast cancels a constant track bias and signed alias. It generally retains the shared receiver drift and smooth response and the phase and physical orbit modes. Allowing arbitrary amplitudes makes an interval bound on any contrast with nonzero response vacuous. At a **fixed** position and phase, projecting onto the orthogonal complement of the complete nuisance design would give an exact relaxed residual bound. To exclude the proposal, it would have to stay above a necessary track-wise limit for every allowed position and phase, or prove the original adoption loss unattainable. Varying geometry changes that projection, and the saved trace does not contain the candidate's row-level geometry and failed residual vectors across those domains. The earlier catalogue block bound is a different calculation against fixed nominal geometry at an anchor with original ambiguity fallback.
