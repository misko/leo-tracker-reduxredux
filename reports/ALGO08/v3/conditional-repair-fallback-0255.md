## Why the new fit can be scored independently

The ordinary repair passed all **3,643** retained curved observations in both arms and passed the position and phase gates. A 17-observation predictive track failed in the fitted arm, of 650 remaining predictive observations. Both repair positions had optimized the joint objective. The candidate used them as seeds and solved both arms again with the same retained curved measurements, assignment, weights and prior and zero predictive entries. Each arm passed every curved-track check and converged in two phase refreshes. Final changes in phase were below **0.0004 s** and the final position movement was zero in both arms. The ordinary signed update passed and the exterior rule recorded both final positions as interior with zero exterior probes.
