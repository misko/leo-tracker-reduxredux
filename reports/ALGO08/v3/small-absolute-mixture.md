## What changed from the earlier mixture

Earlier mixture discovery ran a five-by-five full-catalogue mesh followed by local sampling. It chose up to eight rivals, profiled independent orbit phase for each track hypothesis, and passed curved evidence to the original one-shot or the rebuilt staged finish. The dense result lost owners, and giving the earlier solver the exact curved owner still left its predictive evidence and paired route inadequate. This trial visits the full catalogue just once at the offered centre on actual observation times, keeps four rivals per arc, and includes shorter tracks whose hypotheses share an eligible curved satellite. It refines the same slate locally, using one phase nuisance per body across all arcs, and the released shared radio intercept, drift, smooth and RF calibration columns. Both arms independently see the same observations, priors, integer-alias slate and work limits. No owner or fitted state from either arm, named v3, or a reference site enters the other arm's inference.
