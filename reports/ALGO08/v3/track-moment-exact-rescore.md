The bounded fixed-state audit isolates a direct failure in **quadratic Doppler interpolation**. At the southwest corner for `SCAN-RX1-CH7-T008-RF12209687500.000000`, replacing exact Doppler with its quadratic interpolant changed the nominee from NORAD 63592 to 64194 under the original full-time visibility and profile. None of the 60 comparisons changed visibility masks or discrete aliases, and the moment algebra agreed with the dense profile of the same interpolant within 8.95×10⁻⁸ cost units. The moment score preserves all observed energy outside the polynomial basis, but scores it as if predicted Doppler has no component there. The true outside-basis energy depends on the residual after subtracting the exact prediction. The approximate score can understate or overstate the original profile and supplies no bound for a fixed top-K. The audit used zero receiver and phase offsets, and the selected learned branches had the opposite pair of labels on that track. Treat the flip as evidence of a mechanism. The first causal change in the learned search remains unobserved.
