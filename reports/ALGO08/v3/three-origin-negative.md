# A 4 ms negative screen could not skip the 8 ms detector

The frozen three-origin screen skipped **zero** empty 8 ms lanes on the resident original first members of DS30-0008 and DS30-0169. It kept all currently verified detections and left every native call in place. Its measured CPU cost was 1.492 ms on dense DS30-0008 and 1.170 ms on independent DS30-0169. A receive-integrated replay was unnecessary under the preregistered cost gate. ## Original input and signed results

The feature examines twelve small tiles in the first 40,000 native samples. At each tile it uses normalized complex covariance from all three nominal frame origins, requires consistent phase around the triangle, and subtracts the greater of two displaced-origin controls. The old [repeat gate](repeat-negative.md) used the strongest single 40,000-sample-lag magnitude. The [four-frame study](four-frame-primary.md) replaced the original short search and verification outright. Its aligned 6.6444 ms native path saved 5.55–8.56 ms across the four original member pairs but lost seven strong dense 8 ms factors and four on independent DS30-0169. Its dense no-retry saving was already below the 9.940–13.137 ms first-use inner-acquisition gap. The new staged proposal preserved the ability to run the unchanged 8 ms detector for every inconclusive lane.

The lowest finite score among the 25 original short positives on dense training visits 0–31 set the strict omission threshold at `0.00001650722787590175`. Antenna 1 at visit 29 set that floor with an 8 ms margin of 0.28083 and a matching 20 ms signed factor. The ratio of the lowest dense empty score to that threshold was 82.4. In the later dense half even its own minimum positive score lay below its minimum empty score. The transfer used the frozen dense threshold without any DS30-0169 adjustment.

The screen bypassed all 16 two-scan 20 ms anchor lanes per member. This member-stage assay used pinned archived signed probes for the unchanged calls, including all anchor candidates and empties. Published owner assignments, output figures and site endpoints were outside its scope.

## Net ceiling at the measured dense budget

The 77.443 MB dense member allows 273.553 ms at 283.1 MB/s. The two [actual first-use inner clocks](original-dense-intake.md) were 283.492 and 286.690 ms, leaving 9.940–13.137 ms before charging first-use startup, the final held selector, detector-file commit and exit. At the mean of the two earlier verified full 8 ms native CPU rates, 0.8296 ms per short lane, paying the actual screen across 112 dense short lanes requires omitting **14–18** safe empty lanes just to cover these gaps. This is a mean-CPU projection against elapsed headroom, with no per-lane native timer and no credit for skipping original I/Q authentication.

Using the complete dense labels in hindsight, the smallest-score thresholds that omit at least 14 or 18 empty lanes also omit 17 or 26 real 8 ms factors. Of those, 14 or 19 are strong and 11 or 16 have original 20 ms signed counterparts. The two theoretical safe omissions on DS30-0169 with its own hindsight threshold would be worth only an estimated **0.526 ms net** using its archived mean call cost, before integration and without independent replication. These results bound this scalar score policy on the measured inputs. Phase closure plus displaced controls does not give sufficient negative discrimination here, including for strong factors.
