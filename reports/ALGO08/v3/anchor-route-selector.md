## What a held-anchor score could measure

The frozen continuation sees every previously measured same-lane factor within its eight-second window, including 20 ms anchors and measurements that it subsequently drops. Backtesting held anchors with a Graph built from the already selected history packet can expose held CFO indirectly through training membership. The earlier future-repair cross-fit proved this kind of dependence for erased blocks. The later past-anchor exposure policy measured aperture pass rates and used no held CFO prediction. The RF-channel folds, in turn, scored orbital hypotheses on a supplied regional lattice. [Continuation source](../../../tools/pipeline_eval/variants/carrier_continuation.py) · [Temporal dependence](crossfit-future-repair.md) · [Exposure counts](anchor-exposure.md) · [Geographic RF folds](geographic-rf-fold.md)

A receiver-only predictive diagnostic would rebuild both training rosters using nonanchor rows and their original empty opportunities. In each chronological fold it would mask held blocks and the continuation halo before the history filter, then build both track graphs. Held detections would enter only as scored outcomes at their actual hardware times and same radio, channel and RF. Both scores would use the same normalized predictive mixture over competing tracks with the original alias support, noise and clutter term. A missing track would receive only the clutter likelihood. Keeping all common held positives in the denominator would count missing predictions, and normalizing the track mixture would charge competing tracks. Abstention or direction disagreement across held blocks or radios would choose unfiltered. The original chosen route would still need its complete graph with anchors restored. No site label, TLE or offered region would enter the route selector.

This diagnostic could test transfer of signed-frequency prediction. The archive shows lower RF RMS coexisting with larger horizontal error. The 100 m omission failure survives both choices, and the physical options show no established material publication-time gain. The audit stopped before fitting a selector or running either route again. No raw input, held graphs or additional orbital solves ran. Thirteen focused and adjacent component tests passed.
