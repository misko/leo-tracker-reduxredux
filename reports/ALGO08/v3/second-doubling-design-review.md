# Where the next 2× must come from

The comparison baseline is frozen ALGO08v3 on one core. The guarded candidate finishes a complete simulated North fix in 11.224 s against 16.761 s. Half time is 8.381 s to three decimals. Using the underlying clocks, it still needs 2.844 s. Acquisition took 5.349 s and decision and publication took 5.346 s. Holding the rest fixed, reducing each by about 27% would reach half time on this one recording. A weak physical fix and a dense physical fix will have different budgets. Selective frequency refinement already eliminated 600 of 996 North calls and saved 0.538 CPU s inside the refinement. Earlier completion of the process includes launch-to-launch variation. North and South still falsely publish on the test with truth 100 m outside the supplied region. A face-contact veto would also withhold a valid North solution. Physical DS30 is a one-roof dataset. [Selective measurements and both offers](continued-transfer-results.md) · [Physical first-member comparison](continued-cfo-first-0016.md)

The 12 ms receiver has a measured ceiling. Giving away its entire timing and carrier searches projects only 1.61× through exit on North and 1.65× on an earlier dense physical build. Giving away the entire short detector projects 1.83× and 1.81×. Only one of 45 positive lanes offered the same pilot samples, time and frequency in the early and final checks. Its whole early check cost 17.7 µs. The cold solver also has a ceiling. Two real cases had 138 finite-difference probes with zero exact repeated perturbed coordinates. Giving away their measured geometry and setup buys at most 0.378 and 0.143 CPU s. The ordered catalogue rank still cost 3.760 and 2.263 CPU s. [Receiver profile and optimistic ceilings](short-primary-fusion-profile.md) · [Solver profile](cold-kernel-price.md)

Of the earlier three directions, **selective frequency refinement** earned a place. The **lower-precision timing scout** retried all 127 scouts and was slower. **Avoiding solver rescues** proved highly case-dependent. The current solver paid about 1.38 CPU s across two rescue scopes on DS30-0402 and made no rescue fit on DS30-0374. The cheaper exact reuse inside 0402 saved 0.118 CPU s in a narrower comparison. [Lower-precision test](q15-timing-scout.md) · [Actual rescue costs](rescue-phase-prepare.md)

Constructing historical orbit objects only after selecting the required records cut the complete DS30-0016 saved-detection path from **4.444 s ordinary to 4.246 s with an empty index**. A fresh worker using a prebuilt **2.69 MB** index from another recording finished in **4.008 s**, reusing 19 of 20 bulletins. Both positions, 116 assignments per calibration arm, the grid and all eight figures matched. These are separate launches on identical saved detections. The last clock charges loading the index; its initial build ran on the earlier recording. In a preparation-only comparison, the preceding scan took 3.572 CPU s with indexing plus 0.015 s to export, versus 3.742 s ordinary. The index contains source orbit metadata; each request still supplies its own region. ## Ranked experiments

Each row is a proposal. The first experiment should either measure a distinct saving or rule the idea out before any full panel.

| Rank | Direction | Smallest useful test |
|---:|---|---|
| 1 | **Build the orbit-source index once per immutable bulletin.** Store satellite identifiers and exact epochs; construct live propagators only for the selected records. Use the offered region anew on every scan. | Time a fresh process on a later recording with one changed bulletin, including reads, verification, index misses, memory and the eight figures. Preparation alone on DS30-0016 went from 1.768 to 1.581 CPU s cold and 1.126 s reusing a previous recording's in-memory index. A simulation of 64 entries hit 15,748 of 15,866 distinct within-scan body uses over 455 requests. This predicts reuse frequency, not saved seconds. [Request census](orbit_index_reuse.py) |
| 2 | **Test a joint delay and signed-frequency scout on independent pilot samples.** Let one time/frequency slice nominate tiles and another check them. Retain the original verifier and use broad search on unresolved lanes. | Price the scout and all retries on one weak and one dense member. Test lanes with no signal and lanes with a second signal after cancellation. Existing marginal carrier and timing lists together recovered only one of 112 archived factors in a generous overlap test. The new scout must earn its place from original samples. [Carrier evidence](cyclic-carrier.md) · [Timing evidence](joint-envelope.md) · [Original verifier costs](directed-verifier.md) |
| 3 | **Score satellite families in blocks with a conservative upper bound.** A losing block must be unable to enter the exact finalist set for any allowed alias and nuisance parameter. Keep the full catalogue available. | Replay the original rank on two different-signal recordings and measure entire blocks pruned, proof cost, exact ordering and owner assignments. Earlier compressed shortlists lost a winner even after exact rescoring. [Rank costs and failed filters](cold-exact-budget.md) |
| 4 | **Deliver the mixed-aperture input directly from acquisition.** For future recordings, test writing independently verifiable waveform blocks aligned to the actual receiver apertures. Keep the full signal recoverable. | Re-encode one saved member offline as a capture emulator. Measure acquisition-side CPU, write volume and first-use read-to-detector time together. The old format's retrofit took about eight mixed replays to amortize. This is a separate new-recording design and earns no speed credit for a legacy DS30 conversion. [Measured format crossover](seekable-member.md) |
| 5 | **Run the installed exact refinement only on ambiguous satellite assignments.** Use agreement across independent time blocks to identify which assignment branches need the full finish. | Include a known easy survivor, the case that lost 83 assignments, and a translated missing-region control. Preserve both calibration fits and run the full solver whenever the tests disagree. Count full completions and time through publication. [Finish and geographic transfer](long-solver-and-location-transfer.md) |
| 6 | **Build an incremental receiver-to-solver handoff.** As authenticated detections arrive, retain the track statistics that can be updated exactly. At end of recording, recompute every track affected by joins, splits or changed assignments. | Replay a saved positive in chronological order. First measure the time of whole end-of-recording passes this removes, then charge ingestion, invalidation and final exact reevaluation on the same core. Compare the complete track graph and owners in both arms. [Earlier graph savings](cumulative-dense-graph.md) |
| 7 | **Pay short-fix startup once in a resident process.** Keep immutable code, fonts and templates warm; start each recording with independent scientific state. | Run two different small saved positives in reversed order, including a changed region. Record cold start, second request, RSS, and every required PNG. Report cold and warm separately. Weak-case imports previously consumed 0.927 CPU s. [Weak publication overhead](saved-overhead.md) |
| 8 | **Reuse exact solver residuals within one frozen optimization phase.** Cache the complete request and coordinate; discard it when weights, assignments or phase state change. | On one archived dense finalist phase, this saved 0.542 CPU s against its historical control, with all eight cached vectors and all optimizer decisions exact. It was a losing fixed-zero branch. Count savings only on phases the current full solver actually visits. [Measured residual cache](finalist-rejection-bound.md) |
| 9 | **Adapt the number of observations to their independent geometric information.** Use temporal leverage and diversity of Doppler slopes to construct a proposal. Let every original observation verify or reject it. | First measure how often the proposal reaches the original basin on weak, dense and translated cases, and charge exact replay. Truncating the last quarter of a dense recording had roughly doubled its position error. [Time-cutoff experiment](late-prefix-stop.md) |
| 10 | **Test a competing outside-region explanation early.** Compare the best in-region solution with an exterior alternative on a withheld time block before buying an expensive rescue. | Pair a valid boundary fix with the 100 m omissions. Measure useful withdrawals and unnecessary full solves separately. Withholding valid fixes cannot improve the timing result. Prior exterior-descent tests missed both held 100 m omissions. [Boundary evidence](local-exterior-descent.md) |

I would first combine verified orbit construction, a receiver change that skips entire calls, and a solver change that skips entire catalogue blocks or fits. Give each credit at a distinct timing boundary; measure the combination directly against v3. Use one weak physical fix, one dense physical fix and a withhold, with separate held geographic controls, before expanding. Require both the median paired candidate/v3 elapsed ratio and the ratio of summed elapsed times to reach 0.50. Keep every recording in the fix-rate denominator, compare horizontal error for both calibration arms, and check all required outputs. Report missing-region false fixes separately.
