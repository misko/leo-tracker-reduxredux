# Two-scan cancellation ceiling on the mixed signed receiver

Existing raw receipts bound the CPU cost of all second scans by **0.491, 1.449 and 4.716 seconds** on the weak, medium and dense recordings. Those clocks include the indispensable 20 ms primary search and verification on each full anchor. Advancing the same scans to a later stage leaves the receiver CPU charged. The 12 ms primary-only calls have no cancellation or residual search. Short-lane 20 ms fixed-epoch frequency correction is a separate operation. The reference-to-candidate elapsed gaps to half time are **3.232 s** on the warm weak paired full exit, **6.447 s** on the medium launch-to-detections diagnostic, and **20.486 s** on the historical dense full publication. The medium baseline's complete exit was censored by XFS writeback. The dense comparison spans separate source-residency conditions. Even removing all measured full-anchor wall time would leave these gaps. The separate dense zero-wait roster model calls for **3.490 CPU s**, which is **74.0%** of all full-anchor native CPU, or **2.058 ms per paid full call**. The earlier 86-lane matched 20 ms kernel sample saved 1.086–1.184 ms per scheduled lane with primary-only execution. Applying that range uniformly to 1,696 anchors gives **1.842–2.009 CPU s** as a sensitivity calculation on a different signal mix. The direct owner count describes the published selections. Cancellation can also change later primary acceptance through the receiver's chronological history. A prior complete older 20 ms primary-only DS30-0016 test lost 31 selections, changed 109 to 107 owners, and degraded the zero/fitted errors from **1.644/1.662 to 1.910/1.903 km**. An older dense conditional skip suppressed 1,990 second-scan requests and changed 24 lanes, including 10 primaries lost, nine residuals lost, four residuals replaced by primaries and one primary replaced by a residual. The DS30-0155 matched group restore isolated primary-only changes as the main cause of a roughly 0.27 km fitted regression. The existing runs leave the joint effect of current anchor removal, downstream reassignment, and the cost of any later confirmation open. No new waveform or numerical work was needed for this census.
