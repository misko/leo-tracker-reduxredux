# What would halve ALGO08v3 time?

The best measured complete simulated fix takes **10.835 s** against **16.761 s** for frozen v3 on one core. On North, acquisition took **5.235 s**, decision and publication **5.009 s**, and launch and other costs **0.591 s** by subtraction. The half-time target is **8.381 s**. Cutting acquisition and decision/publication by **24% each** would just reach it if the remainder stayed fixed. This is a budget for experiments. No combined physical positive-fix measurement yet qualifies this build. [Complete North comparison](combined-raw.md)

The named clocks include historical same-recording controls. These rows have different cost boundaries and builds. Adding or averaging them would obscure the performance of the actual candidate. The exact regional check matters. A previous cache delivered matching positions on a containing offer while losing that check. Both frozen v3 and the corrected research solver also accepted a translated DS30-0016 region roughly 180 km from truth. [Regional controls and corrected timings](portable-omitted-region.md) · [South raw and saved controls](south-cfo-route.md)

South requires a deeper cut. Acquisition took 5.136 s and decision plus publication 4.968 s. If launch and other costs stay fixed, cutting both stages by about **35%** meets its 7.185 s target. The guarded and all-paid South builds chose the same 456 observations but the guarded build lost four of 294 final satellite assignments per arm. This limits attribution of the timing difference to any individual optimization. [New South replay and eight verified figures](combined-raw-south.md)

## A practical trade on the weak physical fix

The original matched ALGO08v3 weak-fix clock was 10.824 s. Its half-time target is 5.412 s for 991.735 MB of compressed input. At 275 MB/s, about the rate measured on the first member, the full acquisition would take **3.606 s**, leaving **1.806 s for everything else**. At 350 MB/s it would leave **2.578 s**. Keeping only the separately measured 3.012 s decision/publication interval and granting all other overhead for free would require **413 MB/s**. No row assumes later members actually run at the first member's speed. Saving one real downstream second changes that optimistic requirement to **292 MB/s**. This makes removing a whole solver phase worth investigating alongside the receiver. ## Ranked directions

The ranking prioritizes potential on current original recordings, a decisive small experiment, and preservation of geographic transfer. Ideas for future recordings have a separate accounting boundary. All proposed gains require measurement.

| Priority | Direction | Experiment that would change the decision |
|---:|---|---|
| 1 | Start the fast solver from an assignment that the current search already produces. | The independent nine-point warm start cost **10.562 CPU s** on dense DS30-0008, above the existing **5.808 s** discovery stage, and failed held-visit checks. The translated weak offer also fell back. For a replacement, reserve verification visits before nomination, take the first hypothesis from the usual rank on the remaining visits, and count only work *after* that rank that could be skipped. Time this optimistic ceiling before implementing a new fit. [Sealed dense and omitted-region forecasts](held-spatial-start.md) |
| 2 | Keep the accuracy gains from 8 ms signal histories and buy later samples only for ambiguous cases. | On saved weak input, 8 ms changed errors from 976 / 1,452 to 490 / 980 m, while saved time increased from 4.449 to 4.516 s. North went from 113 / 132 to 112 / 128 m and 5.817 to 6.050 s. The observed primary savings minus projected tail costs and observed extra saved-process time leave roughly −0.040 s weak and −0.011 s North. This mixes stage clocks and supplies only a screening estimate. Any successor needs fewer tail purchases or a cheaper solve. [Measured endpoints and tail assumptions](eight-ms-held-fit.md) |
| 3 | Defer the second-signal search until finding it could change the position decision. | Use a first pass on all visits and retain a traceable residual for possible second detections. On saved dense and weak inputs, first give the scheduler free knowledge of which second signals change a fix or a geographic decision. That measures its optimistic ceiling. Then test a causal implementation that charges deferred reads, cancellation, false negatives and complete fallback. Include cases where several individually weak second signals jointly change the solution. |
| 4 | Use a coarse frequency estimate to prove which fine measurements can change an assignment. | For a frozen tentative assignment, bound the score change over the measurement's frequency uncertainty. Pay refinement when an alternative can win or the bound is unavailable. Replay with all measurements bought to count wrong skips, recovered observations and actual unused work beyond existing continuation. |
| 5 | Change how the catalogue is queried across neighboring position trials. | Build conservative bounds for the whole spatial cell and time interval, including alias and nuisance uncertainty. Evaluate the exact full scorer on survivors. Price bound construction and every fallback at two region sizes, then check complete rankings. Current exact pointwise block pruning saved 0.113–0.384 CPU s at ranking and failed to improve the medium complete process. [Measured block pruning](catalogue-block-portable-0316.md) |
| 6 | Try an independent competing-region fit before repeating expensive assignment repairs. | Use disjoint time blocks to support the competing explanation, carry weak or ambiguous cases through the normal solver, and charge every extra probe. Test a valid boundary fix alongside near and far omissions. This is a geographic control first. Credit speed only where it removes work while preserving valid fixes. [Previous exterior checks](local-exterior-descent.md) |
| 7 | Move exact track construction into acquisition so each observation is processed once. | Measure repeat passes on an authentic saved stream. Maintain reversible per-track statistics as detections arrive, including contributions from receiver visits without signals. Recompute joins and splits, then compare ordered assignments and geographic decisions. Count CPU in the receiver as well as CPU removed from the solver. |
| 8 | Keep analytically invariant rendering work across the eight required figures. | Trace the current publisher after its existing PNG optimizations. Bound removable geometry, layout and drawing across views before coding. Vary region, observations, font state and calibration, and compare every decoded image and complete exit time. |
| 9 | For future captures, store the measured bit width of each block losslessly. | The final comparison on one physical 64-visit member took 444.30 ms ordinary versus 342.49 ms packed (1.30×) from capture write through first verified analysis, with 2.45% less storage. All samples recovered exactly. Actual legacy conversion and independent readback cost 191.14 + 167.70 ms, and the measured legacy first read cost 215.16 ms versus 289.59 ms ordinary. That implies roughly five uses to pay back conversion and readback when retaining both copies. Replicate on a different receiver gain or sample-width distribution with full-width fallback. This ranks first among prospective capture-format changes. [Codec and boundary checks](ci16-packed-storage.md) |
| 10 | Choose costly optional optimizations by observable workload rather than enabling all of them. | Predict whether block ranking pays using catalogue and curve counts from the current recording. Fix the rule on development inputs and compare against always-on and always-off on untouched inputs. Keep the general full-catalogue route and charge the dispatch cost. |

The tested repeated-pilot FFT bank drops out of the shortlist. Even granting the true frequency residue and free folding and verification, the downsampled bank with half the delay trials took **1,499 µs/lane**, against **356 µs/lane** available even if it displaced the entire measured front end. The apparently fast variant assumed a fictitious sample period and a coarser delay grid than the accepted tolerance. [Native cost and correctness measurements](repeat-fold-bank.md)

For any shortcut with fallback, measure time saved on accepted cases, time lost on rejected cases, and both frequencies. Keep the region and thresholds frozen before revealing the reference position. Compare the same recording through all required outputs and process exit on one core. Both the median paired candidate/v3 time ratio and ratio of summed times must reach 0.50, with fix rate, both arms' horizontal errors, and missing-region decisions reported separately. The first discriminating comparisons are one weak physical fix, one dense positive, a withhold, and held geographic controls. A broad panel follows a candidate that clears those smaller tests.
