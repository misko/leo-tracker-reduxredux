# Why the DS30-0016 exterior check allowed the false fix

The two radios had unique best positions on the 24 far probes, separated by **more than 25 km**. The archived `eligible_exterior_separation` status proves that the full 96-point rule would also have allowed the fit. The site was well outside the sampled geometry. Its offset was approximately **170.464 km west and 6.321 km south** of the translated centre. The closest sampled point in the same local frame was **107.972 km** from it. The published fit's wholesale change of owners did not trigger the check. The guard first evaluates two rings at 37.5 and 62.5 km along four face normals, with tangential offsets of −5, 0 and +5 km. Those 24 points are the only positions eligible for a 25 km minimum clearance outside the face. When unique maxima disagree by over one width, a remaining point cannot make the rule pass. A better inside point kills the positive exterior gain, and a higher-scoring near-exterior point lacks clearance. A tie also fails. The routine reports `complete=false` because it skips documenting the other 72 scores. Both DS30 calibration fits touched the north face and activated the check. ## What the guard could see

`graph_points` is the number of observation IDs present in its disjoint track subset. `original_points` and `receiver_points` count all input observations, including those that contribute zero because they lack a scored track. The physical omission saw **301 of 506 observations** across 12 tracks, with original denominators **318 / 188** across radios. The earlier physical containing proof has the same aggregate support. The receipt lacks a per-radio Graph count or a track membership digest, so it leaves row-by-row support parity and which radio lost informative observations unresolved.

`catalogue_orbits` and `potentially_visible_orbits` describe a separate filter. The guard starts from all **11,181** causal orbits and builds 554 viable phase-geometry labels using the horizon envelope over all 96 original coordinates and the propagation checks. At a scored location it then requires each candidate to be above the horizon throughout the particular track. The full-catalogue denominator supplies the mixture prior. The scorer uses signed within-track frequency contrasts and sums them by receiver. It receives neither the final in-offer ownership roster nor a comparison with the original containing fit. The fact that all 115 final owners changed is independently verified and useful for comparing publications. It does not establish that their containing-owner families were among the 554 labels, were time-visible on its scored tracks, or provided strong likelihood at any individual probe. [Track and catalogue scopes in the actual scorer](../../../tools/pipeline_eval/variants/experimental/regional_reassignment.py) · [Signed owner comparison](history-risk-0016-omitted.md)

Westward probes ran, but their range stopped near 62.5 km west. The recorded site and its previously published containing coordinates never entered the physical probe set. The early-negative receipt omits the far winners' coordinates, their exact separation, score margins, and candidate NORAD survival and likelihood at each track and point. The existing 554 count and retained observation total cannot determine whether either containing-owner family contributed on the sampled geometry. The failed case therefore establishes an unsampled site direction and distance, plus measured receiver disagreement on the sampled lattice. Its receipt does not establish which support or catalogue stage caused that disagreement.

## Proposed bounded follow-up

Run a saved guard-only diagnostic with the identical published packet, original causal catalogue and original two physical offers. Capture exact pre-Graph observation IDs and track IDs by receiver, the propagation and horizon masks with surviving NORADs, and each radio's eligible maxima and margins. Score the complete original 96-point lattice once for telemetry, then eight additional positions along the four original face normals at four and eight offer widths. Freeze these radii, phases, likelihood and catalogue policy before reading a score. Use the same prepared data and normalization for both sets. Seal the location-independent scores before one separately marked diagnostic score at the existing site reference on the physical omission. That extra point would help distinguish missing coverage from a scorer that still fails to support the authentic region even when given its location. The site point and the old owner labels remain diagnostic inputs after the main scores seal.

Charge **at most 3.5 CPU s per offer** including all admission, catalogue and Graph preparation, projection, and scoring. Stop after the two physical offers if the wider scout cannot localize a stronger common region, or the added geometry removes or changes the recorded support unexpectedly. Only if the diagnosis merits transfer, run the same fixed scout on the saved North far omission and both tight mirror offers with their respective matched saved inputs. That makes the total cap **17.5 CPU s** and **521 point scores**. Preserve the original terminal decisions during the assay. A plausible direction must provide site-near bilateral support on the physical omission, preserve the established North far support and avoid a new far indication on the tight valid control. Record any continuing near-omission failure. The current evidence supports instrumenting the gate and testing coverage before considering a new withholding rule.
