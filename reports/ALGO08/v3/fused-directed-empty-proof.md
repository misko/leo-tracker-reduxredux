# Can the paid short callback supply a longer empty-lane rescue?

The frozen bracket rule cannot run inside the target lane’s original chronological 8 ms callback. Its next same-stream donor arrives **0.353–1.779 s later** across all **64,639** issued proposals in the first 30. The rule also uses final candidate Graph membership. Native waveform state for a delayed query could be retained in a new implementation. The existing receipts prove **zero exact retained-stencil matches** and contain **zero timed bracket-eligible directed calls**. This establishes a causal obstruction to same-invocation fusion and leaves delayed incremental cost unresolved. ## Exact support and negative controls

The typed check requires the same original opportunity and waveform digest, matching pilot and arithmetic identity, and exact binary64 signed CFO and fractional offset with the same native integer epoch. A published CFO can include a measured residual, so it cannot stand in for the original scout oscillator even on a positive lane. The prior 12 ms reuse experiment proved most of its positive rows identical and observed one real weak example where the scout and reported CFO differed by **443.89 Hz** and every exact prefix frame mismatch followed. Its success required a synchronous native handoff on already confirmed **positive** lanes. The current proposal targets **empty** lanes, with a different aperture and later query. [Exact 12 ms prefix transfer](short-prefix-correlations.md)

For each sealed opportunity the reducer calculates the actual native integer center, its wrapped five-center stencil, and the quarter-period control with the same CFO. It checks the original sealed query counts and right-donor waits independently on 0049, 0019, 0086 and 0274. Even with the requested seed supplied for free and all rows fully retained, the 8 ms stencil contributes at most **1,615,975 of 4,776,318** long integer frame rows (**33.83%**). The other **3,160,343** rows are beyond that short verifier’s work. With the quarter-period shift, the 64,639 controls have **zero identical integer-stencil frame rows** with the unshifted long query. These are exact coordinate counts for the named native stencil, with no waveform-derived match, fractional verification, or time saving inferred. The 48,803 distinct target visits correspond to **78.085 GB** of full decoded stereo sample extent if all such windows were retained whole until delayed query availability. A prefix proof or compact buffer would require its own measured lifetime and preparation cost. The oldest directly paid one-seed native control averaged **85.07 µs CPU** on the dense donor’s 44 installed-empty lanes with nominal proposed seeds. Its measured positive mean was **849.66 µs CPU**. The negative donor is approximately **9.7×** the first-30 additive allowance, and the positive donor approximately **96×**. These direct-call CPU means describe a different input and execution path. They do not prove a lower bound on an as-yet unmeasured fused callback. In particular the input buffer, same-lane timed phase and explicitly saved frame proof needed to isolate a fused delta were absent. An exact delayed reuse claim needs, for the same enrolled empty lane, the broad gate’s reached state and exact acquired seed or decline reason, the commanded mixer and true original waveform identity, the retained per-frame pre-reduction products with sample and arithmetic bounds, and the final query’s exact request and availability time. A paid comparison would also need the additional query CPU and elapsed, cache preparation/retention, complete tail read, and unchanged output checks. The archived metadata cannot decide that implementation’s cost or acceptance rate. Three focused tests exercise exact identity, signed bit differences, stencil coverage and rejection of late or changed seeds. This audit took no waveform, orbit, native, solver or CPU2 timing work.
