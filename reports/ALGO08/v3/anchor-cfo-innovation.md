The proposed correction increased mean absolute signed CFO disagreement from **74.12 to 85.77 Hz** and the 95th-percentile absolute error from **188.92 to 200.11 Hz** on 401 corrected, strictly matched held measurements. All three held recordings worsened on both metrics. It applied to only **412 of 11,757** candidate short positives. The technique fails the frozen advance gate and does not justify a receiver change. These differences use installed selections as a comparison and supply no localization or fitted-RF result. ## Causal substitute and held error

The substitute forecasts an 8 ms CFO at an existing full anchor from three recent short positives in the same receiver, edge, channel and actual RF. It takes half of the measured full-minus-forecast innovation when the difference is within 250 signed Hz. It clears the offset on the next full anchor and only applies it to later short positives within two seconds whose original measurements agree with their own past-only forecast. History retains original measurements and empties. No installed value from the held target or any earlier target, owner, geographic reference or waveform enters the rule. The assay leaves the native epoch, factor admission, ranks and margins intact. It does not lift CFO aliases or claim that a history fit uniquely identifies a satellite.
