## What the released likelihood can compress

For a fixed arm, mask and phase linearization, let (D_g) be a noise group’s diagonal point-noise matrix, (z_g=D_g^{-1}(	ext{lifted CFO}-	ext{Doppler intercept}-	ext{design}	imes	ext{prior mean})), and (X_g) the corresponding noise-normalized and prior-scaled design. Three quantities suffice for the group’s conditional quadratic at a given coefficient vector: (G_g=X_g^mathsf{T}X_g), (h_g=X_g^mathsf{T}z_g), and (s_g=z_g^mathsf{T}z_g). The expected conditional energy is (s_g-2h_g^mathsf{T}mu+mu^mathsf{T}G_gmu+mathrm{tr}(G_gSigma)). The existing native kernel already computes the group contractions before iterating the robust precisions. Each posterior and subsequent rate update still needs that arm’s power, precision, priors and group counts. Separate predictive terms also require the curved posterior covariance, predictive noise and bias groups. For a scalar objective one can contract their design with the same moment algebra after accounting for that covariance and bias. Original per-row innovations and the likelihood’s optimizer residual coordinates still need row-level synthesis. [Existing group and Schur decomposition](../single-core-2x/solver-schur.md) · [Released weighting and conditioning](signed-factor-roof.md) · [Predictive reduction](grid-predictive-reduction.md)

At fixed support, a weighted time basis (B_g=D_g^{-1}T_g) would allow a position-independent contraction if all changing responses and design columns could be expressed in its span. Cache the Gram of ([D_g^{-1}y_g, D_g^{-1}X_{g,mathrm{fixed}}, B_g]), then contract with each query’s response and design coefficients. Static receiver, RF calibration and smoothing columns can enter exactly, including their cross-products with the time basis. The fitted arm needs its extra RF column and its own phase coefficients and robust weights. A scalar quadratic recovered this way is exact in real arithmetic for the represented functions. A generic temporal polynomial does not exactly represent the orbital terms. The released range-rate projection already has a factor of (1/lVertmathbf p-mathbf xVert) for satellite position (mathbf p) and observer ECEF (mathbf x). Even where the orbit bank is linear in time, the pointwise range is generally the square root of a quadratic. Nominal Doppler, its two shifted phase projections and orbital modes require separate accuracy bounds. Original elevation penalties and gates need their own bounds. Rounding in reorganized Gram products and the optimizer’s use of the residual vector are additional differences from exact released replay. [Released ECEF projection and phase geometry](../../../src/leo/analysis/research/continuous_curve_fit.py) · [Fixed-column Gram experiment](../single-core-2x/solver-fixed-gram.md)

An endpoint CFO prediction gives only one radial component, after nuisance terms have been combined with orbital terms. Even if that radial component were known separately, adding a velocity component perpendicular to the endpoint line of sight would leave endpoint Doppler unchanged and generally change predicted Doppler at an off-axis query. The owner export has only observation ID, NORAD, conditional or predictive role, and measured, predicted and residual Hz. It has no timestamps, track or fragment IDs, per-point noise, powers, aliases, RF, phase reference or nominal orbit state. The observation ID is a digest. NORAD or contiguous file order cannot replace the original noise groups. The endpoint residuals and the map’s scalar scores therefore give no data-derived interpolation certificate between nodes or across the original offer. ## Prior evidence and the missing input

The earlier full-catalogue proposal already compressed every observed response into 20-second quadratic time moments while preserving orthogonal data energy. It cut 0374 broad plus exact rank from 4.400 to 1.357 CPU s. On dense 0008 it omitted the original winner before exact rescoring and worsened both calibration positions. An independent fixed-state corner check demonstrated a nominee flip from Doppler interpolation alone with unchanged visibility and aliases. Sixteen-knot discovery and salvage also failed the dense compatibility gate after restoring all rows. A separate derivative-only optimizer proposal had a 0.311 CPU s dense ceiling after original exact acceptance and final refreshes. The aperture-factor work changes the measured receiver factors themselves, so its clocks cannot price any of these fit shortcuts. [All-response time moments](../single-core-2x/solver-track-moments.md) · [Isolated interpolation failure](track-moment-exact-rescore.md) · [Track thinning with exact finish](track-quadrature.md) · [Optimizer-only ceiling](optimizer-quadrature.md) · [Receiver-aperture trial](aperture-factor-position.md)

The one distinct unresolved extension is a compressed scalar marginal likelihood at *fixed, already selected support*, including both calibration posteriors and the predictive term. To check it once, use one genuine selected-context capture with ordered rows and groups, clock/RF, aliases, noise, family powers and priors, both arms’ phase references, original offer and orbit-state interpolation and mode data. Bind those data to the node outputs and reproduce the original objective before comparing a reduced calculation at existing centre, edge and corner nodes. Bounding interpolation over the whole offer would additionally require a uniform range and derivative enclosure across every crossed orbit and phase-offset interval, with the nuisance posterior and predictive covariance sensitivity accounted for. No such operands or certificate are contained in the owner files. No benchmark, solver, orbit-body or waveform access, and no runtime changes were needed for this audit.
