# What it would take to halve ALGO08v3 runtime

The comparator is installed ALGO08v3 on one core. The two gates are the median of paired candidate/v3 elapsed ratios and total candidate elapsed divided by total v3 elapsed. Both must be at most 0.500 from original signed input through process exit, including the eight figures for every published fix. A candidate also has to retain fix coverage, position accuracy in both calibration arms and geographic withholding. DS30 is a one-site archive.

## Observations that change the design

- **The original three shortcuts have a measured ceiling.** Stopping losing refinements, reusing fixed solver layouts, and pruning fine recovery by region together address an estimated 12.34 of the 54.55 CPU seconds needed on dense v3 even if their enclosing operations become free. Their combined prototype preserved positions and all eight figures on two physical recordings. It saved just 0.16 CPU s on the matched-cache weak case. [Original #1, #2 and #4](bounds-combined.md)
- **The short receiver makes real progress on dense data.** A complete DS30-0008 candidate took 95.43 s versus 149.89 s for historical v3, or 1.57×. Its CPU was 67.35 versus 109.10 s. The candidate began with 2.84% of the payload resident versus 76.75% for v3. On identical saved detections, replacing regional scoring in a later candidate cut solve CPU from 30.47 to 26.10 s while reproducing all owners, both positions and eight PNGs. Those saved seconds have yet to be measured in a matched raw pair. [Raw run](dense-full-short-raw.md) · [Solver pair](regional-dense-pair.md)
- **A faster receiver can yield a slower fix.** On DS30-0057 the candidate reached detections at 15.45 s versus 18.01 s, but reached the terminal at 42.34 versus 39.59 s. It improved horizontal error from 426/525 m to 240/297 m for zero/fitted calibration even as the RF residual increased. The candidate published in 62.52 s. The fresh stock publication timed out while syncing its figures. Both withheld on the 18-visit DS30-0227, where complete elapsed time was 13.77 versus 13.65 s. The latter alone precludes a paired median ratio of 0.500 across these two diagnostics. [Matched raw diagnostics](short-raw-diagnostic.md)
- **Where evidence comes from matters.** Using both earlier and later signal history added nine assigned observations on the weak physical case and preserved all existing owners. In the north simulation the same rule lost 77 assignments and added 75. Fixed-zero error climbed from 106 m on v3 to 434 m. On the southern simulation the rule improved both estimates. Simple cross-radio confirmation recovered zero extra observations on these three cases. Both near-edge missing-region tests and containing offers belong in every quality screen. [Transfer results](short-subset-update.md) · [Near-edge evidence](geographic-jackknife.md)
- **Some exact shortcuts are worth keeping for narrow reasons.** Bypassing the regional catalogue when there are zero graph tracks removed 2.07 CPU s on the withheld case and reproduced its terminal. A bounded decoder recovered 93.4–93.8 MiB compared with full-member buffering and exactly reproduced detections. Acquisition CPU against the released reader changed by +2.85 and −17.83 ms in reversed order on one signed member. Neither observation establishes faster dense end-to-end processing. [Empty-graph pair](measurements/empty-graph-pair.json) · [Decoder](fused-window-ring.md)

## How much headroom is there?

The dense receiver processed 8,182 MB in 60.449 s elapsed, of which 32.403 s was acquisition CPU. A 250 MB/s target allows 32.728 s **total**, leaving only **0.325 s outside CPU** at the same CPU cost. The measured difference between elapsed and CPU was 28.046 s and includes scheduling and I/O waits. Even a read-ahead design capable of hiding almost all of it would have very little margin. Every CPU second removed makes another second of exposed wait affordable on that workload. [Recorded acquisition inputs](measurements/dense-measured-budget.json)

Even if that prefix rate transferred to a complete recording, a 16 s solve would still miss on the first four. This favors bounded local repairs and spending most solver engineering effort on moving below 16 s. ## Ten experiments in recommended order

| Rank | Direction | Smallest useful test and decision |
|---:|---|---|
| 1 | **Protect established tracks and repair locally.** Attach a future-supported detection only to an existing same-radio, same-RF track when its Doppler, alias and satellite remain compatible. | On weak physical and northern/southern simulated packets, list gained/lost owners, time the incremental fit, compare both position estimates and omissions. An eligible anchor is only an opportunity until the refit succeeds. |
| 2 | **Update a fit only where an assignment changed.** Cache the linear nuisance-parameter design for unchanged tracks. Update affected rows when ranking alternative assignments and recompute the full robust objective for finalists. | Replay one ambiguous dense family and one weak family. Measure affected rows, exact winner/rank, full refinement time and fallback bill. Common robust weights cannot be assumed across arms or trials. |
| 3 | **Verify file integrity while feeding the receiver in sample order.** Use bounded decode storage and measure where each copy and each wait happens across consecutive signed members. | Alternate original and candidate on a few already cached members, then check a naturally cold prefix. Retain original digests, order, failure behavior and memory bound. Report acquisition MB/s, CPU, and elapsed outside CPU separately. |
| 4 | **Eliminate unnecessary waveform work before the expensive verifier.** Try a conservative partial-correlation upper bound from the unprocessed pilot and signal energies. Reject only if even the best possible completion cannot pass the existing threshold. | Price an ideal rejection bound on saved weak and dense lanes, including the cost of obtaining energies and searching all eligible timing/frequency hypotheses. Stop if certificates cost more than the verification they avoid. |
| 5 | **Spend exact waveform checks on observations that resolve a positional ambiguity.** Use the disagreement between viable assignments over the supplied region to request a longer aperture on particular visits. | Compute best-case recovery and reread costs using saved short/full counterparts. Freeze the selection rule before applying it at another location. Include any extra input pass in the clock. |
| 6 | **Reuse neighboring grid fits.** Warm-start each required grid point from a neighboring point, carry both calibration arms separately, and fall back to the original start on ambiguity. | Run the full 179-point saved grid. Compare pointwise scores, failed cells, minima, both rendered maps and all retries. Grid export alone was about 1.93 s, so assess savings across numerical evidence preparation as well. |
| 7 | **Score only the smallest track subset needed to certify a losing association branch.** Accumulate a conservative bound on its best achievable robust objective with the remaining tracks. | On a dense finalist race, count exact fits eliminated net of bound computation. Include alias alternatives and both calibration extrema. Restore full evidence for every surviving branch. |
| 8 | **Recognize impossible regional checks early.** Extend the zero-track shortcut only where graph structure proves the existing precheck cannot form its required disjoint evidence set. | Compare decisions and reasons on zero-, one- and multi-track saved cases and the omitted-region simulations. The final solver still makes the publication decision. |
| 9 | **Use geometry-balanced observations for initial ranking.** Select epochs and satellites that constrain both horizontal directions after nuisance parameters are accounted for. Restore all observations for final scoring and figures. | Measure the weakest horizontal direction on several offered positions and compare shortlist recall with full ranking. Stop if the saved solve requires frequent full reruns or loses a viable location. |
| 10 | **Price publication on its actual filesystem.** Stage figure bytes and batch independent work before mandatory synchronization. Keep atomic publication and each durability check. | Compare identical saved products in both orders, separating render CPU, writes and sync wait. Verify hashes and interrupted retries. Transfer from a healthy local filesystem to the serving filesystem requires separate evidence. |

The first test for #10 is complete. On healthy local ext4, batching saved 16.76 ms when run second and added 1.44 ms when run first. All eleven files matched and both builds made the same fourteen sync calls. This gives no repeatable elapsed saving to enter in the model. The earlier timeout occurred on a separate XFS volume. [Paired writeback experiment](png-batched-writeback.md)

The first combined prototype should include a quality-preserving local repair, a materially cheaper solve, and an authenticated receiver with measured acquisition headroom. Use saved packets to reject ideas cheaply. Advance to four paired raw runs only after the evidence supports both timing gates and preserves both calibration arms on the geographic controls, then to the frozen sixteen. Report missed fixes, false fixes on omitted regions and horizontal error separately from frequency residual RMS. Continue to use a single low-priority benchmark slot on Gauss.
