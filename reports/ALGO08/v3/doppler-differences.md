With the original curved and predictive owners supplied, covariance-weighted within-track Doppler differences still gave **9.568 km** horizontal error on physical DS30-0016 and **7.526 km** on public North. The two calibration arms gave identical positions on each recording because the fitted RF coefficient cancels exactly within each fixed-RF track. A separate fit preserving the track means could retain the RF calibration column, but its horizontal errors reached **14.939 km** on DS30-0016 and **12.386 km** on North. No method passed the accuracy gate, so the trial ended before a paid assignment proposal or new publication. ## What the experiment isolates

Each case used the original offered square, strict causal TLE archive and signed detector packet already staged in RAM. A current saved combined worker reproduced the exact locked curved and predictive owners and their signed aliases from the earlier witness, giving 116 observations across three tracks on weak and 347 observations across 18 tracks on North. The diagnostic received the actual CFO values and declared conditional RF noise, including the released predictive noise multiplier. It initialized at the supplied centre, used the causal phase reference for exact orbital projections, and never received the oracle endpoints, donor residuals or learned weights. The reference was used only in the scorer after both children exited. [Input and oracle verification](doppler_difference_trial.py) · [Earlier owner witness](centre-oracle-step.md)

For adjacent differences (D), the joint noise is (D\operatorname{diag}(\sigma^2)D^\top). A sample shared by two adjacent differences contributes a negative covariance to their errors. Computing GLS in the original row space by subtracting the inverse-variance mean and dividing by each noise scale gives the same quadratic norm. The scope assumes independent declared per-observation noise before taking differences. It does not represent the full robust Gamma uncertainty, shared posterior covariance or additional unmodeled temporal dependence. Through both inputs, the released RF term (200000(\mathrm{RF}_{\rm GHz}-11.5)), the signed discrete alias and the receiver-band offset each stay constant within one graph track. All pairwise differences therefore erase them. No choice of times or covariance weights can estimate (c) from these differences alone. The predeclared second estimator additionally retains one independent weighted mean per track with the original 300 Hz track-bias scale plus its measurement variance. Both estimators project the complete actual shared drift, receiver smooth, phase and supported orbit mode design without shrinkage. An original 2D trust loop recalculates exact released geometry and the projected loss at each trial point. This differs from the previous Gaussian ridge, which kept absolute CFO and finite priors on the shared nuisance terms, and from the earlier arc design, which used a relative nominal information criterion and paid the original exact fit. Time quadrature and secant work retain the released robust objective and target fewer derivative evaluations. Profiled Gamma accelerates an inner update of that objective.
