# Frozen interpolation of archived regional mixture scores

Use only the six public containing and omitted offers in the pinned `geographic-rf-fold.json.gz` archive. Verify that each offer has the original ordered 48 inside and 48 exterior coordinates, exact previously reconstructed evidence and the original paired native digest. Use the archived pooled score, which combines both receivers. The estimator receives only the published square, original 96 point coordinates and pooled scores. The other receipt fields and all survey/reference positions are outside the estimation interface. Seal all six estimates before reference scoring. The three sites and offers have been studied previously.

Find the best of all 96 samples. Preserve original probe order for a tied discrete maximum and explicitly abstain from interpolation on a best-score tie. Take the 12 nearest points including the winner, sorted by squared Euclidean distance from it and original probe index. Express positions as offsets from the winner divided by the positive square width and subtract its score. Fit the six quadratic coefficients by unweighted least squares on those 12 points. Require full column rank, a design condition number at most `1e6`, and finite coefficients and stationary coordinates. The Hessian must be strictly negative definite by more than `32 * binary64 epsilon * max(1, max absolute Hessian coefficient)`. Require the stationary point to lie within the closed convex hull of the 12 points, allowing `1e-12` width-scaled boundary roundoff. Record all failures and retain the independent discrete maximum. Use no alternative neighborhood size, clipping, extrapolation or case-specific tuning.

Evaluate the sign of signed face clearance using the original square, with exactly zero inside. Report discrete and valid interpolated position, error, clearance and side. Count invalid interpolations separately and evaluate the explicit discrete fallback for an overall decision. Compare against the existing original position calibration arms for the same offer where available. Quantify each change in position error and containment judgment on the previously known development sites. A geometric seed has value if it improves the surveyed starting error. These data cannot measure convergence or the reliability of a new withholding policy.

Replay the released terminal short circuit by reading the archived receiver scores to derive how many coordinates it would have asked for on the identical lattice. Record the archived wall and CPU cost of all 96 fresh scores and the measured offline interpolation cost. The probe count difference describes the extra required score evaluations when starting from a terminal-only calculation. It supplies no runtime projection for the additional coordinates. Use the shared Gauss lock and one low-priority numerical thread for timed work. No propagation, fits, raw reads, new offers or release changes.
