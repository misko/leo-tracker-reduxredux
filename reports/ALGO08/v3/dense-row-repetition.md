# Repeated normalized design rows in the dense robust fit

All **3,661** normalized design rows were distinct within their **82** original noise groups on the genuine short DS30-0008 fit. Exact row aggregation could remove **zero of 1,298,941 Gram products** and **zero of 68,911 right-hand-side products**. The distinct-row fallback reused the original shadow statistics bit for bit. The installed posterior ran unchanged and returned the original objective, **48,735.79707797666**, with a checked factor. The census used one fresh curved posterior at the offered-region centre in the fixed-zero arm. The original 261-column design had 16–19 active columns per group and 12–100 rows per group. The same six site-independent hardware columns already distinguished every retained row after dividing by its original noise scale. The released constructor computes these columns from receiver, channel and time. It changes only orbital terms or other columns when moving the site or changing the phase reference. The fitted calibration adds a separate RF column. The archived grid establishes shared track support and identical noise scales and group IDs across the two arms at all 177 paired fresh coordinates. On that fixed support, the unique hardware rows rule out full-row repetitions in either arm as the geographic point changes, even where a subset of geometry values agrees. Other fits can use other masks, group layouts or bases. They were outside this census. [Original grid operand audit](grid-cross-arm-posterior.md) · [Installed-boundary checks](dense_row_repetition.py)

The native kernel constructs each noise group's Gram and right-hand side once, then applies the group's changing Gamma precision to those statistics. For identical normalized design \(z\) within one group, the candidate substitutes \(mzz^\top\) and \(z\sum_i y_i/\sigma_i\) for \(m\) separate products. Every original response, residual energy term, and per-group precision stays in the likelihood. Synthetic duplicate rows show that rearrangement can change binary64 results even with exactly matching design bytes. Real-valued equivalence would need a numerical guard in a runtime candidate. In the saved census the all-distinct fallback gave exactly zero Gram and right-hand-side drift.
