## What the byte ceiling buys

Naive DSP demand would omit **6 prefixes and 52 tails**, or **39,040,000 raw bytes**. Buying the actual CFO retries raises demand by 2,560,000 raw bytes and 1,920,008 packed bytes over the earlier unrefined DSP path. The earlier packed first-use trial still opened and authenticated every encoded byte, despite skipping raw decode for some blocks. Keeping that encoded verification gives **zero packed-byte read savings**. Independently precommitted demand-only storage could omit **29,200,116 bytes** of encoded payload, at most 146.0 ms of ideal serial transfer at 200 MB/s before fixed and access costs. Both quantities are ceilings at this member's observed block sizes. [Earlier first-use boundary](ci16-packed-storage.md)

In the measured full packed runs, the median encoded authentication clock was **44.40 ms** and full decode, checksum and array formation took **92.09 ms**. Giving away the entire latter clock would limit the gain of a decode-only proposal against ordinary acquisition to about **87.04 ms per member** at these clocks. This grants decode of the requested 63.36 MB for free and omits any new demand scheduling, so it overstates the opportunity. A byte-proportional decode model assigns about 35.11 ms to the unused 38.125% and projects **30.05 ms** per member after the measured packed slowdown. It supplies neither an actual saved CPU clock nor a full-recording inference. Preserving the installed live full-verification boundary permits **zero skipped input bytes**.

## Authority and implementation

The installed source verifies the signed manifest and index, the ordinary compressed object, each original full-window CI16 SHA-256 and the ordered complete decoded member SHA-256. The native `raw_recording_authority_digest` subsequently commits to the inventory and ordered full-window digests. A prefix and tail hash cannot be combined into the original SHA-256. Even signed per-block commitments describe expected bytes and only authenticate current storage bytes when checked against data or an independently trusted storage-verification proof. With the existing contract, missing or corrupt unused blocks must still fail the same acquisition before publication. The prototype retains the full verification boundary. [Installed sample checks](../../../src/leo/storage/short_window.py) · [Preserved source path](packed_combined_input.py)

An independently attested capture or conversion could bind the exact manifest and index, full decoded hashes, encoded and decoded block hashes, order and sizes, and the lossless codec identity at the same time it validates and durably seals the whole stream. Checking every compressed block against such an independent binding would give a proof of the decoded equivalence of unread decoded tails through the deterministic codec. Relying on that proof in place of the installed per-run decoded verification needs explicit contract review. Omitting encoded reads as well requires a separately reviewed verified-read contract that permits relying on the capture attestation for unrequested blocks. It would catch corrupt requested blocks on demand. It would no longer establish that an unread tail remains retrievable during that analysis. A filesystem Merkle proof for requested ranges has the same availability limit. This is a prospective trust and verification boundary, separate from adopting the existing read-only input adapter.

After review, an aperture-aware port can authenticate metadata and evaluate the present admission gate before loading samples. Advance the real schedule once per admitted visit, supply an owned 120,000-sample array to the guarded short ABI and the real 200,000-sample array to a full anchor. The existing causal CFO policy triggers a single shared tail read only after a paid short decision. The refinement receives the actual full CI16 and retains the normal exact fallback. The smallest runtime test is a newly sealed miniature fixture with an invalid visit, a full anchor, a short visit without retry and a paid stereo retry. Compare its full and demand paths for exact signed probes, ordered authority, shared tail count and corruption outcomes. While the existing integrity policy remains in force, that same fixture must still reject an unread corrupted tail before sealing. This review measured the demand counts without opening any waveform or timing another receiver.

The two focused tests cover shared paid stereo tails, invalid visits, wrong paid lanes and reordered blocks.
