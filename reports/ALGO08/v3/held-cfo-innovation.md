# A held-out CFO residual finds disagreements but fails the strict correction gate

On held DS30-0086, the residual found **12 signed-CFO disagreements in 37 flagged positive short lanes**. The same-size hash control found **two**. Substituting the held-out forecast moved all 12 flagged disagreements closer to the installed frequency and restored 10 to within 250 Hz. The substitution also moved nine previously in-range carriers outside that window. Keeping the original measured arrival, the residual rule gained five strict matches and lost nine, a **net loss of four**. The hash gained one and lost three. DS30-0086 met the minimum count for the frozen decision and missed its strict-match gate. The study stopped before DS30-0019 and DS30-0274. [Frozen protocol](held-cfo-innovation-protocol.md) · [Candidate-only selection](measurements/held-cfo-innovation-plan.json) · [Scored receipt](measurements/held-cfo-innovation-score.json)

The first hypothesis was that a large held-out residual flags real signed-CFO disagreement. The second was that taking the forecast reduces the error enough to restore usable matches. The already sealed odd-short predictor dropped the complete target visit on both radios before winner selection and Graph formation. It used adjacent unique signed-alias donors on the same Graph piece, receiver and RF. This experiment reused those independent forecasts, then reopened each original candidate measurement to flag cases where its signed CFO differed from the forecast by more than 250 Hz. The equal-count hash draws from the same originally positive lanes with a forecast and applies the same forecast replacement. The strict outcome is ±250 signed Hz and ±2 physical arrival samples on the same installed winner. [Original blind forecast and Graph custody](odd-short-interpolation.md)

The held residual captured **12 of 272** original signed-CFO misses on candidate-positive odd short visits, a recall of 4.4%. A forecast existed for 22 of those 272. Of the 1,817 original candidate-positive lanes on the held visits, 469 had no installed winner, 272 had a signed CFO miss and 1,076 already passed the joint window. On 0049 the corresponding counts were 91, 42 and 161 of 294. Neither case had a delay-only miss among original in-range carriers. Some large CFO misses also fell outside the arrival window, so retaining the measured arrival admits fewer rescued carriers. Substituting the forecast arrival as well gave an auxiliary net gain of one on 0086. Across all flagged 0086 lanes with an installed winner, the median absolute frequency error increased by 34 Hz with the residual rule and decreased by 16 Hz with the hash.

Of the 37 flagged lanes on 0086, 36 have an installed winner and 24 originally meet both thresholds. A chooser with access to the reference answers could keep the old winner on its successes and use the forecast on any recoverable miss. That ceiling is **34/36 for CFO alone** (two outside both choices), **29/36 for the joint window keeping the measured arrival** (seven outside both), and **34/36 for the joint window when the forecast carries its own arrival** (two outside both). This ceiling prices neither a real discriminator nor its receiver cost. The one installed-empty query remains unscored.

All 1,622 and 8,372 original short detector calls remain charged. The proposed corrections would touch 15 of 294 and 37 of 1,817 original positive lanes in the held subsets, leaving 279 and 1,780 unchanged. An installed-empty lane has no measured reference CFO, and an installed/candidate mismatch can reflect different peaks. The test establishes reference agreement at matched hardware windows. It supplies no new detector, emission, or position evidence.
