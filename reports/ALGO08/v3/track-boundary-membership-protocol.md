# Opposite-radio matched track boundary test

Use DS30-0049 for development and the first eight subsequent chronological verified pairs in `field-first30-ZvFir1` for held scoring. Do not choose recordings by a position or gate outcome. Rebind the original native packets, decision receipts, immutable aperture schedules and opportunities. Use the original installed Graph on the retained installed packet only. It decomposes by receiver and RF lane. Use each original Graph piece as a donor for membership of the *other* receiver on the same visit. Preserve both directions. Never fit a position, open waveforms or take a timing lease.

Keep donor pieces with at least eight unique observations and eight seconds of observed span. Split any piece at an adjacent gap over eight seconds. Define start and end windows inside each retained donor piece, each of width `min(4 seconds, observed span/4)`. Establish a target-radio frequency and fractional epoch offset only from simultaneous interior full/full anchors for which the target's two selected factors are canonically identical. Require at least two such rows spanning two seconds, with every calibration row within ±250 signed Hz and ±2 samples of the corresponding median difference. Use the raw reported offsets in each radio with no alias or frame wrapping. This piece intercept uses no boundary target labels. At boundary visits, each target method independently belongs to the donor component if its selected peak, after this intercept, lies within that same ±250 Hz and ±2 sample gate of the contemporaneous donor factor. No candidate enters by its own track construction or future position.

Pair start and end opportunities within a donor piece by distance from their respective boundary, separately for original short visits and full/full anchors. Give each edge the same number of observations by dropping unpaired trailing opportunities. Let `D` be the difference of the mixed-minus-installed membership rate at the end and start. The predeclared primary statistic is `D_short - D_full` on pieces with at least one balanced pair of each aperture. A first 8 ms aperture predicts a positive contrast if it loses arrivals that the full aperture sees later in the visit and retains departures that the full aperture misses later. Compute an equal-piece estimate within each recording and an equal-recording held mean, with a fixed-seed 95% case-bootstrap interval. Report all qualifying short-only and full-only pair exposures separately and keep the calibration data out of the boundary tables. Require at least five eligible held recordings and a positive interval before treating the result as a direction for a detector design.

This is a retrospective membership comparison conditional on a contemporaneous opposite-radio detection and the original Graph piece. The installed donor, continued full selection, periodic anchor placement, cross-radio matching errors and sparse revisit times can affect observed piece boundaries. Neither a true physical emitter transition nor the state inside a missing pilot aperture is observed. Full/full anchors control the original packet-selection difference at their own scheduled visits. A receiver replay or causal rule would require distinct evidence and cannot be priced from edge counts alone.
