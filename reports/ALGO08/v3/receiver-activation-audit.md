# Which measured receiver changes are active in the sparse 20/8 route?

The sparse original-input route has no missing receiver replacement that has qualified for exact output and reproducible whole-acquisition savings. The highest measured missing opportunity is guarded phase rotation inside the 20 ms anchor verifier. It changes some continuous score bits and needs its own recipe. The strongest exact missing candidate is the selective pilot-support loader. Its native CPU gain did not pass the signed acquisition gate.

## Executed bindings

Both integrated-original DS30-0016 orders verified 808 original rows, admitted 713 stereo visits, and paid **186 full / 1,240 short lane calls**. Full and short callback CPU were **0.468–0.476 s** and **1.009–1.027 s**, respectively. The other acquisition work used **1.416–1.419 CPU s**. Native 8 ms runs on seven of each eight admitted visits within a pilot-edge, channel and actual-RF stream, with original full anchors and independent evaluation of both antennas. The final held selector returned `unfiltered`, with **zero refinement calls**. DS30-0359 has an inferred **1,296 full / 9,024 short** cadence from the authentic installed 20 ms packet. Neither the candidate's shortlisted lanes nor its actual raw CPU on that recording have been measured. The existing donor model places authenticated acquisition at **22.57–26.35 CPU s**. For an exact-output candidate, keep the registered binary pair. If a distinct detector recipe with tightly qualified continuous-score drift is acceptable, the best-supported next measurement would change **only the full-anchor verifier rotation** on the installed full base, keeping the sparse short and both scans. Retain the exact absolute-phase fallback for unsupported targets and near gates, eligibility or rank ties. A resource-authorized follow-up would verify dense, weak and shifted-center cases on the composed route through acquisition, then validate both calibration arms and signed owner selection where it publishes. The 8 ms oscillator transfer and any combination with selective loading would each need its own comparison. This desk audit used existing receipts and binaries only, with no waveform reads or benchmark lease.
