## Where the map enters the pipeline

The selected context already contains both final positions, owners, calibration and phase references before `prepare_packet` builds the grid. It first verifies each endpoint's full published objective and constructs owner residuals and orbit lanes. The grid calls the same marginal likelihood during presentation preparation, including curved posterior export, predictive integration, extra-family score and the elevation penalty. The exterior policy and selected observations precede this call. Only the position figure uses the numerical grid's scores. A shifted grid minimum would change the map without moving the published fix. The existing sidecar describes actual evaluations of the original likelihood at its requested coordinates. A complete estimated map needs its own research provenance. The read-only audit verified all four original terminal, path and grid bindings and all 32 original PNG identities, dimensions and PNG integrity. Official results and files stayed intact. ## What the unchecked map would change

Holding the same 51 paid pairs and ignoring the guard gives the following post hoc scores. The contour comparison uses the same triangulation of the original requested coordinates and 25 comparison points per line segment at relative losses 2.30, 4.50 and 9.21. A mode here is a discrete local minimum among sampled mesh neighbors, with a low-loss mode within 9.21 units of that map's minimum. The counts and distances describe the numerical map. They do not define confidence coverage in kilometres for the true location.

The two dense arms replaced their low-loss minima with a neighboring grid location, and up to 25 points switched contour membership at a tested level. Weak added three low-loss modes at fixed zero and one with fitted calibration. South added a low-loss mode in both arms on both offers and also created high-loss corner modes on some maps. The weakest checked dense sentinel still disagreed by 0.413 and 0.099 objective units in the two arms. The 0.25 empirical radius would require both of a cell's sentinel errors to be at most 0.06225 in both arms. South sentinels missed individual held errors up to 65.687 despite detecting enough disagreement to trigger full fallback. These results leave zero reduction under the frozen schedule. The two compatible held dense values are a hindsight count for this one surrogate, with contour and mode constraints waived. Pricing them as `2/178` of the entire measured evidence stage gives 0.063 CPU s before scheduling. At least 64 dense locations would need to be skipped to reach two seconds even under that optimistic proportional model. The exact-only contract and the lack of a source-derived error enclosure further limit usable interpolation. Held sentinels do not rule out a localized low mode between paid nodes. An owned test demonstrates two functions that agree at every paid location and disagree on the map minimum. The location likelihood can change through robust weights, predictive covariance and elevation penalties as position moves, and the candidate supplies no derivative bound for those regimes.
