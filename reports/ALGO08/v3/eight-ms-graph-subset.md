# Two added observations change the prepared North graph

Keeping all original 12 ms measurements, **two 8 ms-only factors reproduced the entire North addition-only graph**. Each was the sole sufficient factor in its changed RF/receiver/channel stream. Removing either factor from the full 652-lane addition restored the original graph on that stream. The other 650 additions left the complete original graph partition and curvature flags intact. On DS30-0016, one extra factor changed the graph even though the earlier addition-only position, RF RMS and final owners stayed exact.
