# The held-anchor route changes under imposed detector dropout

One fixed stereo blackout changed **history → unfiltered on both North and South**, while leaving each recording’s geometry and surviving measurements fixed. The change persisted when scored on only the **same 128 untouched native full anchors per radio** before and after dropout. This establishes that changing short-observation availability alone can reverse the frozen whole-recording route. [Frozen crossed audit](selector-occupancy-protocol.md) · [Per-opportunity and block evidence](measurements/selector-occupancy.json.gz)

The public simulation archive has one RF waveform per site. Its alternative offers reuse those waveforms. North and South have different geometry and pilot edges, among other differences. No independently verified rerender varies noise, interference or underlying signal visibility at either fixed site. This audit crosses the genuine signed 20/12 ms detector output from each site with the same location-independent record-level experiment. The blackout erases every factor on both radios in selected blocks of 16 issued visits. It retains the original visit roster, clocks, candidate values on unaffected visits, and cadence. The fixed mask realized **224/1,200 stereo visits** on each site. [Public simulation design](../regional-release/README.md) · [Audit implementation](selector_occupancy.py)

Unfiltered positives fall from 483/476 to 377/377 on North and 305/303 to 237/234 on South. Removing measured visits also changes which later rows qualify under the original carrier history. On the common native anchors, the treatment-minus-control decline excludes zero for every radio under both existing paired 128- and 256-visit bootstraps. The treated gains themselves have intervals spanning zero. The blocks reflect sensitivity within these two captures. [Per-radio block intervals](measurements/selector-occupancy.json.gz)

The blackout models unavailable detector output. It does not generate new interference, noisy timing or CFO measurements, or a fresh receiver response. The altered objects are explicitly unqualified for native publication. The observed route flips do not identify why the earlier physical and simulated rosters differed, and no position, RF fit or regional decision was estimated from the changed roster.
