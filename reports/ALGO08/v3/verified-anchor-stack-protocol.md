# A causal carrier stack from verified full receiver anchors

Use the same saved first 256 South stereo visits as the trajectory-oracle study. Pin the raw manifest and original installed profile-guided v3 detector. The prior archive must establish fewer than six repeating same-RF opportunities in the local DS26/DS30 clips. Do not open cold physical I/Q.

The receiver keeps histories separately by antenna, actual RF, channel and pilot edge. Force a complete two-scan measurement once per eight opportunities per lane and again on the following same-lane opportunity when a full measurement finds its first nonempty new track. A full confirmation updates the history. A directed result never seeds a later forecast. Clear the forecast when the complete measurement returns no factor, has multiple verified factors or cannot link to the prior full measurement. Treat two preceding unique full measurements, at most 8 seconds apart and at most 16 seconds stale, as the only source of carrier and arrival timing. Bound carrier and wrapped arrival slopes to 8 kHz/s and 1,000 raw samples/s. Derive the timing seed by extrapolating the wrapped hardware-counter arrival phase and restoring window-relative phase. Derive the frequency bank from the signed anchor secant, with slope adjustments `−150, 0, +150` Hz/s and offsets `−500, 0, +500` Hz. The unknown current phase is estimated from these past measurements.

Score the nine local CFO paths from current CI16 using the previous native cyclic spectrum. For the stacked arm sum the standardized surface at the corresponding predicted frequency on at most four same-lane opportunities since the latest full anchor, including the current one. The current-only arm uses the same work and recovery policy with just the current score. Send the best signed carrier and the extrapolated arrival through the existing independent native refine and exact-versus-rolled waveform verifier. The scheduled-only control pays the same anchors and uses the same documented discovery trigger, with no continuation prediction. It can therefore measure the gain due to prediction and not just sparse full detection.

Inspect the highest standardized in-band carrier score on every nonforced lane. Pay a complete two-scan reacquisition and discard the directed result when this score is at least 10 and there is no valid predicted bank, the strongest peak is farther than 4.55 kHz from every current bank hypothesis, or independent verification rejects the nominated factor. A weak prediction may still run without satisfying the new-arrival trigger. Count all scheduled searches, confirmation warm-ups, blank scans, declined directed calls, new arrivals and any post-confirmation full retry. Two-carrier anchor ambiguity invalidates the single-track bank. The arrival trigger may fail for a hidden residual companion. Score losses explicitly rather than granting recovery from invisible future rows.

Each arm reads the same authenticated waveform and receives only previous full factors, signed tuning and CI16. Run one contiguous warm resident prefix against the installed full native receiver. Score afterward by rank, signed CFO (250 Hz) and wrapped absolute timing (two native samples), with selected chronological continuation computed independently for each arm. Report held-factor and new-arrival recall, decoy calls, stage clocks, and the cost of perfect retry routing plus original costs for each paid full acquisition. Use 95% factor and selected-continuation coverage with no unmatched selected factor, and at most 0.60 CPU ms per admitted lane, as the screen for advancement. Separately model the dense 6,170-factor ideal using the original measured 1-in-8 anchor CPU and the cheapest existing exact-timing oracle verifier, with no free transfer of the simulation's purity or trigger quality.
