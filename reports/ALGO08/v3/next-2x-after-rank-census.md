# Halving ALGO08v3 runtime after the receiver and assignment experiments

The matched physical result for the combined 20/12 ms receiver and solver is **12.108 → 9.075 seconds** across two warm DS30 recordings on one Gauss core. One recording produced a position and one withheld. The median candidate/v3 elapsed ratio was **0.568** and the ratio of totals was **0.750**. Each must reach 0.500 from the original signed samples through publication and process exit. The 100-recording release comparison measured an earlier change of baseline, from deployed v2 to v3. [Matched raw runs](unified-short-layout.md) · [Earlier v2 comparison](https://github.com/misko/leo-tracker-reduxredux/blob/d5ccea7dca5cdd1e272159029d91a760a9856449/reports/ALGO08/v3/ds30-100/README.md)

## What changes the engineering decision

On these two recordings the summed budget is **6.054 s**. The current candidate needs to lose **3.021 s**, about one-third of its own runtime, to reach it. Future paired recordings determine whether that saving carries across the workload.

## Ten experiments in priority order

The ranking mixes new designs with revisions to earlier experiments. Each first test can use saved data. All timings below described as targets are budgets, not measured gains.

| Rank | Design | First test and reason to stop |
|---:|---|---|
| 1 | **Updated — make the science-preserving solver the integration baseline.** Start from v3 observation selection and phase updates. Apply the faster graph, regional scoring and figure production separately wherever they reproduce the same science, then combine. | On 0155 and 0160 hold detections fixed. Cross the original and candidate observation lists with the original and candidate fitting code, retaining arm-specific inputs. This separates observation selection, fit changes and their interaction. Use timing only for combinations that recover both positions and the expected fix decision. [Earlier scope of exact reuse](dense-assignment-reuse.md) |
| 2 | **New — give difficult position searches a local derivative.** Within a smooth region of a finalist's fit, compute the change of predicted signed frequency with horizontal position alongside the prediction. Use it to propose a step, then accept using the full original robust objective. | Replay the longest optimizer trace. Check derivatives against finite differences, especially near alias changes, a region boundary and poor geometry. Count all retries and both phase refreshes. Stop if costly objective evaluations or total cold-fit time fail to fall. |
| 3 | **New — filter a block of receiver hypotheses with a conservative signal-energy bound.** Accumulate part of a matched-filter dot product, then bound the most the remaining samples could add using measured residual and template energy. If an entire block cannot reach the incumbent, skip its remaining dots. Leave timing and frequency choices open on each antenna. | On stored weak and dense lanes record the rejection fraction and exact operations removed, including the cost of constructing the bounds. ## When to expand the benchmark

Start with the paired observation-list experiment in row 1, then measure the cost of a finalist's objective evaluations in row 2 and the receiver ceiling in row 3. Include the verified drawing change in the final combined timing. Price row 4 separately as a higher-risk receiver alternative. A dense model with the measured 22.756 CPU s solve needs an effective **283 MB/s** on the first four enrolled cases. The dense receiver previously used 32.403 CPU s for 8.182 GB, equivalent to at most **253 MB/s** even with zero waits. Under those assumptions, acquisition still needs at least **3.49 CPU s** of savings. A faster solver changes that requirement. The short, warm recording achieved 292 MB/s but does not establish sustained dense speed. [Joint budget and assumptions](warm-recording-budget.md)

Measure a fix and a withdrawal, an ambiguous later-ranked winner, and a dense input before expanding. Preserve signed inputs, both calibration arms, position and fix coverage, and the eight figures. A physically different site or separately identified simulation with a containing and a missing region provides a geographic test. All DS30 physical recordings come from one roof. Schedule one low-priority Gauss benchmark at a time.
