## Work that could be shared

The measured Graph and orbit costs total 3.406 CPU s on DS30-0008 and 2.496 CPU s on DS30-0057. Graph lanes finalize separately by receiver, channel and RF. With the same unfiltered inputs and Graph profile, a trial can pass the seven nonheld lane results through to the ordinary solver and calculate the held lane later. The orbit archive bodies and causal current/previous selections depend on the cutoff. Parsed records and pre-observer states can be shared when the catalogue and time interval match. Historical stock preparation uses the offered screen center, while this trial used the actual offer center. Candidate eligibility and observer projections must respect that difference. The sealed study did not measure sharing across those two screens.

The center nomination clock includes geometry projection and the training track loop. Reusing position-independent orbit geometry is eligible where its output equals the same-center training slice. Each fold must compute the relative evidence for its own training-defined roster and exclude the held RF before fitting shared noise, receiver calibration and phase. Full-data shortlist entries, noise weights, phase states, receiver parameters and post-fit gates would leak held measurements. The held score itself is additional work. The trial did not time these fold-specific fits. With all measured Graph and orbit work optimistically shared, the observed binding-plus-nomination allowance is **2.032 CPU s** on DS30-0008 and **0.364 CPU s** on DS30-0057, or **2.395 CPU s** combined before nuisance training or held scoring. This arithmetic uses the measured study stages and grants shared setup and process startup for free. On DS30-0008 alone it already exceeds the measured **0.738 CPU s** repair cost.

A lower-cost follow-up can compare the same training-only shortlist against the existing native geographic projection and contiguous track scores at one center. Reuse only exactly matched pre-RF orbit states and the seven independent Graph lanes. The training score needs a bounded top-two result for each curved track, so it can avoid carrying the all-catalogue matrices into later stages. The 8-second research budget would leave **5.605 CPU s** after the projected 2.395 s above for the two sets of fixed-center nuisance fits and held likelihoods. These amounts define a ceiling to test after proving exact reuse. A deployable change needs a separate measurement under the smaller repair budget.
