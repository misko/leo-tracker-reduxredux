# Frozen causal policy after the exposure screen

Freeze this rule before opening the South receiver results. Public South is the held site for the policy. North, weak DS30-0016 and dense DS30-0008 were used only for the exposure screen. Compare the original unfiltered 12 ms packets at all four sites. Also describe the existing same-schedule 8 ms packets on North and DS30-0016. The 12 ms policy alone is eligible for the downstream solver test.

Use 128 consecutive issued hardware visit indexes per block, aligned to visit zero. For a radio in the current block, use only the previous two complete blocks of admitted original unfiltered opportunities, pooled over RF streams. For each past block, calculate the positive rates from the 20 ms anchors and from the short primaries. Let `A` be the larger of the two anchor rates and `S` the smaller of the two short rates. Preserve every current result unless both modes have exposure in both history blocks and `0 < A < S`. This conservatively requires persistent separation and a positive anchored rate. Initial blocks and gaps in history pass through.

When active, use the same two complete blocks to collect positive short-primary native verification margins for the current radio, actual RF, channel and pilot edge. With `n` available historical positive margins, set `k = ceil(n * A / S)`. Preserve the current primary when there is no applicable history or `k >= n`. Otherwise retain it exactly when its verified margin is at least the `k`th highest historical margin, including ties. This is an empirical past margin cutoff, with no absolute cutoff. All raw positives update future history regardless of their policy decision. All anchors, empty results, acquisition metadata and signed primary field values remain as measured. The first eligibility is after issued visit 255 and the computation uses no future rows, so it requires no extra wait for a current decision. Neither stock outputs nor coordinates enter the rule.

Apply once on the frozen saved packets, including the held South. Report all original positive factor identities and their decisions, anchor and short counts, the first eligible/active/removal visits and hardware time, and the actual processing clock. Use the same 1% limit for removals among South's v3-present opportunities on each radio, as a reference compatibility check with unknown signal truth.
