# Three-origin short negative screen protocol

Test whether a probe can certify sufficient absence of usable pilot support to skip the existing unchanged 8 ms native search. The experiment is a negative-screen assay over exactly two resident original first members. The [four-frame primary](four-frame-primary.md) already establishes that replacing the receiver with a complete 6.6444 ms detector loses signed factors and costs more through exit. The [single lag repeat score](repeat-negative.md) took the maximum magnitude of an independent 40,000-sample-lag pair. The new score uses a joint three-origin phase closure in the first 40,000 native samples, with relative controls. It does not run a pilot, FFT, history selector, temporal tracker or full short detector first.

For each antenna independently, examine twelve equally spaced positions over the nominal frame. At each position, take 64 samples at stride four from nominal offsets `0`, `13333`, `26667`. Use complex mean-removed pair covariances, the weakest of the three normalized squared coherence values, and multiply by the positive part of the cosine of the summed phase around the triangle. A three-origin repetition under arbitrary per-origin phase shifts should close. Compute the same statistic for controls with the second and third origins shifted by `(+61, -61)` and `(-61, +61)` samples. Subtract the larger control from the nominal value. Use the maximum nonnegative contrast across tiles. Both alternative controls suppress a broad class of tones coherent across all origins, and constant/invalid tiles return an indeterminate result routed to full 8 ms. This is a statistical test, with no mathematical guarantee against future or weak in-prefix signal. All four original samples per stereo time step remain authenticated.

Use original physical DS30-0008 visits 0–31 for the score threshold, at the lowest finite positive label from the two archived byte-matched 20/8 ms controls. If that set is empty the threshold is zero. A score must be strictly below the floor to omit an 8 ms lane. Keep ties, indeterminate inputs and all original 20 ms two-scan anchors. Freeze threshold policy, input authorities and statistic before opening any waveform. Score once on DS30-0008 visits 32–63 and on all DS30-0169 first-member visits 0–63. The second recording is a transfer for this statistic. Prior results on these members do not make them unseen recordings in the larger programme. There is no parameter tuning or alternative spectral search after seeing these outputs.

Read each signed member at most once per physical recording on the shared single low-priority CPU2 slot. Require the existing tmpfs path, full cache residency before and after, exact installed reader and codec, complete index and payload verification, per-window hashes, original accepted windows, account for all empty lane opportunities, and retain the original probe clocks and ordering from the signed 20/8 ms reference. The capture step stores only feature values, clocks, and identity; archival positive outcomes are overlaid in the separate scoring step. Report lost current positives, strong positives (`fractional_margin >= .10`), and lost current positives with a counterpart in the exact installed 20 ms output using two wrapped samples and 250 Hz. Any retained factor would still be produced by the unchanged 8 ms call. No fit on a lossy packet.

Require zero missed current 8 ms positives in both evaluation scopes before designing a receiver-integrated replay. Also require the estimated saving to cover the larger of the actual dense first-use inner-acquisition gaps to the 283.1 MB/s target. The assay measures all lane feature CPU and wall, including ctypes dispatch, inside a separately timed complete decode. The projection values each safely omitted lane at the mean 8 ms call cost from both archived orderings. Per-lane costs, storage savings and new through-exit speed are unmeasured, and the projection excludes integration overhead and downstream effects. Give a descriptive upper safe-empty count under a *single scalar threshold* chosen with hindsight over each scope, with no scoring from future visits to a runtime receiver. Stop after this assay if either gate fails. A small synthetic component test exercises a coherent pulse, a stationary tone, prefix isolation, and fail-closed decisions.
