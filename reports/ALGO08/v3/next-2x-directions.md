# Where to look for the next 2×

The objective is half the median paired and summed elapsed time from original signed I/Q through publication of the complete result on one core. The comparator is installed ALGO08v3. Protect fix coverage, both calibration arms, position accuracy, regional withholding and all required figures. The results below describe three small development cases, one of them physical. They do not qualify a version.

## Put acquisition and solving on one budget

The current dense short-receiver path processed 8.182 GB in 60.45 s, about 135 MB/s. Its numerical solver used 26.10 CPU s in a separate saved-detection run. The fastest observed acquisition prefix reached 231.52 MB/s for 192 visits. Applying the same effective rates and a scaled solver clock to the four already enrolled recordings gives the following design targets. The table keeps all historical input sizes, baseline clocks, publication charges and other modeled costs fixed. The proposed rates cover all acquisition work, including waveform processing. [Model and fixed roster](regional_dense_budget.py) · [Measured inputs](regional-dense-pair.md)

| Acquisition assumption | Dense solver assumption | Projected median / summed elapsed fraction | Interpretation |
|---|---:|---:|---|
| 135 MB/s, measured whole-file rate | 26.10 s | 0.833 / 0.808 | Current measured inputs |
| 231.52 MB/s, measured prefix | 26.10 s | 0.587 / 0.555 | Optimistic full-file throughput still misses |
| 225 MB/s sustained | 12.00 s | 0.495 / 0.484 | Solver-heavy design target |
| 250 MB/s sustained | 16.00 s | 0.488 / 0.471 | Joint design target |
| 275 MB/s sustained | 20.00 s | 0.488 / 0.464 | Acquisition-heavy design target |

The **250 MB/s plus 16 s** point would require about 1.85× the measured dense acquisition throughput and a 39% reduction in numerical solve CPU. Its modeled median leaves little margin for machine or recording variability. Actual paired launches decide both gates.

## Ten ranked directions

The ranking prioritizes opportunities that can change the joint budget or protect quality while doing so. The experiments in the third column are proposed falsification tests.

| Rank | Direction | First test and decision |
|---:|---|---|
| 1 | **Read, decompress, verify and feed samples in one streaming path.** Keep both required hashes, window order, rejection rules and original signed files. Stream into the detector's usable layout and avoid extra copies. | On one dense signed member, separately measure bytes copied, hash/decode CPU, native CPU and storage wait. Compare the whole verified path, alternating order at matched cache residency. Include a corrupted-window and bad-member-digest test. Budget against sustained 225–250 MB/s. Previous decoder swaps and direct I/O alone gave inconsistent whole-path results. |
| 2 | **Use short detections to propose tracks, then recover uncertain or geometrically valuable evidence.** Retain candidates rejected by the causal rule with their uncertainty. Let agreement on other times or RFs, and loss of independent position information, trigger exact checks on the measured signal. | On the saved northern and weak physical packets, mark which extra or removed observations actually influence the fix. First price a perfect retry selector, then test a causal selector without coordinates or stock labels. Charge verification or a second read. Stop if weak and northern quality cannot coexist within budget. |
| 3 | **Solve for position and nuisance parameters together with variable projection.** For each trial position and plausible assignment, eliminate the linear receiver and calibration parameters in the inner problem. Revisit ambiguous assignments and recompute original robust costs on original rows at the finalists. | Replay a single dense ambiguous track family and one weak family. Measure complete ranking, finishing and repair time. Compare exact objective, disagreement in assignments and final positions for both calibrations. A prior mixture initialization saved time but increased dense error by about 1 km. |
| 4 | **Allocate inference work by independent Doppler geometry.** Keep every signed measurement. For initial ranking, spend on time-separated slopes and independent satellite directions in the offered region, accounting for nuisance uncertainty. Restore all rows before accepting and rendering. | Compute an optimistic information-versus-work curve on saved tracks with balanced time, radio and RF coverage. Compare its weakest horizontal direction with the full fit at multiple trial positions. Previous fixed one-third designs and 16-point thinning lost information or triggered fallback. |
| 5 | **Screen joint association branches using conservative bounds on the actual ranking objective.** Bound complete competing assignments across alias, orbit and receiver uncertainties. Prune only when their best possible cost cannot overtake a retained branch. | Try one ambiguous dense branch and two mirrored simulated offers. Count whole expensive fits that disappear and time the certificates. Per-edge incompatibility alone could not safely delete candidates that still contribute to rankings. |
| 6 | **Batch exact full-grid scoring and share geometry across calibration arms.** Preserve every required map point and each arm's distinct robust weights, objective and fallback. Reuse common orbital and geometric terms across blocks of positions. | Benchmark one real grid tile in both arms. Compare all scores, failed cells, minima and rendered artifacts. The native grid exports cost 1.93 s in a previous profile. Any larger claimed saving must be demonstrated in the encompassing evidence stage. |
| 7 | **Overlap pending input reads with computation on the same core.** Issue bounded reads for upcoming signed members while the receiver processes the current one. Preserve source validation and charge all reads and waits through exit. | Compare identical work on a few adjacent members at matched cache residency, then reverse order. Record CPU and blocked elapsed separately. Previous read-ahead improved an intermediate timestamp without improving the entire run. Stop if the wait is merely shifted to publication. |
| 8 | **Share computation between regional admission and the final exterior check.** Cache only terms with identical dependencies, including epochs, receiver state, phase, calibration and offered region. Recompute terms changed by the fit. | The northern near-edge solve performed 25 early and 25 later probes. Compare their input keys and likelihood values, identify exact reusable work, and price it. Require identical outcomes on valid and omitted offers. The northern post-fit check took only 0.24 CPU s, so this is a secondary opportunity. |
| 9 | **Price complete publication separately on short recordings.** Reuse verified catalog parsing, prepared plotting data and renderer setup across all eight images within one job. Charge writing and decoding the final images. | Replay the identical sealed terminal into two fresh output roots. Alternate order, compare process CPU, blocked wall time, pixel hashes, and path/grid products. Publication varied by 16.75 s on the weak bounds pair while total CPU differed by 0.16 s. A wall-time swing alone cannot establish compute savings. |
| 10 | **For future scans, evaluate an independently authenticated, seekable capture format.** Align chunks with visits or antenna lanes and bind their hashes, order and scope into a versioned manifest. This could let a selective receiver avoid decoding bytes it never uses. | Build a throwaway sidecar for one saved member and compare bytes read, verification cost and storage overhead. Report one-time conversion separately and test corruption, reordering and omission. A changed input contract belongs to a prospective acquisition project. The existing benchmark still starts from the original recordings and charges any conversion. |

### Evidence informing the ranking

- On the northern simulation the unchanged frequency-history filter reduced fixed-zero error from 390 m to 128 m. On physical DS30-0016 it raised fixed-zero error from 1,311 m to 2,473 m while reducing RF RMS. This motivates testing which observations constrain position before imposing another hard selection rule. [Raw experiment and controlled interventions](north-causal-transfer.md)
- A 2.5 MS/s verifier retained most coherent pilot power, but the weakest symbol retained 88.5%. Even granting every native detector call for free misses both four-recording gates under the complete acquisition and current solver assumptions. [Receiver ceiling](multirate-verifier-envelope.md)
- The dense fit had no duplicate normalized design rows in the 3,661-observation, 82-group census. The original #1, #2 and #4 shortcuts together address an estimated 12.34 of the 54.55 CPU s needed on dense installed v3 even when their entire target operations are free. [Solver census](dense-row-repetition.md) · [Shortcut ceilings](bounds-combined.md)
- Existing input authentication covers every original decoded sample. Keep that invariant for current benchmarks. All 106 dense compressed members contain admitted visits. [Coverage audit](authenticated-input-coverage.md) · [Ingress profile](dense-ingress-attribution.md)
- Geographic separation matters independently of elapsed time. The current likelihood distinguished 52.5 km omissions but published on 100 m omissions on the tested northern and southern cases. Near-edge containing and omitted offers must both be scored. [Geographic uncertainty study](geographic-jackknife.md)

The first implementation experiment should pair **#1 with #2** on one saved member and the existing weak and northern packets. In parallel, price **#3** using a saved fit. Promote to four matched raw runs only after the component evidence supports a joint timing point and protects both calibration arms on the small development controls. Keep the one-core benchmark slot exclusive and the held-out locations sealed.
