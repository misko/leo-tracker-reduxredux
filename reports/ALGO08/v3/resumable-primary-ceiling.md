# What an exact 8→12 ms primary resume could save

On the 336 saved dense nonanchor lanes, the frozen confidence rule needs to recover **82.51–108.77 ms** of repeated work per batch to beat direct 12 ms acquisition at its measured dispatch cost. That is **453–598 µs per retried lane**. Resuming the original 12 ms calculation can preserve its retry outputs and still leaves the same **19 dense and five weak errors** on accepted 8 ms lanes. The answer-key oracle has a much smaller performance bar. It already won two of the three dense restart comparisons and would need **11.37 ms** of reusable native work in the third. ## Clock intervals and comparison scope

Let `A` be the sum of measured 8 ms native calls, `D` the direct 12 ms native clock, `L` the complete direct clock, and `P` the confidence-triggered clock including all restarts and dispatch. The oracle restart clock `O` is `A` plus the measured direct 12 ms costs of precisely the lanes which fail the published comparison tolerance. Clock differences below pair repeats in the original order. The archived **126.22 ms** dense anchor cost is common and cancels.

| Dense repeat | Frozen restart excess (P-L) | Required per 182 frozen retries | Minimum share of total early native (A) | Minimum share of actual retry native CPU | Oracle excess (O-D) |
|---|---:|---:|---:|---:|---:|
| 1 | 82.512 ms | 453.4 µs | 29.6% | 52.1% | −10.277 ms |
| 2 | 108.766 ms | 597.6 µs | 36.9% | 67.3% | +11.374 ms |
| 3 | 86.147 ms | 473.3 µs | 29.8% | 52.5% | −9.424 ms |

These are break-even savings with the measured first look and dispatch held fixed, before charging any work to save or restore state. Actual early CPU on the retried lanes can be anywhere from zero to `A` with these aggregate clocks. Actual native retry CPU is bounded above by `P − A`, which also contains dispatch. The corresponding minimum shares are `(P − L) / A` and `(P − L) / (P − A)`. Assigning the population mean early CPU to the 182 retries instead gives **54.7%, 68.2%, 55.0%** of early CPU on retried lanes. This last calculation assumes equal mean early cost for accepted and retried inputs. The receipts retain lane outputs and batch timings. Even granting free extensions and dispatch, retaining every first look caps the savings against direct 12 ms at `L − A`, or **52.86–77.80 ms** across the dense repeats. The oracle's analogous native-only ceiling `D − A` is **51.39–76.18 ms**.

The ideal oracle retries 59 dense lanes. In repeat two it needs **192.8 µs per retry**, or **18.1%** of the measured 62.760 ms of direct 12 ms work on its actual retry set. The other two repeats need no overlap to win. On the 36 weak lanes both restart schedules win all three comparisons at their respective scopes. The oracle retries five weak lanes. The frozen rule retries two different ones and misses all five disagreements. Adding the known misses to the frozen schedule would require **201 dense and seven weak** continued calls. The new lanes' exact cost is unmeasured. The oracle's criterion allows the same rank within two wrapped samples and 250 Hz or matching empty outputs. ## State the exact 12 ms calculation can carry forward

| Stage | Reusable before the longer output is known | Work the longer aperture requires |
|---|---|---|
| Lane conversion and stride-four FIR | First 80,000 native samples with the same mixer center. Of 20,000 old decimated outputs, the first **19,990** keep the same 81-tap support and tile alignment. | Convert the additional 40,000 samples, compute the new FIR outputs and repair the last ten 8 ms FIR edge outputs. |
| Frame statistics | Preserve the first **five normalized planes** and all **15** unique entries of the original pre-Jacobi covariance. Its last old plane ends far enough before 80,000 that the FIR edge change does not affect it. | Add three planes and **21** covariance entries. Run the original eight-dimensional Jacobi solve and construct both extended frame projections. Saving only the diagonalized five-frame covariance loses the required state. |
| Timing and carrier scout | Persistent filters and FFT plans remain shared within the existing receiver context. | Both projected modes can change. The frozen computation provides no completed lag or carrier FFT result that is generally valid across apertures. The 8 lag forward/inverse pairs and the 12-by-2 carrier FFTs use the new projections and timing picks. |
| Full-rate refinement | If the exact coarse CFO, wrapped sample ranges and arithmetic path recur, retain up to the first **five of eight** frame contributions to each nine-offset block matrix. | The new scout picks can be different. Extend matching block matrices and recompute parity scores, frequency correction and ordering. Matching final outputs at tolerance does not demonstrate exact coarse-request reuse. |
| Scout confirmation and fractional verifier | Per-frame products and transforms may survive when the same refined CFO, epoch, pilot and sampling geometry recur and the earlier gate actually evaluated those frames. | The early scout gate changes from two frame groups to four. Longer aggregate spectra and the recalculated fractional vertex and gate may need different operands. Neither the old gate result nor its fractional margin can be carried as the 12 ms result. |

These are operation counts and state sizes. The per-lane CPU they could recover remains unmeasured. Reusing all five old normalized planes still requires both extended projections and their FFTs. An exact resume alone cannot repair the confidence rule's accepted-lane errors. The oracle comparison leaves room for a different routing decision.
