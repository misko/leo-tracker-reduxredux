## Conditional grouping and likelihood limits

The replay uses the published 20/8 candidate packets and pinned release Graph sources. Original track and assigned-point counts reproduce on every recording. The development case and first two held cases also reproduce the earlier candidate-only Graph pair lists exactly. Each eligible middle short observation uses its immediate neighbors in the original unambiguous piece. Both hardware gaps must be at most one second, with product at most 0.25 seconds squared. The lifted secant is limited to 8 kHz/s, and the signed interpolation may differ from the original middle CFO by at most 250 Hz. The half blend changes only a diagnostic CFO value. It keeps the original Graph alias lift and the middle's epoch, margin, measured status, and identity. The original middle also participated in choosing the Graph piece and in the innovation guard. This is a conditional measurement comparison. [Earlier Graph dependence and pair-moment assay](signed-cfo-time.md) · [Causal anchor innovation](anchor-cfo-innovation.md) · [Owner-group interpolation](owner-path-correction.md).

The full 20 ms selected reference shares the short detector's first 8 ms. Errors to that reference cannot distinguish short-measurement noise from full-measurement noise or from their covariance. Even with independent original measurement errors, neighboring blended rows reuse the same observations and acquire off-diagonal covariance. The interpolation uses the original hardware counters. The short and full apertures can have different effective measurement epochs, and curvature can bias a linear interpolation even when the clock joins exactly. [Earlier effective-epoch analysis](sparse8-cfo-epoch.md).

For fixed choices, the half blend is an invertible linear map on each piece. An eligible row places half its weight on itself and positive weights summing to half on its two neighbors. A nonzero vector in the null space would force equal maximum magnitudes with alternating signs all the way to an unchanged endpoint, a contradiction. Transforming predictions and observations together and using the transformed covariance (A\Sigma A^T) preserves the fixed-model information. A fit that uses blended CFOs as independent observations changes the error model. Neither the reference agreement nor the fixed-model identity estimates a geographic gain.
