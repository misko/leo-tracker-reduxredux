# Sparse signed cancellation and the budget for a cheaper first 20 ms scout

The saved signed residual audit found that **at least 96.16%** of complex samples changed after rounding and clipping. At most **0.456%** of quarter-rate FIR stencils were unchanged, and every covariance row saw changed input. A separate all-20 ms installed callback comparison on six DS30-0049 members leaves just **42.3 µs per issued lane** for all first-pass work under the first-30 half-time model, even after giving the entire primary-only callback away. The existing safe shortlist and exact directed-verifier trials give no route inside that allowance. This is a source and receipt comparison. No waveform was opened and no benchmark lease was used. [Recomputed counts and clocks](measurements/full20-residual-delta-roof.json).

## The residual search rebuilds its own modes and score surfaces

The installed all-20 ms `2258f592…` and the archived relink have identical SHA-256. The relink uses the verified packed `pilot_cancel.o` and timing `5ffc8eb3…` objects. `measure` confirms the primary scout, then invokes the resident signed cancellation at zero mixer center. Shifted centers use the original stereo scratch path. In both cases cancellation fits a complex coefficient separately in each pilot block and writes an actual rounded and clipped signed CI16 residual. The second `detect_lane` decimates that residual, normalizes all 14 centered 4,740-sample frame rows, forms their covariance, obtains two new modes by Jacobi rotations, and runs the same lag, carrier, local refinement and independent pilot checks. The final verifier already loads original I/Q once and applies the two separate proposed timing and CFO interpretations there. [Installed call graph and symbols](receiver-activation-audit.md), [Packed canceller](../single-core-2x/receiver-packed-cancellation.md).

Write the signed residual as \(y=x+\Delta\) where \(\Delta\) is formed from the actual quantized samples. A real linear FIR allows \(D(y)=D(x)+D(\Delta)\). To reproduce the installed floating-point evaluation exactly, dense changed stencils still need their original accumulation order. A covariance update would need new means and scales and the two mixed cross terms as well as the correction covariance. The independently computed eigenvectors affect both complete modes. The downstream lag statistic multiplies each mode at displaced samples, adds mode products, and normalizes by local energy. Both carrier spectra are formed on the resulting modes at newly chosen epochs. Original bank bins supply neither the residual modes nor a safe upper bound on their winning score. [Full timing and carrier geometry](selective-carrier-transform.md).

The changed-support census comes from the earlier `950c2b…` detector, whose signed subtraction has the same support geometry. It covers 92 paid residual scans, including 38 exported rank-one factors. Ninety-one had exactly 200 fully reusable FIR stencils out of 50,000. One had 228. None of 1,288 rows was unchanged. The published donor-based budget for the clean inputs and FIR is **0.27%** of whole native CPU even after crediting the best observed reuse fraction to both scans. The direct residual-spectrum prototype used the signed quantized correction with an independent linear projection bank. When driven by exact first-pass cancellation, it recovered **2/36** residual seeds. The guarded predecessor needed the original search on **68/68** lanes and took **1.45×** original native CPU. These are different archived kernels and measured replay scopes, and the install audit only transferred the original operators and shape. [Signed support and clocks](../single-core-2x/receiver-cancel-support.md), [Measured linear delta and fallback](../single-core-2x/receiver-projection.md).

An independent current full-only DS30-0169 trace had 60 residual reaches among 102 lanes. Transferring the earlier matched packed stage cost of 329 µs per reached cancellation to that roster gives a **19.74 ms** complete cancellation-stage elimination allowance, or **6.77%** of the shipped 291.50 ms. This deliberately removes synthesis, every gain dot product, and subtraction and clipping. SIMD grouping across repeated disjoint block IDs would save part of the dot product alone. There is insufficient stage budget to justify a new timed implementation for the broad target. The signed DS30-0049 factorial evaluates the actual installed two-scan ABI and a primary-only helper using the same installed timing and verifier. The helper reproduces each emitted rank-zero factor. The archived 20 ms/continuation diagonal matches the original prefix. These sums use both measured native alternatives on every admitted lane, including the 92 originally scheduled anchors. Grant the entire measured primary helper free, including its original confirmation and fractional verification. The remaining paired differential is **538.12 µs per issued lane** before the new first-stage proposal, original confirmation, original first-factor scoring and every uncertainty retry. The conditional first-30 model allows **580.38 µs per issued lane** when the prior charted outside-native clocks are held fixed and saved CPU transfers one for one to wall. The entire allowance for that new work is therefore **42.25 µs per lane**. Giving away every native empty call while keeping all positive work leaves **1,266.32 µs/lane**. Giving away both all empties and the entire primary helper on positives still leaves **523.08 µs/lane**. The remaining 57.30 µs per issued lane would allow at most 173.58 µs per positive if the empty gate were free. This first-30 model is limited by the sum gate. It transfers the 0049 six-member lane mix. Whole-field residual and primary times remain unmeasured. The earlier directed replay provides an independent check. With the exact original timing and signed CFO for both exported ranks, advance knowledge of the empty lanes and no residual cancellation where the oracle already knew it was unnecessary, the original-score path still cost **845.75 µs per issued dense lane** and **1,670.16 µs per weak lane**. It reproduced all 138 ordered factors. The dense cost includes 605.29 µs per issued lane inside the original-sample verifier alone. These are favorable measured interfaces with the broad scouts removed. A new verifier or a genuinely shared scout and verifier could move that scope. A first-pass shortlist with the current residual and verification sequence has no comparable measured room. [Byte-exact directed oracle](directed-verifier.md).

Missing evidence is the obstacle to accepting a cheap shortlist. For a partial linear template projection, the unmeasured complement can contribute up to the product of its input and template norms. A zero projected result cannot exclude a high full score when most of the template remains in that complement. The two measured half-band sketches retained just 1.78–3.71% of pilot power. This observation concerns the template projection. The released GLRT compares exact and rolled normalized spectra with different score units, so a template-energy percentage cannot serve as a rejection cutoff. A different representation would need measured broad empty and positive selectivity, a numerical certificate for unresolved original candidates, and a complete cost on the current ABI. No new pilot threshold, detector policy or native implementation follows from this audit.
