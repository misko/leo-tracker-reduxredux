# Held-epoch calibration of continuation as variance information

Use the original unfiltered short DS30-0016 and public northern containing detector packets. Read no stock factors, stock owners, site reference or TLE while calibrating. A candidate has directional support if at least one of the frozen chronological and reverse continuation passes. Treat a measured factor with neither pass as an uncertainty candidate. Classify neither as a new measurement type or a deletion. Preserve the exact detector factors and full released graph in the unfiltered estimator. Analyze 12 ms opportunities separately from already full slots to avoid calling a cadence or aperture effect directional uncertainty.

Build two independent donor graphs from the original short packet's whole stereo visits. Alternating contiguous four-second hardware-counter intervals are the held epoch groups. Each donor graph uses only the other parity's observations and the installed graph configuration. For each held opportunity, find independently reconstructed same-receiver, channel and exact RF tracks with three donors on each side within eight seconds. Use the nearest three on each side and require the closest on either side within three seconds. Fit one centered quadratic to those six donor values. Require finite full rank, donor RMS ≤450 Hz, and a local slope within ±8,000 Hz/s. Skip an opportunity if more than one track qualifies. The held CFO, timing phase, margin and directional mark must not choose the donor track or polynomial. Evaluate the held signed CFO after lifting into the donor's 227.27 kHz alias branch, then divide the innovation by `sqrt(1+h)` using the held prediction leverage `h`. Use the donor graph's existing margin-law sigma at the held measurement's margin, or the existing constant sigma if its BIC test declines heteroscedasticity. This produces a cross-epoch innovation proxy rather than a directly observed measurement variance. It excludes out-of-bracket and ambiguous cases and can include shared curve misspecification or weak association.

For a positive increment to be identified, require each recording to supply at least 12 uniquely bracketed unsupported short factors on at least four distinct held visits and two exact receiver/RF lanes, and at least 24 supported short factors. Estimate the common positive variance ratio from the ratio of median squared normalized innovations in the unsupported and supported subsets. A recording's ratio must exceed one and improve the held Gaussian log score for unsupported observations when applied to the independent other recording. For score calibration multiply the held normalized variance by the baseline scale from that recording's supported rows, estimated by dividing their median squared innovation by the standard normal squared median 0.4549364231. Report all missing configurations and use no numeric weight if either recording lacks support or the effects disagree. No precision candidate is fitted or tested on the dense recordings at that point.
