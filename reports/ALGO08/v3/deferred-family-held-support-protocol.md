# Held evidence for a deferred same-radio family

Take the target family and its two selected training intervals from
the previously sealed, owner-blind deferred nomination. The family
identity is a nomination input, never an activation rule. Reproduce
the exact ordered active observations in the original mixed detector
packet and the custodian's second-screen tape. Use no survey or
installed outcome in holdout selection.

For the held-opportunity census use the released Graph `max_gap_s`
as the guard on either side of each training interval, plus the
signed maximum hardware aperture when comparing acquisition starts.
Reject every training observation ID by explicit set membership.
Use all guard-separated RX2 observations as the first diagnostic
cohort, across the original recorded lanes. Also enumerate later or
earlier observations on the original training RX1 lanes as a temporal
cohort. Retain each maximal contiguous segment of an original Graph
track, splitting at the embargo and the released Graph gap. A
potential isolated curve uses the released contiguous support minimum
of 12 points and original Graph minimum span. These are opportunity
preconditions. Visibility, orbit identity and noise gates remain
separate. Count the signed opportunities with zero accepted detection,
short Graph segments and detections without a Graph curve.

Compare held segments against the original per-track selected cold
signatures, salvage candidate-index shortlists and retained alternatives,
and signed predictive labels on the same frozen packet. An interval
overlap only locates a candidate already visible in those receipts.
The preselection itself observed the complete recording and may not
certify independent family support. Absence of an archived assignment
or shortlist appearance does not establish physical invisibility.

A genuine numeric check requires previously authenticated causal
family and rival predictions, visibility and uncertainties on the
held observation epochs under the same offered region and catalogue.
Use raw measured CFO values and explicit no-detection opportunities.
Use frozen receiver and orbital nuisance state trained on other,
nonheld observations in each calibration arm. Family identity, aliases
and rival set must be frozen without using held scores. The original
full-packet cold, Graph, or position map may have used held values for
candidate selection, position, family weights, shared radio drift,
calibration, orbital phase, or predictive support. Audit those
dependencies by source and observed owner inclusion. Passing a time
embargo cannot remove this leakage. Without a source-bound training-only
state and predictions, retain a no-evidence result and report exact
opportunity counts instead of scoring a surrogate.

Read only existing signed detector evidence, compact receipts and
source. If necessary rebuild the deterministic observation-to-Graph
roster in memory and verify masks against the already sealed tape.
Do not collect RF, read IQ or ephemeris, warm a cache, launch a worker
or solver, or repeat the isolated family fit. Seal the test inputs and
scope before inspecting held family or outcome values.
