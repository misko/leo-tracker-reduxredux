Construct its probabilities sequentially before every outcome. Fit normalized multinomial corrections with those log probabilities as fixed offsets.

O adds signed nominal contrast to receiver imbalance with a nonnegative coefficient. Common category coding is [0,1,1,2], imbalance coding [0,-1,1,0]. Geometry is averaged using original nominee prior mass and visibility; omitted mass contributes zero, with no renormalization. This is a posterior-mean-feature response experiment, not a nominee identity update.

Require positive equal-record O−U, O−P and O−each-control, each positive above 1e-6 in at least four of six records. Also report P/U/O versus uncorrected causal baseline.
