# DS8 joint 40-scan fit: reduced disagreement, residual local alternatives

The highest qualified training-score fit is **416.763 m from the supplied roof reference**. The other start finishes 534.335 m away, at a location 273.484 m from the selected endpoint. Pooling the two conflicting 24-scan selections reduces their original 2,078.112 m separation, but does not produce identical endpoints or establish reliable sub-km accuracy.

## Experiment and selection

The [protocol](PROTOCOL.md) freezes the union of 40 unique recordings: eight shared, sixteen original-only, sixteen rank-only. Both starts use exactly the same observations, masks, candidate banks and zero-height q020 likelihood. Each starts at one prior N24 location with independently fitted training timings for all forty scans. Shared E/N and all forty timings then vary. Selection uses the highest qualified training score, without reference-distance or held-score selection.

| Initial location | Scans | Final distance to reference | Training log score | Held log score | Runtime |
|---|---:|---:|---:|---:|---:|
| Original N24, selected | 40 | 416.763 m | −423358.682 | −279809.431 | 43.80 s |
| Rank-selected N24 | 40 | 534.335 m | −423362.044 | −279822.028 | 40.96 s |

Both starts qualify, with no boundary solution. Maximum projected gradient is 0.001630 and maximum finite-difference gradient discrepancy is 0.00002595. Both finish within their 180-second caps; neither required a retry. Two qualified endpoints are evidence of residual initialization sensitivity, not proof that either is globally optimal.

For context, the original block-selected N24 fit was 389.826 m from the reference; the chronological-rank N24 fit was 1876.825 m away. N40 uses 67% more scans than N24. Its result cannot be attributed to an improved estimator at an equal data budget, and is slightly farther from the reference than the original N24 result.

## Matched observation comparison

The controls hold each prior N24 location fixed and fit timings on all forty scans using training observations. Positive values below mean the selected N40 fit improves log score on exactly the same observations. Held samples do not select fit parameters, but come from these same recordings; they are not independent geographic validation.

| Group | Scans | Training Δ vs original location | Held Δ vs original location | Training Δ vs rank location | Held Δ vs rank location |
|---|---:|---:|---:|---:|---:|
| Shared | 8 | +43.604 | +13.299 | −44.028 | +25.200 |
| Original-only | 16 | −85.996 | −82.904 | +442.490 | +325.393 |
| Rank-only | 16 | +97.759 | +86.733 | −136.833 | −158.837 |
| Union | 40 | +55.366 | +17.128 | +261.629 | +191.756 |

The joint fit improves aggregate training and held scores over both fixed-location controls. Nevertheless, each exclusive group loses held score relative to its own original location. This is consistent with a compromise between conflicting recording groups; it does not identify the physical cause of that conflict.

## Consequence

More pooled observations can reduce a particular selection failure, but scan count alone is not an accuracy guarantee. The related [rank-selection control](../2026_09_29_rank24_control/README.md) and [DS10 evaluation](../2026_09_29_ds10_geographic/README.md) remain adverse evidence for general transfer. Do not choose scan subsets or receiver height using reference distance.

The next useful diagnostic is to identify which scan timing/association alternatives separate these two N40 endpoints, then test any correction on held recording groups with a fixed selection rule. This experiment alone does not justify production promotion.

The reference is `[37.849056280893684, -122.48575489722863]`; its uncertainty is unsupplied. Distances use a spherical radius of 6,371,008.8 m. The model still assumes zero receiver height and uses site-conditioned candidate banks. Reference distance is descriptive, not a calibrated error bound.
