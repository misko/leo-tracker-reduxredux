# Prior controls that the new DS24 tests must retain

These are existing DS23 development results, not new DS24 validation. Several older prior qualifications used chronological pre-RF TLE splits; they must not be described as randomized group validation. No earlier favorable row is a deployable per-scan choice.

- **Do not add a fast receiver basis just because residuals correlate.** The first reciprocal receiver-prediction probes (217/257/273/274) rejected fast and half-shared alternatives: on 217, fast freedom improved omitted RX1 score by 7.27 but worsened RX2 by 1.65 and moved c0 error 1.025→1.509 km. Source: `reports/2026_10_19_ds23_clock_measurement_controls/README.md`. The current raw-CFO, whole-family contrast witness is distinct because it holds every owner of the test family together and adds wrong-receiver/time-shift predictors; retain those controls.
- **Even positive conditional prediction does not establish position improvement.** The later fixed 100 Hz fast-RX1 model qualified on 12/20 original scans. Its prediction-qualified policy changed fitted-c mean/median 1.266/0.896→1.242/0.974 km; median worsened. The precise dense-202 result was a different observation/noise regime and cannot be substituted into that cohort. Source: `reports/2026_10_19_ds23_dense_receiver_prediction/README.md`. The new 50 Hz experiment therefore still needs its complete 24-case position denominator and paired fallback.
- **Do not reinterpret a better radio covariance score as a better position model.** Radio-qualified temporal covariance changed fitted-c mean/median 1.1774/0.8111→1.2444/0.8505 km on 20 scans; additionally learning a radio-only REML scale gave 1.2485/0.8505. Sources: `reports/2026_10_19_algo03_temporal_noise/local_evaluation.json` and `reports/2026_10_19_algo03_temporal_noise_scaled/local_evaluation.json`. The new RX-specific raw-graph noise law tests a different pooling assumption; it should retain the existing pointwise floor/cap and robust Gamma model, change curved and predictive noise consistently, and report RF versus position effects separately.
- **Do not replace robust fragment noise with arbitrary pointwise freedom.** In the seven-case same-marginal-prior study, point-level noise passed only 4/7 paired trials and block-of-four noise 3/7. With exact fallback, fitted-c mean changed 1.1677→1.2438 and 1.2232 km; the declared RF-selected policy retained the incumbent in all seven. Source: `reports/2026_10_21_ds23_observation_noise/independent_replay_audit.json`. RX pooling is a distinct prefit noise-law test, not permission to learn one precision per point from location residuals.
- **Do not repeat an arbitrary phase/orbit-width sweep.** Earlier-TLE qualification admitted the more flexible phase variance law on 18/20 cases, yet the selected policy worsened fitted-c mean/median 1.266/0.896→1.380/1.038 km. Generic phase×2, orbit×3, 1000 Hz fragment, drift and smooth-width controls on 0268 did not demonstrate a 0.5 km solution. Sources: `reports/2026_10_18_ds23_prior_refinements/README.md` and `reports/2026_10_20_dbg01_algo02_ds23_0268/README.md`. A causal joint correlation with unchanged marginal scales is distinct, but requires complete normalized prior accounting, covariance/coordinate parity tests, causal history qualification and the same retained RF data. TLE innovations are prediction proxies, not reference ephemeris truth.
- **Do not score a covariance or prior by residual quadratic alone.** Shared cross-RF family constants improved normalized RF evidence by about 71.95 in the dense-202 identity experiment while worsening position. Log volume, prior/Gamma and prediction terms can move the optimum. Source: `reports/DS23_POSITION_PROGRESS.md`, dense-measurement/shared-constant checkpoint. The new controls preserve normalized objectives for diagnosis while adoption comes from the frozen RF witness plus original paired gates, not an in-sample score contest across changed likelihoods.

The common rule is to preserve original measurement IDs, labels, aliases, masks and likelihood powers; match both c arms and cold 5×48 budgets; keep every failed trial and exact baseline fallback; and delay geographic evaluation until model qualification is frozen. No new regional multistarts are justified by these controls.
