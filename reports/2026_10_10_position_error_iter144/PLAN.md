# Causal catalogue sensitivity preparation

This diagnostic uses the same 12 clean iteration 135 members and their saved **fresh timestamp** endpoints. Both c arms share that member's fitted-selected bank and original measurements, as in 135. It estimates no orbital prior or covariance, changes no fitted position, and selects no model by position error. Iteration 140 remains the primary position experiment. No catalogue queries, propagation, model calls, numerical freezing or recording jobs occur in this preparation.

## Fixed earlier catalogue rule

Keep the original pinned snapshot and its provider. Enumerate earlier collection times within 24 hours of its collection time, newest first with digest tie-break. Inspect at most 10 distinct earlier payload digests, skipping repeats of the original digest. Select the first shared snapshot having any changed **common original-bank NORAD ID**, comparing exact validated element-line pairs; satellite names do not qualify. One snapshot applies to both arms and all satellite IDs. Never assemble per-satellite catalogues from whichever update appears promising.

Missing-only and malformed-only snapshots do not qualify as changed. Record every inspected snapshot, including unavailable payloads, unchanged IDs, changed IDs, missing IDs and malformed IDs. Unavailable reads and parse failures count toward the fixed inspection cap. The future public reader must fetch only these same capped 10 distinct payloads, one at a time before selection; the pure prototype's supplied records dictionary does not authorize unbounded prefetch. If none qualifies, retain an explicit no-changed-source outcome. This rule does not imply that an older source is worse or that update differences are independent orbit errors.

## Prediction-space decomposition

Use iteration 111 adapter.construct at each saved timestamp endpoint to obtain original soft responsibilities, weights R/sigma², physical spatial derivatives and every available nuisance derivative with c0 RF locks. For valid common candidates, compute delta frequency in Hz. Report total weighted energy, its nuisance-span component, additional spatial-span component after nuisance removal, and remaining component outside both spans. projection.py is a dense synthetic reference implementation with normalized columns and explicit original-row-count rank tolerance, without an N-by-N matrix or coefficient fitting. Missing candidates must not shrink the tolerance's row authority. It is not a memory-bounded real-data runner: a streamed thin-QR implementation and resource guard must first match this reference before recording use.

Preserve every original ID's outcome and original responsibility mass omitted by missing/malformed/propagation-failed candidates. Partial-bank quantities describe only valid common rows; they cannot establish full-bank energy or full-member comparability. Report all 12 member statuses and both arms. Local free-span absorption ignores actual priors and bounds and is not an achievable fitted correction or localization covariance.

The future runner requires a reviewed source/input closure, deterministic snapshot receipts, bounded propagation/resource policy, independent original-objective admission, full failure coverage and tests for unchanged-source parity before any recording access. Current pure tests cover causal selection, caps, missing/malformed accounting, orthogonal/confounded span decomposition, unit rescaling and zero weights. No numerical protocol is frozen.

## Executable preparation limits

The prepared runner uses one worker and admits exactly two saved endpoints, one per arm. Clean132 reconstruction separately performs one bank reduction and one control objective evaluation to derive satellite centres; these are inherited construction work, not endpoint admissions. Construction failures retain an incomplete flag rather than inventing completed call counts. Alternative work is one public catalogue propagation and two ordinary geometry predictions per arm. No optimizer runs.

A conservative 2 GiB estimated workspace guard precedes conditional-design allocation. It accounts for simultaneous admitted arms, adapter Jacobian copies, retained nuisance designs and bounded QR workspace. The adapter still allocates a dense observation-by-satellite-by-parameter Jacobian; streaming bounds subsequent projection workspace only. Full Jacobians are released after copying spatial columns, and arm designs are released after projection.

Public archive payloads are ASCII strings. Whole-payload parse failures and duplicate NORAD records fail closed; public propagation filters are reported without claiming that every rejected object had an SGP4 error. Snapshot read/parse failures consume the fixed ten-payload budget. Missing or filtered candidates retain explicit omitted responsibility mass. Event-normalizer effects are reported only when every original candidate survives; partial-bank normalizers are unavailable. Partial admissions and completed arm diagnostics survive later failures. Raw 135 result hashes are preparation provenance, separate from inference-bound endpoint projections.
