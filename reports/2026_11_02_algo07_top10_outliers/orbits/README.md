Every case has two influential-family targets and its longest saved unassociated source. Additional 0014/57898 and 0209/66482 controls are explicitly posthoc, motivated by the completed omission ablations.

Each causal and first-post-epoch bank contains approximately 11,100 satellites. Every propagation-valid candidate received the same coarse nomination budget; the matched c arms share the top-three finalists per nuisance model plus the incumbent for exact native-point fitting. This is a complete eligible-catalogue coarse search with finalist exact fits, not an exact fit of every satellite.

The diagnostic orbit model replaces the full production satellite nuisance block with phase plus constant/rate. Its RMS cannot be read as a published baseline residual or gate failure. The full-versus-restricted figures and family CSVs show this distinction explicitly. Whole-family comparisons test conditional consistency of the single-track shortlist; they do not establish unique identity. Frozen receiver calibration remains fixed, and per-track constants are diagnostic lower bounds.
