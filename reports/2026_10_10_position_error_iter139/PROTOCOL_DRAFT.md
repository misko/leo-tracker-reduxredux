# Conditional means versus residual products

Preparation only: no recording evaluation, freeze or parameter selection is
authorized by this draft. Reuse all twelve consumed members and immutable
iteration 138 pair/support receipts. Bind their protocol, terminal receipts,
claims and exact source closure before execution. Preserve every failed member
and every zero-mass group, with no replacement or quality filter.

Reconstruct the unchanged original B7 model through clean 131/132 ports. At each
original saved fitted-c and c=0 endpoint evaluate once, verify its saved objective
within 1e-6 and reproduce iteration 138 pair score and shared-label mass before
computing this diagnostic. At most two endpoint calls per member; no optimizer,
IQ, reference coordinates, position errors or new acquisition. Pair identity,
order, acquisition groups and alternating block are copied exactly from 138,
never selected from residuals or assignments. Both arms use identical pairs.

For a pair (i,j) and satellite k, use soft weight w=R_ik R_jk and standardized
residuals x=r_ik/125, y=r_jk/125. Within each frozen RX/channel/RF/edge group,
satellite and alternating block, retain pair count, total fractional mass,
weighted sums of x, y and xy, and their weighted means. All candidate satellites
remain represented; zero mass produces unavailable means, never invented zeros.
Summing weighted xy across all records must reproduce the ordinary audit score.

Predict each target block using the other block's weighted mean x and mean y.
Report that mean product and the target weighted sum of
`(x - mean_x_other) * (y - mean_y_other)`. Aggregate only where both training and
target mass are positive, and report excluded fractional mass explicitly. Also
report the raw product on that exact same available subset. Do not compare a
subset-centered total to a full-coverage raw total without disclosure. No minimum
mass threshold, satellite winner, shrinkage setting or rho is chosen.

Persistent conditional means can explain positive products without stochastic
correlation. Conversely, an unexplained product does not establish hardware
covariance: nuisance parameters and responsibilities came from the entire
recording, and the mean predictions are not independent held-out predictions.
Alternating blocks prevent direct target-block mean reuse but do not remove
shared-fit dependence or selection by detection. Treat the outputs as a
descriptive decomposition, with no significance, uncertainty or accuracy claim.

The next decision remains separate: consistent unexplained dependence could
justify a synthetic normalized correlated-mixture kernel. This diagnostic alone
cannot choose a correlation parameter or authorize a positioning experiment.
