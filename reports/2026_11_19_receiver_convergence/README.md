# Receiver changes measured from GLRT to position

Open the gallery for one position-error chart per tested change and cohort. Each roof chart pairs the candidate with all four original v5 options on the same recording IDs. The upper curve counts every recording offered to the method. The dots and median diamond in the lower plot describe the positions it issued. A separate diagnostic row shows forced estimates when the receiver saved them. The calibration switch displays fitted `c` or `c = 0` at the same observation and search budget. ## Where the errors change

| Change | Input and comparison | Fitted-calibration result |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Secant position fitter, five orbital phase fits | Six same-core pairs from complete native GLRT through compact v5 | All six returned in both calibration settings. Median fitted error was 0.831 km versus 0.832 km for stock. The largest coordinate shift in either arm was 19.63 m. The median paired whole-worker speedup was 1.52×. [Scores, runtime and a nonlinear example where the solver stalls](position-fit/README.md) |
| Same secant fitter on eight independent recordings | Four DS26 and four DS29 recordings selected from the fixed confirmation list | All eight returned. Fitted median/P95 were 0.528/0.922 km versus 0.527/0.923 km for no-repair v5. All eight were within 5% of v5 or better in both calibration settings. The maximum fitted position shift was 18.7 m. [Independent predictions and comparison](secant-confirmation/README.md) |
| Two early orbital phase fits in place of five | Complete recorded GLRT through compact v5, six timed pairs and 21 independently selected roof recordings | All 21 confirmation fixes returned. Median error rose from 0.758 to 0.956 km and P95 from 2.486 to 2.691 km. Only 10/21 individual errors fell within 10% of v5 or better, although median conditional Doppler RMS changed by less than 1 Hz. [Independent confirmation](phase-confirmation/README.md) · [Timing and selection of the initial branch](phase-budget/README.md) |
| Secant position fit plus two early orbit fits | Six complete detector streams through compact v5, with both single changes also measured | All six returned. Median fitted error rose from 0.747 to 0.971 km on DS26 and from 0.917 to 1.042 km on DS29. Median stock-to-combined CPU ratio was 1.68× across separately pinned runs. [Scores and timing boundaries](combined-fit/README.md) |
| Reuse the likelihood at an identical fitted position | Four complete native-GLRT recordings through the compact cavity fit | All four positions, conditional RF errors and decisions matched stock in both calibration settings. Reuse removed 584 of 844 endpoint solves and saved 0.09–0.74 CPU seconds per recording. Whole-worker CPU rose on two recordings and fell on two. [Paired runs and counters](likelihood/README.md) |
| Approximate the GLRT frequency bank and verify the winner | First implementation, 24 scheduled IQ windows across two recordings | All emitted detector results matched the installed producer. Full detector CPU rose 7.2% on holdout plus extension. The chart labels downstream position as unmeasured for this implementation. [Initial detector qualification](conditioning/whole-retry/qualification.json) |
| Refine the detector fallback to distinguish observable winners | Revised implementation, 32 scheduled IQ windows from the same two recordings, plus eight synthetic controls | All emitted results matched. Full detector CPU fell 14.7% on holdout plus extension and 2.9% / 13.9% on new blind DS26 / DS29 visits. Through v5, both positions matched in both calibration settings. Those fits used 16 and 12 fresh valid windows, respectively, with the remaining observations from the archives. [Raw-IQ samples and position receipts](conditioning/README.md) |
| Exact geometry in the lean track fit | Fourteen roof recordings, common tracked points and causal orbit predictions | On six timing recordings the median fell from 2.136 to 1.427 km with 5/6 fixes. On eight other DS26 recordings it fell from 1.377 to 1.066 km with 3/8 fixes. Original v5 returned 14/14. [Matched observations and assignments](track-fit/README.md) |
| Exact geometry after the ordinary detector | Six complete sets of stored IQ-derived factors, with fresh tracking and fitting | Four fixes from each receiver. The median fell from 1.666 to 0.996 km, while P95 rose from 2.345 to 2.523 km. Two recording errors improved and two worsened. Two of the six upstream factor sets also have complete raw-IQ replay receipts. [Recording-level comparison](raw-orbit-fit/README.md) |
| Expensive reference detection every 64 sweeps | Six full detector-factor streams through compact v5. One scheduled eight-channel sweep on each of two recordings also replayed directly from IQ | All six returned. Median error rose from 1.102 km with the ordinary detector to 1.330 km with periodic reference detection. The capture-era detector control reached 0.832 km. [Detections, positions and estimated detector cost](anchors/README.md) |
| Compare the offered tile against eight neighboring tiles | Six positive and three omitted roof queries, plus two fresh simulated four-site cohorts with a correct and an omitted tile per site | It withheld all six roof fixes. With perturbations it issued 1/4 correct-tile fixes and one 75.5 km false fix on an omitted tile. The previous joint-pass receiver issued 4/4 correct-tile fixes and withheld all four omissions on those identical simulated queries. [Recorded roof](region-challenge/README.md) · [Fresh simulated sites](fresh-transfer/README.md) |
| Transfer both lean track fitters to other sites | Four measured single-beacon sites and four widely separated, simulated multi-channel sites, each offered the correct or an incorrect tile | On the compatible simulations both issued 3/4 correct-tile fixes, and neither issued on an omission. The fitted median rose from 0.316 to 0.390 km with exact geometry. All eight measured queries withheld. The measured waveform supplies only one independent RF lane. [Transfer measurements](track-transfer/README.md) |

DS26 and DS29 were collected at the same installation. The multisite simulations and measured single-beacon recordings have their own panels. Timings use individual CPU cores on the archive host. Some trials start with stored detector outputs or tracks, and each figure and report specifies this boundary. [Dataset and timing definitions](../../tools/pipeline_eval/README.md)

## Rebuild the figures

```sh
.venv/bin/python -m tools.pipeline_eval figures --panel convergence --out /tmp/receiver-convergence
.venv/bin/python -m tools.pipeline_eval figures --panel atlas --out /tmp/all-receiver-charts
```

Both commands use saved predictions. To replay an experiment on its original inputs, use the protocol and commands in its linked report.
