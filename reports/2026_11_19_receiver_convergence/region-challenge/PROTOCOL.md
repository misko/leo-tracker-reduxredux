# Competing region hypotheses

Replay the six matched archive timing IDs and the first supplied offer in the three corresponding DS29 omitted-region queries. The input is original native GLRT probes, hardware clock and RF metadata, and the same causal TLE bytes as the archive joint-pass control. Each worker receives only the original single 25 km offer. Match the cohort and retain forced and returned estimates for both calibration arms. Use one worker per CPU, two CPUs and a 100 s deadline.

The nine hypotheses are the supplied square and its eight adjacent equal squares in a common local east/north plane. Each receives five starts, one at its center and one at each edge midpoint. Shared starts reuse their training results. Build one causal orbit bank for the entire 3 × 3 extent. Use the independent native triplet tracks, the same seven candidate phase shifts and the same joint carrier/frame identity procedure at all starts. The training winner of each square initializes its fit. Keep the chosen identities and orbit retiming fixed for the final location fit.

Reserve the final 1.4 s of each 10 s interval on the hardware clock before any tracking. These windows never enter identity, phase selection, coefficient estimation or location optimization. Use the remaining native observations for tracklet formation and the first two thirds of each recovered tracklet to fit. In each arm and square, fit the two position coordinates within the square and profile the same receiver half-band offsets, drifts and fractional transmitter offsets. Fit up to two robust reweights of the training frequency loss. Include the per-pass affine frame-delay residual when the prefix supports it. The fitted calibration arm adds the common RF term. Use a 16-evaluation budget and the same initialization for both arms.

Use the same held probes for all nine hypotheses. A gated held candidate is eligible when its time lies in a training tracklet on the same receiver and RF lane. Score the average likelihood of its gated candidate peaks and given predicted emitters against circular uniform clutter. Use 200 Hz carrier and 3 µs frame-delay scales, a 0.8 coherent-carrier mixture weight, and an independent 0.8 phase-coherence weight when a frame term is available. A model with no emitter at the probe has unit likelihood ratio. Count each 10 s block once by averaging its probe log likelihood ratios. Require at least four held blocks and 12 held probes with a predicted emitter.

The inside hypothesis must beat the highest outside score and have at least 8:1 odds against the equal-weight outside mixture. It must also pass the frozen joint-pass return bounds on carrier RMS, inlier fraction, frame RMS, orbit/channel/time support and distance from the offered edge, now tested on the reserved observations. If the policy withholds, keep the forced inside coordinates. No outside position is published. Report comparisons and conditional RF residuals separately for both arms and the archived incumbent. Freeze the method before an independent simulation draws held labels.
