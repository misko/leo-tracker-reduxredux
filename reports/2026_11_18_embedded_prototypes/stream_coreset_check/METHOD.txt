Supplementary same-panel compatibility check

Use prototype #1's original Replay, including the native chronological
reader, admission, linking, and segment retention through its full scan
deadline. Freeze once, build the same causal TLE bank and track descriptors,
and compute the common receiver bias seed. Use prototype #6's existing
geometry-12 selection directly on those descriptors. The control submits all
descriptors from the same stream. Both use the same existing 49 grid cells,
12 candidates per track, inlier cost < 12, minimum 4 tracks and 3 satellites,
and 4 robust solve passes per c arm. Each policy fixes its own assignments
and selected observations across the c=0 and fitted-c arms. Conditional
information diagnostics use #6's unregularized inlier Jacobian.

Inputs are the eight shared DS26 cases, using native.json and the causal TLE
snapshots under /srv/bulk/leo-dev/algo07v3-shortcuts-20261107/inputs/. This
check uses the panel search center as its only location input. It compares
the two stream outputs without geographic scoring and sits outside the ten
primary rankings. Absolute CFO RMS uses each policy's retained inliers.

Each case's arms are the geometry-12 endpoints. The same-stream control is
cases[].full_control.arms. Primary wall time is measured on one host thread
from archived detector output through both fits, including archive decoding,
candidate filtering, tracking, orbit/descriptor and bias work, and selection.
The control's additional_wall_s measures its downstream call after reuse of
that preparation. Its logical_total_wall_s adds the measured shared stages
to the measured control's grid and fits. Diagnostics are outside the clocks.
IQ, detector runtime and target board performance are outside timing scope.

After fitting, reopen prototype #1's saved results and require exact equality
of stream counters and the observation hash, TLE receipt, descriptor counts,
and the ordered list of assigned satellites. Check endpoint differences to
1e-10 degrees, RMS differences to 1e-8 Hz, and grid score differences to
1e-10. This is the execution's correctness test on the same eight scans.

The full control reproduced the saved assignments, endpoints, RF RMS and
grid scores exactly on all eight scans. Both policies estimated all scans
in both arms. Median grid work was 7,056 candidate-cell evaluations and 156
fit rows per robust iteration for geometry-12, compared with 37,632 and 715
for full. Median total host time was 421.4 ms for geometry-12 and 423.2 ms for
full. Shared work alone took 416.9 ms. Both had rank two after deleting any
one inlier satellite. The fitted-c weakest-axis information median was
0.026 km^-2 for geometry-12 and 0.198 km^-2 for full. Within geometry-12, c
reduced the paired median CFO RMS by 1.96 Hz and reduced RMS in seven scans.
