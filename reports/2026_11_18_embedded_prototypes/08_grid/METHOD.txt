Marginalized coarse-to-fine position grid

The primary design scores a 25 km square in east/north coordinates at the
shared regional center. It chooses the highest-scoring cell after two bounded
subdivision levels. The coarse-only control chooses the highest-scoring cell
from the same initial 49 evaluations. The design was fixed before scoring
geography.

Start with 7 × 7 cell centers at 3.571 km spacing. Refine the four highest
scoring centers spaced at least 7.143 km from each other. Split each into 3 × 3
children, then split the best child of each refined parent again. Reuse the
unchanged center's score when splitting. This gives 113 unique evaluations,
49 coarse and 64 additional evaluations per arm, and 113 leaves partitioning
the original box. The finest cells have a 0.397 km width. Adaptive refinement
can choose different centers for each arm under the same budget and policy.

Receive anonymous curved graph tracks from tracks001/candidate at the end of
each scan. Use the grid support's longest-64 policy, 12 sampled CFOs per
track, and up to 12 candidate identities per retained track. The support code
constructs the candidate descriptors from causal Starlink TLEs under each
scan's start cutoff, with 5-second table knots and a linear regional Doppler
approximation. Both c arms use the same descriptors. The recorded graph
metadata has a null alias period. The scorer uses the panel support's
4.4 microsecond convention, corresponding to a 227272.7 Hz alias period, to
choose an integer frequency branch per track/candidate/cell. The fixed
nearest branch is an approximation to a wrapped mixture.

Sum candidate components and a fixed clutter term per track. Each candidate
component integrates an independent Gaussian track timing nuisance with a
2 s standard deviation. The score combines a 180 Hz shape scale and 550 Hz
level scale and treats each correlated track as one contribution. Five EM
updates per cell profile four shared receiver/side offsets relative to a
common, data-derived bias vote. Their Gaussian prior standard deviation is
32 kHz. The fitted-c arm adds a shared linear RF term with a zero-centered
200 kHz/GHz prior. The c0 arm fixes that term to zero. Positions use the same
search box and no additional position prior. Conditional timing for the
selected identity gives diagnostic absolute CFO RMS. Paired RMS uses the
intersection of tracks with the same selected identity in both arms.

The result includes each cell's center, width, and profile score. It also
reports the gap to the strongest sampled alternative at least 5 km away,
area within two score units of the peak, and whether the winning cell touches
the box. Those diagnostics depend on the sampled grid, nuisance fit, and
clutter scale. They provide no calibrated geographic probability. The grid
can miss peaks narrower than its coarse spacing or outside the box.

Held-out checks reserve every fourth complete descriptor by mean track time.
Fit position, calibration, and the lane bias vote on remaining tracks.
Evaluate the held tracks with these shared terms frozen and sum identities,
independent track timing, and clutter at those fixed terms. This measures
internal transfer within an archived scan graph. The upstream graph was
formed after the scan and the check does not measure a pre-deadline forecast.
No evaluation positions, previous pose, v5 answers, or saved identity labels
entered the solver. No raw-IQ, on-device, or first-fix test was run.

Use test_grid.py in place of grid.py to run the synthetic check alone. It
compares the analytic timing integral with quadrature, verifies tiling and
budgets, and recovers a planted position with RF calibration. The replay
times wall-clock host compute after acquisition, including graph JSON read,
causal TLE construction, descriptor generation, the bias vote, and both grid
arms. Coarse scores are reused inside that time. It times the optional
holdout check separately. Logged array sizes exclude Python object overhead.

Both arms returned estimates in all eight cases. Refined in-sample score rose
in all cases. Whole held-track scores rose by about 0.010 and 0.011 per track
on average for c0 and fitted c. Two scans in each arm show an alternative
more than 5 km away within two score units. Two winning cells per arm touch
the search box. Fitting c reduces the median paired CFO RMS from 174.5 to
168.3 Hz, with changes in both directions across cases. Mean refined held
score changes by +0.0036 per track. The estimated cell changes by 0.397 km
in four cases and stays at the same center in the other four. Geographic
error is scored independently from the emitted endpoints.
