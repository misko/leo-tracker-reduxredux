Causal gated pilot search and blind reacquisition

Primary design
Per receiver, channel and RF lane, a blind scout seeds up to three confirmed
margin-gated pilot hypotheses. A scout runs at the first visit and at most four
visits after the last blind search. For a live hypothesis the next search is a
circular rectangle in aliased CFO and sample-counter-referenced frame phase.
Single-hit rectangles have broad rate allowances. A second hit initializes
linear rates; later hits update them by exponential averaging. The fixed gate
grows on coasting, expires after five seconds, and permits only the strongest
eligible peak to propagate to the position tracker. Two consecutive guided
misses trigger a blind search on that same probe. An unsuccessful blind search
clears the lane to idle until its next scout. Each lane makes decisions using
its own prior accepted peaks, current search returns and visit count.

Data and test scope
All eight panel cases run on the pinned 10 MS/s, 20 ms native detector-candidate
exports in /srv/bulk/leo-dev/algo07v3-shortcuts-20261107/inputs. For each case
the guided path emits the best surviving archived candidate and the control
emits the archived all-blind winner. Both use the same downstream bounded
stream linker, causal TLE orbit bank, 7 x 7 regional grid, score threshold and
refinement budget. For each frontend, c=0 and fitted-c share observations,
satellite assignments, other priors and iteration limits. True position and v5
outputs are absent from this run. `compared_with_blind` gives endpoint motion
and own-fit RMS changes. Each frontend fits its own tracked samples, so
cross-frontend RMS differences are descriptive. The separate panel scorer
judges geographic accuracy.

`wall_s` is host monotonic time covering native JSON load, decoded candidate
replay, all-blind winner representation validation, the causal orbit bank,
guided track linking and both guided endpoint fits. The blind endpoint fit is
timed separately with the shared bank already built. The rectangular
frequency–phase area divided by the full search area is a search-cell proxy.
Multiple rectangles are charged by the clipped sum of their areas, and rescue
is charged for both the missed local gate and full blind search. This proxy
excludes folding, fixed DSP costs, candidate generation and raw sample I/O.
Wall time measures the replay and position solver on the host.

The candidate-replay synthetic check exercises both circular boundaries, a substitute, lost-lock
rescue, an idle lane, a periodic scout, and isolated receiver state. The panel
run compares exact emitted rows on each causal half-prefix against the full run
and verifies the all-blind control against the shared winner iterator.

Panel result
The scheduler charged 28.01% of the full blind search-cell proxy and reproduced
12,403 of 35,391 archived winners (35.05%). It emitted 63 substitutes, with
median absolute wrapped CFO separation from the archived winner of 74.4 kHz.
Idle visits accounted for 22,730 of 22,925 omissions. Full rescue found a winner
on 46 of 8,146 visits. In this panel the loss trigger mainly fired in short
gaps, then left the returning pilots invisible until a scout. This operating
point sacrifices too much winner coverage to claim equivalent detection.

Both arms estimated all eight endpoints for both frontends. The guided endpoints
moved a median 2.34 km (c=0) and 2.35 km (fitted-c) from their all-blind controls.
Within the guided frontend, fitted-c shifted endpoints by a median 0.11 km.
The median paired change in own-fit RMS was -3.51 Hz. Geographic error requires
the separate panel scorer.

Supplemental raw IQ check
`iq_check.py` read 62 MiB of compressed IQ from the first short-window archive
chunk across eight scans. `sudo -n -u leo` ran only bounded, read-only `head` and
`dd` operations. Decompression, sample digest verification, and analysis ran
as the invoking user. The capture source was dual RX, 10 MS/s, signed CI16. All
detector comparisons use physical RX1 and sample-counter-matched input with
verified per-window SHA-256. The fixed raw windows were sequences 0 and 8 on
panel positions 1, 4 and 8, and sequences 0 and 1 on the other five scans.
Sequences 0 and 8 are the first two distinct visits to the same lane about
0.355 s apart. Reaching sequence 8 required a larger compressed prefix, so
that replay was limited to three fixed cases to stay within the read budget.

The sampled detector adapts the existing two-lag folded scout to 10 MS/s. It
evaluates 64 known Qin symbols against the 17-symbol-rolled control with five
CFO alias hypotheses spaced at the OFDM symbol rate. Blind and guided use the same score and 0.10
exploratory margin. Both include the full folded proposal cost. The guided bank
admits only phase proposals and CFO bins in the predicted rectangles, using a
pruned DFT for admissible frequency cells. The full bank uses a 512-bin FFT per
candidate. The matching criterion is joint wrapped CFO within 8 kHz and frame
phase within 1.6 microseconds. This is a small waveform sanity test of the
restricted bank. It does not qualify native detector parity, false-alarm rate,
or actual raw coverage and runtime over the complete scans.

The raw blind bank and archived native blind detector called the same nine
of 16 windows positive, and eight of those nine winners met the joint match
criterion. For the 10 ms check, a blind bank on the first half seeded the gate
for the second half of window 0. Guided and paired blind processing saw the
same second-half samples. The five cases with an available seed yielded five
joint matches. Median host bank time was 33.75 ms blind and 25.10 ms guided.
At the three actual revisits the primary scheduler's archived prior candidate
peaks supplied the gates. One positive guided winner matched blind, a second
positive selected a different peak, and both banks rejected the third visit.
Median host bank time was 66.50 ms blind and 30.31 ms guided. Timings include
the shared fold computation and exclude data loading and template creation.
The waveform check and timing are recorded in `iq-check.json`. All position
endpoints and full-panel scheduler measurements still come from `result.json`.
