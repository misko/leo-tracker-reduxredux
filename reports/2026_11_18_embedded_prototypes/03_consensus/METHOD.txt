Geometry-balanced leave-satellite-family-fold-out consensus

Use the shared twelve-candidate descriptor, lane-bias vote and 7 by 7
regional vote. Freeze each selected track's winning candidate with cost
below 12 and lift one frequency alias at the grid hypothesis. A satellite
family comprises all selected tracks with the same inferred catalogue
ID. Identical observations, times, RF and inferred IDs are collapsed.
The full-scan graph is evaluated at the end of its acquisition.

Order families by the strength of their demeaned east/north gradient
information after profiling orbit phase. Assign each family to the
smallest fold that gains the most regularized position information.
Fit one all-family hypothesis and up to four leave-one-fold-out fits.
Each fit uses four fixed track IRLS passes and weights the members of
each family by inverse family size. Per-family orbit timing and global
receiver offsets, drifts and optional RF coefficient are eliminated by
Schur complements. Solve the remaining bounded east/north quadratic
over the declared 25 km box. Score each whole family once by its median
whitened track error plus its profiled phase penalty, capped at 9. A
single inlier refit can replace the initial winner if it improves the
same criterion. Require at least four inlier tracks from three families.
The cap is the square of three whitened residual units and applies to
the combined shape, level and phase statistic.

The two arms share observations, candidates, aliases, folds, thresholds,
iteration limits and all priors apart from adding a jointly fitted c
with the shared zero-mean 200000 Hz/GHz prior. The primary frequency
RMS scores all unique shortlisted tracks using the endpoint coefficients
and a profiled phase per family, including excluded families. Inlier
RMS and per-family consensus loss are recorded separately. The compact
control is the shared cold solver on the identical initial tracks and
grid vote. Its absolute RMS uses its jointly optimized family phases.
Neither position differences nor reference coordinates were used to
choose the algorithm or score RF fit.

Inputs are the read-only anonymous archived graph tracks and the
latest eligible TLE snapshot collected by scan start, restricted to
eligible Starlink element epochs from the preceding 48 hours. Both
paths are supplied by the shared benchmark. The selected TLE digest
and stage timings are in result.json. No saved satellite assignments,
v5 output, or geographic truth enters the prototype.

Wall time starts before reading the graph JSON and ends after both
consensus arms on one host numerical thread. The separately timed
compact comparison starts after the same common frontend. The profile
does not include IQ processing, construction of the graph, on-device
runtime, or partial-scan availability. Two fixed-size per-track Gram
arrays support the inner fit. Reported bytes cover these arrays only.
The runnable checks cover bounded quadratic elimination, fold balance,
and exact file-backed replay equality after adding five copies of an
already selected family at the consensus input.
An additional sensitivity check on the same case adds alternating
6 kHz error to every track in that family after shortlisting. The output
records family rejection and displacement relative to each solver's
clean estimate.
