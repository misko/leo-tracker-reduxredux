# Fixed detector-winner prior control

Status: implemented and independently source-reviewed; **no DS21 calculation or fit by the component author**. The coordinator owns the final source/input freeze and execution, after its separately authorized observation-weighting control.

DS21-039's sealed inventory loses 3,908 of 7,622 passing alternatives within native ambiguity cells. Earlier equal-weight all-candidate controls improved fitted-c position on DS21-005 and worsened it on DS21-023; they do not justify a general improvement claim. This small experiment retains alternatives while preserving the original detector winner's prior preference. It does not convert detector margin into calibrated odds.

For B available cells in an original window, each cell retains total conditional signal prior 1/B. A singleton receives 1/B. A cell containing n>1 candidates gives its original metadata winner (1−δ)/B and each other candidate δ/[B(n−1)]. The only conditions are **δ=0** and **δ=0.25**; δ is a native-candidate prior fraction, not the RF coefficient c. The original winner remains the existing minimum of (−margin, rank, ID), determined before any predictions. Visibility masks afterward, without restoring lost visible prior mass. Clutter mass, uniform satellite mass and all physical priors remain unchanged.

The separate `DetectorWinnerPriorObjective` reuses the existing all-candidate singleton likelihood and gradient, changing only its metadata emission prior. It retains every exact native ID, raw CFO and receive time. Zero-prior alternatives remain present at δ=0; that condition must reproduce the unchanged original collapsed model. Sampling-circle ambiguity remains. The factory refuses temporal families, measured waveform evidence or a different physical frame/layout. There is no receiver/satellite identity coupling or measured waveform claim.

Four fitted-c optimizer calls use two identical physical starts in each condition: the original all-band winner and the sealed seven-call refinement's training-selected free winner. Both come from the original all-band model. Every call uses the original physical seed-5 center and its unchanged 25 km disk, 20 seconds and 600 iterations. Warm and weighting outcomes are not new starts. Maximum optimizer allocation is **80 seconds**, with 145 calculation seconds, 165 internal seconds and external TERM/KILL deadlines of 175/180 seconds. Preserve all failures and nonstationary outcomes; atomic launch claims prohibit orphan retries.

Before fitting, both source states must replay their historical objective and NLL within 10⁻⁷. The δ=0 model must also match the original objective, NLL, vector gradient, nuisance gradient and aggregated satellite/clutter posterior using 10⁻⁸ absolute and 10⁻¹¹ relative tolerances. This exercises expanded-slot arithmetic rather than dispatching to the old model. Selected outcomes must replay their condition's objective and NLL within 10⁻⁷. Posterior RMS, alternative signal mass and frequency likelihood are separate from the coordinator's eventual post-seal position evaluation.

The δ=0 winner pool retains the two fully stationary original-prior source incumbents plus new stationary δ=0 fits. The δ=0.25 pool includes only its new fully stationary fits; original-prior stationarity cannot transfer automatically to the changed objective. Selection uses training objective only. An unavailable condition stays unavailable.
