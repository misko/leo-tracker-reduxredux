  candidate pool by the unchanged original rule.

  Only its exact documented nonconvergence exception permits the existing v2

  No failed solver output or replacement seed is substituted.

## Failure, metadata and reference gates

The narrow metadata reader validates the original sequential A/high descriptive
override while preserving all raw numerical fields. Serializable callback results and launch/
termination receipts persist before subsequent diagnosis, so a later error cannot
silently discard an optimizer outcome. Nonstationary results remain in the call
inventory and are ineligible for selection. Orphan/failed slices cannot resume;
finalizing a unit failure requires an explicit reason and externally verified
terminal-process evidence. Finalization preserves existing artifacts and cannot
coexist with a successful unit terminal.

Every arm must seal all 12 outcomes, including finalized failures and unavailable
results, before it can participate in evaluation. **There is no single-arm
evaluator.** `evaluate_pair(original_directory, all_first_directory,
output_directory=...)` validates both complete 12-outcome seals, their exact
condition identities and common training/selector/source provenance. It persists
a separate paired training terminal before its first reference call. Only then
does it join the 14 predetermined model/view rows to the configured reference.
Unavailable results stay unavailable rather than acquiring a fabricated state.
The output directory is a separate paired-report namespace.

Held observations and locked cases are not prepared or used. Reference coordinates
are absent from initialization, fitting and training selection. Geographic errors
use the configured station reference, not independently surveyed truth. Training
NLL/objective/posterior frequency diagnostics, stationarity and boundary flags
must be reported separately from position accuracy. Different model objectives
are not interchangeable validation scores or confidence estimates.

before any mutation. Cached units validate original seeds/options/centers/layout,
