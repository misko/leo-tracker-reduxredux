# Fixed-state native residual structure

This evaluation compares the original DS21-039 all-view training winner with the sealed seven-call refinement winner on the same original all-view windows, native candidates, bank, calibration frame and priors. It makes zero optimizer calls and uses neither held observations nor reference positions. The root recipe is `reports/2026_10_13_ds21_039_root_cause/correlation-run.py`; its frozen protocol and source archive are the execution authority.

The array-only helper `summarize_native_residual_correlation` consumes original-window signal mass, conditional native residual mean and conditional native residual RMS. Blocks are `floor(original observation center time / 20 seconds)`. It preserves missing block labels and excludes exact-zero signal support from residual moments. Posterior mass is normalized before products, including subnormal positive mass.

For a group/block with window signal masses \(m_i\), let \(w_i=m_i/\sum m_i\). The reported mean is \(\sum w_i\mu_i\), second moment is \(\sum w_i R_i^2\), and nominal independent-window comparison is \(\sigma^2\sum w_i^2\). This last quantity is a model proxy, not measured sampling variance or an effective sample size. Zero-support groups retain counts and mass but have null residual moments.

Each group is centered by its own signal-weighted pooled mean. Squared block means and lag products average supported blocks/pairs equally. Lag-one pairs require consecutive original integer labels and positive support in both blocks; gaps and zero-support blocks never become neighbors. Contemporaneous cross-products use only common supported block labels, with each group retaining its own pooled centering. These are descriptive centered cross-moments, not covariance estimates. Common time bins do not establish simultaneous measurements or a shared emitter.

Prespecified axes are receiver, band, channel, exact RF, receiver × band and receiver × channel. The recipe retains endpoint definitions, original window IDs, native source replay checks, block support/gaps, sparse pair counts and summaries. It checks the frozen source and ancestor bindings before and after the two endpoint audits. Its ceiling is 110 internal seconds, TERM at 115 seconds and KILL at 120 seconds. A failed or incomplete audit remains unavailable; it does not trigger new fits.

Posterior-selected in-sample residuals share fitted intercept/drift constraints, and model misspecification can create centered patterns. Changes between endpoints indicate model/mode sensitivity; persistence can motivate a separately declared covariance sensitivity control. Neither outcome proves receiver clock noise, physical hardware calibration, independent sample count, position accuracy or confidence. No covariance fitting, significance test or weight tuning is part of this audit.

Component verification: 12 synthetic tests cover IID residuals, shared block offsets, retained trends, original gaps, zero support, subnormal mass, fractional mass, label/order invariance and invalid inputs.
