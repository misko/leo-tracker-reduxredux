# One fixed likelihood-to-prior scale sensitivity

Proposed next control: original native likelihood at **alpha=1** versus a single predeclared **alpha=0.25**. This is a regularization sensitivity, not a hardware correction, estimated noise covariance or inferred independent sample count. It is proposed from training/source evidence; neither alpha nor the parameter/prior choices are selected using evaluated positions. No scientific run is authorized by this document alone.

The equal-band/block controls preserve total window weight N and leave the overall likelihood-to-prior scale untested. The receiver RF contrast has first-order training-score support but did not explain the localization deficit. Fixed-state residual cross-products change signs between existing modes, and extreme channel ratios can come from almost unsupported conditional tails. Those observations do not justify adding a clock covariance model or assigning a calibrated stronger UTC/TLE/hardware prior. The existing relative timing allowance also compensates association mismatch, so removing it outright would confound this diagnosis with an unverified orbit-error assertion.

For the original marginalized observation NLL L and exact existing Gaussian prior penalty P, use `O_alpha = alpha * L + P`. Temper **after** branch/satellite/clutter marginalization, not the complete-data emissions. At a fixed state, native satellite/branch/clutter posteriors, visibility masking and normalized predictive likelihood remain original. Scale only observation NLL and its physical-vector/nuisance derivatives; add every prior and derivative once.

Keep sigma=125 Hz, existing native signal/clutter and uniform satellite/ambiguity priors, 43-satellite bank, 2,798 original all-view training windows, actual native times/RF and original calibration frames. Keep common c fitted with its original ±5,000 Hz/GHz box, receiver offsets/drifts (drifts ±60 Hz/s), the two original group contrasts with 50,000 Hz Gaussian scale, common timing sigma=3 s and box ±10 s, relative timing sigma=2 s, original zero-sum basis and coverage-clipped total satellite-shift boxes ±20 s. Keep zero temporal edges, neutral waveform evidence and all original visibility rules. No new receiver RF contrast is added.

After multiplying the complete objective by `1/alpha`, this control is algebraically equivalent to strengthening **all** existing Gaussian penalties by `1/alpha`: alpha=0.25 corresponds to halving their standard deviations in that equivalent representation (precision ×4). Thus it cannot isolate one particular timing, group or clock prior, and it must not be presented as a calibrated prior estimate. The implementation preserves P explicitly so the ledger and intended intervention remain clear.

Use the exact original-all winner and sealed seven-call refinement winner as the two physical starts in both conditions, unchanged including relative timings and group nuisance. Every call uses the original all-view seed-5 center and 25 km disk, fitted c only, 20 seconds and 600 iterations. Four new calls total (at most 80 optimizer seconds); 165 internal seconds, TERM at 175 and KILL at 180. The alpha=1 baseline may retain the two sealed stationary source incumbents; alpha=0.25 selects only its actual new stationary fits. Failed/nonstationary calls remain failures without retries or replacement starts. No alpha sweep or fitted alpha.

Before fitting, actual alpha=1 arithmetic must replay both saved objectives, original NLLs, both gradients and posteriors. Synthetic tests must check alpha=0.25 value/prior decomposition, unchanged fixed-state probabilities and analytic finite differences over geography, receiver affine terms, fitted c, common/relative timing and group nuisance; arbitrary positive alpha must not silently rescale priors. Bind source bytes/archives, input/bank/native evidence, original two starts, old-seven authority and policy before execution.

The falsifiable training diagnosis is whether the current likelihood scale encourages calibration/timing excursions that disappear under stronger joint regularization while preserving useful signal support. Report each condition's own composite objective, **untempered normalized NLL**, alpha-scaled NLL, prior ledger, native RMS, signal/clutter/association changes, nuisance/timing magnitudes, stationarity and active bounds separately. Composite scores across alpha are not one common likelihood and must not be used to select alpha. A changed fit with lower prior cost alone does not establish improved localization or correct associations.

Seal all four outcomes and training selections before root joins position errors. Retain both conditions and failures against the user's <0.5 km goal. A negative result rejects promotion of this particular quarter-scale sensitivity; it does not falsify every noise/correlation/prior model. A positive development result still requires a fixed transfer test and the full development inventory before any locked release. This proposal executes no predictor, optimizer, held scoring, reference lookup, new RF or locked-data operation and changes no published contract or frozen source.
