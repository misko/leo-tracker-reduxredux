# Evaluation after the RF seal

Do not open new geographic outcomes until both the scientific RF seal and the
coordinator's explicit release are present. Freeze the evaluator/source before
that read. This branch performs no position fit, source nomination, prior
tuning, gate repair or candidate selection using geography.

The denominator is the original four DS24 development cases, 0010–0013, in both
c=0 and fitted-c arms. Preserve every selected case, failure and timeout, without
replacement. Compare the exact saved reference, any matched execution control,
the raw expanded-uncertainty endpoint and the declared policy endpoint. A
rejected trial retains the exact reference as hypothetical research-policy output.
The research policy requires paired conservative gates, phase/Gamma convergence,
and complete original accepted-ID preservation, including postfit-only IDs.
This ID condition is additional to the original residual gates. Complete,
successful matched-cold and candidate execution with no recorded fit failures
is a separate predeclared research eligibility prerequisite.
The operational policy is separately fixed to the reference for every case;
its position/error changes must be exactly zero. Raw diagnostic movement remains
visible regardless of qualification. Missing endpoints/errors are null, never
zero or an omitted case.

Report mean, median, maximum and descriptive P95 errors, every paired error
change and improved/worsened/unchanged counts. Four scans do not estimate a
reliable population tail. Report matched c effects, RF objective/RMS changes,
phase/Gamma/numerical status, primitive residual gates, owner-support rejection,
full likelihood membership and postfit accepted IDs separately. Do not compare
raw residual means as if covariance-normalized objectives were identical.

Figures should show every reference/control/raw/policy error and paired change,
RF-versus-position movement, and the physical-covariance/qualification mechanism
from sealed arrays. Clearly mark failed and unqualified diagnostic endpoints.
Geography cannot choose an arm, endpoint, mode set, threshold, source or fallback.
The existing evaluation site is an explicit same-deployment assumption, not a
new position prior or a new-location validation. Include source/seal hashes and
the exact reference identity in all derived tables.

The publication will include the new protocol/source/results/audits and compact
derived evidence in the clean publisher, with explicit external input/runtime
replay requirements. Root tracked changes and all prior published studies remain
unchanged. No standalone full input archive is implied.
