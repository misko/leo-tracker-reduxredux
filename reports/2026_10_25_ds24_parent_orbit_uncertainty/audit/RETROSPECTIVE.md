# Independent scientific critique

Three bottlenecks have substantial support, with different levels of certainty.

1. **Position is strongly confounded with timing and physical shape.** The earlier known-nuisance calculation changes the median conditional weak-axis scale from 534 m to 110 m with timing known and 56 m with timing/shape known. These are unavailable-information controls, not achieved accuracy or calibrated confidence. The paired synthetic factorial reduces mean error from 0.460/0.469 km to 0.295/0.297 km when both generating deviations are removed; separate timing and shape interventions each matter. Its identities are known by construction and inference is unchanged. It does not establish the real-data error floor. The new saved-metadata inventory identifies a concrete current implementation omission: 33/89 active family instances, contributing 1,544 likelihood IDs, have no three-mode shape prior. Sources: [phase/shape report](/srv/bulk/leo/research/ds24-phase-shape-validation-r01/README.md), [factorial contrasts](/srv/bulk/leo/research/ds24-phase-shape-validation-r01/synthetic/factorial_contrasts.json), [missing-mode inventory](/srv/bulk/leo/research/ds24-nomination-information-r01/audit/shape_support_inventory.json).

2. **Discrete identities and independent geometry remain uncertain.** Real 0011/0012 comparisons have close candidate alternatives; repeated NORAD labels and a good assigned-identity fit are not independent corroboration. The 0013 whole-owner witnesses were historically label-selected and all RX1, so a common RX1 discrepancy remains possible. The new full-catalogue conditional simulation retains all eight physical truths without forcing them into the shortlist, but only seven physical orderings per arm qualify and none of the four polynomial-null orderings qualifies. It therefore supplies no calibrated real identity error rate. Conditional information ranking chooses a shorter owner on three of four scans, but assumes the archived identity and a local Gaussian model. Sources: [causal identity findings](/srv/bulk/leo/research/ds24-identity-components-r01/identity/FINDINGS.md), [nomination/information audit](/srv/bulk/leo/research/ds24-nomination-information-r01/audit/FINDINGS.md).

3. **Residual fit is a poor proxy for localization benefit.** The later RX1 correction test improves fitted-c mean error only 1.239054→1.223610 km while maximum error worsens 3.405038→3.665544 km. In 0007, RMS improves 5.917 Hz while position worsens 260.506 m. The matched cold control exactly equals the reference, excluding that tested restart/budget explanation. Receiver effects remain plausible; generic receiver flexibility is not a demonstrated general fix. Source: [sealed later-panel table](/srv/bulk/leo/research/ds24-phase-shape-validation-r01/validation/report/positions.csv).

Low-value repeats without a changed hypothesis are generic continuous multistart (30 tested restarts agree within 2.06 cm), maximizing association percentage, and cleaner CFO alone (322 RX2 curves improve median scatter 78.07→22.18 Hz without a qualified cohort localization gain). More DS23 native recovery improves postfit coverage 70.71→74.41% but mean error only 1.434→1.426 km and worsens P95. Blindly repeating extra RX1 basis flexibility or the old all-candidate uncertainty control is also weak: earlier DSX01 outcomes were mixed. These experiments limit those particular mechanisms; they do not falsify every discrete identity change, physical receiver hypothesis or properly qualified recovery. The historical results and distinct cohorts are sourced in the [accumulated report](/srv/bulk/leo/research/ds24-phase-shape-validation-r01/README.md) and [prior-art review](../review/FINDINGS.md).

Use the frozen RF-only triage and matched cold fits on all four cases if triggered. Separate likelihood fit, gate residuals, local information and actual position; keep the reference operational. This directly tests a measured omission rather than merely adding points or flexibility. Its fixed-state audit now passes; no fit outcome is assumed here.

2. **A qualified identity falsification/held-owner test.** Reuse the same sealed synthetic draws to address the actual visibility and numerical guard failures under a separately frozen physical model: impossible phases contribute zero likelihood without renormalizing away their prior mass, and irrelevant alternatives need defensible evidence bounds. Preserve ordinary complete-catalogue nomination, misses and an unresolved outcome; do not force truth or reroll seeds. For real-data follow-up, select a complete cross-receiver discovery/held-owner design by metadata before reading either response, compare competing identities symmetrically, and carry shared orbit/radio uncertainty jointly. Do not train a receiver correction on the held target and claim independent corroboration. If suitable existing owners are unavailable, that is the outcome. The existing same-RX1 witnesses cannot resolve receiver versus common-orbit error.
