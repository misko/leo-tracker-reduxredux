The following source-audit corrections and interpretation limits matter for the final report. Population source fixes below were checked directly in the amended `run.py`, `audit.py`, `summarize.py`, `execution_amendment.json`, and `audit_guard_amendment.json`; they are not merely proposed changes. Earlier receipts and the original runner remain preserved.

1. **Prefit GPS guard: resolved in amended source and final decision replay.** The original population runner computed `error(x)` before candidate fitting and used a 0.02 m saved-error replay assertion as a condition for continuing the case. This was a comparator-provenance assertion rather than GPS tuning, but a mismatch could have triggered fallback through evaluation truth. The amended worker instead verifies saved **RF coordinates** directly, along with objective and coverage. Geography agreement is now reporting metadata after the RF decision and does not assert or affect acceptance. Final `audit.py` recomputes eligibility without calling the geography function. The combined runner and final ensemble qualification also have no GPS-dependent decision rule. Original receipts must not be described as having read truth exclusively postfit; final results use the amended audit rule.

2. **RF score thresholds and ensemble weights are diagnostic.** Scaling three existing physical feature directions retains their unit Gaussian latent priors; the existing hierarchical bound includes covariance volume and normalizing constants. No missing prior-scale Jacobian was found: integration occurs over the same standardized coefficients, so the scaled design supplies the changed covariance. However, per-track likelihood powers create generalized evidence, the Gamma posterior is variational, and nonlinear phase-reference changes alter the local approximation. Consequently `exp(-objective/2)` weights are not calibrated identity probabilities, exact Bayes factors, or total position uncertainty. The code correctly labels them diagnostic; the final prose should retain those limits. The population score threshold is a predeclared guard, not a calibrated statistical test.

3. **Nonconverged matched-control comparator: resolved by an explicit generic guard amendment.** The original population guard required candidate qualification and both score gains, but allowed a nonclosed matched SD1 control to provide a comparator. Both amended worker acceptance and final `audit.py` now require **both control arms' optimizer success and phase closure**. This is an additional generic eligibility rule recorded in `audit_guard_amendment.json`, applied uniformly rather than selected by scan or GPS outcome. It changes the original guard and must be reported as an amendment, not silently attributed to the original protocol. Full residual-gate qualification of controls is recorded separately and is not a required condition of this amended optimizer/closure guard. This still does not turn the generalized approximate scores into exact likelihood evidence.

4. **Stale resume-result trust: resolved by the final audited route and complete-cohort binding check.** The execution runner can still reuse an existing non-running receipt. The new `audit.py` checks each receipt against the current protocol hash, actual input hash, worker-recorded input hash, and exact saved baseline metadata, while recording the worker file hash. It reconstructs older endpoints lacking full-union evidence, replays the amended acceptance rule, and writes separate `audited_result.json` and `final_audit.json` files. The amended summarizer reads **only audited results** and supplies exact baseline fallback for unaudited cases. This removes raw cached-result trust from final selection. The independent final check now verifies all 205 audited records and their current worker/input/protocol/source bindings, with zero numerical workers running. The complete-cohort result is [passed](population_final_review.json).

Other reviewed invariants held: shared history is completed before all incumbent/candidate models; the ledger remains causal; mapped hypotheses share observations and budgets within both c arms; physical modes survive `e.build` and its in-place calculate cache; the final combined qualifier checks raw phase closure, optimizer status, gates, finite objective/position, no lost or unrelated reassignments, and mixture boundary mass strictly below 0.01. Existing accepted ALGO07 repairs take an explicit preservation branch before ALGO06 reconstruction, and failed/capped/rejected population cases retain exact saved baseline errors, objectives, and coverage. The selected population coverage excludes incidental extension proposals. The amended population inventory checks full curved+conditional-entry+phase-mixture unions and their fitted identity maps against baseline published owners, rejecting duplicate IDs.

The final qualifier's mixture-interior rule was already specified in the frozen combined protocol even though the original raw runner omitted it. Applying that rule in the separate final pass is a correction of eligibility implementation, not a new post-outcome threshold. The final qualifier source and outputs should be included in the publication hash manifest.

## Inherited DS26-0104 contract limitation

The population worker fails its baseline full-union assertion before invoking family selection, covariance scaling, or the solver. An independent no-refit [baseline reconstruction diagnostic](baseline_contract_DS26-0104.json), generated by [baseline_contract_diagnostic.py](baseline_contract_diagnostic.py), reproduces this in both c arms: **3,486 published owned IDs versus 3,463 fitted likelihood IDs** (2,865 curved + 598 conditional; no phase-mixture family). The discrepancy is exactly the 23-point native graph track `SCAN-RX1-CH4-T005-RF11459687500.000000`, published under NORAD 68067. These 23 IDs are absent from the reconstructed curved/conditional/mixture likelihood union, with no extra fitted IDs or identity changes among shared IDs. They are also absent from the curved fit's source rows, rather than merely excluded by its point mask.

The saved baseline objective and ownership replay succeed; the stricter union-equals-ownership requirement exposes an inherited difference between association ownership and the inference likelihood. It does not establish that those published associations are false and is not a mutation introduced by this prototype. The experiment preserves exact ALGO07 fallback and does not repair this ownership contract. Missing existing physical-mode support in DS26-0018/0092 is likewise an explicit unsupported-context fallback, not permission to invent new orbit columns.

The population's final audit also identifies DS26-0179 before prototype fitting: 3,275 published owners versus 3,244 likelihood IDs, with 31 absent IDs under NORAD 62593, no extra IDs and no shared-ID identity changes in either c arm. Its exact saved comparator is preserved under the same unsupported-baseline-contract outcome.

The extra retries were stopped, sealed second-attempt timeout receipts were restored, and the original resume deadline was retained. No fit or geography calculation occurs in this verification.

The same verifier checks every final worker/audit/selected-result binding against current protocol, actual native input, audit source and driver source hashes; accepted native unions, fitted identity hashes and likelihood-piece counts; exact saved ALGO07 fallbacks including the three existing repairs; both matched controls' phase closure and optimizer success; candidate base/exact/mixture gates; and recomputed RF improvement thresholds. Its three tests reject stale worker bindings, changed fallback metadata, and acceptance against a nonclosed matched control. Together with prior solver/union tests, nine component tests pass.
