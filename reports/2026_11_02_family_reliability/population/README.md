# Fixed family-SD4 plus half-damped cohort stress test

**Do not promote the blanket SD4 rule.** On the 203 recordings held from the two-scan model development, fitted-c mean error improves only 10.3 m, with 15 improvements and 18 regressions; p90 worsens by 11.0 m. The fresh cohort's worst regression is 1,456.5 m. RF-score gains therefore do not establish reliable localization gains.

All 205 recordings have sealed decisions: **168 completed candidate fit pairs**, 81 completed matched-control pairs, 35 accepted candidates, 133 rejected candidates, 20 recordings without an RF trigger, three exact preserved ALGO07 repairs, two families without existing physical modes, two unsupported baseline-evidence unions, and **ten timeouts**. The unsupported and capped recordings retain saved ALGO07 and are not completed new-model evaluations. Every accepted pair passes full candidate RF qualification, complete native-ID/fitted-owner preservation, and matched-control optimizer success and phase closure; all published coverage counts remain fixed. Full control residual-gate qualification is reported separately and is not an acceptance requirement.

The unsupported baseline unions are DS26-0104 (3,486 published owners versus 3,463 fitted IDs; 23 missing for 68067) and DS26-0179 (3,275 versus 3,244; 31 missing for 62593). Both retain their exact saved comparator. The ten timeout fallbacks are DS24-0079; DS26-0006/0086/0116/0128/0142/0152/0183/0255; and DS28-0015. All saved/selected reporting metrics are finite, no running receipts or unattempted placeholders remain, and the five population tests pass.

This is an offline stress test of a frozen research candidate against the exact saved ALGO07 comparator on 205 recent recordings. It is not a promotion evaluation: development predictive comparisons favored SD4 in RF score, but those predictive folds have qualification limits. The final report must distinguish the two model-development scans (DS26-0070 and DS26-0309) from the remaining 203 recordings. No candidate accuracy aggregate is used to select model parameters or acceptance rules.

The comparator contains 25 DS24, 148 DS26, 13 DS27, 14 DS28, and five fresh recordings, deduplicated by the original native recording identity tuple. The original source population has 229 members and 24 unavailable results; they do not enter the 205-record metric denominator. The identities, source input paths/hashes, c-arm saved positions' RF objectives, coverage counts, errors, and authority are frozen in `protocol.json`.

Three existing ALGO07 guarded replacements (DS26-0034, DS26-0066, DS26-0251) are preserved exactly from saved ALGO07 outputs. They are never reconstructed as ALGO06 comparators. Every other comparator is reconstructed with frozen ALGO07 science and verified against the saved normalized RF objective, saved source RF coordinates, and published native owners. Baseline GPS agreement is a separate post-decision reporting audit and cannot cause candidate fallback.

For each applicable recording the candidate takes the first generic RF influence-eligible family from `e.rank_families`, widens only its three **existing** physical Gaussian orbital standard deviations to 4×, and uses half-damped phase-reference updates. It adds no slopes, offsets, orbital modes, or receiver freedom. A family without existing physical modes remains inapplicable and falls back; no alternate family is substituted. Both c arms and the matched SD1/half control use the same saved fitted-c RF position/reference, observations, aliases, candidate identities, other priors, and 15-round / 35-iteration / 140-evaluation budgets. RF likelihood normalization, including the covariance log determinant and the existing fractional-power convention, remains unchanged.

Acceptance requires, in both candidate arms, optimizer success, phase closure at 0.01 s, all original approximate/exact/mixture gates, mixture boundary mass below 0.01, preservation of the complete native likelihood union and fitted owner map, and no lost or unrelated reassigned published owners. Both matched control arms must have optimizer success and phase closure. The candidate must beat both its matched control and the saved ALGO07 normalized full objective by strictly more than `2 log(3)` in each c arm. Every other result keeps the exact saved ALGO07 comparator. Position error never enters this decision.

Published coverage is fixed before and after the experiment. Inherited graph-membership routines can propose incidental previously unowned extensions, but these never enter fitting, are never adopted, and receive no recovery credit. Complete native evidence includes the kept curved points, conditional entries, and phase-mixture-family entries; duplicate use across likelihood pieces is rejected. Native-set and fitted-owner hashes are checked across both c arms and every model context.

The original protocol and source are retained. The execution amendment runs both candidate arms first and omits matched controls once candidate qualification or the frozen saved-baseline RF-score gain has already failed; those controls are explicitly `not_run_RF_candidate_qualification_or_saved_score_guard_failed`. This RF-only short circuit preserves the acceptance conjunction. A scientific audit amendment removed an initial preselection baseline-GPS assertion, required control convergence, and expanded a curved-only ID check to the complete likelihood union. Earlier worker receipts remain available, and all final decisions are replayed using the audited guard before aggregate candidate GPS reporting. The amended guards are generic and were fixed before aggregate candidate accuracy inspection.

Execution begins with eight BLAS=1 workers, supplemented by four authorized workers after the parallel predictive work completes. All run at nice 10. The main queue uses lexical case IDs and the supplementary queue uses reverse lexical case IDs, independently of geography. Each fit attempt has a 600 s cap and each cohort execution window is bounded to 1,200 s. A bounded resume may finish unattempted records, retaining original capped/failed attempt receipts. An unattempted fallback is not counted as a completed scientific evaluation.

Final reporting uses `audited_result.json`, not provisional cached worker decisions. The final audit validates the frozen protocol and input hashes, the exact saved comparator, full native-union/owner invariants, endpoint score replay when reconstructing older receipts, and the amended acceptance guard. Any unaudited result remains explicitly unverified and uses baseline fallback.

During a resume-manager reload, existing locked workers continued their second attempts. After those attempts exhausted 600 s, the replacement manager incorrectly started seven redundant serial retries. Those retries were stopped after 1,008 total worker-seconds, and the sealed second-attempt timeout receipts were restored. No concurrent duplicate fitting occurred, and no aborted-retry result is selected. Exhausted attempts are now terminal and the original resume deadline is retained. The correction and all affected receipt hashes are recorded in `orchestration_correction.json`. For a capped attempt with no completed candidate pair, the final audit verifies only the exact saved-comparator/input/protocol binding; the new model's full-native evidence and fit are explicitly not evaluated.

At the hard deadline one exited worker left a partial `running` checkpoint because manager finalization had stopped. That checkpoint is preserved and classified as an exhausted-window fallback, with no completed candidate pair claimed; `terminal_receipt_reconciliation.json` binds the original and final receipt hashes.

Artifacts: `case_ledger.csv` lists each recording's final decision and rejection/cap reason; `attempt_ledger.csv` preserves execution-window attempt events. `positions.csv` separates saved, candidate, and selected RF scores from postfit GPS outcomes; `cohort_statistics.csv` and `summary.json` report mean, median, p90, maximum, improvements, regressions, and frozen coverage by dataset/c arm and development/held subset. `cohort-comparison.png` compares final selected errors; `RF-vs-geography.png` displays candidate RF gains and geography changes separately. The lean `publication_manifest.json` hashes reviewable summary artifacts; detailed case JSONs stay local. The final aggregate result is generated only after the 205-record audit is complete.

Reproduction from the worktree root uses `/srv/bulk/leo-dev/fast-algo02-production-v1/.venv/bin/python`, `PYTHONPATH=src:tools`, and `OPENBLAS_NUM_THREADS=OMP_NUM_THREADS=MKL_NUM_THREADS=1`. `run.py --all` executes the frozen policy; `accelerate.py` supplies four additional workers; `audit.py --workers 12` validates every saved result without new RF fitting; `summarize.py` renders final audited results. `test_population.py` checks exact existing-ALGO07 preservation, exact failure fallback, and full-native/phase qualification requirements. No production code, UI, ownership output, golden fixture, or database is changed. Publication of research artifacts is handled by the parent report.
