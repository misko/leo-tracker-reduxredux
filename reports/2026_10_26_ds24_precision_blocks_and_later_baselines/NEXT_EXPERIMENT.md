## Separate measurement transfer from learned-weight movement

Inject native refined-minus-original CFO into every existing occurrence of its
native ID, including predictive rows if present. Add the delta directly to the
existing graph value: **no extra RF scaling**, alias search, graph reconstruction
or updated cached polynomial fit. Use the identical corrected vector in c=0
and fitted-c. Preserve all other observations, identities, masks, powers,
causal priors and shared curved/predictive covariance.

The smallest primary mechanism comparison is original versus refined CFO under
each later baseline's **original fixed noise and full nuisance-prior packet**.
With four cases and two c arms this requires sixteen matched conditional fits,
eight original and eight refined, using the established
[frozen-packet model](../2026_10_26_ds24_cfo_precision_factorial/solver/PROTOCOL.md).
Reproduce each saved same-state posterior/residual/predictive port first, then
use identical cold starts, phase refreshes and budgets for each pair. A saved VI
endpoint is not automatically the original conditional-Gaussian optimized cell.
This gives a CFO-only model effect without reopening packet selection or the
full factorial. It does not establish operational VI performance.

If operational-style transfer is also a declared objective, prospectively add
the ordinary original/refined VI pairs with the unchanged Gamma updates and
matched cold controls. Keep their results separate from fixed-packet effects;
do not add this branch because an earlier geographic result is favorable.
The difference between VI and frozen-conditional movements includes precision
feedback and objective/model differences, not a measured noise-only cause.
Current [mixed-block results](mechanism/FINDINGS.md) already show why this
distinction matters. No new packet sweep is needed for the first later test.

## Minimal guards, roles and decisions

One measurement owner prepares/replays the complete requests; one inference
owner applies the exact delta and runs the declared matched fits; one reviewer
checks the frozen ports and terminal saved artifacts. Reuse the existing parity,
native-ID nonmutation/zero-delta and fitted-c packet-layout fixtures. One compact
preflight and one terminal check per stage suffice; a new general framework is
unnecessary. Root freezes the budget and policy and releases evaluation only
after the RF seal.

- Stop measurement on missing runtime/payload authority or parity disagreement;
  retain every slot and do not repair by reselecting a candidate or runtime.
- Stop complete-repair claims on incomplete owner coverage; numerical fallback
  remains an explicit part of the estimator, never a dropped observation.
- Stop qualified position claims if matched original controls fail, fixed
  likelihood IDs change, or the predeclared original/paired gates fail. Report
  primitive gates, postfit-only losses and the existing 0004 absolute-phase
  overrun separately. Nomination fidelity does not waive those distinctions.
- Report score/frequency changes, fixed-packet displacement and ordinary VI
  results separately. Geographic errors require the confirmed evaluation-only
  site receipt and no inference access. Retain original-reference fallback and
  all four case outcomes; no operational promotion follows from a four-case gain.

The decisive outcome is whether the frozen repair supplies repeatable,
RF-supported measurement changes and transferable matched position benefit,
rather than merely a higher score or favorable learned packet. A failure closes
this transfer rule; a small positive result earns a broader fixed-cohort test.
