# Causal orbit-revision support

Inventory only; freeze before execution. Use all 3,983 eligible conditional
residual cases in the nine DS7–DS9 eight-scan panels. For each scan select the
latest Space-Track archive snapshot collected strictly before start minus 505 s.
Separately select the latest snapshots at least 6 hours and 24 hours older than
that latest snapshot. Preserve full case denominator and candidate numbers.

Count presence and distinct element-line pairs, distinguishing changed epoch
from changed bytes at equal epoch. Report candidate element age relative to
capture, including future epochs explicitly, and collection ages. Verify archive
digests through TleArchiveReader. Do not infer independence from provider names,
or covariance from older/newer GP disagreement. No RF, propagation, model fitting,
provider download or archive writes. One process, 120-second cap.

This determines feasibility of an independently sourced revision-sensitivity
assay. Revised GP elements are external to our RF fitting, but are not independent
precision truth; disagreement does not identify which orbit is more accurate.
