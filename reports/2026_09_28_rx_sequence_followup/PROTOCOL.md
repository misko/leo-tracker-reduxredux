# Sequence support diagnostic, before another fitted model

This is an exploratory audit of the frozen receiver-order pilot, not confirmation on fresh data. Preserve the prior bank, partitions, mapping, match gates and all outcomes unchanged. No new satellite ranking, frequency fitting, alias selection, beam fitting or RF collection.

Audit all seventeen hypothesis-period sequences with at least one unique hit on both receivers, including the single uncensored lag. Keep matched and ambiguous raw candidate IDs, original scores, source support centers, paired-epoch compatibility, missing/ambiguous windows, run lengths and observation cadence. A first-hit interval is bracketed only by an earlier eligible non-hit and that first hit; earlier ambiguity makes that interval unavailable. These are sampled detection transitions, not proven physical entry times. Do not select a more favorable case after inspection.

Separately inspect all thirty frozen training posteriors for entropy, maximum conditional weight, exact zeros and omitted catalogue mass. This diagnostic consumes only the bank and partition assignment, not future outcomes. Conditional probability saturation is a model-support limitation, not evidence of known identity. Do not floor probabilities or broaden the shortlist in response to these outcomes.

The next-model design must retain clutter, nondetections and alternate candidate assignments, handle dependence between the receivers and between duplicated track hypotheses, and define an ungated predictive endpoint independent of reception conditioning. Distinguish proposed methodology from evaluated results. Respect the existing six-record calibration/four-record evaluation division; these reused observations cannot become blind confirmation.

Freeze script/input hashes and command before execution. Each descriptive export is limited to one CPU, 4 GiB address space and 300 seconds; use only existing local JSON/JSONL artifacts. Preserve any failed attempt and its cause. No numerical campaign is authorized by this diagnostic.
