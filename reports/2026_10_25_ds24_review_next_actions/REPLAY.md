This is a documentary decision review. It performs no new simulation,
nomination, calibration, position fitting or geographic evaluation. The
completed experiments and their original failures, protocols and replay
requirements remain in the linked reports.

`prior_art/` copies six existing research source snapshots and three saved
neighbor/geometry/copula receipts solely to support the historical inventory.
They are unchanged old evidence, not new executions or a complete runnable
closure. Their original locations and exact hashes are in the source manifest.

The two PNG figures are copied byte-for-byte from commit
`339fdd0f53b8fc8f5fc3822273294716fcc0422d`, report
`reports/2026_10_25_ds24_evidence_synthesis`. Their exact data bindings and
`make_figures.py` remain in that publication. They summarize separate
experiments and conditional information controls; they do not include new
numerical results from this review. No figure was regenerated here.

`source_manifest.json` binds the reviewed narrative, independent notes and
reused images to their original source bytes. `publication_manifest.json`
records all compact published files except itself. The directory has no raw
recordings, full input/runtime closure, derived numerical archive or release
assets. Restoring this review means checking those file hashes; it does not
reproduce the historical scientific experiments.

The next experiments described in the review are proposals. They retain
explicit caps, unresolved/fallback outcomes and matched c arms where relevant;
none is launched or operationally adopted by publishing the review.
