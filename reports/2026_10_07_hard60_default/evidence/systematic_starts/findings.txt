Interpretation
The fixed recipe recovers the successful solution without a hand-repaired clock
start. The useful geographic start was already present in the original candidate
pool. It had been omitted from V16's earlier slope replay; adding it back and
allowing a 25 km refinement disk made the successful region available. The
production runner already combines discovery basins across methods, so that
particular omission must not be described as a production union bug.

The known receiver is 19.216 km from that grid center. This explains retrospectively
why the original 12.5 km disk was too small. The replay fits and ranks candidates
without reference coordinates and evaluates geographic error afterward.

The +/-60 bound is a selected guardrail, not the demonstrated main cause of the
large gain: the corrected unrestricted search already reached 2.322 km. A bad
local branch can still satisfy +/-60 while having severe receiver-pair mismatch.
Standard starts and region coverage are essential alongside the slope limit.

This remains an offline, one-capture confirmation using frozen candidate banks
and calibration. Automatic region expansion, general paired-residual gates,
full discovery with the new prior, and continuous-filter integration remain
unimplemented. See protocol.txt for the repeatable procedure and next-step rules.
