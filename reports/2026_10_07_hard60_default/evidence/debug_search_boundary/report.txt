The local search disk truncates the T1AT basin. Doubling it to 25 km gives
converged fits for both RF arms, with better objectives and better evaluated
positions. The V16 basin also reaches converged interior fits in the wider
disk, but its position gets worse. Numerical convergence and frequency fit
therefore do not justify a localization claim.

Controlled experiment
Used deployed 5600cd81e5f2-r2 numerical implementation, public saved-input and
checkpoint readers, exact frozen TLE digest/time and original observation
inventory. Saved fitted-c associated vector is the shared continuation start
for both arms; zero-c changes only c to zero. Receiver baseline, candidate
subset, prior, timing state, score and constraints other than local radius are
held fixed. Each fit has equal 35 s / 600 iteration budget; BLAS is single
threaded and fits run sequentially. No RF was collected and production stores
were never written. The reference position enters only evaluation afterward.

Both selected fitted starts were the saved associated rows. Their original
objectives reprice exactly: T1AT 38299.732897682734 with 34 satellites;
V16 41550.91800665939 with 48. Basin grid coordinates uniquely identify the
12.5 km hierarchy spacing, so the deployed max(12.5,spacing/sqrt(2)) rule gives
an original 12.5 km disk even after union of the two searches. Centers remain
the saved association initial positions, not the continuation positions.

T1AT wider fits land 22.257 km and 20.725 km from local center. The fitted arm
improves objective by 439.449 versus its original saved fit. Compared with
the matched zero-c fit, its objective is 451.911 lower while position error is
1982.616 m higher. Fitted frequency RMS 108.235 Hz versus zero-c 155.798 Hz
likewise does not establish better localization. Calibration selection penalties
are constant within each basin (T1AT 2.858543; V16 3.007290), so adding them
does not change any within-basin comparison.

V16 wider fits land 16.305 km and 17.844 km from center. Fitted objective gains
92.192, but error grows 3866.358 m. Zero-c has a further 252.150 lower objective
than fitted-c while error is another 1431.965 m worse. This is evidence that
local disk expansion alone cannot repair the V16 inference. It is not proof
of a particular physical-model defect or of global optimality.

Extra fixed-disk budget leaves both fitted objectives unchanged to roundoff.
All fixed-disk rows fail the independent stationarity threshold; V16 zero-c
even reports optimizer-success while the KKT audit reports not converged.
At fixed-disk machine precision, stationarity can vary without material score
or position change. A larger wall budget alone is not a supported remedy.

Limits and next bounded tests
These are local diagnostics at two frozen candidate/baseline banks, not a
complete all-basin selection rerun. The published V16 zero-c winner from
point:225:25 (305.643 km error) is not retested here. Warm-starting zero-c from
the saved fitted solution changes its initialization relative to that original
published arm, but both arms here have the same controlled start and budget.
No inference winner was chosen using reference error. There is no independent
held-out localization validation. Each 25 km result is stationary locally,
not demonstrated globally optimal.

Next test a bounded, truth-free expansion policy only when local disk is
active: preserve the association center, expand to 25 km, rerun both arms with
matched starts and budgets, and include converged receipts in all-basin
selection. Report domain changes explicitly. Audit V16 timing/association
and scoring against independent observations before claiming a fix; its score
improvement demonstrably trades against accuracy here.
