WHAT WAS APPLIED
Frozen Oct5 empirical cone fit from20 earlier captures, with primary elliptical
half-opening angles30.339/21.552deg, receiver-specific rolls and fitted soft edge.
Nominal axes are RX0west(270deg azimuth,70deg elevation) and RX1east(90/70).
These are conditional receiver-presence models, not measured absolute beam gains.

For each observed receiver/window, the cone marginal supplies a positive prior
multiplier (.1+.9*marginal)^(strength*support). Satellite priors are renormalized
to preserve the original total detection mass. Unsupported empirical sky is
neutral. All observation rows and satellites remain in the model. These are
SOFT priors: outside-cone and opposite-side candidates are not hard excluded.
No peer non-detections are invented. Cone parameters were not refit to these
failures. The cone changes geometry-dependent assignment priors; all V16
frequency/timing settings, frozen receiver calibration and bank choices remain.

This distinction matters: a strong frequency match can retain responsibility
outside a soft cone. The result does not test a hard cone-membership constraint.

WHAT EACH MARK MEANS
opposite_east_west: RX0 observation assigned to a direction with east>0, or
RX1 assigned to east<0. High directions can overlap cones; this is not proof of
impossible reception. For these symmetric axes it also means nearer the other
receiver's nominal axis.
outside_empirical_ellipse: direction lies outside the frozen cone boundary,
accounting for different major/minor openings and each receiver's axial roll.
behind_nominal_boresight: angular separation from nominal axis exceeds90deg.
below_horizon: local ENU up component is negative; independent of RX boresight.
