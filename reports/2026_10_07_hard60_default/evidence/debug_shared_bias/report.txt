Limitations and next step
These are conditional influence fits, not independent holdouts: all rows informed
the frozen calibration, selected satellite bank, basin and archived starts.
Association responsibilities are recomputed, but upstream selection leakage is
retained. No full global search or V16 repetition was performed. The late-half
free-c optimizer minimum is 6.8763 objective units ABOVE its zero-c minimum, even
though the c0 solution is feasible for free-c. That is a real nested-arm search
gap, so the free-c table row must not be treated as a global minimum. All attempts
are retained; none was selected by roof distance. The finite-start comparison
does not establish statistically significant receiver or channel defects.

Next practical test: inspect time/channel/satellite residual and assignment
stability around the predeclared midpoint, then rerun a bounded chronological
cross-fit with calibration/association trained exclusively on the opposite time
block, both matched c arms, and the zero-c incumbent explicitly admitted as a
feasible free-c envelope. Report held-block predictive frequency score and position
stability separately. Do not deploy early-row deletion based on this roof result.
