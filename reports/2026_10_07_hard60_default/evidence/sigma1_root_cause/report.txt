The earlier sigma sweep continued each arm's best ORIGINAL .15s candidate,
fixed across all options. At sigma1, an associated candidate became preferable
but was only repriced, leaving a large gradient. We now refined both original
associated and zero-timing starts separately within each arm, across all six
original final basins. The resulting best fits are stationary and about7.06km

Simply completing that missing optimization does not cure the bias.

A concrete mechanism was tested and did not explain the bias. The winning point
is near its calibration grid point, suggesting the frozen receiver correction
might absorb geographic information. We removed only the nonlinear correction,
retained the affine baseline, and adjusted the own-arm offset/slope seeds to
preserve their affine prediction contribution. Matched ON/OFF fits, both arms,
same bank/domain/budget all converge. OFF increases errors by132-136m and worsens
data fit slightly. This does not support nonlinear receiver-baseline anchoring
as the main cause in this bank/local solution. It does not test upstream effects
of calibration on bank selection. The free affine receiver terms absorb the
retained affine baseline, so it is not a hidden fixed affine position constraint.

Remaining leads are strained satellite assignments and joint RF/time geometry.
Satellite61946 has the largest position-gradient contribution;56289 has strong
support with an opposing contribution. Some fitted relative timing shifts remain
many seconds. These are targets for inspecting cross-channel/RX Doppler tracks
and identity consistency, not established incorrect satellite identities. The
next attribution test should balance RF/time coverage and isolate association
evidence. Deleting observations because they improve reference error is not a
validated remedy.

for fitted c and8.609s for zero c. Errors remain43.660km/304.224km. The fitted
endpoint has outward spatial descent blocked by its local disk, and tangential
derivative is essentially zero. Therefore the prior KKT failure was budget
truncation; fixing convergence does not fix search coverage or location.

Expanded fitted-c selection comes from the already-retained near-T1AT bank.
It improves score to40700.091 and position error to29.197km. That endpoint is
11.685km from its center, INSIDE the original12.5km disk. Expansion therefore
also changes the nonconvex optimization path; its benefit here is not simply
admitting that endpoint. The original near-bank solution lost final ranking
because its score was42376.109. The bank itself was never missing.

The expanded near-bank25km disk DOES contain the known receiver. A separate
fixed-position diagnostic found a feasible reference candidate with score
40574.860, beating the expanded selected40700.091 by125.231. This reference
candidate is not stationary, but feasibility and its lower score suffice to
demonstrate that the selected fit is not globally optimal over this domain.
It is not a usable truth-free localization result. Other wrong-position fixed
profiles also find lower feasible scores, so this does not establish that the
globally optimized model would select the reference position. All12 boundary
fixed-position oracle selections fail the8s stationarity audit; no profile
global-optimality or physical-model verdict is claimed from them.

Practical search work supported by evidence: allow score/constraint-driven
expansion from active local boundaries while retaining incumbents; refine both
timing starts; and give nonstationary candidates enough bounded continuation
before ranking them as completed fits. These measures require matched-cohort
evaluation. Wider domains alone already failed to produce an accurate position
here, and c=0 remains roughly303km wrong.
