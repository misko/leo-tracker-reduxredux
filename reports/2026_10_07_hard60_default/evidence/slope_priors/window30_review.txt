Conclusion
----------
Window30 is a reproducible diagnostic candidate, with no selected hard-bound
pileup. Its differences from control are driven by finite local-search paths
among interior solutions. The earlier 1939 failure and guard2109 errors remain.
These six retrospectively selected captures do not justify choosing or
deploying a production slope prior. Further evaluation should preserve all
matched c ablations, input/seed provenance, and width-nesting failure flags.
