Predeclared options:

Frequency sigma remains 125 Hz, common timing sigma 3 seconds, detection budget

All original final candidate regions are retained: 6, 6, 4, and 6 respectively
for 18:38, 19:39, 21:39, and 22:09, with original calibrated baselines, satellite
sets, and local disks. Every original associated and zero-timing solution is
repriced under every option and remains a selectable candidate if feasible.
There is no choice of regions by reference-position accuracy.

Each option continues the best ORIGINAL fit of its OWN c arm within every region.
That seed is fixed across the six options by its original objective score.
No solution is transferred between c arms, between options, or between captures.
This is a conditional continuation experiment, not a fresh end-to-end rerun:
its c controls inherit the original matched searches and shared fitted-c upstream
calibration/association. It does not expand spatial discovery or rerun calibration.

Each region/arm/option receives a 12-second continuation cap and at most300
continuous optimizer iterations. For mode-search options, timing evaluations
consume that same cap, leaving less time for continuous refinement. The timing
sweep keeps every window and satellite, tries resetting one absolute orbit shift
to the mean of the others, and accepts only decreases of the complete unchanged
score for that option. It stops at the shared deadline if necessary. Controls
can spend their whole cap on continuous optimization. All original incumbents
remain available, so a timed-out continuation cannot erase earlier evidence.
There are264 arm/region/option continuations in total; nominal aggregate cap
52.8 optimizer minutes, distributed over four single-thread processes.

Selections use each option's own total score. Truth is used only after every
selection, for great-circle position error. Fixed-c convergence is never copied
to free-c. All candidates, including repriced old states, receive an independent
KKT/feasibility audit under the actual option. Boundary, time-limit, and unresolved
nested-model score gaps remain reported. No zero-c candidate envelope is used.

Compare position accuracy against BOTH the published original and the equally
