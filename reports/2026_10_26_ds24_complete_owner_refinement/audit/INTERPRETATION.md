# What the complete-owner comparison can establish

The intervention changes the continuous maximum inside the original detector
frequency cell. It preserves the requested candidate, epoch, window timestamp,
native observation ID and fixed graph/symbol aliases. The same corrected vector
enters both c arms. The position comparison therefore measures this estimator
transfer under fixed assignments and the inherited localization model.

A larger exact GLRT score is an optimization result under the existing pilot
model. Without true field CFO it does not establish a less biased physical
measurement. The target and rolled control are maximized separately; the
exact-minus-control margin need not increase. The present numerical guards
certify local calculation consistency, not calibrated frequency uncertainty.

Likewise, scatter around a fixed low-degree polynomial is descriptive. The
polynomial can absorb part of the physical Doppler curve and smooth nuisance
error. Correcting a discrete estimator can increase that scatter, or lower it
while leaving the component aligned with the position derivative unchanged.
The earlier smooth-bias susceptibility is not a conversion from CFO RMS to
geographic improvement. A saved-matrix response to the actual signed correction
can help explain a result, but remains a local approximation with fixed nuisance
precisions. The actual matched nonlinear original/refined solves are primary.

The original fits must reproduce the saved parents before interpreting the
intervention. Fixed curved and predictive likelihood membership must remain
exact; endpoint-dependent postfit compatibility may change and must be reported.
Original residual, owner, phase, convergence and accepted-ID guards remain
necessary qualification checks. Passing them does not validate every physical
model assumption, while a failed guard alone does not identify the physical
cause. Raw diagnostics, paired research qualification and the operational policy
must remain separate. The operational reference is unchanged in this wave.

These four owners in two RF-selected development scans test transfer, not a
cohort error rate or independent generalization. Any useful position change
would justify a later frozen block with complete coverage and matched c arms;
it would not establish that finite-bin bias is the dominant remaining error.
The estimator itself is prior work, as documented in
[the attribution correction](PRIOR_ART_CORRECTION.md).
