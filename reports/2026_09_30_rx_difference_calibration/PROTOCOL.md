# Cross-receiver difference scale calibration

Test whether paired RX disagreement predicts later paired disagreement, without subtracting a shared candidate slope from geographic observations. Use existing strong-candidate residual cases from the nine DS7–9 eight-scan panels as calibration donors and the frozen DS10 N24 cases as target. Candidate identity is compared only within each scan. This is exposed-data transfer, not blind validation.

Pair RX0/RX1 tracks on identical scan, RF and candidate. Match observation times by mutual nearest neighbor within 1 microsecond (timestamp inspection before this protocol showed most simultaneous RX observations differ at sub-microsecond precision). Keep only both-training or both-held pairs; mixed-mask pairs are excluded. Require >=8 training pairs spanning >=5 seconds and >=3 held pairs. All qualifying track pairs remain; possible repeated source observations and reciprocal dependence are not treated as independent trials.

For each track pair, subtract its training median RX0-minus-RX1 residual. Define a training scale feature as max(1 Hz, 1.4826 times training median absolute centered difference). Predict held centered differences with a zero-centered Student-t4 density: constant scale versus log(scale)=a+b log(feature). Fit donor parameters using equal dataset, equal scan weights within each donor dataset, then equal observation weights within scan. Use L-BFGS-B, a in [−5,10], b in [−2,2], maxiter=200, ftol=1e−12, gtol=1e−7. Require optimizer success, interior parameters, max gradient <=1e−5, and finite-difference gradient discrepancy <=1e−5.

DS10 does not fit calibration parameters. Report equal-target-scan mean log-score difference, coverage, and per-scan signs. No geographic fit or individual-RX variance interpretation: differences can cancel shared errors, include association error, and need not measure absolute CFO uncertainty. Three tests cover shared-signal cancellation, held-value isolation and analytic gradient agreement. Cap execution at 180 seconds, preserve all failures and do not tune thresholds after results.
