# Association bridge required after receiver-response validation

The four-state fit addresses a necessary receiver-response question. Even if O
beats U and every signed-feature control, its latent nominee weights were updated
from these binary detector responses, not independently supported satellite
frequencies. A successful fit alone does not finish the receiver-geometry
association objective.

If the response passes the predeclared held controls, freeze its parameters and
preprocessing before a second experiment. Use an early direction-update segment
and a later frequency-scoring segment separated by the existing grouped-window
boundaries. Compare frozen orbit-increment predictive frequency densities with
and without the early receiver-response update to nominee weights. Use a
frequency-only causal baseline, explicit absent and omitted states, unchanged
candidate sets, priors, CFO and alias authority. Score every outcome before its
update; never use a later frequency to select the early segment or its geometry.

Receiver-state outcomes are derived from the same detections that supply the
frequency sets. Their likelihood cannot simply be multiplied by an existing
candidate-set likelihood that already scores detection counts: that would count
the detection evidence twice. The bridge must either use nonoverlapping update
and score segments with a well-defined conditional frequency score, or derive
one coherent joint likelihood with a single count component. Freeze this choice
and prove normalization before launching it.

Evaluate candidate permutation alongside receiver swap and signed reversal. A
gain shared by all candidates can improve presence prediction but cannot supply
identity discrimination. Preserve the full recording population and report
geometric disagreement as a descriptive covariate, not an outcome-selected gate.

If the four-state model fails held signed-feature controls, diagnose temporal
transfer and sensitivity calibration using the current calibration evidence.
Do not spend a new confirmation panel on a model that has already failed its
development gate. Physical cable mapping and world orientation remain unresolved
until supported by independent authority or a predeclared sign nuisance tested
on unused records.
