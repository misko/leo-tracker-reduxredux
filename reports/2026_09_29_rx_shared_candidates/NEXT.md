but most pairs already agree strongly. Do not assume that this will change
geographic estimates materially. First test held frequency prediction at the
same original q020 positions and training timings, retaining all eighteen panels.

Use the frozen one-to-one training-selected pairs. Each observation must appear
once. Preserve unpaired tracks and both receivers' signal/background alternatives.

of independent and shared candidate assignment. Shared identity means the same
catalogue row under a verified common snapshot, not two separately chosen best
satellites. Define the prior over retained candidates explicitly; avoid inserting
the measured posterior agreement mass as if it were independent new evidence.

contrast likelihood. It then tests identity coupling, not frequency stitching
or measured common receiver calibration. State the cross-RX noise assumption
explicitly; a product likelihood does not establish independent physical errors.
Define behavior for empty common visible support before scoring. Never silently
drop a pair or renormalize an impossible hypothesis without accounting for its
prior mass. Keep q020 background handling consistent across alternatives.

densities, receiver-swap symmetry, held isolation, correct/incorrect synthetic

strengths and control pairings before execution. Use the same shuffle control
where available with explicit matched denominators. No held result chooses a
candidate bank, coupling strength or pair membership within this experiment.

coupling. Report all declared alternatives, coverage and failures. No model in
