# Conditional next scientific decision

No new numerical experiment or correlation parameter is selected here. The
iteration 136/138 score is a derivative at independence at already-fitted
endpoints, not a calibrated covariance estimate. Its sign cannot establish
position improvement.

If scores are small, mixed across the predefined blocks, or confined to c=0,
stop this covariance branch. A single positive pooled sum can be dominated by
one recording, many pairs from one group, or a few residual products. The
report's recording, group, block and shared-label-mass summaries are needed
before interpreting that sum. Unsupported or overlapping acquisitions remain
unpaired and do not provide evidence for independent measurement replication.

If positive scores recur in both arms and both blocks across recordings, the
lean next step is a separately frozen mean-versus-covariance diagnostic. On the
same disjoint acquisition pairs, report responsibility-weighted first and second
residual means alongside their product for each predetermined group/block.
Cross-block mean-product predictions can show whether a persistent conditional
mean explains the positive product. This is a diagnostic decomposition only:
soft assignment and parameters fitted to the full recording prevent an unbiased
hardware covariance interpretation. It would require at most the same two
endpoint calls per member, with no position optimizer or rho sweep. It is not
authorized or frozen by this note.

If a mean explanation dominates, improving the physical mean model is more
appropriate than discounting observations. If unexplained within-pair dependence
remains consistent, first validate the normalized correlated-mixture kernel on
synthetic data, including an exact independent limit and circular winding
behavior. Only then propose a separately reviewed, globally fixed matched-c
position trial. Do not infer a rho value or choose members from these scores.

This sequence differs from the negative [density weighting experiment](../2026_10_08_position_error_iter17/README.md)
and [label-persistence pilot](../2026_10_09_position_error_iter110/DECISION.md):
the proposed mechanism is conditional emission covariance with unchanged
singleton-event probability, as derived in [the frozen audit plan](../2026_10_10_position_error_iter136/PLAN.md).
The [receiver-contrast analysis](../2026_10_09_position_error_iter90/DECISION.md)
already showed that existing clock terms can explain apparent satellite-specific
structure. A covariance extension must survive that warning rather than simply
relabeling model bias as noise. This consumed twelve-member panel provides no
held-out validation and does not replace the standalone position-error goal.
