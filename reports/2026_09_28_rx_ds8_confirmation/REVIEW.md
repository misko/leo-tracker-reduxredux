## Outcome audit

The completed score passed `audit_results.py`; the bound result is recorded in
`audit-results.json`. All four frozen readiness recordings are eligible, with two exact RF lanes
per recording. The audit reconstructed every exported role total from the individual window rows,
verified the common causal reference across both fit families and every control, and reproduced all
102 equal-record summaries and sign counts. It also verified the result's direct input hashes and
every source hash in the exclusive score launch receipt.

Unique opportunity accounting is explicit. The partition contains 3,551 reception plus
held-frequency source windows. Exactly 1,767 belong to the eight selected geometry lane identities
and appear once each in the dataset; the remaining 1,784 have other session/channel/edge/RF lane
identities. Comparing both receiver RF values found zero missing windows within a selected lane and
zero included windows outside one. Per-lane `identity_or_timestamp_mismatch` counters overlap and
must not be added as if they were unique exclusions.

The primary held-period result does not support promoting tilt geometry. Uniform-trained and
causal-trained T score below the common causal reference by 0.000736751 and 0.004776337 nats per
window respectively, with only one positive recording in each family. T beats D on all four, but
the receiver-swap contrast is positive on only three of four uniform-trained records and two of
four causal-trained records. The much larger causal-reference gain over uniform phase density,
8.776343 nats per window on all four records, is frequency-continuity evidence and cannot be
attributed to receiver tilt or satellite identity. The README headline, tables, denominators and
interpretive limits agree with the audited JSON.
