# Coherent expansion readiness, before natural timing outcomes

The sparse panel has four native target times per channel/RX, which cannot
create an eight-point original graph track by itself. A positive raw screen
therefore has two useful downstream paths: augment a nearby existing dense
track, or nominate a separately frozen bounded consecutive-visit expansion.
Neither path can choose a satellite from an old localization outcome.

First stage the confirmed later-sample CFOs and rerun original graph_rf_lanes
on all original rows plus new owners. Count new curved, graph-only and unresolved
owners. Report changes in old memberships and radio-noise proxy weights. A
confirmed pilot that follows a far FFT winner remains discovery-only unless it
also passes the frozen earlier-frequency width. It can still be tested against
the full causal catalogue if distinct-time coherence develops.

For a second round, root should freeze before additional raw scoring: a small
RF-only survivor nomination rule, the maximum lane/seed count, consecutive-visit
time span and target cap, history/mode policy, unchanged timing/confirmation
rules, matched controls and per-case computation budget. Preserve exact donor
and new sample ownership across the overlapping blocks. Root approval of that
dependency is already within the user's task; no extra human permission is
needed for existing-recording reanalysis.

Twenty-second raw-curvature span is the original minimum, but an expansion need
not supply every new point: it can extend an existing well-sampled curve. A new
standalone curve also requires original minimum point count, residual/quality
and causal-association gates. Do not lower them after seeing failures. If the
survivors stay isolated or an alias/identity cannot be resolved, report that
specific information bottleneck. Passing more pilot gates alone does not imply
smaller regional position error.

Only after this stage passes should matched bounded baseline/candidate c0 and
fitted-c regional searches run. Full catalogue and ephemeris bank time/phase
support must be audited; changed observations never inherit an accepted satellite
identity. Geographic error is opened only after sealing those inference outputs.
