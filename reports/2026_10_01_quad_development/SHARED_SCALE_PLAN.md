# Conditional satellite-group residual scale

Hypothesis: tracks assigned to the same satellite within one scan share residual reliability that independent track Student-t scales do not capture. Keep every observation and all original physical branch priors. Freeze baseline identities and group membership (scan,NORAD); background-assigned tracks retain their original fixed background branches. This is a conditional warm model pilot, not dynamic association or a cold algorithm.

For each signal group concatenate residuals and Jacobians, with block-diagonal original covariance C. A single Gamma(2,2) latent precision shared by the group gives the normalized multivariate Student-t4 density of this concatenation. Replace only the sum of per-track residual log densities in the original selected physical scores with that group density. Constants and branch priors remain. Singleton groups must give exactly the original physical score. The per-group weight is (4+D)/(4+Q). Degrees of freedom remain4 without tuning. This adds dependence in scale, not cross-track linear covariance; a bad track can reduce the weight of otherwise useful tracks.

A research composite port can expose a single allowed signal branch to the existing optimizer, returning concatenated observations/predictions and the complete corrected physical score. All alternative branches are impossible. Physical observation IDs must remain unique. This is legitimate only with fixed group identities; do not claim independent scoring for dynamically regrouped associations.

Prerequisites: synthetic normalized-density equivalence to direct block construction, exact singleton preservation, physical prior preservation, analytic group-gradient versus finite differences, and real fixed-state singleton/objective/gradient checks on first single eachDS. Hold the covariance fixed as in the original predictor and preserve global support checks. Seal all sources and real inputs. Each real prerequisite has a90-second cap, shared lock and single-thread execution. No geographic scoring at this stage.

After prerequisites pass, a separate versioned runner may warm-fit the shared-scale arm and an original per-track fixed-association control on the first single of DS9/DS10/DS11, with64updates,60seconds fitting and90seconds external including audit. Audit convergence/objective/gradient/stationarity before separate geographic evaluation. Proceed to predefined first pairs/quads only if all three pilots are numerically accepted, median paired geographic change is negative, and none worsens by more than1m. Otherwise stop expansion of this fixed nu4 model. This strict small-pilot gate is a development decision, not a significance test. No production promotion or replacement of prior results.
