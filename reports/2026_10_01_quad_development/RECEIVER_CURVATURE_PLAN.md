# Conditional prediction test for receiver-shared curvature

Use the accepted optimized-acquisition first single from DS9, DS10 and DS11. Freeze those positions, satellite assignments, clock/epoch/drift values, retained eight-point observations and covariance matrices. This is an exposed, conditional residual diagnostic: the baseline fit already used all observations. Leaving groups out when fitting the new residual correction does not turn it into independent localization validation.

The existing mean includes receiver-specific linear drift, multiplied by reference_frequency / track_rf_frequency and projected through the track's Helmert contrasts. Verify the new linear design against the existing prediction Jacobian. Form one common scan time u=(t-center)/scale, where center and scale are the midpoint and half-span of the selected signal observation times. Do not recenter quadratic time separately in each track: that would change the shared model.

Compare three conditional residual means: zero correction; an additional linear correction per receiver; and an additional linear plus quadratic correction per receiver. The two basis functions are B[u*I(receiver=r)]*RFscale and B[u^2*I(receiver=r)]*RFscale. Constants disappear through the contrasts. Include linear terms in the quadratic arm to control for leftover linear drift or shrinkage; do not attribute a gain over zero alone to curvature.

For every fitted NORAD identity, withhold all its signal tracks across both receivers. Fit correction coefficients only on the other identities using the original multivariate Student-t4 residual objective and fixed per-track covariance. Use zero initialization, at most 64 IRLS updates, no coefficient prior or width tuning, and a positive-curvature scaled-gradient stopping check. Require full design rank; record nonconvergence or rank failures without replacing cases. Background-assigned tracks have no selected-satellite residual and are counted explicitly outside this diagnostic.

Evaluate the held group's plug-in Student-t log score under all three corrections. No held residual enters coefficient fitting. Report pooled gain per contrast coordinate, median group gain, number of improving groups, coefficient stability and coverage. These are conditional plug-in scores: uncertainty in the new coefficients, the original fitted state and identities is not integrated. No geography or reference error is accessed.

Use a 90-second external cap per scan and the shared lock; no localization fits or RF collection. Tests cover common-time basis behavior, exact linear-Jacobian correspondence on real ports, robust fitting on synthetic residuals and whole-identity exclusion across receivers. Seal sources and inputs before reporting.

Proceed toward a separate localization-model pilot only if all folds are valid and the quadratic arm beats the linear control in both pooled and median held-group score on each of the three scans. Otherwise retain the baseline and diagnose the mixed/negative evidence; do not tune a width against geography. Even a passing diagnostic would require an explicit coefficient prior, identifiability checks, exact zero-term equivalence and derivative tests before a bounded refit pilot. It would not identify a physical oscillator fault.
