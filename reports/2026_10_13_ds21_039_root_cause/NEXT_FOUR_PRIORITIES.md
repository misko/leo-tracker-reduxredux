# DS21-039: next four investigation priorities

The target remains below 0.5 km on every eligible CH1–8 DS21 recording. It is not achieved. DS21-039's original joint-model errors were 3.759 km for CH1–4, 2.557 km for CH5–8 and 4.291 km for CH1–8. Seven stationary warm continuations improved the training-selected all-band result to 4.073 km. This demonstrates an optimization deficit but leaves most of the position error unresolved. See the [existing investigation record](README.md).

These priorities update the original four directions using the completed negative controls. They do not authorize reference-derived seeds, per-case model selection, or changes to frozen experiments. New science retains fitted `c` only.

The first band-function investigation is now complete. Its [capacity diagnostic](../2026_10_13_ds21_039_association/BAND_FUNCTION_CAPACITY_RESULTS.md) confirms a 13.798 Hz RMS representation loss; the [matched four-fit control](../2026_10_13_ds21_039_association/NATIVE_BAND_CALIBRATION_RESULTS.md) adds the missing three dimensions and improves training/held likelihood, but worsens position from 4.072515 to 4.186817 km. All four fits are stationary. This is not a promoted localization fix. Execution priority now shifts to timing/calibration ambiguity, assignment attribution and coverage; further calibration extensions need new evidence. The original four workstream identifiers below remain stable.

The [saved-state timing/calibration factorial](../2026_10_13_ds21_039_association/TIMING_CALIBRATION_FACTORIAL_RESULTS.md) now supplies the first combined test for directions 2 and 3. It completed 18 evaluations and zero fits in 18.760 seconds: two endpoint replays and four nuisance combinations at each of four saved 1 km displaced positions. Timing removes roughly 7,038–8,051 NLL units relative to geography-only states, versus 30–307 for calibration alone; positive interactions of 24–273 prevent adding those gains. Fully adjusted states retain their endpoint visibility and sampling integers, with only 15–29 satellite/clutter MAP changes among 2,798 windows. Receiver and band contributions change sign across directions. This prioritizes timing–position identifiability and attribution of the remaining small endpoint differences; it does not identify a universal timing fault, justify tighter priors, or produce a new position estimate. Future timing interventions require independent physical evidence and synthetic recovery rather than reference-error tuning.

For direction 4, the separately qualified legacy adapter has completed the [DS21-039 discovery coverage transfer](../2026_10_13_ds21_039_association/DISCOVERY_COVERAGE_039_RESULTS.md): 100 points, 300 fits, 296 stationary, in 337.358 seconds. All 81 fitted states at 27 shared points reproduce the historical values exactly. The broader grid's high/all minimum at (-106.25, -93.75) km is only 8.839 km from the low winner at (-112.5, -87.5) km. The original low-first selector therefore drops it under the unchanged 12.5 km separation rule; the distant second proposal is a fallback rather than the high-band minimum. This provides a training-objective rationale for a matched all-first selection control, not reference-derived seeds or a new grid budget. The selected discovery-point errors are 26.283 / 306.219 / 38.433 km; these are not final A/B/C estimates. Downstream initialization needs a separately qualified authority adapter, and no new localization fix is promoted.

The [first DS21-025 baseline](../2026_10_13_ds21_release_readiness/THREE_MODEL_025_RESULTS.md) is also complete: 54 calls, 52 stationary, all-band errors 3.297 / 8.315 / 1.122 km for A/B/C. Generic reweighting is lower priority: equal-band, equal-block and combined weighting did not resolve DS21-039; quarter-likelihood weighting also failed the five-case transfer. A receiver-specific RF contrast slightly worsened accuracy. Those results argue against repeating broad sweeps without new evidence.

## Full DS21 acceptance sequence

1. Diagnose DS21-039 and test only evidence-supported changes on the existing development cases. Include matched CH1–4, CH5–8 and CH1–8 comparisons; explicitly identify half-band results that inherit all-band initialization.
2. Evaluate the selected candidate on all 34 fast CH1–8 development recordings. Preserve failures and report per-recording error, median, upper quantiles, maximum and the count below 0.5 km. A lower frequency residual alone is insufficient.
3. Freeze one algorithm and its selection rule before evaluating the 20 locked fast recordings. Reference coordinates enter after fitting and selection are sealed. No choosing whichever model is closest to the reference for each recording.
4. Publish all 54 eligible fast recordings, with failures counted as misses. The 54 adaptive CH1–4 recordings receive a separate coverage report; they cannot establish CH1–8 performance. Disclose any reuse of locked cases after a failed release.

Accuracy currently means distance to the configured reference, which is not independently surveyed ground truth. The 0.5 km requirement remains a release gate rather than a guaranteed outcome. No new RF collection is required for this plan.
