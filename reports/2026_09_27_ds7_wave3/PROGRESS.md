# DS7 sub-kilometre goal: current progress

The full DS7 goal remains active and unachieved. The final chronological group
now has a qualified **443.02 m** result with the predeclared equal-position mean.
The same method scores **1,930.07 m** on the first group. See the authoritative
[wave-3 results](RESULTS.md) for the matched comparison and limitations.

Sixteen independent recordings and two complete groups have been evaluated.
Only two of those individual recordings are below 1 km. The remaining 72
recordings have not yet been prepared for this estimator, and no full88 pooled
estimate has run. The 443 m group must not substitute for missing coverage.

SOL performed the residual audit, final-group fits, combined-export equivalence
test and synthetic clock recovery. Terra prepared and audited cached inputs;
the coordinator completed the bounded final preparation stages, verified
provenance and scored sealed predictions. No new RF or raw IQ was used.

The frequency-unit audit ruled out a proposed scaling defect. The residual
audit exposes model mismatch and inconsistent receiver/channel slopes. The
synthetic nonlinear clock test passes its numerical recovery tolerances, but
does not provide independent hardware calibration or a real-data positioning
improvement. Its outcome is a prerequisite result, not model admission.

Next work is to expand the frozen estimator and averaging controls toward the
full corpus, using the exactly equivalent combined preparation path where
appropriate. Keep bounded leases, complete member accounting, every failure,
and inference/reference separation. Any new physical-state model needs its own
predeclared real-data admission and evaluation specification.
