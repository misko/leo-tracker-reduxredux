**Three scientific priorities, not another numerical campaign**

The strongest established limitation remains weak separation of position from
orbital timing/shape and shared receiver terms, conditional on assumed labels.
The local phase/weak-position correlation .935 and known-nuisance scales
534/110/56 m diagnose ambiguity; they are not measured accuracy floors.
All DS24 channel-control cases use 10 MS/s, and nominal feed separation supplies
no measured bearing, so neither sample rate nor triangulation is identified as
the cause. [Information evidence](/srv/bulk/leo/research/ds24-bottlenecks-20261024/information/REPORT.md).

1. **Resolve an actual identity/whole-source challenge.** The real 0011/0012
   candidate competitions are close; historical 0013 corroboration used only
   RX1 sources selected under the same archived label. The current three-owner
   RX2 test is therefore a useful next decision, with all failures and all 124
   native IDs retained. Its result is not assumed here. If warranted, use a
   separately frozen discovery-conditioned comparison with D physical under
   the same candidate in both the same-family and W-unresolved models; see
   [the exact conditional design](CONDITIONAL_DESIGN.md). Shared radio/orbit
   covariance and complete owners matter more than counting repeated labels.
   Stop at ambiguous competitors, incompatible source structure or unresolved
   numerical bounds. Ordinary top8 omission is preserved; do not force a
   favorite identity back in or turn finite-pool ordering into acceptance.
   [Real identity evidence](/srv/bulk/leo/research/ds24-identity-components-r01/identity/FINDINGS.md).

2. **Add a different constraint, not merely more points.** On the identical
   26-owner pool, information ranking preferred a shorter source in three of
   four scans, but implied weak-axis SD gains on 0010-0012 were only about
   5-21 m; the larger 0013 result depended on unqualified 60108. First challenge
   identities, then compare information/count allocation under matched budgets.
   A genuinely independent clock/timing, pilot or orbit reference would be
   stronger still, but its units, capture provenance and observable sensitivity
   must exist before processing. Existing CFO differences cannot be added again
   as independent data, and frame periodicity alone is not absolute transmit
   time. Stop if sources duplicate the same uncertain family/direction or the
   supposed new observable has no identifiable reference.
   [Allocation evidence](/srv/bulk/leo/research/ds24-nomination-information-r01/information/FINDINGS.md).

3. **Test one remaining active-parent coupling under a known identity.** The
   generating factorial implicates both timing and shape, including B(phi)'s
   observation-level interaction. Blanket parent covariance retention gave zero
   qualified pairs and raw mean regressions of 8.4/9.9 m. A distinct small test
   is a coherent nonlinear active-parent phase/B(phi) reference at fixed RF
   states, with the same observations, labels, priors and noise treatment as
   current inference. Begin with score/gradient closure and a fixed runtime cap;
   stop at negligible stable effect or unresolved integration. The earlier
   parent phase slice already found at most 22.8 m predicted movement with
   Gamma and B fixed, so repeating that slice is not new evidence. Do not
   narrow priors or replace a failing gate to manufacture a gain.
   [Factorial and later receiver validation](/srv/bulk/leo/research/ds24-phase-shape-validation-r01/README.md),
   [parent covariance result](/srv/bulk/leo/research/ds24-parent-orbit-uncertainty-r01/README.md),
   [earlier integration scope](/srv/bulk/leo/research/ds24-next-directions-review-r01/methods/FINDINGS.md).

Generic restarts, unchanged receiver bases, indiscriminate coverage expansion,
repeated easy rank1 synthetic truths and blanket cross-family covariance have
weak priority. Cross-satellite means and geometry dependence already have mixed
or negative cohort results. Numerical support repair enables these questions;
it is not the main scientific explanation or a demonstrated position gain.
Keep c0/fitted comparisons, failed attempts and RF-versus-position outcomes
separate, and retain the operational reference until broader evidence supports
a change.
