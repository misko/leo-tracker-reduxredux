**A discovery-conditioned follow-on, if the first stage warrants it**

The current authorized test is three separate RX2 source comparisons. Each
whole source nominates ordinary causal-catalogue top8 from its own observations;
all competing identities and the proper unresolved comparator share that
source's eight-family cavity and the same fixed parent position. No witness is
selected because it favors 60108. The archived rejected labels and earlier
information rankings are exposure history, not candidate-retention rules.

A later corroboration question requires a different, explicitly frozen model.
Let D be the original complete discovery source, W one complete RX2 owner and E
the retained original parent evidence. Nominate candidates using D alone through
the ordinary catalogue screen, without forcing the historical label. Evaluate
all three frozen W separately; do not assume they share one satellite and do
not select the best witness retrospectively.

For candidate j compare two models:

- Same family: D and W share j's causal phase and full physical-mode prior,
  together with the original parent-radio covariance.
- Unresolved witness: D retains exactly the same physical j model; only W's
  physical curve is replaced by the proper polynomial comparator. Radio
  cross-covariance remains shared.

Each owner has its independent original 300 Hz constant. Preserve the finite
alias measure, target noise, source IDs and both c arms. Use the same candidate
union cavity, original parent phase references and nuisance posterior in every
numerator and denominator. A phase posterior conditioned on D is legitimate
predictive inference; it is not permission to refit parent receiver/noise
calibration using W.

Compute `log Z(D,W|E,j) - log Z(D|E,j)` under each model. Their D marginals are
identical, so the conditional model odds also equal the difference between
the two joint log evidences. Replacing D by an unresolved curve in the null
would change the discovery hypothesis and answer a different question.
Independent source scores cannot be multiplied: that loses shared phase,
physical modes and radio covariance. Scores of overlapping discovery/witness
folds cannot be summed as independent significance.

If joint log evidence is bounded by [L_DW,U_DW] and discovery evidence by
[L_D,U_D], the conditional log evidence is bounded by
`[L_DW-U_D, U_DW-L_D]`. Use bound separation for ordering; do not infer absolute
1e-3 precision from it. Common-denominator model odds should be bounded directly
from the joint evidence intervals to avoid unnecessary interval widening.

Stop at unresolved source ownership, causal support, parent numerical guards,
or overlapping numerical/model intervals. A different RX2 identity is a valid
outcome. These are reused development sources, and the original D was already
examined under a historical label. Even a strong conditional result would be
model consistency, not authenticated identity or a calibrated acceptance rate.
No follow-on calculation or position fit is authorized by this design note.
