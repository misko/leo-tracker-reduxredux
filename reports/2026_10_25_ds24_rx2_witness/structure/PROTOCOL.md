rejected-residual plots. CH3's final point and CH4's structure are therefore
historically selected hypotheses, not held-out validation. Keep all three
metadata-frozen RX2 owners and every native point. Do not edit source files,

unweighted least-squares Legendre polynomials of orders 1, 2, 3 and 4 on the
complete source, with time scaled to [-1,1]. This is descriptive structure
analysis, not a new physical candidate score. Freeze the same four orders for

residuals, SSE/RMS, QR hat leverage, exact linear-model leave-one-out residuals
and PRESS RMS. Verify the leverage formula against direct leave-one-out refits
for every point. No noise estimate, likelihood, significance threshold or
degrees-of-freedom claim is fitted to these selected residuals.

Report every native time gap and each point's leverage/leave-one-out change.
Identify the final chronological point and its immediate predecessor by ID,
timestamps and original export provenance. Compare complete-source curvature
with the corresponding descriptive leave-one-out quadratic coefficient for
every omitted point; no resulting reduced dataset is a scientific input.

four-order in-sample versus leave-one-out RMS for all three owners.
For CH4, report lag-one residual correlation, the ordered residual series and
largest adjacent residual jumps for all four orders. Such structure can be
consistent with a join or systematic error but cannot establish its cause.

these unweighted diagnostics deliberately make no calibrated noise claim.
