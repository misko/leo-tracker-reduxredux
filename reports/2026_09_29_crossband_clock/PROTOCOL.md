# Contemporaneous cross-band clock transfer

Frozen before execution. Reuse all nine target eight-scan residual exports and
the same 3,983 eligible cases from the contemporaneous exact-RF experiment.
Exclude target track and candidate number. Same scan, different RF, at least
50% overlap of the target training span, and five donor training samples spanning
five seconds inside that span. Require three distinct other candidate numbers.
Do not lower these requirements after seeing coverage or outcomes.

Two same-RX physical hypotheses: (1) common fractional frequency drift, which
has equal slope in the existing canonical 11.2-GHz units; (2) common additive
native-Hz drift, which multiplies each donor's canonical slope by donor RF /
target RF. Median slopes within candidate number, then median across numbers.
An opposite-RX, different-RF fractional predictor is a control. Zero slope is
the baseline. All arms use the target training mean residual and time, then
score target median absolute held residual. Unsupported arms fall back to zero
and remain in all-case accounting. Report supported and matched subsets apart.

The candidate assignments, positions and recording times are inherited training
fits. This is conditional residual prediction, not calibrated receiver clocks,
independent satellite identity, geographic accuracy or causal forecasting.
RF normalization alone does not justify either physical hypothesis. No new RF,
geographic fit, production change or threshold selection. Coverage histograms
must show whether the failure is lack of other bands, overlap or usable samples.
