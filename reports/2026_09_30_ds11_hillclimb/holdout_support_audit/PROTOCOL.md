Use every observation export from the original 32-scan plan, before bank
eligibility filtering. Verify declared digests, capture binding and consistent
whole-visit labels across all tracks/receivers. Count held rows sharing a track
with training data, held rows within that track's training-time span, and nearest
training-time gaps. These are measurement-row counts, not independent satellites.
No models, new masks, source identity assignments, geographic references or
position fits. Quantify the original split rather than relabeling its results.

Source inspection also distinguishes training-only candidate shortlist scoring
from track construction, which precedes the visit partition. This is a
conditional numerical prediction benchmark, not an end-to-end blind detection
validation. Do not call legitimate same-track held prediction a coding leak.
Do not claim that independence alone guarantees geographic model selection:
the completed source-holdout family selector already failed its screen gate.
