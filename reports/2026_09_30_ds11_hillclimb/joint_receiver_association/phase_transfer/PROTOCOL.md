# Independent acquisition-setting phase controls

The family2 frame-evolution audit found reversed orbital curvature but weak
source-specific transfer beyond a common-curvature control. Test the same
diagnostic on five remaining validated acquisition settings. Select the lowest
index available calibration and validation donor per family from the existing
inventory: family3 18/20, family4 7/11, family5 4/19, family6 0/10,
family7 5/14. Keep family2 as previously inspected exploratory evidence.

Reuse the exact immutable frame exporter and quadratic training-only fitting
functions. Median-pool track fragments and receiver copies by inferred NORAD.
Per family learn phase curvature = intercept + beta*orbital curvature solely on
the calibration recording, robust soft-L1 scale0.01us/s². Freeze that relation
and the calibration median-phase constant control. Score median absolute
curvature error on the distinct validation recording. Also compare all nonzero
cyclic shifts of source orbital curvatures, retaining the fitted coefficients.
This is a deterministic negative control, not an independent-trial p-value.

A family passes feasibility only with a qualified coefficient fit, negative
beta, at least five source groups in each recording, and at least5% lower
validation median error than both constant-clock control and the median cyclic
shift error. Report every family and failure. Passing supports a subsequent
position experiment, not a physical timing law or DS11 improvement. No DS11
positions or errors enter selection or fitting. No offline orbit updates.
