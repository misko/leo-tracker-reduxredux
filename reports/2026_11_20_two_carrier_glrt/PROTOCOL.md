# Testing two carriers per antenna visit

Freeze ten new visits per recording from DS26-0020, 0036, 0066, 0162, 0237 and DS29-0082, 0174, 0519. Rank physical observations by a salted hash and exclude both antennas of all 389 visits used in the earlier receiver study. Use the first antenna selected for each distinct visit. The selector uses neither a detection nor an orbital prediction. Compare with the stored benchmark and its seven satellite assignments separately. The recordings share a roof.

Use the fixed ordinary-band timing-first receiver for the first carrier. Fit one complex amplitude per frame and block of up to 64 known pilot symbols at the measured frequency and timing. Subtract that fitted pilot, then run the same receiver once on the residual. Require both reported candidates to pass the original fractional GLRT on the untouched antenna samples at the same signed frequency and time. Count a second candidate only if it differs by more than two timing samples or 1 kHz from the first, with circular timing. Allow the residual proposal to pass the original verifier independently even if the first proposal fails that verifier. The input is raw signed I/Q, the pilot edge and the recorded passband. Benchmark against the original GLRT and the single-carrier timing-first receiver on the same inputs. Record per-antenna CPU, sequential stereo wall time, phase-randomized controls, returned candidates and wrong signed carriers. The new eight-recording test uses the independently repeated strongest pilot in the ordinary band. Explicit two-signal and single-signal injections give known multiplicity. A second candidate on a real visit without an independent label has unresolved identity.

In the frozen v5 pipeline each antenna visit contributes its highest-margin candidate to tracking. Test the complete five DS26 recordings with that rule, including abstentions and solver failures. Also test a causal alternative that can choose a weaker candidate when earlier raw measurements on the same antenna and channel predict it. It may only use its own past hardware-clock observations. Preserve measured margins and compare both candidate policies on the same factor streams, geometry, catalogue and optimizer settings. Run the original zero and fitted calibration arms. Keep survey coordinates in scoring and report conditional frequency fit, fix availability, and position error separately.

For causal continuation, retain the 12 previous same-channel visits within eight seconds. A straight line from a measured candidate to an earlier one may have signed slope up to 8 kHz/s. Count distinct earlier visits within 800 Hz of the line over at least 0.4 seconds. Choose the lower-margin peak only if at least three earlier visits support it and it wins by at least two visits. A third, more selective policy also withholds a candidate until it has three supporting visits. All policies see the same prior independently verified measurements, including abstentions. These rules are fixed before scoring their position results.
