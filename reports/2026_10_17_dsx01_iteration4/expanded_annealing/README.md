# Expanded-bank annealing control

This experiment applies one declared optimization policy to all five sources. It combines the existing generic candidate expansion with fixed-width annealing, without changing the final 125 Hz likelihood, priors, calibration, candidate selection or association threshold. It is not a new noise model or a satellite-identity claim.

The immutable iteration2 screen receipts supply the same eight additions for both methods and both RF arms. The original causal regional bank is reconstructed and the existing bank-expansion adapter verifies common orbital arrays, physical calibration functions, fixed band contrast, timing transport and bounded-q parameterization. No new screen is run. Both original expanded endpoints are independently replayed against their saved objectives/domains, and the width125 model must reproduce objective, physical/nuisance gradients and posterior bit exactly before fitting.

The paired complete initial state is the saved expanded fitted-c profile; the c=0 seed changes only c. Each arm's own raw expanded profile remains eligible. A fresh 125 Hz continuation and the schedule1000→500→250→125 Hz each receive at most1000 additional objective calls and120 seconds. One incumbent score is included; annealing stage quotas are149/150/150/550. Broad scores never select endpoints. Raw endpoint selection uses original125 training J only; strict KKT<=.001 is separately reported. All upstream source-generation, screen, profile, preparation and qualification work is disclosed outside this extra continuation allowance.

One generic unchanged-model numerical finish may use remaining calls/time inside the unit's original deadline. Its raw parent remains saved, and all primary figures/aggregates exclude finishing rather than mixing numerical histories. Three new tests cover complete paired seeds, unchanged selection authority/candidate ordering and candidate-ID overlap across banks. Six reused tests cover width gradients/nesting/mask invariance and whole-budget finishing behavior. Ruff passes on the new modules.

S01 and S05 are the first execution checks, followed by S02/S03/S04 under the same frozen policy. There are twenty units total and two numerical workers. Every unit has the internal120-second limit; each four-unit case has external TERM615/KILL620 including preparation. All twenty outcomes must seal before position or diagnostic-window scoring. Existing observation arrays only; no reference seeds, window/candidate labels in inference, new RF, provider changes, production writes or historical result changes.

All twenty outcomes sealed before evaluation. `fit_*.json` retain raw and numerical-finish states separately. `evaluation.json`, `raw_fit_*.json` and `eval_*.npz` expose the uniform all-five raw policy. Their overlap reference is the same original published fitted-c state, evaluated under its original bank and compared by catalogue IDs. Numerical finishing has its own evaluation rows and NPZ files. All20084 observations, all693 diagnostic rows, all fifteen windows, intersection RMS and configured-reference distances remain visible, including regressions and nonstationary outcomes. Nothing is promoted.

The original motivation was to combine support gains from two generic strategies. That motivation did not enter inference or selection, and the result fails it: fitted-c window001 stays45/86, while014 declines32→29/39. Window003 remains empty. Model likelihood prefers trading support among curves; larger all-point association totals do not imply preservation of every diagnostic segment or correct emitter identity. Neither these results nor the earlier fixed-bank annealing are mixed per source/window to manufacture a better method. Raw J values are compared only within the same expanded bank/model, not ranked against fixed-bank J.

`comparison.png` and `.pdf` retain the full matched comparison. Numerical finishing remains in `finishing_evaluation.json` and `finished_eval_*.npz`, with all raw states unchanged. The useful result is a reproducible optimization improvement and fewer stationarity failures; the requested support-preserving and localization improvement is not achieved. No promotion or further automatic retry follows.
