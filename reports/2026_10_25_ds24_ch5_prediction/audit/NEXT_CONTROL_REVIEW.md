The completed results support closing the tested active-parent B(phi) mechanism
and keeping CH5/57450 unqualified. CH3 fails to corroborate; CH4 is unresolved by
budget. They do not prove CH5 false. Repeating the unchanged whole-owner
comparison or extending its precision campaign has poor priority. The broad
position/orbit ambiguity remains; conditional information is not achieved accuracy.

The new D-only prediction does supply a more specific ownership hypothesis:
CH4 follows it initially then departs, while CH3's isolated endpoint follows
it much better than its preceding body. This independently predicted pattern
makes a targeted native-ownership/GLRT-alternative trace more discriminating
than another generic orphan mixture or HMM. It is not itself proof of a join,
and the graph alias-zero view is a fixed coordinate, not a fitted favorable alias.

First compare a shared-D physical portion plus a proper unresolved remainder
against whole-source physical and piecewise-unresolved controls on **all**
original points. Marginalize a predeclared normalized split/allocation prior;
do not report an uncharged best cut. Keep D physical in every model, its shared
phase/radio covariance and the same alias measure. This retains a one-phase
integral before attempting an expensive two-independent-orbit model. Trace
ordinary causal competitors and independently coherent continuations before
claiming a two-satellite construction error. Stop if those continuations are
not supported, models remain ambiguous, or the bounded diagnostic is unresolved.
Even a favorable result does not authorize an accepted prefix, deleted remainder
or identity qualification. This concrete first task outranks the generic
matched-law control below.

The proposed common-law nomination control is useful **as a falsification of
the proposal/score pipeline**, not as a new real-world identity validation.
The previous synthetic nomination study generated from one full-parent
conditional background, then scored after a response-selected shortlist
cavity. That comparison was informative but did not isolate nomination loss
under the same generating and scoring law. Repeating easy rank-one physical
draws would not answer a new question.

Use at most two fixed layouts and a few predeclared physically confusable
causal labels plus the proper unresolved generator. Choose labels and seeds
before fresh CFO draws. Freeze exactly the same background mean/covariance for
generation and **every** scored identity/null, together with phase/shape priors,
finite alias measure and detection/visibility convention. Keep any
response-dependent cavity as a separately named sensitivity. If a catalogue
label has active parent orbit evidence but receives a fresh target orbit prior,
declare the control an exogenous-background/target-orbit experiment; it is not
the physical shared-family p(y|E,j) law. Do not hide that distinction in a
“same-law” label.

For each identical observation vector retain the ordinary full-catalogue top8
and a **separate** known-generator diagnostic. Never backfill truth into the
ordinary shortlist. Record nomination inclusion, normalized physical/null
comparison, named numerical/parent/source gates, and conditional information
separately. There is currently no calibrated operational identity-acceptance
rule for the predictive-only factor; a numerical pass must not manufacture one.

The useful outcomes are concrete. If the generator is omitted while its
separate exact score clearly exceeds the ordinary nominees under the same law,
the particular screen loss is demonstrated. If it is nominated but remains
ambiguous with correct closure, more search does not create information. If
the assigned-generator density or forward observation identity fails, repair
that specific implementation mismatch. A single noisy wrong ranking is not a
bug or an estimated false-identification rate.

Cap the experiment in minutes and retain failures. Do not extend it to certify
absolute scores of hopeless alternatives or rebuild a broad integrator.
Stop after a concrete mismatch or after the small confusable panel shows no
proposal/oracle distinction. Its result should decide whether the screen
deserves implementation work, not justify a localization rollout. Then move
to an eligible complete source with independently supported identity and a
different conditional information direction; preserve shared uncertainty for
repeated observations of the same family.

The coordinator's current retrospective is scientifically consistent with the
audited outcomes when it preserves these scope distinctions. No additional
