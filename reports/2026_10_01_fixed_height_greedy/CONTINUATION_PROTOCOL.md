# Bounded numerical continuation

The frozen primary experiment remains unchanged. A recording whose winning
primary mode is unresolved may receive one separately sealed continuation.
Selection is by numerical status, never reference error.

Resume saved unresolved seeds in increasing final-objective order, for at most
24 additional iterations per seed and a shared 85-second internal/90-second
external budget. Rebuild the same full scan and original priors. Saved states
are optimization starting points, not posterior priors or new evidence.
Preserve already-converged parent modes and choose the lowest final objective
across every original and resumed mode. Retain unresolved outcomes honestly.

The continuation binds its parent, original inputs, height grid and current
frozen scientific sources. Report parent and continuation runtime together;
this is a two-stage method with up to 180 seconds, not a success under the
original 90-second primary budget. Do not overwrite or relabel primary failures.
