# Independent scientific judgment

The strongest supported limitation is **position–orbit ambiguity under uncertain
whole-source identities**. The local DS24 calculation has median weak-axis SD
534 m, falling to 110 m when timing is declared known and 56 m when timing and
shape are known; median strongest timing/position correlation is .935. These
are conditional model diagnostics and oracle interventions, not achieved
accuracy, a physical error floor, or proof that changing a prior will help.
[Frozen earlier information report](https://github.com/misko/leo-tracker-reduxredux/blob/eb2a282ce7285e2dc6833789e88abd21210f6e78/reports/2026_10_24_ds24_bottlenecks/information/REPORT.md).

Source identity remains a separate limitation. In the new inventory, 146 whole
owners have computable conditional designs, and 24 scoped remaining owners in
12 scans exceed the fixed 5% weak-variance threshold in both arms. Yet three
of the first four chronological picks have zero archived feasible candidates.
Only 0016/59786 was canonically compatible. The 20–47 m predicted weak-SD
reductions do not reverse those failures or establish geographic improvement.
The independent [660-check terminal replay](inventory_terminal.json) verifies
the shared-covariance mathematics, ownership and allocation, not identity.
[Complete inventory and compatibility status](../inventory/FINDINGS.md).

Several causal stories remain unidentified. Thirty continuous restarts agreeing
within 2.06 cm lower the value of more generic starts for those models; they do
not exhaust discrete labels. RX2 remeasurement improves scatter without a
qualified cohort gain, so lower RMS alone is insufficient. Compared fast scans
are all 10 MS/s; their errors cannot identify a sample-rate effect. Nominal
feed angles are sky-coverage metadata, not a calibrated bearing or independent
station baseline. The later eight-scan RX1 test yields only a 15.4 m fitted-c
mean improvement with worse tails, so a development gain does not establish
transfer to new time, hardware or site.

The completed 0011 result raises two scientific comparisons above another
curve-level numerical campaign:

1. **Compare no addition, hard addition and whole-source ambiguity.** First
   freeze source adequacy/abstention semantics and a tractable objective that
   agrees with the bounded pilot at common RF-selected probes. Keep the same
   whole target, causal alternatives, aliases and physical/background laws for
   hard and mixture additions. Then test a small matched c0/fitted-c position
   policy with exact reference fallback; geography follows the RF freeze.
   Stop on inadequate complete-source fits, unresolved numerical checks or
   unavailable budget. Do not repair 0012 by backfilling an easier case. The
   question is improvement over doing nothing, not merely a better score than
   one hard label. Model averaging cannot make incompatible sources adequate.

2. **Qualify the frozen four information-selected whole owners before fitting
   positions.** Keep all four, including the three incompatible assignments;
   use the ordinary causal catalogue pool, whole native IDs, symmetric alias
   priors, shared receiver/orbit covariance and a proper unresolved outcome.
   Trace the actual original rejection and require source adequacy in addition
   to relative finite-pool ordering. Where a separate whole owner exists, test
   discovery-conditioned prediction without copying same-family evidence or
   training on the witness. Stop a source at unresolved identity, incompatible
   full-source residuals, failed original gates or unresolved numerical bounds;
   do not trim, backfill or enlarge its priors to force acceptance. Only a
   preregistered qualified addition proceeds to a matched c0/fitted-c position
   test with exact baseline fallback and separate coverage/likelihood/error
   metrics. This tests recoverable geometry rather than count inflation.

The distinct measurement-selection test should initially stop at **one original
IQ chunk's detector parity** while these two branches resolve. Exhaust the
bounded reader with exact manifest/index/payload checks and reproduce an
unchanged window using a frozen historical detector; current code availability
is not historical parity. Only after parity and signal/noise authority hold,
a later fixed clean/weak/crossing panel could test peak selection and tracking,
retaining misses and incorrect ownership. Stop on unavailable parity,
unsuitable waveform/noise authority or the runtime limit. This isolates a stage
absent from the curve-level controls without another large campaign.

The tiny matched-law nomination control already returned all four generating
physical labels at ordinary rank one, while both deliberately confusable proper
null draws preferred physical candidates. Neither result estimates an
operational false-acceptance rate. There is no reason to repeat easy physical
draws or pursue indefinitely tighter loser integrals as the main science.
Whole-source likelihood mixtures need declared class priors and an unresolved
outcome; their model weights are not calibrated identity probabilities.

These are fixed-position triage calculations. A secant scaled by archived local
information is neither a derivative bound nor an optimized position change.
The result supports one frozen whole-source inference-policy comparison after
its source/abstention semantics are specified; it cannot justify an accuracy
claim or operational promotion. Do not extend the unresolved case indefinitely
or replace the information-selected panel with favorable position outcomes.
