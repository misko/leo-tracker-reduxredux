## Model and input boundary

Each track retains soft probabilities across its shortlisted orbit and alias branches plus a contamination component. Tracks and RF lanes can independently favor the same orbit. Every such branch refers to one satellite-specific timing displacement with one 2.5 s Gaussian prior and a ±7.5 s bound over the capture. Conditional on the current probabilities, each displacement is a scalar quadratic with an analytic clipped minimum. Profiling those terms leaves 8 common receiver coordinates with `c = 0` or 9 with fitted `c`, including the position and lane-clock parameters. Ten fixed generalized EM updates retain the soft alternatives. The final curvature subtracts identity uncertainty, and the published radius profiles the free satellite phases. [Numerical source](../../../../tools/pipeline_eval/variants/shared_orbit_fit.py) · [Locked protocol](PROTOCOL.md)

The linearized Doppler model uses the anonymous native tracks, causal TLEs and the offered tile. It inherits the native shortlist, RF initialization and noise model used in the track-local control. Track choices factor conditionally on the common position, receiver clock and satellite phases. This handles latent dependence through the common parameters but permits several fragments to support one orbit and fits phases as points. Overlapping fragments may still increase apparent support. Position, receiver drift and a satellite's contribution can trade off, especially on short arcs with a nearly constant or linear time slope. [Protocol](PROTOCOL.md)

The held mask is fixed by whole tracks on disjoint 12 s blocks. Both arms forecast them with their training estimates. A candidate omitted from every training shortlist would use zero phase. Every held candidate in this real panel was already in the training shortlist union, whose median size was 154 satellites in the new roof reserve. This measures transfer between time-separated tracks with shared possible owners. It offers no direct cold-orbit test and shortlist membership alone gives no evidence that a phase was identifiable. [Per-arm evidence](paired-positions.jsonl.gz)

Every query starts from digest-verified archived native GLRT factors and TLE in a clean receiver sandbox that constructs the streaming Graph. Each query runs all three backends on that same Graph. The study repeats 10 roofs and three wrong offers from the earlier soft evaluation, then adds eight different DS26 roofs selected by opaque ID and three more wrong offers. Those offers include one close and two far misses. The new recording IDs are disjoint from the diagnosed panel and the specified earlier small challenges in the selection plan. Runtime starts at factors and excludes IQ demodulation. Fresh controls reproduced the full decision and both fitted arms on all 13 archived soft cases and 22 exact cases. Two close offers use freshly matched exact controls. The roof charts include the four original v5 roles from the authentic score archive. Across all eight new roofs, including withheld cases, the median conditional training RMS was 5,455 / 5,033 Hz with shared timing and 556 / 556 Hz with track-local timing. Held conditional RMS was 5,803 / 4,988 Hz versus 297 / 300 Hz. This diagnostic conditions each track on its inlier aliases even when its outlier probability is close to one, so it exposes unsupported tracks. Exact joint fitting reports a different training residual and lacks the same held RF measure. Across all 18 roof queries fitted `c` reduced shared-model conditional training RMS by a paired median of 42.5 Hz. Its paired median geographic improvement was 0.300 km on the 10 common publications, with six improvements and four regressions. [Frequency and geographic score records](scores.csv)

All 24 native queries completed in 57.36 s elapsed over four low-priority pinned x86 lanes in the final run. On the new roof queries, backend-only median wall time was 1.418 s shared, 1.394 s track-local soft and 1.645 s exact. Corresponding CPU medians were 1.106, 1.179 and 1.244 s. Each backend timed both arms. Highest process peak among the shared study queries was 164.1 MiB, including Graph and the other two solvers, under a 512 MiB address-space cap. The shared estimator's source is 214 physical lines. The soft estimator is 235 lines, while the exact estimator plus its robust refinement is 296 + 257 lines. The shared estimator still imports the existing soft shortlist preparation and both share the native frontend, so these are module sizes. The first run used absolute inlier weights for RF reporting. These could underflow on gross outliers and fall back to uniform alias weights. The final replay computes the same conditional inlier weights directly in log space. The optimizer and publication rules were frozen from the first run. The archived and final replays have identical decisions, coordinates, radii and training objectives in all 48 case arms. ## Offsite simulation

An opaque-ID selection from the earlier clock-transfer study yielded one independently simulated offsite capture with four offered tiles. The worker rebuilt the streaming Graph from the sealed public simulated GLRT candidates for each tile. The truth file was checked and opened only after all four predictions were sealed. All three methods withheld both wrong tiles in both arms. Shared timing published the centered tile with errors of 0.895 / 1.269 km, then withheld the valid near-edge tile. Both controls published both valid offers. These four offers reuse one RF realization, so they supply one independent capture.
