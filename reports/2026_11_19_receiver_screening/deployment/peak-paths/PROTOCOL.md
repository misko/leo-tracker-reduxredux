Input is complete native GLRT candidate lists, original per-visit sample clock and actual RF, a single captured causal TLE, and one supplied 25 km tile. No Graph, prepared track, published owner, original fitted state, station truth, or future catalogue enters either receiver. Both the original recording or authentic wrong tile are chosen before inference. Use every visit and at most four highest-ranked native peaks. Peak weights use fractional margin, rank and the capture gate. A peak can support only one selected explanation per receiver visit.

For each physically at-horizon orbit and phase seed −10, 0, +10 s, decode an ordered path with a background start/stop state, active peak states, and up to two missed visits. Gaussian CFO transition cost uses the wrapped departure from predicted orbit slope and a carried local residual level, with fixed start and gap penalties. The decoder keeps one continuous residual level per surviving visit/peak state. It compares an ordered sequence against a background-only score of zero. No time/CFO voting bins are used. Recover per-lane folded clock levels from multiple paths, require a shared phase seed per orbit, then disjointly select path supports. Fit shared east/north position, bounded per-receiver/band clock offset and drift, and bounded residual orbit phase. Run matched `c = 0` and fitted `c` on the exact same selected paths. Replay later held contiguous intervals by applying the training clock/position/orbits without refitting them. Whole held 15 s blocks occur one time in each four, with a 3 s buffer in the training mask. Report fitted and held RF residuals and empty held results separately.
