# Rejected placement pilot

The first blind campaign used a spherical destination function to place a flat square from one corner. On a sphere, the resulting distance sums differ among the four corners. Across all 24 containing panels, the containing corner had the largest sum of distances to the other offers. The median largest-to-smallest spread was 0.359 m. The frozen post-seal geometry audit recovered every containing corner with this cue alone. [Geometry audit](geometry-audit.csv) · [Blind plan](blind-plan.json)

The pilot established a reproducible defect in the placement generator. Its RF scores and execution receipts remain here as an audit record. The primary campaign builds a spherical square around its common centre and reflects a copy across the shared edge. Its tests verify equal distance sums within each panel and equality across the two panels. [Current protocol](../PROTOCOL.md) · [Archived prediction seal](prediction-lock.json)
