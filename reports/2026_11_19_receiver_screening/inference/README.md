# Simplifying the receiver from GLRT through position

The full-roof compact solver returned 135 of 135 positions with a fitted median of 0.989 km and a 95th percentile of 2.817 km. Original no-repair v5 returned 0.988 and 2.819 km. Four individual recordings exceeded v5's error by more than 10%. The tests below probe those differences, the resources needed by a complete position worker, and what happens away from this roof. ## What the calibration buys

The original no-repair v5 fitting and zero-calibration arms used the same observations and tile in each of the 135 cases. Fitting the RF coefficient reduced the reported frequency RMS in 116 cases. In 51 of those 116, the position error increased. In the full 135, fitting improved position on 74 recordings and zero calibration improved it on 61. Turning calibration off gave a pooled median of 0.982 km and a P95 of 2.941 km. The fitted values were 0.988 and 2.819 km. With calibration disabled, 110 of 135 recordings fell within 10% of their own fitted error or better. These counts use the original publication's RF metric for each arm. A smaller median by itself would hide the effect on the other 25 recordings.
