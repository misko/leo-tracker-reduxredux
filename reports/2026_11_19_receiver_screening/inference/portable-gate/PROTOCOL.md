# Portable v5 geographic gate protocol

The offered region must predict held Doppler before the five-refresh coupled receiver searches and publishes. The early decision is common to zero and fitted RF calibration. Both downstream arms use the exact same native observations, candidates, position budget and priors. The instrumented original timed out at conservative recovery on all three historical incomplete queries. An admitted query runs the portable solver through cavity with one change. It keeps the stable paired rejection/refit result and forgoes exhaustive candidate salvage of rejected track prefixes and suffixes. The subsequent identical refit reuses the stable result, with all retained masks and uncertainty priors intact. A gate denial does not produce a forced v5 fit. Deadline expiry is recorded separately from a geographic denial.

The original native validator picks the most confident eligible CFO candidate in each probe. Only the first nine seconds of every 12-second capture interval are visible to the graph track fitter. The remaining three seconds go into the validation pool. Each development arc retains up to 16 evenly spaced observations. A held point is selected by lane and its location within the training arc's span. Held CFO, orbit identity and fit quality play no role in training selection. At each candidate position every screened causal satellite competes, using centered residual with one phase perturbation and a lane CFO offset learned from the training arc. Phase uses the preregistered ±2 s linearization domain and the clipped causal prior width. Elevation must remain nonnegative. The receiver trains at the original 25 tile points and two nested 3 × 3 neighborhoods, using a 5σ maximum train cost and one best vote per distinct satellite. It selects the training winner before reading held residuals.

A held lane supports a satellite when at least 80% of the wrapped native residuals are within 3 × 150 Hz and its prediction stays above the horizon. Admission needs four distinct satellite families, two channels and three distinct minute bins. The thresholds use the configured 150 Hz detector noise, reject isolated coincidences across frequency and time and tolerate a minority of false native peaks. The reported held family RMS clips errors at 6σ for diagnostics. The model is a bounded single-region test. It has no geographic coverage claim outside an offer. Genuine previous orbit epochs and phase history remain required through the original portable preparation.
