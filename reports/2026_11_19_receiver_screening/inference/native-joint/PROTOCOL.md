# Joint orbit decisions from the complete native GLRT input

Keep the frozen native streaming archive, winning-peak, and Graph front end.
Pass its full anonymous Graph output to the frozen joint orbit solver in
one sandboxed worker with a 512 MiB address-space cap. The worker reads
only the recorded native GLRT factors, frozen track configuration, causal
TLE and supplied tile. It rebuilds the tracks in each replay. Compare both
calibration arms and retain the solver's held-data decision and forced point.

The primary cohort is the five matched native-lean recording IDs, DS26-0036,
DS29-0082, DS26-0187, DS26-0197 and DS29-0519. For confirmation exclude their
IDs and the ten prepared-track joint archival IDs from the public roof 135.
For each dataset rank the remaining authentic native-capable IDs by SHA-256
of `native-joint-confirm-v1:` followed by the ID. Take the first two DS26
and first DS29, giving DS26-0231, DS26-0223 and DS29-0316. Replay DS26-0036
with the prior raw-lean `r0` wrong offer in a separate matched control.

Use two workers, pinned to CPU 12 or 13, with one numerical thread per worker
and a 60-second per-attempt deadline. Freeze code, the earlier native package
inventory, the prepared joint solver and all public request packets before
prediction. Capture complete wall and CPU, Graph and solver stages, native
byte and probe counts, VmPeak, RSS, and whether the Graph SHA exactly matches
the prior native-lean replay where available. Keep any failed or withheld
outcome. Seal predictions before reading geographic references, and compare
the primary cases to the existing same-input native-lean outputs and all four
original v5 publication variants. This is a recorded-factor replay. No IQ is
collected or decoded and no v5 solver is used in the candidate.
