Compare the locked sliding-tone coarse grid and original tiled rank guard against a single sparse-epoch implementation. The conditioner, integer refinement, fractional scorer, receiver policy and public output stay fixed. The scout evaluates every sixth raw start in the original 20 ms window with every original tap, anchor and trial frequency. Original full-rate scoring verifies raw starts up to four samples around the eight strongest sampled local peaks for each CFO and includes their actual neighbors. Original ordering and suppression apply to the verified local peaks. Discarding the unsampled regions can lose or change candidates. The wide pilot aperture supplies the physical motivation for nearby starts, with no general bound on retained rank.

Development uses segment 3 sequences 0 and 2 of DS26-0036 and DS29-0082. Held blocks are segments 4 and 8, sequences 0, 2, 5 and 7 in each recording. Time three alternating pairs for each authenticated visit, pinned to CPUs 2 and 3 with numerical threads set to one. Zero missed or additional positives and unchanged positive first choices are necessary to support a detector recommendation. Preserve weaker rejection outcomes in the audit as well.
