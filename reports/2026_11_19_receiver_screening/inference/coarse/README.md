# Stride-six raw-epoch GLRT scout

The scout cut pooled coarse CPU by **2.49×** and full detector CPU by **26.0%** on 15 valid held 20 ms visits. It lost five of 20 capture-era fractional positives, including a receiver's top positive, and six of 21 integer positives. The qualified capture-era detector remains the comparison control. The scout failed the scientific acceptance criteria.

The full detector median across held repeats fell from 420 ms to 304 ms of CPU per 20 ms visit. The control reproduced the installed capture-era public output on all scheduled real visits. Each processed scout payload changed and neither scored receiver produced any extra held positive. Every matched held positive retained its raw margin exactly under one-to-one assignment. Top positive means the accepted candidate with the largest margin. The highest margin among missed held fractional positives was 0.533 against the original 0.025 gate. The detailed audit includes weak and negative candidate margins, fractional offsets, CFOs, and rank fields. The sealed score audit recomputes assignment directly from the immutable timed payloads. The early blocks' initial diagnostic used many-to-many matching and undercounted one DS26 miss. The scout kernel, timed output and downstream factor splice were identical across those audit revisions.

The implementation deinterleaves the raw ADC into six contiguous phases, then computes the same 44-tap complex pilot sums across every sixth epoch hypothesis for all 21 CFOs and all original anchors. It evaluates 46,683 of 279,993 cells per receiver bank. With 12 anchors, it computes 8.13 million normalized anchor terms at the sampled starts, compared with 48.78 million terms for the full epoch lattice. The locked full grid shares sliding-tone products before folding. The scout verifies up to 1,512 raw epochs, plus immediate neighbors, using the original full-rate tile scorer and supplies the original suppression and refinement steps. The temporary phase planes and sparse score row total 2.77 MB per bank call in addition to the reused preparation arrays.

For each edge, the normalized autocorrelation of the overlapping portion of the 44-tap pilot references has median approximately 0.56 at lag three, versus approximately 0.94 at lag one. This describes the nominal template and supplies no bound for omitting starts in recorded IQ. The DS26 amplitude-128 plant was recovered on both receivers at epoch 732. Moving that plant three samples to epoch 735 removed both planted detections and six positive fractional candidates. Both detectors recovered both main adjacent-CFO plants and the strong plant, and neither produced a positive on empty or noise controls. DS29 recovered the off-lattice plant on both receivers though some additional positive candidates changed. The phase dependence and held misses prevent use as a drop-in acquisition shortcut.
