# Joint assignment of Doppler tracks to causal orbits

The comparison starts from the same prepared anonymous GLRT Doppler tracks and causal TLE snapshot. Use robust EM and exact orbit fit at their original release versions. Keep their track whitening, orbit shortlist, radio nuisance scale, time-block holdout, calibration arms and publication limits. The joint receiver changes how it selects the fixed assignments for the common exact final fit.

Each arc retains its original receiver band, exact RF, sample times and CFO values. At fixed site and radio state, assign each arc to an orbit in its original shortlist or to an explicit outlier component. All arcs assigned to one orbit contribute to one bounded, Gaussian-regularized orbital phase. This cost does not impose an exclusive satellite, radio or time slot. Simultaneously visible carriers can select different satellites. Distinct time intervals or receiver/RF combinations can provide joint evidence for one orbit. Overlapping arcs on the same RF and receiver band do not propose a joint two-arc move. The base track projection already deduplicates common observations.

At each of three existing-envelope loss scales, make two coordinate and pair sweeps through the original candidate set, testing at most 128 moves that jointly assign two arcs to a shared candidate. Candidate pair order uses the decrease in the quadratic profile cost from representing the same orbital phase. Moving an arc to the outlier component is always available, with the same capped point budget as an accepted arc. The final loss envelope is 6.25. No thresholds may be changed after examining results. Refit site, phase and radio terms between outer passes using the original bounded regression. Use fixed pooled assignments for both final calibration arms.

Six original timing recordings supply descriptive evidence. Four disjoint confirmations are selected from `ds26-common127` by hashing the recording IDs with prefix `joint-orbits/fresh-archive/v1:`, excluding the original roof roster and earlier exact-fit confirmation and requiring archived native, catalogue, track and v5 request receipts. These recording IDs are DS26-0261, DS26-0259, DS26-0162 and DS26-0060. The confirmation cases are new to this assignment study and use the same physical installation. Use DS26-0036 for the original sealed omitted tile and a time-shuffled within-track CFO control. Full acquisition and orbit metadata remain unchanged for the shuffle. Evaluate publication, forced position, held-track normalized prediction, and conditional fitted CFO separately.

Run all inference and diagnostics in a worker namespace without fresh geographic or v5 labels. Seal outcomes and inferred owners before reading v5 receipts. After seal, verify the original request and v5 receipt. Join inferred owners to unique v5 owners using receiver, channel, time rounded to 1 μs, and folded measured CFO rounded to 1 mHz. Report the number of matched, conflicting and unscorable points as well as agreement among shared points. The published v5 owner is a comparison label.

Once the receiver and experiment source are locked, draw two additional simulated sites within ±3° of (2, −55) and (−32, 24), using the earlier authenticated donor residual and cadence injection. Place a correct center 8–10 km from each site and an omitted center 35–65 km away with independent bearings. Evaluate a candidate-time shuffle as a null. Keep labels and seeds separate until both arms of all three methods have been sealed. Report these small synthetic groups separately from the physical archive. Each query has a 60-second limit and uses one pinned CPU and one numerical thread.
