## What the receiver searched

Each worker read the authenticated complete native factors, generated the original Graph, and read one causal TLE snapshot. The proposed acquisition stage used no published owner, fitted v5 state, GNSS, or future element. It searched every valid, regionally viable satellite in that snapshot at 31 integer-second shifts from −15 to +15. Orbit states come from SGP4 at the scan’s UTC, with Hermite interpolation and Earth-rotation compensation for phase. Five site sentinels covered the supplied 25 km tile using the local Doppler tangent. Up to 12 original points from each eligible curved track contributed mean-removed frequency shape and folded residual level. A nominal-pass c0 lane offset and drift were fit from the acquisition training groups. The score uses 500 Hz shape and 3,000 Hz folded level scales. Scores are dimensionless search costs, without a statistical probability interpretation.

A satellite can be anchored only if two distinct training arcs agree on one site and phase. The chosen basin must improve the nominal phase vote by at least three, lie at least 3 s from zero and win at least one internal check against the other candidates with a margin ≥0.25. The original downstream held bucket takes no part in nomination, calibration or the internal check. The initial launch was interrupted before scientific outputs were read to enforce this separation. The sealed run used the unchanged local ±7.5-second fit and publication gates on a gauge-shifted orbital bank. When there were zero admitted anchors, the nominal bank was reused unchanged. Both c arms shared the same source universe, acquisition and anchor set, then fit using their original RF-calibration policies.
