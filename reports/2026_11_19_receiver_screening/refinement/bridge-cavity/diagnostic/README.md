# Two matched regression traces

The diagnostic replay used the same sealed bridge requests on CPU 20 for DS26-0066 and CPU 22 for DS29-0082. It reproduced both arms’ geographic coordinates and conditional RMS exactly. The counterpart is the decoded, validated archive of the original v5 baseline with the same native input, causal catalogue and roof tile. Distances between stage coordinates below compare the two fits in the common east–north plane. The traces compare identity and observation IDs independent of reference coordinates. ## DS29-0082

The bridge nominated four different orbit labels on the same 30 curved tracks. Two already passed both initial gates and survived both conservative arms. A 14-point track changed from NORAD 67037 to 62556, and a 28-point track changed from NORAD 58445 to 59934. The bridge retained 24 tracks and 808 points in each arm, compared with 28 tracks and 929 points in the original. The conservative coordinates were 2.28 km apart in c0 and 2.08 km apart in fitted c. Those distances narrowed to 0.68 and 0.65 km at the family stage.

The 28 observations form the same held-family fold under both methods. The cavity excludes the held family from shared calibration and updates candidate-specific nuisance parameters on the held rows. In the bridge cavity, original label 58445 scored 73.60 Hz on that fold against 97.55 Hz under bridge incumbent 59934, with predictive gain 25.77. Minimum elevations were respectively 60.35° and 60.72°. The original fit with 58445 scored 70.03 Hz on the same fold. Geometry above the horizon would have passed either identity. The bridge made a cavity proposal, while the compact control made none. The final bridge remained worse by 0.863 km in c0 and 1.022 km in fitted c relative to the full original. This fold supplies a signal of identity ambiguity before publishing a position. Whether a fallback could recover accuracy remains open.

## DS26-0066

The initial label differed on one of 73 tracks, which did not enter the shared initial gated set. Both methods agreed on the satellite identity of all 55 shared conservatively retained tracks. The bridge retained 2,080 points versus 2,535 original in each arm. The omitted support included 12 original retained tracks and the entire NORAD 69823 family. Conservative coordinates were 1.23 km apart in c0 and 1.26 km apart in fitted c, narrowing to 0.66 and 0.70 km at the family stage.

Twelve cavity folds shared the same held observation IDs and incumbent identities. Their minimum elevations changed by less than 0.05° and neither fit gave a positive alternative-candidate gain. The compact control’s final geographic errors were 2.389 and 2.370 km, compared with 2.846 and 2.786 km for the bridge. The full v5 position then benefited from the separately archived NORAD 62025 to 68047 reassociation after the cavity boundary. Bridge conditional RMS improved by roughly 5 Hz despite losing geographic accuracy and support. The retained-count difference required the stock trace to establish, and a standalone threshold on bridge counts remains untested. The two cases do not establish one blind fallback rule.
