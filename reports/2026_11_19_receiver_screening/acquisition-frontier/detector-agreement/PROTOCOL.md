# Do the newly detected pilots agree with the original capture detector?

This retrospective comparison follows the completed, scored two-recording whole-I/Q differential pilot trial. Use every authentic probe in its stored stock and differential runs and the corresponding archived capture-era output. Require identical per-probe metadata and recording clocks for all three inputs. Select each detector’s maximum-margin gated candidate using the existing tie-break by rank. Separate newly positive probes, lost positives, unchanged winners and switches to a different physical timing/frequency family while both detectors are positive.

Test whether each wide winner matches the capture-era winner, another positive capture-era candidate or neither. Use two ADC samples modulo the 750 Hz frame and 1 kHz modulo the detector’s carrier ambiguity. Also report exact absolute-carrier agreement. Retain capture-negative probes as a separate category. The capture-era output is an independent instrument reference. Its gate cannot establish ground truth for a single received pilot or explain geographic error by itself. The fixed classification uses no site information or selected position and does not change either receiver.
