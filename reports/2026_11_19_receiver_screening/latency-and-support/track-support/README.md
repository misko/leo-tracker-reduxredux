# Time-integrated curvature on paced detector packets

Both curvature rules published on all four recordings in both calibration arms. The time-integrated rule improved horizontal error on DS26-0026 and DS29-0316, reproduced DS26-0197 exactly, and raised fitted-c error by about 10 m on DS29-0047. Median error fell by about 41 m at zero c and 43 m at fitted c. The largest error stayed unchanged. [Both-arm position chart](figures.json) · [Individual geographic and conditional RF effects](paired-effects.csv)

This comparison changes the authority for labeling a whole Graph track as curved. The stock rule measures line-detrended Doppler RMS at the received points. The time-integrated rule measures the same quantity on the piecewise-linear curve between received points, with equal weight per supported second. It uses the stock 20 s and 250 Hz cutoffs and excludes intervals beyond the stock Graph gap. Removing a point that lies on its neighboring linear span leaves the integral invariant if the combined span remains supported. The solver receives the original native measurements and exact Graph membership and CFO. Noise calibration, local sub-arc checks, phase refresh and paired acceptance retain their existing rules. The four recordings use actual paced, always-fifth detector output. Their physical drop counts are 78 for DS26-0026, 475 for DS29-0047, 40 for DS26-0197 and 55 for DS29-0316. Each stock/treatment pair uses the same bytes, causal TLE archive, geographical offer and receiver. Both calibration arms use the same selected observations and search budget within each method. The four original v5 methods provide same-recording capture-era references. On DS29-0047 the independent surveyed roof and all four original role outputs are bound by their own native, catalogue, request and tile hashes. The measured Graph support and lifted CFO digest match in all four pairs, and each stock run exactly reproduces the previously published paced position, RF and counters. On DS26-0026, the changed curvature set produces 42 changed NORAD assignments among common final curved points. The number of active curved points falls from 2,899 to 2,826. DS29-0047 and DS29-0316 also retain fewer active curved points, and all shared curved points keep their former satellites. DS29-0316 retains the curved-only path and recovers 24 m and 20 m of error on the real-drop input. The separate complete-factor error was about 0.218 km in each arm. None of the eight fits adopts a cavity trial. [Selected tracks and point assignments](support.json) · [Earlier real-drop trace](../../arrival-and-timing/drop-mechanism/README.md)

All eight factor-to-position services ran under physical 512 MiB cgroups with zero swap, on the same pinned core within each recording. Aggregate process CPU was 340.0 s stock and 326.3 s integrated. Individual wall effects were mixed, including a difference for DS26-0197 where the scientific result was identical. The fastest and slowest service wall times across both methods were 77.7 s and 113.5 s. Conditional RF RMS improved in two pairs, increased in one, and remained exact in one. Objectives are recorded separately from horizontal error and refer to the support used by each fit.
