# Receiver speed, position accuracy and missing regions

These experiments follow two different changes through the complete receiver. Reusing an already computed GLRT score can accelerate the same detector without changing the measurements. Adding a timing region changes which pilots the solver sees and can move the returned position in either direction. The benchmark follows all twenty scheduled roof recordings and gives each of the four original v5 options on that recording. [Full recording roster](complete-roster/README.md) · [Charts by change](index.html) · [Offline figures and data](modification-charts.zip)

All twenty four- and five-region replays returned a position in each calibration setting. The four-region fitted median was 0.776 km and P95 was 1.388 km. Five regions gave 0.824 and 1.533 km. Original v5 gave 0.724 and 1.653 km. Four regions met the 10% allowance for individual recordings on 11/20 and five on 12/20 in both settings. The earlier eight-recording panel had favored five regions on both fitted median and P95. [Twenty-recording fitted distribution](complete-roster/charts/fitted_c/complete-detector-roster-all.png) · [Matched zero-calibration distribution](complete-roster/charts/c0/complete-detector-roster-all.png)

Four regions took 176.7 s median from saved samples through both positions, and five took 214.2 s. Among 132,740 valid detector calls per policy, 55.6% and 21.3% finished within 20 ms. On the six recording pairs with matched CPU assignments, the rates were 91.5% and 52.5%. [Per-run times, input identities and first-attempt sensitivity](complete-timing/README.md)

## Change the computation and keep the measurements

The fifth-region GLRT evaluated a central integer start again during fractional refinement, with the same data, phase and control templates. The new code reuses that result and evaluates four neighbouring starts together. On 96 held two-antenna windows it reproduced all 1,536 candidates in each mode, including every gate decision. Median refinement CPU fell from 9.02 to 7.09 ms. Median full fifth-region detector wall time fell from 30.95 to 28.78 ms in this single-core comparison. [Exact operations, controls and timing](fractional-cost/README.md)

On two complete recordings, the lighter implementation and original five-region detector matched across 26,868 receiver outputs, including all candidates and both computed positions per recording. Detector CPU fell from 128.0 to 116.1 s on DS26-0255 and from 126.3 to 118.6 s on DS29-0330. Full replay wall time rose from 183 to 202 s on the first and fell from 203 to 163 s on the second. The aggregate detector computation and time to final position have different timing boundaries. [Complete replay parity and timing](new-waveforms/README.md) · [Three-way position distributions](new-waveforms/lighter-comparison/charts/README.md)

Processing the two antennas in parallel and reusing the central fractional score reduced five-region median detector time from 31.25 to 19.08 ms on held windows. In a separate five-minute replay of DS26-0026 at its recorded arrival times, the combined receiver emitted 6,604 packets, dropped 155 and excluded 11 hardware-invalid arrivals. Median time from arrival through emission was 21.33 ms. Of the emitted packets, 2,307 were ready within 20 ms. The position error was 0.698 km with zero calibration and 0.672 km with fitted calibration. [Held timings, actual arrival replay and both fits](combined-native/README.md) · [Independent parallel-lane measurements](dual-lane/README.md)

The portable x86-64 receiver accepts a local recording, its causal orbital catalogue, tracker configuration and region. On one five-minute recording it reproduced the archived stock detector output byte for byte and both stock positions in 179.52 s under 512 MiB. Its 1.53 MiB application archive requires the documented scientific Python packages and system FFTW. The static AArch64 package ran from a clean extraction and preserved the x86 candidate order, CFO values and gates across 72 receiver-lane comparisons. Its measured Cortex-A53 emulator times were 373 and 453 ms median wall for the stock and fifth-region detectors. The locally attached development unit has a Cortex-A9, which uses the separately packaged ARMv7 build. Physical board timing remains open. ## Change the measurements and refit

On two roof recordings chosen by acquisition metadata before scoring, adding the fifth timing region lowered fitted error from 1.317 to 0.320 km on DS26-0255 and raised it from 0.402 to 1.522 km on DS29-0330. Original no-repair v5 returned 0.420 and 0.793 km on those recordings. With zero RF calibration the direction of both changes was the same. Each fixed detector met the 10% allowance on one of the two. [Fresh recording distributions](new-waveforms/README.md)

A change to the tracker integrated curvature over the real time between retained measurements. On four actual packet-loss recordings, fitted median error went from 0.699 to 0.657 km and P95 from 0.947 to 0.939 km. Both policies stayed within the original v5 10% allowance on three of four and published all four. The largest gain was 65 m. One recording became 10 m less accurate. [Matched support, position and frequency outcomes](track-support/README.md)

## What transfers to another receiver

The measured Bellaterra, Spain test starts with published VHF Doppler observations from six identified ORBCOMM satellites. The data owners filtered measurements using expected Doppler at their surveyed receiver. A receiver that fitted the Doppler shape with one offset per pass published three of four containing offers at 9.68–10.59 km error and also published two of four offers that omitted the site. The simpler absolute-frequency fit reached 4.15–5.63 km on all four containing offers, with four false publications on omissions. Reversing the measured frequency sequence within each pass led to eight withholds. The original Starlink detectors and their fitted calibration did not run on this VHF input. [Measured geographic scores and time labels](external-position/README.md) · [Owners’ public measurements](https://doi.org/10.34810/DATA3383)

On anonymous native GLRT observations, a separate receiver jointly estimated horizontal position, satellite alternatives and receiver-path drift. Both models withheld on all eight recorded or simulated offers. Sharing the drift improved the two real containing forced estimates to 2.98 and 1.48 km, but the estimated uncertainty radii exceeded the 12.5 km publication limit throughout. [Real containing and missing-region offers, simulated controls and runtime](joint-pass/README.md)

On the disjoint April 7–8 Bellaterra data, a shared receiver-frequency offset gave a 10.376 km containing fix at zero calibration and withheld the omitted region. Fitting a frequency-dependent coefficient gave 10.512 km on the containing offer and also withheld the omission. Held-day Doppler RMS rose from 4.370 to 5.434 Hz with the fitted coefficient. Independent historical records corroborated acquisition of the exact orbital elements for all six satellites before this holdout.
