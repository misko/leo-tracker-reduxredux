# Reduced standalone cavity on two new simulated locations

The 78-source standalone package reproduced the full 1,042-source package’s scientific outputs exactly on four native-GLRT offers at two new simulated sites. Both packages published both legitimate fixes, with errors of 1.49–2.12 km across the two calibration arms. Both also published both far omissions, with errors of 53.61–65.89 km. The omitted-tile failure survives source reduction. The northern omission exercised the final paired cavity refit in both packages.
