# What the anonymous receiver loses before the position fit

Changing only the orbit labels on the **same native GLRT peaks** lowered median forced position error from **7.91 to 2.24 km** with `c = 0` and **7.97 to 1.72 km** with fitted `c`. On the four hash-selected reserves, the respective changes were **4.57 to 1.84 km** and **4.63 to 1.28 km**. The same estimator, measured samples, causal elements, oscillator model, priors, and budget were used in each pair. The v5 identities are privileged archive inputs. ## Which part of the chain explains the difference

Full anonymous support ranged from 607 to 1,478 measured peaks. v5 contributed 2,313–3,456. The exact intersection had 464–911 peaks, with an additional 109–318 opportunities where the receivers selected different alternatives. A physical sample key includes receiver, acquisition counter, and the actual candidate rank. The fit receives raw CFO and actual RF. The ledger also preserves the epoch, native margin, channel, and original role when present. The six archives supplied stable v5 ownership across both original calibration arms and all fitted identities had eligible causal elements. [Physical matches and disagreements](source-audit.json) · [Point ledger](factor-ledger.jsonl.gz)

The case strongest for an assignment failure was DS26-0197. The anonymous and v5 labels differed on 517 of 601 identical measured peaks. Relabeling just those observations reduced RF RMS on the same 61 held points from 21.70 kHz to 237 Hz with `c = 0`. Forced error moved from 24.52 to 7.56 km. The larger v5 support moved it to 3.84 km, while the original published at 0.53 km. These results support orbit assignment as a major bottleneck and leave a residual numerical-model or coverage gap to original v5. The held comparison is conditional on privileged v5 labels learned from the full scan, including the held times. [Shared-peak time and RF example](shared-disagreement-cfo.png)

Denser v5 support mainly improved conditioning once the v5 labels were held fixed. The median local radius dropped from 7.16/7.15 km on the shared subset to 1.87/1.89 km on the full set. The median coordinate error changed from 2.24/1.72 to 2.18/1.84 km. These radii are diagnostics from the regularized local curvature. The different effects on radius and actual error make raw sample count a poor substitute for orbit agreement. On DS29-0519 the full anonymous refit reached 0.75/0.48 km, compared with 1.80/1.18 km on full v5 support. The smaller fit can extract useful geometry when the anonymous upstream is coherent enough. ## Standalone model and scope

The estimator consumes time, RF, measured CFO, supplied NORAD and four receiver/band clock groups. It propagates just the eligible labeled satellites, evaluates WGS84 Doppler, and fits position, per-group offset/drift and a bounded phase per orbit with wrapped robust residuals. The fitted arm adds one physical RF coefficient. The zero arm omits the column exactly. Both arms share initialization, labels, observation masks and numerical budget. The estimator sees a 25 km tile and one causal snapshot, with no v5 phases, harmonic lifts, calibrated offsets, states, tracks or positions. The original v5 request included each single snapshot in its causal history and may have used additional historical elements or corrections. No later TLE enters this study. [Prospective protocol](PROTOCOL.md) · [Causal element epochs and hashes](tle-inventory.csv)

The frozen fit is 310 Python lines plus common orbit and WGS84 numerical helpers, and it calls numpy, scipy and SGP4. Its median two-arm CPU was 0.17–0.22 s per cell including causal catalogue parsing and propagation. The process high-water RSS was about 116–117 MiB across four sequential cells. The anonymous GLRT extraction stage replayed the entire independent-arcs receiver before the fit. It took 4.76–5.78 s CPU and 6.73–12.04 s elapsed including archive loading and sandbox work. The runner sealed 24 two-arm cells in 34 seconds over two single-thread workers. This timing isolates the refit from association. The extra estimator runtime is small compared with the anonymous path search. [Frozen source closure and roster](blind-plan.json) · [Timing and exact inputs](source-audit.json)

The diagnostics were DS26-0197 and DS29-0316. Fresh anonymous reserve fits covered DS26-0313, DS26-0245, DS29-0519 and DS29-0127. These are recorded rooftop captures using supplied tiles. A preliminary CFO-only export could not distinguish 213–678 selected rows per capture with duplicate measured CFO at different ranks. Before geographic scoring, the adapter was corrected to retain the actual decoder backtrace rank. Both old diagnostic endpoints and both holdouts reproduced after tracing. This correction kept the original physical estimator and all forecasts frozen. [Preliminary sample-join receipt](preliminary-cfo-only.json) · [Final prediction seal](prediction-lock.json)

The matched-label result prioritizes a small anonymous assignment stage that can corroborate identity on independent curves and preserve the measured peak rank. The residual gap with full privileged support shows where the receiver-and-phase model still needs work before a deployment publication study.
