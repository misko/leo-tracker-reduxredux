# Shared receiver clock, marginal track offset and noise

One anonymous native Graph and joint-orbit owner selection supplies the training tracks, aliases, prior widths, starts, final masks and robust weights. The existing first-fit white-plus-OU observer, assignment logic and gates stay in place. The published candidate runs its own exact nonlinear final position with the original geometry thresholds. Clock and offsets never use archived owners, aliases, phase or location parameters.

The two combined models use the pinned smooth fractional-frequency receiver clock and a zero-mean 1000 Hz Gaussian offset for every Graph track. The Gaussian integral changes the whitened penalty by a rank-one covariance update and includes its determinant. The matched noise arms use either independent 150 Hz measurement noise or that same noise plus the native 200 Hz, 4 s OU process. The OU clock-only and OU offset-only controls isolate the two nuisance terms under the same first-fit decisions and final evidence. Both calibration arms keep the same observations, owners, alias and exact three-relinearization budget.

A separate diagnostic estimates shared parameters on the original training tracks. For each held track, only its first two thirds are re-shortlisted and used for one common anonymous orbit and CFO alias. Given the training-only shared state and that prefix, the held suffix is scored with its conditional Gaussian density, including determinant and prefix-conditioned OU/offset nuisance. The suffix estimates no shared state, owner, alias or local offset. Raw held RF and suffix prediction RF use the same held observations in all models. These diagnostic scores do not alter the existing publication gate.
