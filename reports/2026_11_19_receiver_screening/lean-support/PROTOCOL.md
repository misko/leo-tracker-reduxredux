# Guarded track prediction for the lean receiver

Use the previously replayed ordinary six-recording cohort and capture-era eight-recording independent cohort only to audit the stock decision. Inspect predicted-track losses, previously fitted orbit identities, training-to-pooled shifts, and conditional fit covariance without geographic references. Compare the original lean and exact-geometry controls unchanged on new cases. The candidate modifies the predictive partition and acceptance evidence. It retains the original causal catalogue, visibility and shortlist size, alias period, noise and phase priors, robust bounded regression, pooled search, and geometry and interior limits. It uses no post-capture orbital bank, saved satellite labels, or GNSS.

For each original physical track with enough samples, select one contiguous eight-second block centered at 35% or 65% of the track span, with the choice fixed by SHA-256 of its first and last time. Require four holdout observations and eight training observations outside an eight-second guard on either side. Short tracks and tracks without adequate sampling stay in training. Rebuild the covariance and curvature of retained tracks using retained samples only. Use the original training-only shortlist and assignment fit. For each training-accepted track with a held block, predict its original held samples from the fixed training orbit, integer alias, receiver calibration, site, and phase. Under the same four-second exponential correlation plus white noise model, compute the conditional innovation of the held samples given residuals outside the guard. No identity, branch, phase, or receiver parameter is re-estimated from the held samples. Another receiver may observe the same orbit at overlapping times. A satellite contributes an independent acceptance count at most once. Conditional innovations within an orbit are not claimed as independent trials.

Require at least two distinct training-separated satellite predictions at the existing whitened per-sample squared-loss limit of 6.25, and mean inverse loss of at least 0.28. Preserve the support floors of four curved tracks, four assigned orbits, two physical radio lanes and three separated identities in the final common pooled assignment. Preserve the 4 km training-to-pooled stability limit, the 6 km conditional geometry radius, and the 0.75 km tile-edge margin. Evaluate held predictions and stability limits separately in both calibration arms on the same training/pooled observations and assignments. Report covariance diagnostics as conditional on the assigned model, without treating them as calibrated absolute confidence or as evidence of satellite identity.

After candidate sources are locked, select the first eight eligible IDs from the frozen `roof16` roster excluding prior lean timing and independent IDs, ordered by SHA-256 of `lean-support/roof/v1:` plus ASCII recording ID. Each uses the existing capture-era complete native GLRT artifact and its bound anonymous track projection. Each wrong-tile pair uses the nearest non-overlapping frozen alternative to the original offered tile, measured by center distance and requiring center separation greater than the 25 km square's diagonal. Reuse the same observation, catalogue and clocks in each pair. Cap each fitter at 45 wall seconds on CPU 10 or 11 with one numerical thread. Rotate method order by case and reverse it for the wrong-tile control.

Seal all predictions, failures, and withheld rows before scoring geography. Report coverage, count within 5 km, conditional RF scores, issued and common-fix median and P95 error per arm, CPU/wall time and RSS. A coverage gain is supported only if at least one additional roof fix lands within 5 km in both arms, no previously issued control fix is lost relative to the better control, no new issue exceeds 5 km, and every wrong tile is withheld. Preserve the otherwise informative failures and avoid tuning on these results.
