# Anonymous measured Doppler on mixed tile boards

The receiver selected the correct search tile on both days at a previously studied Bellaterra installation, without the publisher’s satellite identities or a marked containing tile. It published fixes with **9.239 km** and **10.405 km** error. When the containing tile was replaced, both fits reached a tile boundary and the predeclared rule withheld both. The same numerical estimator without the gate would have published both omitted-region fixes. Coverage on containing boards was 2/2, 1/2 within 10 km and 0/2 within 5 km. The gate produced 0/2 false fixes on omitted boards versus 2/2 for ungated publication. Reversing measured CFO values in time at their original UTC stamps withheld all four boards, with no accepted traces and held robust RMS of 74.59–74.77 Hz. Each isolated one-thread worker ran in 0.739–1.089 s wall and 0.214–0.244 s process CPU, inclusive of both numerical searches and excluding dataset transfer and offline track preparation. It reused each normal estimate for the ungated control.

This boundary starts with measured VHF ORBCOMM carriers after the publisher’s site-conditioned ±500 Hz retention. The custody process used UTC, nominal RF and observed CFO alone to form tracks. On each date, 85 tracks were eligible and 64 were selected without labels. The receiver searched nine historically available candidates from the independent 17-object ORBCOMM OG2 launch membership. Three of nine were genuine candidate orbits absent from all publisher-supplied labels. On the two containing boards, ten and twelve tracks had at least two geometrically visible candidates. The post-lock evaluator found that all 128 accepted chain assignments agreed with the publisher’s labels on the containing boards, and likewise all 95 on the omitted boards. Correct orbital attribution alone did not reject the wrong regions. The offer board is an explicit Catalonia prior, with three 25 km squares per query. A containing board includes one square near Bellaterra without marking it, while its matched omission substitutes another square and keeps both rivals. The receiver sees anonymous carrier traces, the pre-day orbit catalogue and only the supplied tile centers. The two dates use the same physical antenna and system, so the panel measures temporal repeatability under a different inference contract from the previous site study. The independent source check found no additional measured installation with verified survey, absolute measured carrier history and causal ephemerides within the bounded review. The SatForensics release is based on satellite-targeted SatNOGS observations and extracted waterfalls, whose default flowgraphs request model-dependent Doppler corrections. A reversible absolute-frequency and independent-survey join was not established.
