# Alternating pilot searches and testing geographic transfer

A two-antenna receiver usually searches four possible pilot-timing regions on each antenna. Searching a fifth region on just one antenna, alternating at each RF centre, improved the fitted median position error from 0.776 to 0.649 km on 22 roof recordings. The original no-repair v5 median was 0.724 km. Alternating brought 12 of the 22 individual errors within 10% of their original v5 error or better. ## Starting from Doppler and causal orbital elements

On two roof recordings, a new solver identified 325 and 509 pairs of distinct Doppler detections observed at the same time through the same RF path. Differencing cancels a common oscillator offset. The observations left position uncertainty of 53–67 km, and the solver withheld on all four real offered regions. Every observed pair had the same RF, so the per-RF calibration coefficient disappeared with the offset. The zero-calibration branch has numerical results and the fitted coefficient is marked unavailable. [Geometry, forced positions and missing-region controls](paired-doppler/README.md)

A separate experiment hid the satellite IDs and the identity of the correct tile from a receiver processing real ORBCOMM Doppler from Bellaterra, Spain. On two day-long blocks it selected the surveyed tile among three and returned 9.24 and 10.40 km errors. It withheld both boards that omitted the station. Without its boundary check, the receiver would have published 20.06 and 21.19 km errors. The data owners selected observations within 500 Hz of expected Doppler at the surveyed station. This tests anonymous inference on two days at one previously studied VHF site. Independent complete Starlink position replays still require a suitable second-site recording.
