# Coherent single- and dual-channel visits at equal detector-call budgets

Reuse DS26-0164 and DS29-0357 from the burst study. Add one case per dataset from `ds26-common127` and `roof16` by ascending SHA-256 of `channel-dwell-roofs-v1:{recording}`. Exclude the two prior cases and the earlier burst study's exclusions. Require authenticated original capture-era factors and request, matched current and historical causal TLEs, 10 MS/s, 20 ms stereo windows, and enough eligible RF visits to spend each budget. Roster and schedules depend on acquisition metadata and complete-file digests. Seal them before reading any position or reference label.

Choose one primary RF lane per recording by the first byte of SHA-256 of `channel-dwell-rf-v1:{recording}` modulo eight plus one. The second lane for the higher budget is the corresponding lane in the opposite four-channel RF bank. At 96 calls, spend all calls on one lane, using every other valid opportunity in one uninterrupted acquisition interval. At 416 calls, spend 208 consecutive opportunities on each of the two lanes, starting together on their valid-opportunity clocks. The ordinary same-lane cadence is about 0.355 seconds, so both early modes can cover more than 60 seconds of useful temporal support. For each budget a delayed control uses the same lanes and counts and starts at their ordinal 384 (zero-based). There are no survey calls. Both physical receivers share each scheduled visit.

Each solve sees a causal native prefix ending at its own final selected sample end, with only the clocks and exclusions through that visit. For earlier unselected valid visits retain the genuine probe metadata and empty the candidate list. Retain every original selected candidate field including rejected entries. Snapshot trace summaries from the actual installed phase release without changing its calculations. Report Graph chains, curvature, compatible unique satellite IDs and a local east/north Doppler geometry diagnostic when a fitted association exists. Keep observed fits, withholds and failed services in both calibration denominators.

Use CPUs 8/9, one numerical thread, 512 MiB charged memory and zero swap, nice 10 and a 210 s limit per paired service. Report returned positions, threshold counts at 5% and 10% against original v5, the four original controls, and conditional Doppler frequency separately. Use the prior original detector timing panel to transfer mean/min/max call costs. Compute detector queue completion at exact saved delivery times in a single-core FIFO and add the measured stored-factor position time. That scenario includes the wait for the first and final selected visits. It does not measure real-time performance of a single-frequency dwelling radio, variable costs at chosen visits, or new raw detector execution. The intentional schedule uses the exact samples archived under the original scan cadence.
