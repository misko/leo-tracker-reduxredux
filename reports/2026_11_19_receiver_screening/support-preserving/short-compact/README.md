# Can two independent Doppler blocks release compact v5 earlier?

The frozen two-block rule released **0 of 4** recorded cases before the 300-second capture deadline. All twelve recorded 75-second block fits returned both calibration arms. The closest two-block agreement was 1.45 km against a 1 km requirement. Every terminal nominal 95% major radius exceeded the 2 km threshold, ranging from 4.54 to 19.40 km. The complete compact receiver fixed all four recordings. The rule added a median **65.7 seconds of CPU** and gave the same modeled fallback time as beginning the full fit at final delivery. Each independent fit preserved the original UTC bracket, complete dual-receiver visit intervals, valid and invalid slots within the block, and the capture-start causal catalogue. The second fit had no access to first-block observations, track state, or fitted coefficients. Position agreement compared both cold starts with their local profiled residual geometry, retained working orbit support and the actual tile face cleared by the ellipse radius plus 20 m. The profiled geometry supplies a nominal local diagnostic conditional on the selected orbital model. Shared ephemeris and model errors can affect both fits. Frequency residuals are reported per arm separately from horizontal distance. For example, DS29-0082 fitted-c RMS fell from 96.68 Hz on the full exposure to 41.14 Hz on 150–225 s while position error increased from 0.516 to 2.463 km.
