# Which additional pilot measurements improve a position?

Across six complete roof recordings, keeping all four stock timing regions and adding a fifth put **four of six** fixes within 10% of original no-repair v5 or better, in either calibration setting. Among fitted results it reduced the 95th percentile from **2.007 to 1.698 km** while increasing the median from **0.824 to 0.892 km**. Four recordings were reused diagnostics and two were selected before this change was tested. Both new recordings improved, by 33–55 m on DS26-0197 and 616–634 m on DS29-0316 across the two calibration settings. On the same six recordings stock met the 5% allowance on three in each calibration setting, and the 10% allowance on four with zero calibration and three with fitted calibration. Five timing regions met the 5% allowance on four in both settings. On the hardest recording, DS29-0082, fitted error moved from 2.346 km with stock to 2.055 km with replacement of the fourth region and 1.757 km with all five. Original no-repair v5 measured 0.517 km on that recording. Both detector variants used the same compact position solver on every recording. Earlier experiments with the original capture-era detector measurements isolated the solver on a larger 135-recording panel. Running the extra search only on receivers that missed a pilot preserved all 8,519 stock-positive receiver packets on the two fresh recordings. It added 417 and 485 detections. With zero / fitted calibration, DS26-0197 error rose to **1.045 / 1.191 km**, and DS29-0316 reached **0.194 / 0.194 km**. Stock, unconditional fifth and conditional fifth each met the 10% allowance on one of the two recordings in both arms. In a counterbalanced same-core detector test, the conditional search saved time when both receivers already had a stock detection. An extra pass on a negative receiver took longer than evaluating five timing regions together. The full-stream conditional and unconditional timing runs used different host cores. ## Processing samples at their recorded arrival times

Each saved window contains 20 ms of radio data. The median time between deliveries was about 43.5 ms on both recordings. On the host, one detector worker with one waiting slot dropped **24 of 13,535 valid visits** using the four stock timing regions and **95 of 13,535** with five. Median time from scheduled arrival to detector output rose from about 32 ms to 43–44 ms. Every emitted packet matched the corresponding earlier replay, and the decoder had samples ready for every delivery. The four five-minute replays used the original acquisition clocks, one host core per recording and a 512 MiB cap. [Observed queue losses and output times](arrival-queue/paced-replay.csv) · [Timing distributions](arrival-queue/queue-evidence.png) · [Same-core measurements of all three detector policies](arrival-queue/same-core-summary.csv)

All four fits from the retained visits published in both calibration settings. On DS29, five-region error rose from 0.218 / 0.218 km with every visit to **0.725 / 0.682 km** after 55 drops. The four-region result with 16 drops was **0.715 / 0.718 km**. On DS26, five-region error rose from 0.759 / 0.937 to 0.807 / 0.988 km after 40 drops. These results measure one real-time host replay per configuration and recording. ## Sampling fewer channels and running on ARM

Concentrating 416 capture-era GLRT calls on two channels supplied enough repeated observations for all eight sparse fits to publish positions across four recordings and two start times. Their errors ranged from 1.690 to 9.848 km. Every full-input control met the 10% allowance against original v5, and every sparse fit exceeded it. At 96 calls, only three of eight fits published. The calls came from the original scan schedule. Elapsed times for a receiver that would request just those calls are modeled. [Per-recording positions](channel-dwell/scores.csv) · [Geometry and time at each work budget](channel-dwell/README.md)

The full native pilot detector, including its optional fifth timing region, now runs in an offline Cortex-A9 executable. The x86 and QEMU ARM runs matched all 32 stock and 44 five-region gate-positive candidates across eight genuine or control windows. Carrier estimates matched exactly. The largest matched timing difference was less than one trillionth of a sample. These are numerical portability results. ## Transfer to a new location with a recorded background

An independently drawn simulated receiver used generated pilots added to real dual-receiver samples from two roof recordings. The identical signals over matched Gaussian noise give a direct background control. On the containing offer, compact errors were 1.036 / 1.452 km on the recorded background and 1.031 / 1.460 km on Gaussian noise. Original v5 measured 1.032 / 1.448 km on the recorded background. Although more than 300 receiver probes on the donor-only recordings crossed the detector gate, all methods withheld their donor-only positions. [Waveforms and detector evidence](hybrid-site/README.md) · [Matched original and compact results](hybrid-v5/README.md)

With the correct region missing by 1.5 km, ordinary compact and original v5 both published. The fixed 20 m boundary rule withheld both calibration estimates and retained both with the correct region offered. Adding fitted calibration lowered original v5’s conditional frequency RMS by 0.29 Hz on the containing offer while increasing position error by 416 m. These coordinates are simulation truth. Independent physical wideband installations and deadlines on the target board remain open.
