# Prague Ku-band Doppler replay on author-selected tracks

The university deposit contains actual Doppler observations from **11 distinct recordings at one CTU rooftop**. The fixed-geometry replay published **0 of 4** positions on April 24 and **0 of 7** on May 7 with the site inside the offered region. It also withheld every near and far omission. On the containing offer, the forced positions had median horizontal errors of **15.77 km** and **12.11 km**, versus **4.41 km** for the Prague city centre. The ordered observations predicted held frequencies much better than the same frequencies shuffled within tracks. The geographic errors did not improve over the city centre on either date. ## What the primary deposit contains

- Tomáš Heinl recorded the positioning data with a standalone LNB feed, ADALM-PlutoSDR, and GPS-disciplined oscillator. The nominal sky frequency was **11.325 GHz**, with **1.575 GHz** IF and **2.5 MHz** recorded bandwidth. This is narrowband spectral-peak Doppler from the author's capture chain. It does not exercise the wideband GLRT. The author reports one physical roof site and the exact same WGS84 survey tuple appears in all selected recording exports from both dates. [CTU thesis, §6.1 and Table 6.1](https://hdl.handle.net/10467/184304) · [Survey release](survey-release.json)
- The checksummed deposit yields 22 position exports, one MATLAB fusion sweep, and 15 support tables and figures. Its second archive has nine MATLAB source files. Six timestamps appear more than once; the plan keeps the richest track export for each timestamp and resolves ties by the declared folder precedence. The selected files contain relative sample times, raw measured spectral-peak Hz and unshifted author ECEF position and velocity at those times. Filename starts agree with the embedded recording names and the documented UTC parser. The capture clock itself has no independent time audit. [DSpace output archive](https://dspace.cvut.cz/server/api/core/bitstreams/0ec1e6ed-5f42-48c0-b529-496337ec91ca/content) · [Checksums and inventory](source-audit.json) · [Selected recordings](study-plan.json)
- The code does survey-aided orbit identification and then takes measured peaks inside each candidate orbit's predicted frequency window. Even with identifiers stripped at the replay boundary, the measured samples and trajectory pairing carry the author's assistance. Neither archive has IQ, full spectral measurements, or the original orbit catalogue and its publication history. Chapter 5 selects element epochs by absolute distance to the recording start, which does not require a pre-recording epoch or publication. The local historical snapshot tree begins in August 2026, after both visits. The thesis appendix lists source files and provides no public raw-recording link. Figure 6.2 contains actual relative-time spectral pixels, with no visible absolute UTC anchor. This study uses the deposited numeric observations and has no anonymous or causal Prague position result. ## Replaying the author-assisted geometry

The trial profiles one constant frequency offset per track against the signed physical prediction \(-f_\mathrm{RF}\dot{\rho}/c_\mathrm{light}\). Every candidate location and offset uses the first 60% of each track in chronological order. The last 40% supplies held RMS. The same 25 km search uses 121 initial candidates then two 9-candidate refinements, including for the time shuffle. The offer-centre control fits offsets on the same training observations. A single nominal sky RF and the free track intercepts make the RF-dependent coefficient's column exactly redundant. The first prediction receipt records the identical gauge for both arms. The gallery gives fitted `c` the unavailable status and displays the fixed centre once per arm. The replay holds the unshifted ECEF trajectory fixed and discards the author's fitted positions and satellite time/frequency corrections. Heinl's joint solver separately fits pass timing. Median candidate CPU ranged between **1.3 ms and 2.3 ms** by date and offer, after author acquisition and measurement extraction. [Prediction lock](prediction-lock.json) · [Fit code](../../../../tools/pipeline_eval/variants/prague_transfer.py)

All offers derive from a public city centre and fixed east offsets. After the predictions were written, the survey verified a **9.32 km** interior margin in the city offer and **3.18 km** and **40.68 km** gaps to the near and far offers. Publication requires two author tracks, more than **20 m** of room at the nearest face and at least **244.14 Hz** lower held RMS than the offer centre under the same data. Those gates apply to the original and shuffled curves. The raw computed coordinate survives as a forced diagnostic. The survey is used after the prediction lock for geographic errors and membership. It was already used upstream by the author to select the released observations. [Prediction inputs](study-plan.json) · [Score lock](score-lock.json)
