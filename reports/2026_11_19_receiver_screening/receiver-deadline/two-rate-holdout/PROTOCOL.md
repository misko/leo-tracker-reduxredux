# Two-rate detector offline replay

The decision is whether sparse original GLRT packets can make the fast four-region detector useful for simple compact positioning on individual recordings. Compare +5% and +10% error ceilings relative to the original v5 baseline separately for zero and fitted RF calibration. Report missing endpoints, error, and candidate gains per recording. A baseline gate hit without a returned position does not count. Report calibration effects on frequency fit and geometry separately.

Freeze the sample from the 22 complete fast four-region stock streams in the one-lane roster. Keep DS26-0237 and DS29-0082 as timing anchors. Within each series, rank the ten remaining recordings by SHA-256 of canonical JSON `[salt, "membership", input_manifest_sha256]` and retain the first three. Resolve a hash tie by recording ID. Use the physical input manifest supplied by the stock archive. Open no receiver geometry, truth, or error tables before writing the membership and schedule receipt. These arrays were used by earlier receiver studies and do not form an unseen geographic validation set. Preserve the parent roster's fresh and difficult flags.

Replay every valid fast visit. Every twelfth visit to a physical RF center also has a complete original stereo packet. Count valid visits from zero separately for each physical RF. Derive the stagger from SHA-256 of canonical JSON `[salt, "rf-stagger", input_manifest_sha256, rf_hz]` as an integer modulo 12. Excluded raw visits do not advance the counters. Source IDs, hashes, timings, and physical RF are available during selection. Candidate clarity, position, truth, and score have no role in scheduling.

Compare unchanged fast, whole-visit original replacement, original-on-negative-lane fill, and full original. Replacement can remove a useful fast packet. Fill preserves every passing fast lane and uses the complete original physical lane packet only when that lane contains a passing original candidate. Both policies receive the same selected original visits. Join on physical stereo visits, exact capture clocks, and physical lane metadata. A mismatch is an unavailable comparison. Bind all fits to the same truth-free request, TLE snapshots, tile, and compact receiver. Reuse a sealed control only when its complete detector bytes and receiver match. Every completed fit must provide both calibration arms.
