Challenge a wide visit when either physical receiver has a strongest complete margin from 0.0125 inclusive to 0.025 exclusive, or has two distinct passing physical candidates whose margin gap is at most 0.00625. Distinct means more than two wrapped epoch samples or more than 1 kHz of CFO after subtracting the nearest 1/(4.4 microsecond) alias. Entirely empty or weak-below-half-gate receivers do not trigger by themselves. Keep the thresholds fixed for both budgets. To enforce a hard causal budget, group admitted visits into consecutive blocks of 128 and separately 16. The first block earns credit without spending. In each subsequent block, choose the first eligible visit, at most once. A block may spend its credit before completing because the preceding block earned it. Preserve unused credit as unspent and keep the physical exclusions outside the admitted-visit counter.

For each recording and budget, the deterministic uniform control uses the same number of replacements. Distribute that many blocks evenly over the started blocks that have credit and choose each selected block's midpoint visit. This is a same-count retrospective benchmark control. It ignores candidate scores and also satisfies the hard visit cap. Replace both complete native probe rows of each selected visit, preserving all their original candidate fields and ranks.
