## Signed, weak and noise controls

The full and narrowed detectors both recovered all 20 planted and weak receiver signals within two samples and 1 kHz at their planted signed frequencies. The controls cover both pilot sidebands and frequencies near the offer boundary. Both noise-only windows returned no positive factors. Six competing coarse slots disappeared from the strong planted trials while all 12 stock winning coarse slots remained eligible. The strong plants skipped about 7% of supported symbol/bin evaluations.

The independently specified coherent alternative removed the pilot within five-tap groups and checked the resulting circular phases on even and odd frame halves. It admitted 77 of 192 strong planted neighborhoods and zero of 512 real neighborhoods. As an x86 Python allocation diagnostic it cost a median 10.32 ms per recorded window. It reused the sealed complete-grid outputs to test which cells would remain eligible. Its result does not supply a fresh downstream GLRT or a deployable speed measurement.

Both directions preserve the tested real matches mainly by falling back to the full grid. The measured real workloads give no speed or accuracy improvement that justifies spending a full recording holdout on either variant.
