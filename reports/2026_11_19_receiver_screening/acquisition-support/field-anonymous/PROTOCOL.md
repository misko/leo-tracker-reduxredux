# Shared-drift anonymous field receiver

Freeze this one model and its decisions before replay. Sites 01–04 and their repeats comprise development, with sites 05–08 as the disjoint geographic holdout. No measured fits at either cohort are used to adjust the rules. Prior published experiments on this release are known. This is a retrospective blinded replay at eight sites with the same moving instrument, not a newly acquired dataset or an independent hardware cohort.

Read only the first two columns of `main_tone_dict` from the hash-bound MAT files. Keep the released time and measured baseband Hz at 11.325 GHz. Collapse duplicate timestamps by frequency median and apply the already used simultaneous-window harmonic-copy check. Include nonsimultaneous arcs. Retain twelve unique measured timestamps at evenly spaced observation indices in arcs of at least twelve seconds, with at least three seconds between the first and last held timestamps. Eight observations nominate assignments and the last four test them. Both calibration arms are identical. At one RF the scalar `c` is a per-arc constant and subtraction of the early residual mean removes it exactly.

Search a fixed worldwide grid of approximately ten-degree spacing, including land and sea without distinguishing them. Refine the eight strongest early seeds separated by at least 900 km at 275, 95, 30, 10 and 2.5 km spacings. This offer uses no survey point, prior field center or archival query geometry. Separately evaluate the exact four containing and four omitted region centers from the previous full blinded trial. These secondary offers have a historical evaluator placement around surveyed truth and are a matched comparison only. Search each 25 km square on spacings bounded by 5 km and 2 km then 0.5 km refinements, testing all the offered regions in each request.

For each measured arc and trial position, propagate the causal TLE catalogue to the actual sample timestamps. Require candidate visibility. Compare time differences after subtracting the early mean, and profile a single receiver-frequency drift across all arcs at 0, ±2, ±6, or ±20 Hz/s. Each arc proposes the least-residual anonymous orbit on early data, then all arcs contribute bounded squared loss to selection of the shared drift and point. This is a profiled finite-library likelihood with clipped squared loss at 900 Hz RMS. Exact early orbit, drift and arc level carry forward to the held samples. No same-site beacon identities, GNSS location, height, time correction, phase or antenna orientation are turn inputs. The model assumes fixed reported UTC and zero WGS84 ellipsoid height. Every worker sits in a separate network, PID, and mount namespace with one offer and the inference sources only. The controller routes legacy offer IDs, and the scorer reads GNSS only once all predictions are sealed.

Require three distinct inferred orbit IDs with early and late RMS ≤450 Hz, including supporting arcs occupying nonoverlapping windows at least five seconds apart. Require the held score to lead every tested point at least 5 km away, and every other offered region or tested worldwide point at least 25 km away, by one squared source FFT bin. Require at least 1 km edge clearance in offered tiles. A separate matched shape-only null changes each arc timestamp by +225 s when it stays in the recording and −225 s otherwise, preserving the measured arc shape and sampling interval. Rerun the complete point/drift/identity search on the shifted case. Require a held lead of one squared bin over that independently trained negative. Publish bare forced points and all held rival gaps even on rejection. This null tests time specificity; close geographic alternatives remain a separate challenge.

Report held-out sites separately, published accuracy and false publications using all matched denominators, then forced errors, comparison with selected-center and the original full-offer archived forced receiver, both calibrations, held frequency residuals and margins, CPU and wall. The reported wall starts at the prepared observations and excludes raw extraction. Each request has a 75 s deadline, runs alone on CPU 2 at nice 10 and uses one numerical thread. There is no new RF or network acquisition.
