# Independent wideband field data inventory

The public releases and local archives checked on 2026-11-19 yielded **zero qualifying independent wideband localization captures**. The strongest new raw asset is a verified 100 ms Starlink Ku-band sample from UC San Diego. It covers the middle of one channel and has no accompanying capture-time orbit archive. The strongest new longer CFO release is from Bellaterra. It is VHF, and the deposited points were selected using orbit predictions at the documented site. The existing StarLoc and Altrincham panels retain their original beacon and power-spectrum scopes.
