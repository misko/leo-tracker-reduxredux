# From recorded GLRT detections to receiver position

[Browse the 31 distributions from these experiments](charts/index.html) · [21 roof charts in one PDF](charts/fitted_c/modifications.pdf) · [10 location and missing-region charts in one PDF](charts/fitted_c/transfer.pdf)

The compact secant version of v5 is the only receiver in the four-way full-panel comparison that kept all 135 fixes and the original median and upper tail. It returned fitted errors within 10% of each recording's original no-repair error or better on 131/135 recordings. The new receivers can make a decision in about six seconds after loading archived GLRT factors. Their coverage and error distributions show the accuracy cost. The three fast receivers used the same 135 recorded requests, causal orbit catalogues, offered squares and rebuilt anonymous tracks. Compact v5 uses the original native factors and its own preparation and search. Its 121 fresh timings and the three receivers’ independent host runs support elapsed-time estimates. The matched compact-versus-stock timing on six original recordings measured a 1.52× median speedup. These measurements exclude the detector and use host processors. [Recording, catalogue and region bindings](full-comparison/sources.json) · [Paired compact timings](../../2026_11_19_receiver_convergence/position-fit/README.md)

Forty-one recordings reached 1 km with the smooth clock, compared with 36 for the original joint fit and 69 for no-repair v5, all with fitted calibration. The clock reduced in-sample frequency RMS on every roof recording in both arms. Its fitted 95th-percentile position error stayed near 4.75 km. Freezing assignments recovered 14 additional positions and published a new 11.217 km error on DS26-0223. All three fast receivers withheld all nine offers whose squares excluded the roof by 20–42 km. ## What the module experiments changed

| Change and tested boundary | Position and runtime evidence |
| --- | --- |
| Detect all pilot phases with quantized arithmetic | The fixed-point coarse kernel with exact contender checks cut whole-detector CPU by 14.33% on 16 scheduled IQ visits. Both subsequent v5 position fits reproduced the original on one recording with eight substituted visits. [Position chart](../../2026_11_19_position_atlas/screening/fitted_c/dense_q10.png) · [Detector controls](glrt-dense/README.md) |
| Build sparse Doppler tracks | Median track CPU fell from 2.124 to 0.451 s on eight recordings. The five primary recordings yielded four fixes with each tracker. Fitted P95 rose from 1.641 to 3.182 km. [Primary chart](../../2026_11_19_position_atlas/screening/fitted_c/native_simple_tracks-primary.png) · [Confirmation and wrong-offer trials](simple-tracks/README.md) |
| Replay the full ordinary IQ-to-position chain | Two five-minute archived recordings took 131.16 and 109.86 s from IQ to decision on the host. DS26-0036 returned a fitted fix at 0.454 km. DS29-0082 withheld. Replacing the receiver model on the same ordinary factors improved the first recording and worsened the second. [Whole-chain chart](../../2026_11_19_position_atlas/screening/fitted_c/iq_to_joint.png) · [Input and timing receipts](iq-to-joint/README.md) · [Same-input clock comparison](native-clock-donor/README.md) |
| Carry one validated orbit bank through v5 recovery | The two complete workers cut CPU by 6.2% and 6.1%, removed 124 redundant bank validations each and exactly preserved both arm endpoints and recovery decisions. [Position chart](../../2026_11_19_position_atlas/screening/fitted_c/v5_recovery_bank_reuse.png) · [Per-stage timings](v5-reuse/README.md) |
| Compute early v5 Doppler fields only at each track's times | Both complete outputs matched. Ranked-search peaks fell 315 and 210 MiB. Whole workers still peaked at 1,416 and 1,277 MiB, with additional per-track computation. [Position chart](../../2026_11_19_position_atlas/screening/fitted_c/v5_track_projection.png) · [Memory and CPU](early-memory/README.md) |
| Solve only the small receiver block in compiled C | On two real recordings the fixed-owner fit reproduced the host SciPy endpoints within 7 µm in both arms. The AArch64 kernel used about 8.2 KiB for its explicit heap and compiler-reported stack under QEMU. The host generated the orbital linearizations. [Position chart](../../2026_11_19_position_atlas/screening/fitted_c/embedded_schur_fit.png) · [Solver-only boundary](embedded-solve/README.md) |
| Check whether held tracks support the interior of the offered region | Across two fresh synthetic sites the uncertainty-calibrated rule accepted both ordinary containing offers, one of two true near-edge offers and neither omitted offer. It also corrected a known false publication in an exact replay. The initial fixed-noise rule withheld both genuine offers. [New-site chart](../../2026_11_19_position_atlas/screening/fitted_c/tile_region_empirical-fresh.png) · [Original test](region-support/README.md) · [Independent follow-up](region-support/development-noise/README.md) |
| Isolate the satellite ID, alias and orbit phase | On 9,359 common observations both methods used identical CFO aliases. On DS26-0223 the correct candidate was present in the causal catalogue. V5 needed a −9.72 s phase shift for one disputed track, outside the lean ±7.5 s bound. At the archived receiver settings that single shift cut its fitted RMS from about 39.7 kHz to 170 Hz. These are diagnostic replays of a known component. [Position by fixed-owner model](owner-model/owner-oracle-position.png) · [Causal orbit, phase and held residuals](association-evidence/README.md) |
| Give each satellite a timing prior from earlier orbit updates | On eight physical recordings with previously fixed ownership, the history-based prior reduced forced position error in both calibration settings. It cost a median 0.30 CPU s to authenticate and process the previous catalogue. On one of two new simulations the fitted error rose from 135 to 178 m. These fits use a shared privileged mask and have no publication decision. [Frozen protocol](orbit-prior/PROTOCOL.md) · [Scored fits](orbit-prior/scores.json) |

DS26 and DS29 come from one roof. The independent geographic trials above are simulated. The eight previously measured beacon sites supply complementary single-band evidence. The inventory found no additional reference-positioned site with compatible wideband inputs and a verified causal catalogue for this pipeline.
