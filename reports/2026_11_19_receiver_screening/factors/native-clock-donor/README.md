# Shared clock on two ordinary detector donors

These four replays start at authenticated stored ordinary GLRT factors derived from earlier complete recorded IQ replays. Every run reread all factor bytes and reproduced the donor's sealed streaming Graph digest, causal TLE catalogue, cutoff and 25 km offer. No detector or IQ segment was rerun. Both methods used the same train and final assignments, aliases and robust weights. Both use an exact train-only held predictor. The archived as-built joint used its existing linearized held scoring.

The clock reduced fit RF RMS from 176.14 to 149.25 Hz and 147.42 to 122.98 Hz on DS26-0036, and from 206.58 to 192.84 Hz and 149.76 to 139.48 Hz on DS29-0082. Its two geographic arms improved on DS26-0036 and worsened on DS29-0082. The fitted coefficient reduced in-sample RMS and geographic error in both captures for both exact held methods. Held RMS rose after fitting the coefficient on DS29-0082 in both methods.

As-built ordinary joint returned 1.112 km and 0.454 km on DS26-0036 and withheld both arms on DS29-0082. The exact clock-off receiver had the same underlying forced endpoints and published both arms on the latter case under its exact held prediction. The archived compact secant fit returned 0.218/0.180 km and 2.789/2.654 km on the same ordinary factors and causal catalogue. The four separately archived capture-era roles are genuine executions on the same recordings and catalogue with their own historical detector factors. On DS26-0036 all four returned 1.278/1.208 km. On DS29-0082 the no-repair and selective-gap roles returned 0.567/0.517 km, and the two omission roles returned 0.115/0.486 km.

DS26-0036 contributed 22,518,405 factor bytes, 13,530 probes, 3,610 winning peaks and 104 anonymous Graph tracks. DS29-0082 contributed 16,908,503 bytes, 9,736 probes, 2,044 winning peaks and 70 tracks. Fresh clock runs used 4.14 s and 2.60 s of worker CPU and peaked at 142 MiB and 139 MiB RSS with the inherited 512 MiB address-space cap. These timings include factor read, peak selection, streaming Graph, catalogue propagation and clock fit on one pinned CPU.
