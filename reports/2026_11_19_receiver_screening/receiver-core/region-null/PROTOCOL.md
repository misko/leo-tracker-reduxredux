# Causal track-time permutation study

The worker sees a complete native factor packet, the tracking configuration, the supplied 25 km square and a causal TLE snapshot. It runs the frozen native smooth receiver once through GLRT and anonymous Graph, then scores the same Graph without using its orbit labels or position. Neither the offered-tile label nor any survey coordinate is passed to the worker.

Take up to 24 distinct RF/30 s track buckets, balanced across eligible RF lanes. Compare the demeaned curve at eight times with exact causal Doppler at each candidate point. At each track-point-orbit combination eliminate one constant CFO and allow the same bounded phase shift with the same Gaussian prior. An arc can nominate one orbit, and repeated nominations share at most one orbit's support. Search 36 interior and 36 wide comparison nodes in four cardinal sectors, with three depths and three lateral offsets each. Outside nodes are 14, 22 and 31 km from center along the dominant coordinate. This resolves a coarse inside/outside hypothesis while keeping the same geographic trial budget in every draw.

Move each whole arc's midpoint among arcs in the same RF band, carrying the observed times relative to midpoint and all CFO shape with it. Assess 19 distinct nonidentity draws from fixed seed 31206. The observed maximum across all 72 points must strictly beat the greatest maximum from all 19 draws. The winning side must have at least four separately nominated orbits and exceed the other side by at least one orbit-equivalent. Only an inside win permits a position from the original receiver. The time-permutation rank equals 1/20 when this requirement holds. Its calibration depends on exchangeability of track midpoints within RF band and is assessed here, with no claim of a population false-fix bound. Eliminating arbitrary per-arc constants makes the gate exactly invariant to an RF-proportional constant clock term. Existing c=0 and fitted-c endpoints, frequency errors and controls remain separately measured.

Before evaluation the fixed code and this protocol are hashed. Draw a fresh 256-bit seed after recording the source hashes. Generate two anonymous native simulations from random positions in the 2° boxes centered at (31°, 76°) and (−32°, 147°) at causal catalogue ages of 2.25 and 7.5 hours. Supply centered, 1.5 km inside, 1.5 km outside and 15 km outside squares per site. Add one shuffled native negative per site, preserving RF lane, occupancy and peak-list marginals while permuting the complete peak lists among occupied times. Negative controls have no receiver position for scoring. Two archived roof recordings, DS26-0102 and DS26-0070, repeat all four existing offers with their exact native inputs and unchanged tiles. These are two recordings from one receiver site, including previous false-fix diagnostics. Evaluator labels are stored separately and opened for scoring after the prediction lock.

The primary endpoints are retained publications on containing offers and false publications on omitted offers for each calibration arm. Count withheld, ineligible, failed and unattempted outcomes in their scheduled denominators. Show dependence among offers of the same capture, original held and all-track RF errors separately, independent support counts, inside/outside discrimination, empirical-null rank, and observed whole-worker and gate costs. The ext4 workspace uses two single-threaded CPU16/17 workers, 512 MiB virtual address space per worker, an 80 s worker deadline and a 12 minute batch deadline. No live RF.
