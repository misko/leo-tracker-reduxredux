# Prospective frame starts from small causal tracklets

Use complete original four-start wide output streams from DS26-0036 and DS29-0082 for development and DS26-0122, DS26-0237, DS29-0174 and DS29-0247 as reserves. The optional fixed DS26-0162 and DS29-0519 full-stride control streams must authenticate independently before a separate extension. Read one probe's current sample counter and lane. Emit 0–4 distinct integer start centers from earlier winners in that exact lane. Write the proposal row before reading this probe's candidate list or advancing any state with its winner.

Maintain up to eight tracklets for each of at most 64 physical RX/channel/RF/edge lanes. Each tracklet holds a prior point pair at most, its count of associated observations and a local serial for ties. Expire tracks after 2.6 seconds. Seed or advance a track pair only if its integer-counter-corrected fractional epoch drift lies within 50 ppm and its folded CFO difference is at most 1.5 kHz plus 6 kHz per elapsed second. A mature assignment also needs the linear epoch prediction within 20 samples and a second interval between half and twice the first. Pick the eligible mature track with lowest normalized phase plus CFO innovation. In its absence, pick the best compatible singleton by normalized first-interval drift and CFO, or insert a new singleton. Retain the latest pair and the count, evicting the oldest tracklet if a full lane needs another seed. For prospective starts use only the linear integer-counter plus epoch slope of each eligible prior pair. Prefer more associated observations, then more recent peaks, then the local serial. Collapse starts within two samples and cap the union at four. An empty result declares ineligibility, with the original scout as an implied external fallback.

Freeze all per-probe proposals before opening capture-era or current-observation score joins. Score absolute circular start error within 1, 2 and 20 samples at authentic gated wide winners and capture-era gated winners. Distinguish capture targets on probes where unchanged wide has no positive, and capture targets absent from the admitted wide family list even when another wide candidate passes. Use the frozen 2-sample/1-kHz folded rule for the latter. Stratify target margins at 0.1. The same-lane same-30-second-block half-turn of target epochs is a chance control. Preserve each condition's target identities and exclude singleton permutation strata from the paired rates. Report all target counts, proposal eligibility, hits, fallback rates, stage CPU, and genuine four-start scout center comparisons where independently recorded. Current probe candidates, track ownership inferred from future data, site and orbit labels never choose a start.

The prospective study replays winners from the unchanged wide detector as they become available. It measures the causal proposal algorithm before any current IQ or winner is observed. A held saved-IQ hint test may use these sealed proposals as an open-loop witness. A replacement detector's closed-loop feedback and position are separate measurements.
