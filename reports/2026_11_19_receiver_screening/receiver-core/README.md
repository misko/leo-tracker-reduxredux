# Where the fast receiver loses accuracy

Changing the detector inputs accounts for nearly all of the measured geographic penalty on four matched roof recordings. On identical fast wide-detector factors, original no-repair v5 and the compact phase-release solver returned coordinates within 16 m of one another in both calibration settings. On DS29-0082 the fitted error of original v5 rose from 0.517 to 2.349 km when the input changed from capture-era to wide. Restoring the original solver on those wide factors changed the error by 3.4 m. Three original production workers completed. DS26-0122 reached the 600 s cap, and a separate original numerical replay finished in 199.29 s. Its positive control reproduced the completed full worker’s coordinates, conditional RMS and frequency scores exactly. ## What the detector measurements establish

Only 90 of 19,948 confidently matched raw winners differed by a whole CFO alias on six recordings. Across four directly observed track builds, the 295 remaining branch differences among 9,968 jointly owned matched samples were constant within their overlapping pairs of tracks. Changing these raw alias indices failed to recover the large DS29-0082 error. On that recording the wide detector’s Graph added 428 observation IDs and lost 165, and its median track span fell from 24.10 s to 16.30 s. A donor fine-frequency correction lowered conditional RMS while worsening fitted position by 66 m. [Actual winners, track ownership, controlled interventions and positions](alias-attribution/README.md)

Two earlier points on a known track contain a strong timing cue. On the two confirmation wide recordings, a linear pilot-epoch forecast fell within two samples on 1,663/2,012 comparable targets and the same-lane shuffled control hit zero. Adding the Doppler slope gave 1,636/2,012. Only 2,033 of 6,916 consecutive triples met the cadence requirements before the historical gate. The existing frequency-only Graph supplied the memberships using the entire recording. An earlier approach that simply carried forward the latest positive on a lane recovered no missed starts. [Known-track pilot timing](track-timing/README.md) · [Single-lane forecast, real retune cadence and raw envelope control](epoch-carry/README.md)

A causal tracker then kept several earlier hypotheses in each RF lane. It proposed a time center before either receiver’s current results were available on 37,673/104,162 probes. Among 2,877 capture-era winning pilot families absent from the ordinary wide output, 95 fell within two samples of a hint and 462 within 20 samples. Only 59 of 563 comparable missed winners landed within two samples, against 0/563 for the matched negative control. The compiled full fractional GLRT accepted the hints on 24 receiver visits in a separate raw-window witness. Its 16 paired raw windows contained zero archived positives, so that witness verifies the call path. [All eight recorded streams](causal-track-scout/README.md) · [Actual hinted GLRT and raw controls](hinted-glrt/README.md)

Pilot timing also received a separate test as a direct measure of propagation distance. On 204 orbital arcs, adding the physical travel-time term increased held timing error on all four roof recordings. The compiled detector recovered all 30 known signed waveform shifts. On the two held recordings, Doppler alone already distinguished the surveyed site from every tested 25 km alternative. Adding timing supplied no observed improvement in that geographic comparison. [Signed waveform controls and held predictions](range-epoch/README.md)
