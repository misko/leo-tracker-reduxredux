# How many missing pilots are still in the wide detector's output?

The selected weak peaks have already been scored. This follow-up asks how many additional capture-era winners any exact choice among the exported wide candidates could recover on the same eight recordings. This is an oracle ceiling. It uses the capture-era winner on every capture-positive, wide-negative probe. At this boundary no candidate may be synthesized or moved. The original selection, comparisons and thresholds stay sealed.

Classify a probe by whether it has any finite measured wide epoch and folded CFO, an epoch within two samples of the capture-era winner, both that epoch and a CFO within 1,000 Hz, and such a match with positive exact-over-control margin. Record the first failed requirement. Also count jointly matched probes at the wider 20-sample epoch tolerance and the same frequency tolerance as a secondary diagnostic. Keep the exact 750 Hz frame and sample rate, actual RF, physical receiver, opportunity and counters matched. Use the previous strict tests for completeness, frequency wrapping and chronology. Validate the per-recording denominators against the original sealed scores. Conditional availability quantifies an upper bound for a selector with access only to exported candidates. It does not supply a usable online selection rule.
