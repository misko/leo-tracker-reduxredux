# Independent orbital arcs from native GLRT

The receiver published **1/6 matched offers** in both calibration arms. Its only publication was **7.45 km with `c = 0` and 6.74 km with fitted `c`** on DS26-0084. Paired-only fusion published the same case at 6.41/5.79 km. All four original v5 roles returned 1.22/1.26 km there and published on all six matched cases. Retaining unpaired visits provided more orbital evidence and a cheaper search than the elapsed-time path control, but did not improve matched publication or accuracy over that control. The worker takes only anonymous native GLRT lists, their acquisition clock, one causal TLE catalogue, and a single offered 25 km tile. Same-pilot relative CFO calibration passed in 15/16 RF-band instances. The calibration is used to find a corresponding raw peak near the verified pair's folded CFO midpoint. The original raw CFO and receiver clock group carry the fit. Unmatched peaks remain eligible even in uncalibrated bands. A short folded-increment screen evaluates 4,608 full ordered orbital paths per input, versus 42,528–49,968 for elapsed-time paths and 116–833 for paired-only fusion. The independent variant adds 447 Python lines and uses the frozen 409-line pair module, 473-line path module, 82-line C decoder and 323-line orbit geometry module. These physical line counts include unused entry points. The compute reduction against elapsed-time decoding comes with a larger source closure. The sandbox exposes the listed scientific sources and common orbit primitives. [Prospective protocol](PROTOCOL.md) · [Candidate and input source lock](blind-plan.json)

On DS26-0197 and DS29-0316, the receiver retained 825 and 953 training peaks across 16 and 18 orbit hypotheses. Paired-only fusion retained 395 and 258 across six and five hypotheses. The independent receiver collapsed 37 and 19 duplicated points in the diagnostics and retained 89 and 117 held `c = 0` points, compared with 13 and 85 for paired-only. Its local radii were still 8.59 and 6.42 km, above the 6 km limit. When the shared position and clocks were re-estimated without the proposed orbit, an alternate TLE predicted the same held GLRT observations better in each case. On DS26-0197, 66330 scored 36.54 versus 5.36 for the training-selected 51784. On DS29-0316, 63513 scored 38.36 while the training-selected 58444 had no held decode. These are falsification checks on shortlisted identities and do not establish the alternatives as true owners. On the published reserve, both leave-orbit checks beat their available rivals. The paired coefficient experiment uses identical arcs, search limits and phase budgets. The `c = 0` design omits the RF column entirely. Across six matched cases, fitted `c` lowered median conditional training RMS from 712.9 to 708.0 Hz and raised median held RMS from 336.9 to 353.6 Hz. On the sole published case, it lowered conditional RMS from 256.5 to 253.7 Hz and held RMS from 265.8 to 247.5 Hz. Held RMS is conditional on the frozen decoder finding peaks within its 1.4 kHz RF limit and can use a different set in each arm. Publication and actual distance are scored separately from RF. The independent reserve IDs were selected by hash without geographic scores. They are all DS26 because the previous paired and elapsed studies plus the timing roster exhaust DS29 matched archive IDs. The two diagnostics and the omitted cohort include both schedules. The final benchmark sealed nine independent-arc attempts, eight new exact-input control attempts, and eight inherited control receipts matched by native input, catalogue and authenticated request. Total batch wall time was 93 seconds on cores 8–10 at nice 10. Fresh wall times include native loading, sandbox startup and kernel compilation. Inherited controls ran in earlier bounded batches and per-worker CPU is the more comparable measure. Exploratory diagnostics initially hit the omitted width field in the offer contract. The final implementation uses the existing 25 km default. Duplicate exclusion and leave-orbit clock seeds were tightened before freezing the reserve runs.
