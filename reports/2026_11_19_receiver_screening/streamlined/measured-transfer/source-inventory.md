# Independent measured wideband source check

The inspected collections yielded zero independent captures compatible with the Qin wideband replay. Admission required complex IQ or full native detector candidates, RF and an acquisition clock at file level, an independently located receiving setup, enough pilot coverage and signal duration for a position attempt, plus causal TLE provenance. A wider search might expose additional data. These are the sources actually checked.
