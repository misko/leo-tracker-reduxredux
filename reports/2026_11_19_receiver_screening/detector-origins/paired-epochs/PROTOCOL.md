# Two-receiver epoch proposals on the expanded CFO detector

Stage boundary is authentic dual CI16 to fractional GLRT candidates. Base control is the released expanded regional build at SHA-256 `db80b772cf9f52941cc0073067816e571308cb43c3096516b77f73ed54986c66`. On the 10 MS/s dual-receiver short-window API, evaluate each lane’s existing clipped ±1 neighborhoods and also those from the other lane. Preserve every original lane neighborhood, exact epoch membership, existing ascending output order, four heuristic proposals per lane, and the native CFO, candidate cap and gates. Ordinary dwell APIs, other sample rates and single-receiver contexts retain their original scope. Use no geographic information.

Measure DS26-0036 and DS29-0082 development, alongside DS26-0122 and DS29-0247 held. Use segment 4 and 10, sequences 0, 2, 5 and 7, with capture manifests, indexes, and per-window decompressed CI16 all validated under SHA-256. Keep the two receiver IDs distinct even on one frame counter. Use the authenticated capture-era positive list as reference, with one-to-one matching under the existing ±2 sample and ±1 kHz alias tolerances and original fractional gate. Report added/lost capture-era positives per receiver and out-of-reference detections separately. Also report end-to-end timings on the very same window in four rotated repeats of pinned wide, overlay disabled, and overlay union. Position runs are independent and out of scope for this stage study.

Canary controls cover mechanically duplicated zero-delay IQ, equal epoch with different oscillator/noise, nonzero epoch separation, and noise-only. Test both pilot edges, supported ordinary 120 ms dense and sparse calls, single RX and a lower sample rate. Condition real pilot delay estimates on the existing authenticated same-RF/same-counter co-epoch and relative-CFO pairing bounds. The estimate is conditional on passing those bounds.
