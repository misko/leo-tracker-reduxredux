# Absolute clock information in measured field Doppler arcs

Use all eleven existing StarLoc captures at eight physical sites. The first capture in each site group supplies the potential calibration donor, and the three second captures are separate observations at the same sites. Grant the released author orbit labels and the GNSS survey position and height to this conditional diagnostic. Preserve the measured `main_tone_dict` frequency and time, exact per-capture cutoffs, and matching causal catalogue. The source labels were assigned with location assistance. There is no blind position solver in this measurement.

Use every valid labelled arc, including arcs without simultaneous neighbours. Consolidate exact repeated timestamps by median, then take the temporal median actual timestamp in each 2 s arc bin. Remove the shorter arc in an already defined harmonic-like duplicate. Divide each retained arc chronologically into early and late halves. Remove unknown carrier levels using one constant per arc and allow one common linear receiver frequency drift. Calibrate those terms on early observations only. The single nominal RF gives the same answer for zero and fitted `c`, which lies in the unknown level span.

Compare exact late propagation for fixed reported UTC, an effective clock shift trained on the early observations in the current capture, and a median shift from the other primary sites. The effective shift uses a first-order Doppler derivative on early samples and a bounded ±2 s window. At the chosen shift, refit the early carrier and drift terms against exact propagation, then carry them unchanged into the late prediction. Exclude all observations of the target site from its donor clock and residual scale, including repeats. A fitted shift includes causal ephemeris and unmodelled frequency effects.

At the surveyed point, compute the local position information of late samples using exact +/−0.25 km spatial and +/−0.05 s time derivatives. Profile out arc carrier levels and receiver frequency drift. Compare fixed UTC, a 50 ms or 500 ms Gaussian UTC reference, free common UTC, and freely drifting per-orbit epochs. The independent-error scale is the larger of the bin-quantization RMS and median fixed-UTC held RMS from the other primary sites. Also report the conditioning scale using the FFT-bin floor alone. Separately compare held measured RF residuals at the surveyed point and at four predeclared axes 5 km away under fixed and external-donor UTC. Each alternative receives equally flexible early levels and drift. Keep the per-capture conditions matched. These are labelled evaluator controls on held measurements, conditional on height and catalogue, and publish no coordinates.
