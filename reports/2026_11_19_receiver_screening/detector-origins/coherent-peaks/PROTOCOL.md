# Causal three-visit peak continuation

The filter sees the complete chronological ordinary GLRT factors, probe duration, hardware sample counter, actual RF and lane identity. It runs before the existing standalone 78-source native factor-to-position receiver. It sees no capture-era factors, orbit identities, position, reference, later visits, or orbit catalogue. A lane is one receiver, channel and actual RF. Its last two distinct visits with an ordinary gate-passing winner are the only anchors. The anchors always use the original maximum-margin passing peak, even when a prior intervention selected a different peak. An ambiguous current probe retains one passing peak only when it is the unique continuation of those anchors. All other probes retain the original candidates, and all margin-rejected rows remain in the input.

The two gaps must each be at most 2.6 seconds. Folded CFO is unwrapped in each gap at `1 / 4.4 µs`, with absolute rate bounded at `6500 Hz/s`. Frame phase is the integer plus fractional peak epoch and the hardware probe-start counter, modulo `Fs / 750`. Its drift must stay within `350 samples/s` at `10 MS/s`, scaled by sample rate. These physical envelopes follow the earlier anonymous orbital-input work. Assuming approximately linear CFO across these two gaps, the delay-derived frame drift changes by `−Fs / RF` times the change in midpoint CFO. A constant satellite epoch and constant combined oscillator and receiver clock-rate error drop out. Accept a phase residual within one sample at each endpoint, propagated across the two gaps, plus one inverse-probe-duration CFO bin. The gate leaves all passing rows available when two peaks satisfy the relation or when there is no support. It guarantees no dropped positive probe at its own boundary. Track and fix withholds downstream remain possible. It does not use a simultaneous second receiver, average frame or CFO across satellites, or select an orbit.

Strict ordinary factors and filtered factors run through the same published standalone receiver, location offer, track configuration, session timing, screen, and strict pre-capture catalogue history. Each invocation yields both `c = 0` and fitted-`c`. Native-preparation and external prefilter times are recorded separately. The two native jobs use CPU 14 and 15, one thread, nice 10, 512 MiB with zero swap, and the previously exercised 165-second service cap. Six pairs are ordered by case ID before workers start. Original v5 publications are fetched for comparison only after the new outputs seal. Report publication, forced position, distances, conditional RF, and operational failures separately.

The four existing matched factor cases are DS26-0036, DS26-0237, DS29-0082, and DS29-0174. The holdout roster uses the separately published complete-ordinary receipt inventory, excluding those four and ranking remaining cases within each dataset by SHA-256 of `coherent-peaks-v1/<case>`. One case per family yields DS26-0122 and DS29-0247. The holdout captures were not used to choose the rule. All six are archive measurements at one physical location with the true region supplied. These cases measure selector and accuracy transfer across recordings.
