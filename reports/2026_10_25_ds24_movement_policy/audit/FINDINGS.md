# Independent scientific, artifact and reporting audit

The frozen movement experiment and the final, explicitly versioned recovery
control pass their declared construction and saved-artifact checks. This does
not validate a production movement gate, identify satellites independently or
establish transfer to new sites. The geographic evaluation is a previously
examined, same-site development comparison.

## Movement construction and numerical reconstruction

The independent inventory reconstructs all 34 frozen fast recordings, including
the ten missing-contract cases, and the exact 24-case analysis intersection.
The original likelihood comprises 66,110 curved and 11,957 predictive IDs; every
ID has one family owner and the two roles are disjoint. All 345 coordinator file
bindings and the original dirty-working-tree diff were checked before launch.

Both prescribed PCG64 partitions were reconstructed independently for all 24
scans. All receiver/channel owners of each held family are removed from both
training calibration and training position factors. Both c arms retain identical
partitions and physical inputs. The original numerical kernel remains unchanged
apart from the prescribed partitioning and richer, exclusive saved artifacts.

All **144 jobs, 576 position fits, 1,152 joint covariance vectors and 3,456
noise-scale scores** are accounted for. An independent observation-space SVD
reconstructs every joint Gaussian score, with maximum absolute discrepancy
**2.48e-10**. Saved design matrices and posterior precision factors also reproduce
the predictive uncertainty matrices. All 24 complete policy decisions, original
eligibility/adoption bindings and exact paired fallbacks match independently.
The two-job resource barrier expanded to six workers solely through the frozen
RAM/CPU rule; the batch completed in **791.42 seconds** within its 2,700-second
global cap.

Completion does not mean convergence of every fit. Five train-phase checks fail
across four jobs, affecting 0015, 0075 and 0095. Those failures remain in the
scores and trigger the required baseline fallbacks. No fold, observation, seed
or failed arm is removed. Three scans adopt the RX1 pair: 0077, 0091 and 0099.

The new partitions do not reproduce the earlier two-case distinction. For 0017,
one partition supports movement and the other opposes it under both predictors
and c arms. Both new partitions support movement in 0095; it falls back because
of train-phase convergence failures. These are dependent crossfit summaries of
different subset-trained endpoint pairs, not independent replications or a
direct holdout of the saved full-data displacement.

The numerical score is exact for each saved Gaussian vector. It remains a
moment approximation to the original Student/Gamma predictive model, conditional
on inherited full-RF graph membership, labels, aliases, powers, noise and mode
support. It does not integrate training-position uncertainty or nonlinear held
phase uncertainty. The new redaction test checks fixed-position training
calculations in both models and c arms; it is not a rerun of all 576 trajectories.

## Recovery corrections and immutable evidence

Both scans have seven graph proposals and two support-aware proposals. Every
ordinary and final graph endpoint and every final support endpoint was rebuilt
using the original numerical constructors at its saved position. Objectives
replay exactly, and original gate decisions and semantic mode support agree.
No audit performs a position refit.

The original support reporting adapter recomputed its protected set from an
augmented mask, differing from the fitter's original-parent context. The first
preflight missed that augmented-context path. The original support stages
correctly failed replay and stayed at baseline; their files and seals remain
immutable. Repair01 explicitly restores the original protection set and
checkpoints fitted arms before replay. Its real-context preflight compares
features, priors, coefficients, Gamma precisions and objectives against the
original fitter context, with exact agreement. Repeated frozen support proposals
are charged against the original remaining case/global budgets; no graph fit or
new candidate is added.

Repair01's strict preservation guard then counted dormant layout coordinates as
incumbent evidence. Eligibility02 applies the original helper's active curved
and predictive incumbent definition to all four saved support trials, without
refitting. Every originally active incumbent mode and all parent accepted and
likelihood IDs remain protected. The only disabled shape modes belong to the
newly active one-owner nominee. The stricter original decisions remain as a
separate baseline-fallback control. This correction was frozen and audited
before geographic evaluation.

The final support-aware control adds one active curved family per scan, with
49 points in 0095 and 43 in 0087. The graph policy retains baseline in both.
Frequency scores, local information sensitivities, association coverage and
geographic error remain separate outcomes.

## Reporting checks

Geographic reporting runs only after the movement, original recovery, repair01
and eligibility02 scientific seals and independent audits. A separate spherical
unit-vector/atan2 calculation checks all policy errors against the reporter's
haversine calculation, with maximum difference **9.86e-13 km**. The audit checks
204 membership/position rows, 12 summary rows, 16 recovery-policy rows and 1,484
numeric cells. Rejected and ordinary/final proposal endpoints are published
separately; trial-level final eligibility columns are named explicitly.

Native03 supersedes the original mixed-coordinate DBG01 views. Independent
native-ID checks verify all 15,749 plotted source points with exactly zero CFO
or time discrepancy. Headers distinguish compatible all-role associations from
the actual curved-plus-predictive position likelihood, using original winner
GLRT points as denominator. Superseded figures remain in the raw evidence
archive and are not primary report figures.

The movement gate improves fitted-c mean error by only 9 m, with unchanged median
and P95, and fails the prespecified development advancement criterion. Recovery
substantially worsens 0095 and slightly improves 0087. These negative outcomes
are retained; no new ALGO is promoted and no threshold is retuned.
