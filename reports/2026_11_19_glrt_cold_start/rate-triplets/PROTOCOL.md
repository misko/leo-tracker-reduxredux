# Native GLRT rate-triplet receiver

Use DS26-0036 and DS29-0082 first, then DS26-0122, DS26-0237, DS29-0174 and DS29-0247 under the same limits. The input is the complete archived capture-era GLRT candidate set, its hardware clock and RF metadata, and the latest archived catalogue collected before the capture cutoff. Position inference receives no labels, prior position, phase correction, historical tracks, satellite assignments, or prepared Doppler. Evaluate a globe-wide search and an optional run with the frozen single 25 km search tile. Keep the modes separate.

Allow three distinct gate-passing peaks per antenna and RF visit, merging peaks separated by under 160 Hz modulo the 4.4 µs CFO ambiguity. Seed an independent lane with three peaks over at most 2.4 seconds, a first rate magnitude of at most 6,500 Hz/s and a third-point innovation of at most 550 Hz. Extend using a straight line over the most recent seven points, with a 2.5 s maximum gap and 850 Hz innovation. Require 18 points and 12 seconds across a lane segment, cap its length at 45 s, reject cubic residuals above 250 Hz or curvature below 140 Hz RMS after subtracting a line. Keep at most 28 segments, ranked by curvature and cubic residual. Compare seven samples of the inferred shape after removing its mean to corresponding projected orbit Doppler samples.

Use 1,536 equal-area spherical points for the first global search. Retain up to twelve geographically distinct candidates, with 650 km separation initially. Refine at 100 km, 25 km, 6 km and 1.5 km. Decrease the shape residual scale from 3,000 to 180 Hz as the grid becomes denser. For the supplied tile, use a mesh at 3 km and the same final 1.5 km and 0.35 km refinement. Require predicted elevation above 5° at each segment midpoint. At the best shape endpoint, assign up to four potential orbit labels per segment using shape agreement and a common circular CFO intercept by receiver and half-band. Freeze the selected assignments and wrap integers before both calibration fits.

Fit position and four possible grouped intercepts and drifts, with the same robust loss, fixed observations, orbit identities, bounds and evaluation budget in both arms. The fitted arm adds a single linear RF offset in Hz/GHz with a zero-centered 20,000 Hz/GHz penalty scale. Require four distinct satellite identities, multiple RF channels, a median centered shape residual of at most 300 Hz, and a conditional frequency RMS of at most 1,200 Hz before returning an endpoint. Require a margin of at least 1.5 consensus units over an alternative global basin over 250 km away. Record every attempted forced endpoint, returned status, diagnostics and failure. Score geography after both arms complete and report Doppler fit separately.

Before inspecting errors, test a planted synthetic lane and physical-orbit position, an uncorrelated candidate stream, and a null association test. Run at most one worker per pinned CPU, with one numerical thread and a 240 s deadline. Use isolated mount and network namespaces and send the sanitized packet on stdin. Each worker sees its code and numerical packages. The host reads truth only in a separate scoring command after both prediction files exist. Never acquire new RF.
