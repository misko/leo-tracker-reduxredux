# Track-free folded Doppler protocol

Use the authenticated native GLRT point stream and the causal Starlink catalogue. The sample counter and sealed UTC bracket supply timestamps. Assume a stationary receiver at WGS84 height zero. The blind arm starts at 768 equal-area Fibonacci cells with six seeds separated by at least 450 km. The optional prior-assisted arm takes only the original offer center and a 12.5 km radius. Both arms search near their training seeds at fixed scales. The blind steps are 240, 70, 22, 7, 2 and 0.6 km. The offered tile begins on a 4 km mesh and uses the same last two steps. The training objective selects each center. Each recording supplies its complete available GLRT inventory, subject to the same declared point cap and presence gate.

Take at most two passing fractional peaks per receiver, exact tuned RF, channel and second, ranked by fractional margin and separated by at least 1 kHz modulo 227.273 kHz. Form no observation links. Propagate all named Starlink elements with finite physical state across the capture at two-second spacing. At each site, predict ECEF range-rate Doppler, linearly interpolate the two endpoint predictions in each interval and use support at 20 degrees elevation or higher. The native accumulator buffers one site and orbit at a time.

For each orbit, receiver, RF and channel, wrap each carrier minus its predicted Doppler. Estimate one circular constant offset from four-second training bins, scoring at most one vote per offset and bin. Every third bin is held out. The two neighboring offset cells corresponding to a residual have half-width `227.273 kHz / number of offsets`. The blind search uses 16 offset cells initially, then 32, 64, 128, 256, 512 and 1,024. The tile begins at 256. Compare support with equal-density uniform wrapped residuals within each bin, using an added unit variance for stability. These scores are descriptive standardized excess counts. Temporal signal dependence and the wide site search preclude a literal false-alarm probability.

Each site takes up to four training profiles, greedily distinct in both channel and catalogue satellite. A profile needs five training bins and a training excess score of at least two. Held bins can neither select a site nor change a profile's satellite, offset or rank. A site is reported only if at least three profiles each have four held bins and held excess at least three, and its aggregate positive held excess leads separated trained finalists by at least three. The separation is 250 km globally and 6 km in the tile. A rejected winner remains available as a diagnostic guess when training supplied a profile. Report support and score independently from geographic error.

The free constant offset per exact RF absorbs any `c × (RF / GHz − 11.5)` exactly, even modulo the pilot ambiguity. Profile the sum of the RF term and residual transmitter offset in both arms. The `c = 0` and fitted-`c` arms therefore use the same data, orbit hypotheses, offsets in observable coordinates, search budget, validation scores and decision. The fitted coefficient itself is unidentifiable in this model.

Before archive evaluation, exercise synthetic moving sources with distinct channels and analogue carrier offsets, random CFO clutter, a pure uniform CFO null, held-bin perturbations and the calibration offset identity. Run DS26-0036 and DS29-0082 first. The optional timed expansion uses DS26-0122, DS26-0237, DS29-0174 and DS29-0247 on the same frozen method. Coordinates or error labels are opened only after writing results and their SHA-256 lock.
