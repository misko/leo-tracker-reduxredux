# Primary-agent result review

The primary agent reviewed the executed source, result, renderer, and plot,
and supplied three independent unit tests. This is not an external scientific
review. All 1,260 planned rows completed without a recorded failure. The saved
result seal and its source, protocol, locations, and prepared-input hashes match
the current files. The tests verify NORAD-group fold stability, preservation of
receiver counts within shuffle strata, and isolation of orientation selection
from held-angle perturbations.

The fold medians in the report and plot are medians of five separately fitted
held-fold weighted quantiles. They are not pooled track quantiles. Shuffle
ranges describe these 20 controls; counts are not calibrated probabilities of
correct association. The six locations and their candidate identities were
already selected using Doppler. Holding out NORAD groups for orientation does
not make this a fully independent location or association validation.

An additional descriptive aggregation, weighting each fold's held 80% angular
quantile by its held duration, gives the following values for mapping [0, 1]:

| Group | Location | Actual mean (degrees) | Median control mean (degrees) |
|---|---|---:|---:|
| First | Coarse 1 | 23.448 | 28.223 |
| First | Coarse 2 | 22.451 | 26.224 |
| First | Final | 18.571 | 23.564 |
| Second | Coarse 1 | 25.564 | 29.891 |
| Second | Coarse 2 | 26.874 | 29.727 |
| Second | Final | 21.384 | 27.219 |

This is a mean of quantiles, not an 80% containment angle for pooled data.
At this aggregation actual labels outperform all 20 controls at all six cells,
including coarse alternatives. Thus directional receiver information exists
under this diagnostic, but it does not by itself identify the correct cell.
The weaker 95% results in the main report remain material and must not be
replaced by this more favorable aggregation.

## Prepared-input provenance caveat

`PREEXECUTION_AMENDMENT.json` is a copied historical amendment from the original
pointing-cone experiment. Its timestamp and source refer to that earlier run,
not to the new generalization execution. Likewise `prepared_inputs.json` was
generated by intercepting the old cone profiler: its identities, locations,
input bindings, and time-join diagnostics are inputs to this experiment, but
its zero-valued scenario/cone placeholders are not scientific results. Only
`results.json` contains the new cross-validation measurements. The prepared
artifact is preserved byte-for-byte to maintain its recorded hash.

The generalization runner still recomputes prepared inputs on reproduction;
archive/cache access and the bound metadata are required. No position fitting,
new satellite assignment, absolute pointing calibration, or sub-300 m accuracy
has been established by this report.
