## What the original pipeline actually couples

Pinned authority is `/opt/leo-fast-auto/f141dbb30321bc8f9e8183576c511431ea60a6b3/worker/src`. The public [pipeline policy and preparation](</opt/leo-fast-auto/f141dbb30321bc8f9e8183576c511431ea60a6b3/worker/src/leo/application/shared_fast_position.py:37>) uses 24 hierarchical spatial points, three channel views, one shape start per view, six bootstrap hypotheses, staged A and joint B sources, then two exact shared-q final fits. Clock nodes cover the available observations at 30 s spacing. The input is full available inference data, with no pretend development holdout.

At each discovery point, [discovery](</opt/leo-fast-auto/f141dbb30321bc8f9e8183576c511431ea60a6b3/worker/src/leo/application/channel_group_discovery.py:58>) bootstraps identities/timings independently from the supplied channel views, unions those candidates, and fits calibration at that fixed geographic point. The hierarchical refinement priority is the all-view objective. Three separated points are selected using low/high/all fit scores. Thus candidate availability and the visited geographic hierarchy are already response-dependent and mutually coupled. The [bootstrap](</opt/leo-fast-auto/f141dbb30321bc8f9e8183576c511431ea60a6b3/worker/src/leo/analysis/regional_position_bootstrap.py:94>) scans timing −20…20 s in 2 s steps; candidate/track matches and receiver correction seeds are not metadata-only catalogue membership.

The [source construction](</opt/leo-fast-auto/f141dbb30321bc8f9e8183576c511431ea60a6b3/worker/src/leo/application/shared_fast_position.py:228>) adds three offset-invariant shape winners to the three cold centers, reboots all views at those six centers, and makes a shared candidate union. [Seed transfer](</opt/leo-fast-auto/f141dbb30321bc8f9e8183576c511431ea60a6b3/worker/src/leo/analysis/channel_group_seeds.py:35>) transports complete physical receiver/RF correction functions into the target design, aggregates timing proposals, and uses fallback timings for union members absent from a particular bootstrap. It is not an independent geographic-only seed.

For each source, A fits low channels, freezes that complete correction and geometry while estimating high-minus-low constants/timings, then freezes those constants for final all-channel calibration/position fitting. B jointly fits its calibration. Source starts use their own 25 km local disks. [Selection](</opt/leo-fast-auto/f141dbb30321bc8f9e8183576c511431ea60a6b3/worker/src/leo/application/shared_fast_position.py:393>) prescreens A sources with the final shared model at neutral q, chooses a genuine B donor separately, transports its complete physical correction, and compares two strict final shared-q endpoints under the same final likelihood. Source selection therefore changes geography, clock, contrasts, timings and ultimately q together. Neither an A/B source-name change nor candidate overlap identifies a unique cause.

This is partial reoptimization with frozen donor contrasts, not evidence of a conditional-normalizer frequency-gradient bug. At identical predictions, hard visibility and uniform q, the old and shared frequency gradients agree. Subsequent geometry/timing/clock/q changes can still move the coupled contrast optimum.

## Clock flexibility and generic remedies

The actual production callsite [channel_group_refinement.py:31–32](</opt/leo-fast-auto/f141dbb30321bc8f9e8183576c511431ea60a6b3/worker/src/leo/application/channel_group_refinement.py:31>) passes `prior_scale=4` to `JointClockObjective`. [Replay](</opt/leo-fast-auto/f141dbb30321bc8f9e8183576c511431ea60a6b3/worker/src/leo/application/shared_orbit_replay.py:13>) reconstructs originals through that same objective before A/B transport. The class default1 is **not** the production precision. Its [exact matrix](</opt/leo-fast-auto/f141dbb30321bc8f9e8183576c511431ea60a6b3/worker/src/leo/analysis/hard60_joint_clock.py:19>) is `(I/50² + (D2 null).T(D2 null)/25²)/4²`: coefficient-amplitude scale200 Hz and second-knot-difference scale100 Hz. These are penalty scales, not independent posterior knot uncertainties. Use the actual stored/model precision in any ridge audit.

There are two separate receiver residual-clock functions, common across bands within a receiver, not one clock shared between receivers. Their node null space removes constant and linear components; separate affine receiver intercept/slope terms remain, slopes bounded±60 Hz/s. Clock coefficients have±2000 Hz bounds. The gauge removes duplicate affine coordinates but cannot stop smooth orbital/geographic errors or per-satellite timing shifts from resembling clock/calibration functions. The existing half-cold local winning-label diagnostic finds calibration+independent timings explaining99.84%east/99.61%north frequency-Jacobian norms. It is conditional local evidence, not a posterior covariance or proof of global nonidentifiability.

A generic initializer can preserve complete calibration, rank geographically separated starts under the **same** target bank/objective/domain and inspect calibration/timing-projected geographic information rather than pick a region by raw fit alone. But more starts cannot create missing information. Stronger clock/timing priors or tying receiver clocks require independent hardware/calibration authority and tests on excluded positions; tuning them toward known coordinates would not be a remedy. A future physical calibration prior learned from independent recordings, or independent coherent trajectory/pilot information, is more defensible than silently suppressing nuisance freedom until the position looks right. The proposed four-fit assay first tests whether stored initialization matters under one model; it does not claim to resolve this structural ambiguity.
