# Fixed-bank S01 initialization assay

This bounded diagnostic tests a combined initialization effect, conditional on one fixed causal Space-Track 59-object bank. It does not isolate the effects of ephemeris provider, discovery, geography or calibration individually. S01 is specified because the two existing complete public pipeline runs share its exact input; no new endpoint or start is selected by configured-reference distance or requested-window recovery.

The two declared donors are the original published HF46 complete endpoint and the saved complete ST59 endpoint in `/tmp/three-track-investigation/space-track-pipeline-rerun/output/terminal.json`. Both are replayed through public contracts, immutable stage receipts and pinned local ephemerides. The target is the saved ST59 model and ephemerides with the already qualified joint receiver-band adapter. It uses all 4,797 original winning observations in their frozen order, the same broad 250 km regional prior and original parameter bounds. Donor-specific additional 25 km local disks are not imposed.

Transport requires identical observation values/metadata, regional coordinate frame, RF calibration design and residual-clock basis. Physical calibration coefficients and full residual-clock coefficients carry exactly; each receiver's absolute high-band constant carries exactly into the joint model. Reject incompatible frames or transport errors above 1e-8 Hz. There is no least-squares adjustment or silent clipping. The fitted-c state is carried directly; c=0 zeroes only the donor RF coefficient before carrying the remaining complete correction.

Map donor total satellite timing by identity into the fixed target order. A target-only identity receives the donor common timing. Preserve every overlapping total timing, recompute the target common mean and zero-sum coordinates, and disclose dropped and added identities. Reject infeasible total/common timing instead of changing the rule. Both initial states receive identical neutral detection parameters (all target tail coordinates zero, q=1.6/59); donor q is deliberately not transported. Both have all original physical, clock, q and absolute-band coordinates free during fitting. Band sigma is 50,000 Hz exactly once; common/relative timing sigmas remain 3/2 s.

The reused public solver can place an exactly active timing-bound seed 1e-9 s into its box. Qualification discloses the maximum solver canonicalization change and rejects anything above 2e-9 in any physical coordinate; it does not repair an infeasible transported state.

Four outcomes: two donor starts times fitted-c/conditional c=0. Each receives 1,000 actual objective calls and 120 active seconds using the same existing refit port. A single worker is sufficient; at most two numerical workers overall. One external 600 s TERM/605 s KILL cap includes preparation, qualification and all four fits. Source qualification and independent terminal audits are disclosed outside inference quotas. No profiles, extra starts, endpoint retries or station/reference reads occur. Upstream discovery and donor states used fitted c, so c=0 is a downstream conditional sensitivity.

Before fits: component transport tests, immutable source/protocol/runtime/input bindings, exact native frozen/joint J/NLL/gradient/posterior closure, all four seed correction/timing/q/domain checks and actual-data band derivative checks. Source hashes are rechecked before inference. Every raw fit and independent strict/domain audit must freeze before observation-level window evaluation. Keep nonstationary outcomes; no threshold relaxation or accepted-only selection. No cross-donor winner selection is necessary for this diagnostic.

No production, publication, new RF or sealed-evidence mutation occurs.
