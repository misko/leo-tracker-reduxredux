Both cases improve training objective and NLL. On 035 the posterior RMS increases despite lower NLL and slightly smaller position error; these are different measures. The two-case error median changes from 207.410403 to 119.239233 km. All new selected final fits meet the unchanged full KKT ≤0.001 and physical feasibility criteria.

The intervention preserves each original low endpoint's geography, complete common/RF/clock correction and absolute satellite timings during the high stage. Only the two supported receiver high-minus-low offsets are fitted, on their original uncentred physical alias branch. The original full joint final solver then releases common calibration, clock, geography and satellite timing, while retaining the newly learned contrast. Its common/clock initialization preserves the full own-low correction function; every actual final seed has closure checked against that function plus its declared high-only shift. No parameter clipping, new candidate bank, new geographic proposal or truth-derived seed was introduced.

Both arms inherit the same six original own low outcomes per case and make fresh high/final attempts. Every one of the twelve fresh original path replays matches its original sealed source within 1e-8, including missing continuations and rejected stages. No old high/final fit is reused. Baseline 053 high 2 remains rejected at KKT 3.407081. The treatment rejects 053 high 1 at conditional offset-only KKT 0.001089415 and does not run its final stage. Original 035 low 0 remains rejected at KKT 0.001811078 in both arms, with zero continuation. The remaining five 035 paths complete in each arm. These failures remain in the twelve-unit seals and eligible-denominator accounting; no weaker stopping tolerance or retry is applied.

Actual cost is 42 new corpus fit calls: 22 on 053 and 20 on 035, plus the twelve previously paid low attempts. The prespecified maximum was 48 calls; retaining 035's failed low limits possible continuation to 44, and the two rejected 053 high attempts reduce actual cost to 42. Each new fit retains the original 20 second/600 iteration limit. Twenty-four explicit bounded units complete with four single-thread workers in 36.524 seconds. Whole-unit limits are 120 seconds internally, external TERM at 135 and KILL at 140. All units and both case matrices sealed before configured-reference evaluation. Selection uses only the lowest stationary own final training objective.

The two actual zero-fit qualifications take 0.554 and 0.568 seconds. All eleven eligible low common/RF/clock functions close exactly on the original high rows, and their satellite timing vectors are copied unchanged. The 053 low-2 timing minimum slack is 3.55e-15 seconds; it is retained at its physical boundary rather than moved inward. Qualification makes zero objectives, predictions, fits or reference calls. The 035 failed low is represented explicitly rather than assigned a conditional high state.

The v1 adapter refused 035 because it required six stationary lows; it made no corpus fit calls. Its already frozen 053 source and successful zero-fit qualification remain intact. A separate v2 source/schema and fresh namespaces explicitly handle failed inherited low outcomes in both arms. Source commit b03bf8395 and five component tests cover genuine original high/final solvers, durable native serialization, strict conditional/full certificates, both-arm zero continuation after a failed low, orphan rejection and a rehashed wrong-inherited-low rejection. The earlier same algorithm gave mixed results on 009/013 and 023/025, so these improvements do not establish a universal remedy.
