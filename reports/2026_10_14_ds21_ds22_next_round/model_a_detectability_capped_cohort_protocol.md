# Prospective capped A detectability cohort candidate

This is a fixed new candidate protocol for all 78 published development recordings: 34 DS21 coverage cases and 44 original DS22 cases. It does not replace the baseline cohort with successful pilots, claim equivalence to an all-path search, or choose between models by position error. No mass fit run has started. All targets remain configured and unconfirmed independently.

## Two complete sources shared by every view and model

For each recording, freeze exactly this reference-free rule:

1. Always retain the genuine original B full training winner, preserving its own receiver contrasts and original disk through the exact centered-to-raw intercept transport.
2. Among eligible original A-final complete states, choose the minimum **full-view zero-q conditional objective**, including its own frozen 50 kHz contrast prior once. Break ties by original path index. Shared and contrast q nest at zero, so the prescreen is common to both.

The full-view prescreen runs once. Its two source identities, original complete physical/clock states, own frozen contrasts, disks, and zero-q starts remain identical across all/low/high and shared/contrast fits. No per-view alternative source, pilot endpoint, per-case model choice, configured reference, or error-triggered expansion is allowed. Failed or unavailable source transport is recorded explicitly, never silently projected or replaced. Historical legacy endpoints are initializers, not q-converged incumbents.

This cap is a search tradeoff. In the 029 training-only pilot, A path 4 has the best zero-q objective, while path 5 later wins the six-path contrast search. Therefore the capped policy may omit a better q basin. This known limitation is accepted prospectively; neither a favorable source nor a model can be substituted by retrospective accuracy.

## Three matched conditional views

Each source receives full CH1–8, CH1–4, and CH5–8 likelihood fits in each q model. The bank, original prior, full RF/time/clock frame, frozen source correction function, smooth horizon width **1e−5**, physical/nuisance bounds, and original own local disk are preserved. Native observation rows are selected by exact channel masks and scored once; no excluded measurement values enter a view likelihood. The full-training fraction f is computed from actual full-training row counts and frozen across every mask. It is not an unweighted average of receiver fractions and is not recomputed as 0 or 1 for a half view.

These are **conditional full-derived half-view fits**. Their discovery bank, calibration functions, original geography/clock/timing states, and prescreen are inherited from full training. They are not independent half-only A pipelines. Original A did not publish independent halves, so this protocol cannot establish the user's independent half-only comparison by relabelling these diagnostics. Independent halves require additional selected-band discovery, preprocessing, calibration, source and prior qualifications; that remaining work must stay explicit.

## Half-view q identifiability and prior mapping

Each satellite propensity uses the existing K−1 zero-sum catalogue basis and the unchanged detection-budget normalization. Shared q retains prior 0.5||theta||² and coefficient bounds ±2 in every view.

For band q, retain both effective vectors L/H, bounds ±2, and the existing prior

`0.5||(1−f)L + fH||² + 0.5||H−L||²`.

The absent band's coefficients have no likelihood information in a half view. They remain uniquely determined by the positive-definite coupled prior, not by measured evidence. The per-coordinate precision matrix has entries A=1+(1−f)², C=f(1−f)−1, D=1+f², determinant 1. Exact MAP profiling gives:

| Scored view | Absent MAP | Observed profile precision |
|---|---|---|
| Low | H=−C/D L | 1/(1+f²) |
| High | L=−C/A H | 1/[1+(1−f)²] |

Both absent multipliers lie in (0,1), so an observed coefficient inside ±2 gives an absent MAP inside ±2. Owned tests verify stationarity, prior-value equality, and bound feasibility for f=0.01,0.25,0.5,0.8,0.99 and both view endpoints. The implementation should initially retain the original full coupled parameterization and its own non-diagonal-prior fitter, auditing every latent coordinate. It must not reset the absent block to zero, substitute the shared prior, or pretend the absent vector is data-identified. Analytic profiling can be used only after a separate exact objective/gradient/bound qualification. Training J is not a cross-model marginal-likelihood selector.

## Numerical budget and bounded scheduling

Every fit, regardless of view, source, or model, receives **20 s / 2,000 actual objective evaluations / 600 iterations**, with one independent audit reserved inside the evaluation/time budget. Strict feasibility and scaled KKT≤0.001 remain unchanged. All outcomes and own-family training selectors must seal before any new position join. Failures remain in the full denominators. Report both algorithms separately, including strict source counts and missing estimates; do not publish a per-case minimum error curve.

Two sources × three views × two q models = **12 fits per recording, at most 936 total** (312 full + 624 half). DS21 contributes 408 calls and DS22 528, a 70.5% reduction from the 3174-call all-eligible-path proposal. Sources are shared across views; neither model gets a larger source or optimizer budget.

Prepare each case once, then execute its finite twelve-call ledger. Admission must first measure preparation and authority checks under representative load. Proposed case envelope: 240 s maximum numerical work, ≤45 s preparation plus audited source/serialization reserve, **360 s internal / 375 s TERM / 380 s KILL**. No arbitrary deadline growth or automatic retry follows a failure. A case that cannot meet admission is unavailable until an explicitly new runtime version qualifies.

Run no more than twelve cases in a wave with six single-thread workers and **900 s maximum whole-wave time**. This permits two bounded case batches and avoids an unbounded monolith. Seven finite waves cover all 78 cases. Pilot fit durations of roughly 5–7 s suggest around 20–25 minutes for the fit phase with case preparation, but this is an estimate, not admission evidence. Conservative pure numerical maxima at six workers are 52 minutes plus about 10 minutes of preparation; each wave remains ≤15 minutes and is reported before refilling. Do not begin mass fitting until actual qualification and admission proof passes.
