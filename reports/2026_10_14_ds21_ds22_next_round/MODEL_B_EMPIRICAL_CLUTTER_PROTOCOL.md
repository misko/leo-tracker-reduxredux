The conditional selected-CFO model on alias circle A=1/4.4e-6Hz is

`p(y|state,RX,CH,RF) = [lambda*g_fold(y|RX,CH,RF) + sum_visible q/(1-q)*phi_A(y-prediction)] / [lambda + sum_visible q/(1-q)]`.

Here q=detection_budget/K, sigma125Hz, folded Gaussian physics, bank, orbital common/relative timing, complete receiver affine/RF/common-clock/group calibration and every existing physical/nuisance prior remain unchanged. The baseline sets g=1/A. The extension estimates g independently of every model prediction or geography, using **only own-fold selected measured CFOs** in each exact RX/channel/RF lane. Fixed16 equal circular bins anchored at−A/2 have32 total uniform pseudowindows per lane (2 per bin). Bin masses `(count+2)/(N+32)` sum exactly to one; density is mass×16/A. Unseen lanes use uniform1/A. No bandwidth, bin count, strength, temporal weight, signal budget, detector-margin-to-odds conversion or reference-driven tuning is fitted. The background adds no localization optimizer parameters.

Histogram discontinuities are in **fixed recorded measurement y**, not optimizer coordinates: the density vector is frozen before model evaluation. Moving location, timing or calibration cannot cross these bins. Existing hard satellite visibility remains the original model behavior and is audited separately. Gaussian and background terms each integrate to unit circular density, with the stated conditional denominator including all visible satellite odds. Unit integrated density, zero-visible behavior, exact uniform source nesting, all physical/clock/group gradients, normalization, fold isolation, finite inputs and circle equivalence require owned tests before actual qualification.
