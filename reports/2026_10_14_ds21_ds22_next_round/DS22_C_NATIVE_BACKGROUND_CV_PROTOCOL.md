# Proposed matched native background CV: 24 fixed-position calls, no releases

The native background qualification passed on DS22 C053/C075. This next proposal tests predictive frequency evidence, not position accuracy: **2 cases × 3 existing training-derived geographies × 2 source folds × 2 backgrounds = 24 fixed-position calls**. There are no free-position calls, automatic retries or inherited accepted fallback states. The baseline is the **normalized conditional uniform native model**, never the historical raw likelihood.

The three geographies and complete non-geographic starting states are exactly those pinned in `ds22-native-background-qualification-v1`: full/LOW/HIGH original44-bank C053 states after exact half-frame transport, and full/LOW/HIGH selected87-bank no-clock C075 states. For each role/fold, both arms receive the same complete starting vector and nuisance state. Keep original own source disk, all physical/group/timing priors, fitted c, sigma, waveform evidence, native timestamps/branch inventory and receiver lifts. No satellite propensity, receiver clock, bandwidth or histogram hyperparameter tuning is added.

Use the already saved random whole20s fold maps, seed20261014, without redraw. Fit each histogram only on its own fold, freeze it before optimization, and use that same histogram to score the opposite fold. Both arms share observations and target calibration frame. The empirical arm has no fitted histogram parameters: its16-bin/32-pseudowindow density is an immutable own-fold input. Full-derived discovery, candidate bank, frames and initial states remain shared; this is conditional development CV, not independent validation.

## Exact proposed numerical budget and acceptance

Each call gets one SLSQP invocation, **60 seconds total including a five-second independent-audit reserve, at most2000 actual objective evaluations including audits, and at most600 total iterations**. Optimization stops at55 seconds or1998 objective calls, reserving at most two additional calls for independent best-feasible and selected-stationary replay. No Hessian, Newton finish, restart, diagonal preconditioner or hidden extra source fallback is included. Thus this is matched against the unscaled native chart, not a claim that the previous C053 preconditioning result generalizes across folds or roles.

Implement this as a new immutable private native fixed-position fitter derived from the already tested native fixed-chart port, with explicit counter checks added before each optimizer/audit evaluation. Its objective is the qualified native wrapper; it must not reconstruct a folded model. The underlying native `_Problem` and original parameterization supply physical constraints/Jacobians; nuisance limits/scales remain original. Tests must exercise real counter exhaustion, audit reserve and strict original-scale stationarity, rather than mocking the outer driver alone. Runtime actual objective/iteration counts and optimizer versus total wall times are separate fields.

Before fitting, source-bound qualification replays each raw complete state under both target arms and saves its exact physical/nuisance domain. The declared initializer policy allows only the original fitter's sub-nanosecond timing-margin canonicalization: all geography/calibration coordinates and nuisance values exactly unchanged; maximum physical per-satellite timing delta≤1.1e-9s; the second initializer application bitwise stable. Record both states and objective deltas; reject any other projection or infeasibility, with no optimizer call. Canonicalization is identical across backgrounds/folds. If no change is needed, record zero. Original25km/141.421km domains are source-specific and cannot be enlarged.

Persist feasible trajectory records. The minimum feasible objective is diagnostic only, never automatically accepted. A selected result must independently replay objective/NLL within1e-7, preserve fixed XY exactly, satisfy original physical/nuisance constraints, and pass unchanged original-scale KKT≤0.001 within the total time/evaluation budget. If final auditing runs out of its reserve, record unavailable. A solver-success flag alone is insufficient. A stationary result worse than a known feasible initializer is explicitly reported as incomplete optimization; no claim of a profile optimum follows.

## Execution and predictive barrier

Each scientific unit is one call, with bounded preparation allowance and independent external TERM195/KILL200 seconds; internal total preparation/work cap180seconds. Admit a fit only when its full60-second numerical budget and independent completion reserve remain. Use at most four single-thread workers and finite waves of at most eight units/420seconds, adjusting downward for global allocation. Source closure includes recursive imported native cores, fitter, tests, fold helper and exact original authorities. Frozen-source changes require a separate version, not mutation of a running experiment.

All24 attempted outcomes, including rejected or unavailable results, must be globally sealed before any opposite-fold prediction. Only strict accepted source-fold states are predicted; a two-fold aggregate requires both folds accepted. Fixed geography is retained throughout. No reference, original held or locked observations are read, and no new full/half position ranking is produced.

Predict each accepted endpoint under **both** its own frozen empirical density and the matched uniform density. Report the direct density substitution effect at fixed parameters separately from the parameter-optimization effect. This is required by the completed folded-B control: an optimized empirical fit can improve aggregate predictive NLL even when substituting its background density at the same endpoint makes the score worse. Report frequency NLL without priors, sample counts, signal/clutter posterior, and predeclared channel/RX/time-block summaries; retain negative strata and incomplete folds. Do not infer useful background generalization from a lower optimized score alone.
