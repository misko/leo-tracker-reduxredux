# C075 matched fresh 20/40-second continuation control

Both all-origin propensity fold profiles were accepted, but their full-data continuations stopped at the nominal 20-second deadline with solver_success false: fold 0 used 342 evaluations and ended KKT 0.0278079; fold 1 used 235 evaluations and ended KKT 0.00731696. Their objectives were below the sole accepted propensity endpoint. Original iteration counts were not persisted and are not inferred from evaluation counts.

This prospective four-call experiment restarts from **both original accepted fixed-fold profiles**, never the failed full-data endpoints. Each source receives a fresh 20-second fit and a fresh 40-second fit, in that fixed order, with identical full-training propensity model, original 25 km disk, physical/nuisance state, priors and 600-iteration bound. No new seed is chosen by configured distance. There are two fold workers, each with at most two calls, internal 180-second / TERM 195-second / KILL 200-second limits. The optimizer iteration cap is not an objective-evaluation cap. Nominal solver time excludes independent final auditing.

Before fits, three objective evaluations qualify the two source states and replay the accepted propensity incumbent. Source initializers must change no coordinate by more than 1e-12; no silent canonicalization is allowed. Each budget selector includes its accepted fresh fits and the identical previously accepted propensity incumbent. All four outcomes must seal before configured-reference comparison. Strict KKT 0.001 remains unchanged, failures remain ineligible and no automatic retry is permitted.

Each invocation records process CPU time, wall time and returned evaluation count. This diagnoses a budget change under current conditions, not historical CPU contention. The experiment adds four calls to the earlier 23-call fold search and does not replace those outcomes or the original cohort. All discovery and folds are conditional development; no original held or locked observations are used. Configured-station distances remain unconfirmed, and availability alone does not justify promotion.
