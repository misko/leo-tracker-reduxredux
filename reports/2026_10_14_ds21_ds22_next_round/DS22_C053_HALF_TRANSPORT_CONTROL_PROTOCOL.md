# C053 complete-half transport and matched domain controls

This prospective fitted-c experiment follows the exact native functional transport qualification. Both original half winners have a negative training-only outward derivative at their own 25 km boundary, confirmed by central differences. A 1 km displacement increases the objective, so the local derivative is not evidence of monotonic improvement over a larger distance.

The first six calls are LOW and HIGH complete-state free fits at matched 25/50 km radii, and two full fixed-geography profiles initialized by the exact transported complete LOW/HIGH states. All six outcomes must finish before releases. Each accepted full profile receives a 25 km free release; its matched 50 km release occurs only if that full model's own radial derivative screen triggers. Nonstationary profiles and untriggered expansions produce explicit skipped receipts. At most ten fits occur. Centers remain the original source centers; no reference or held evidence selects starts or domains.

Every fit uses 60 optimizer seconds and 600 iterations, strict original KKT acceptance, and unchanged priors/bank/native evidence. Actual objective calls (including the fitter audit) are counted separately; 600 is not an objective-call cap. Independent audit calls are also recorded. Each unit has a 180 second internal bound and independent external TERM/KILL bounds of 195/200 seconds; at most four workers run in finite waves. Original accepted full/half incumbents remain eligible for their respective training-objective selectors. All ten outcomes seal before configured-unconfirmed reference comparison. This is a conditional shared-discovery, unequal-history search experiment, not independent half discovery or an accuracy guarantee.
