# Original native-C search coverage across DS21 and DS22

The configured reference lies inside the original geographic prior in all 78 development cases, but outside every final native-C search disk in six cases. Thus part of the original large-error tail has a structural search-coverage cause. This does not explain the full cohort's median: 72 cases have at least one disk containing the reference, which also does not guarantee that the optimizer finds or selects a good solution.

These gaps are measured in the original local optimization coordinates, not asserted as exact geodesic position-error bounds. DS22's reference remains configured and unconfirmed. The audit concerns the six saved native-C full-band search domains per case, including domains whose optimization failed; it does not assert that every other model uses identical domains.

The full inventory is authenticated against the previously published 78-case source manifest. All terminal/protocol hashes and all six native-C call options per case were checked before reading the reference. Prepared-cache unit receipts and DS22-133's recovered completed calls are handled explicitly. Every case is retained. The inverse geographic mapping is checked by a round trip through the original forward mapping. This diagnostic performed zero objectives, orbit predictions, or optimizer calls. Reference coordinates did not create, rank, or change any search domain or initializer.

This is a historical cohort audit, not an evaluation of later prototypes. In particular, the later DS22-075 87-satellite coverage experiment already included all 16 coarse full-prior cells in addition to the six original disks. Its failures cannot be explained solely by this original-domain exclusion. DS22-059's new continuation, however, preserved an original disk and therefore cannot test whether a near-reference native-C solution would win under complete coverage.

The next native-C coverage qualification for DS22-059 uses all 16 existing coarse cells and their training-derived physical calibration functions, retaining original controls and enforcing the original global prior. No cell is chosen using this table. Broad coverage and correct physical initialization are necessary controls for the tail; better model discrimination and calibration remain necessary for the median target. No full-cohort performance curve or accepted estimate changes as a result of this audit.

A follow-up joins the saved endpoint acceptance and original minimum-training-objective selector to those same domains. It reproduces all published selected native-C distances and unavailable outcomes, without fitting or changing selection. Categories are ordered: reference exclusion is reported first, even if that case also has no accepted estimate.

Of the 49 cases in the final category, 40 selected estimates still exceed 1 km (18 DS21 and 22 DS22). Their conditional medians are 2.205118 km and 2.138679 km, respectively. These subset medians are diagnostic only and do not replace the full-cohort performance figures.
