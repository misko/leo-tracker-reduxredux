# Expanded-discovery channel comparison: 17 DS21 cases

The available after cohort includes DS21-035, even though its missing before-C estimate excludes it from the 16-pair histogram. All results use fitted c. These are original-budget expanded-discovery results, excluding subsequent experimental fixes. The JSON binds the 17 evaluated sources and records all 51 full-band estimates plus available half comparisons.

035 and 029 are the two largest full-band errors in all three models. Model A errors are respectively 188.807 and 116.683 km. On 035, B improves over both halves but remains inaccurate; C is worse than both. On 029, B lies between the halves and C is worse than both. The large half-band errors in both cases require investigation of the common candidate/search/scoring pipeline as well as band combination.

These results cover only 17 of 34 development cases. Neither the 0.5 km median target nor consistent full-band superiority is demonstrated.
