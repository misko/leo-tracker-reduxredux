No starts, observations, satellite sets, priors, receiver calibration, cone settings, or 4 km search bounds change. Both RF arms are included.

205 cases processed. Of 54 initially unconverged cases, 38 now pass convergence; 16 remain provisional.

A lower objective establishes numerical improvement for the same loss, not better position accuracy. The table reports both independently. Convergence does not prove a global minimum. Results are provisional until the intended cohort is processed.
