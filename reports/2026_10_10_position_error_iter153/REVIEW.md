# Independent source and algebra review

The pair expansion, sign and curvature weighting in [DERIVATION.md](DERIVATION.md) are correct for its restricted single-signal, fixed-amplitude symbol-correlation model. No tests, numerical computation, recordings or reference outcomes were accessed for this review.

For a pair, the quadratic phase difference is `(t_m²−t_n²)/2 = (t_m−t_n)(t_m+t_n)/2`. With the stated positive signal phase and negative trial rotation, a positive chirp therefore gives `q_hat−f0 = kappa*t_eff`, not its negative. Minimizing the quadratic phase loss weights pair midpoints by `a_m*a_n*(t_m−t_n)²`. Ordered pairs double both numerator and denominator. For N equally spaced unit-amplitude symbols, their ordered squared-difference sum is `N²(N²−1)d²/6`. A frame-wide amplitude A multiplies this weight by A². Frames have equal weight only when amplitudes and admitted symbol layouts are equal; a common score denominator does not normalize each frame independently.

## Actual estimator caveats

[Native `glrt`](../../src/leo/analysis/native_presence/presence.c) forms raw complex correlations over 64 symbols, FFTs each frame, sums frame powers, and divides by the sum of squared per-frame L1 correlation amplitudes. This denominator is independent of the residual CFO bin for the fixed workspace. It does not alter the winning bin or the effective-time formula under the restricted model. Exact and control templates have separate spectra/denominators; the reported residual CFO comes from the exact-template peak, not the peak of their difference.

The FFT trial phase advances by nominal symbol index and the declared symbol period. Treating each correlation as a sample at its physical symbol centre is an approximation: interpolation, template energy distribution and within-symbol chirp determine its complex phase and magnitude. Acquired-CFO removal is fixed before the residual-bin search. Thus the stated q and f0 should use a consistent residual-frequency origin, adding the acquired CFO only when comparing absolute tracking frequency. The omitted acquired-CFO frame phase cancels in each frame's power.

The native source admits complete 64-symbol frames and stops at the first unsupported frame. Build-time diversity/energy-support options can select a late symbol region, with an early-region fallback. Current source inspection alone does not identify which build produced a historical observation. The frozen estimator/backend, flags, template and interpolation path must be bound before a later synthetic or IQ comparison. The nonnegative `a_jm` assumption is especially restrictive with noise, interfering signals, unmatched templates or appreciable phase rotation within a symbol; arbitrary complex correlations do not imply positive pair weights or the claimed convex-hull time bound.

## Public metadata do not identify the required weights

[TrackingCandidate](../../src/leo/contracts/scanner_tracking.py) persists epoch, fractional epoch offset, tracking CFO, exact/control aggregate scores, margin and admission flag. Probe metadata supply sample rate through TrackingInput, probe duration/start, receiver/RF, counters and source identity. They do **not** supply the individual complex symbol correlations, per-symbol amplitudes, per-frame curvature or signal occupancy. Aggregate GLRT scores cannot invert these missing weights.

[The public projection](../../src/leo/application/scanner_trajectory.py) reconstructs nominal support geometry and exposes an arithmetic support centre and factorial moments. [Its support helper](../../src/leo/application/persistent_hop_trajectory.py) loops over early symbols and skips individual unsupported symbols. This is not identical to the inspected native complete-frame stopping rule, and it does not encode optional late-region selection. That is a source-level policy distinction, not evidence that it affected any examined recording. It needs an explicit parity test before calling reconstructed intervals the estimator's exact admitted support.

Consequently metadata can support a declared equal-amplitude nominal-support oracle and loose time bounds, but cannot recover the actual `a_jm*a_jn` curvature weights. Recovering those would require a separately authorized, source-bound reconstruction of the original correlation workspace from existing IQ through public read ports, preserving observation selection and admission. No new RF would be necessary, but no such replay is authorized here. An operational timestamp correction or position-improvement claim is not justified by this derivation alone.
