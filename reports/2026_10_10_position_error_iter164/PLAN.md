# Full consumed-cohort discovery comparison: preparation decision

The goal remains mean standalone position error 0.4 km on DS16/DS17/DS18 and
newer recordings, with no reference-guided inference. Iteration 163 repaired
a calibration local minimum but gained only 2.13 m over ordinary fitted-c
positions on twelve consumed scans. Do not expand that conditional selector.

Instead, prepare a full-cohort comparison of the native and zero-c-led
**single-pass discovery policies from iteration 151**. Its pilot progression
gate passed; the later negative iteration 154 tested an added handoff repair,
not the underlying discovery comparison. This is the separately reviewed
broader-computation decision required by 151's plan. It is not deployment
approval or a claim of parity with B7's multiple separation settings.

Use the complete previously consumed iteration-107 cohort: DS16 63, DS17 51,
DS18 34 and newer development 45, total 193. Preserve authoritative membership,
including DS16's fifteen members outside the original 48, and all historical
consumption labels. No readiness or quality exclusion, no member replacements,
no new RF collection, no closed reserve outcomes and no declaration of unseen
validation. Report input failures explicitly.

Keep the 151 observations, candidate-generation/retention rules, search and
fit budgets, existing promotion qualification, priors and final c arms matched
between policies. Do not add 154's repair, 163's conditional selector, new
hyperparameters or reference-based candidate regions. Reference coordinates
and errors enter only postseal geographic evaluation. Both c=0 and fitted-c
results, per-dataset mean/median/p95/worst, convergence, fallbacks, paired
regressions, coverage and incremental cost must be reported separately.

Before numerical launch, audit exact source/input/runtime and public-port
binding availability for all 193 members. Establish complete numerical budgets
and expected wall time from the measured pilot, with at most two single-thread
workers. Reuse any old work only on proven exact policy, input, source and
runtime parity; otherwise perform fresh matched work or preserve a failure.
Do not silently treat earlier multiseparation results as single-pass controls.
Source preparation and metadata inventory may proceed now; freeze and publish
the final protocol before any recording reconstruction or objective calls.

All selections across the full cohort must seal before aggregate position
evaluation. Publish durable progress and preserve exclusive claims; observation
timeouts never authorize duplicate workers or retries. Long offline computation
does not authorize RF campaigns. This comparison tests a potential general
search-region improvement and cannot by itself establish a route to 0.4 km
typical error or replace independent validation.

## Staged resource decision

Proceed with fresh matched work, preserving the exact 151 per-member policy.
Freeze all 193 members now, ordered by label within DS16, DS17, DS18 and newer,
then interleave these four groups. Run the first four (one from each group),
review measured resource use, and continue in fixed batches of sixteen (final
batch thirteen). Both shards must terminate each batch before the next starts.
Resource checkpoints do not expose geographic errors or select membership.
Every pending or failed member remains in coverage; no partial-cohort accuracy
claim substitutes for the full comparison.

The pilot extrapolation is 52.9 worker-hours, about 26.45 hours with two workers.
The inherited allowances total 268.1 worker-hours, about 134 hours at two workers
before soft overruns and overhead. Neither is a hard wall-time bound. The initial
four-member checkpoint projects to about 33 minutes, with a nominal allowance
of 2.78 hours at two workers before overruns. This staged launch uses existing
recordings only and does not modify deployed B7.
