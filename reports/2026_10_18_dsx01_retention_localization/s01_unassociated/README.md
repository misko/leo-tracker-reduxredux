# DSX01 S01: unassociated GLRT and graph tracks

Original measured CFO is plotted against scan time. Coloured dots and lines show unassociated GLRT points and their saved empirical polynomials; grey crosses show unassociated GLRT outside retained polynomials. Colours identify the same graph track across all methods. Native alias changes and gaps over 5 seconds are not bridged. Each method uses its own independently fitted position and the final exact-audited assignments. No association or fit was changed for these plots.
