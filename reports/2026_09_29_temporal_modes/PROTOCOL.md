# Optimizer alternatives versus disjoint-panel disagreement

Use the three cold and three timing-grid starts already saved for each of the 18 eight-scan fits. Preserve unqualified starts in counts but exclude them from mode distances. Require the saved selected score to equal the best qualified score within 1e-6. Define near-best alternatives by training-score deficit <=1 nat, without consulting reference distance.

Within each fit report all-qualified diameter and near-best diameter in the model's local east/north kilometre coordinates. Within each dataset and panel family, verify the three panels are pairwise disjoint and cover the same 24 scans. Compare all three panel pairs: selected-point separation and minimum separation of their near-best alternatives. The latter asks whether available optimizer alternatives can reconcile the windows; it is not a search for all mathematical modes.

As a separate exploratory agreement check, freeze a 1 km strict selected-point separation cutoff. For accepted pairs only, report the reference distance of the arithmetic midpoint of their geographic coordinates. This is a descriptive shared-location candidate, not a likelihood fit or certified confidence region. Report all pairs, including rejected pairs and their midpoint distances; do not select thresholds or panels from outcomes. Interleaved balanced sets are scan-disjoint but not separate time blocks.

No new numerical fitting, radio collection or production changes. Results remain conditional on six local starts, site-conditioned candidate banks, previously exposed observations and a reference with unquantified uncertainty.
