# Fixed-bank conditional prediction diagnostic

The reserved-window diagnostic does **not** consistently reject the wrong regional banks. All 32 fits finished in 159.54 seconds total, with 17/32 converged. Only five of sixteen near/far comparison pairs have both fits converged. Signs below summarize conditional data NLL preferences, not significance tests or identity confirmation.

|---|---|---:|---:|
| 6bf407… | late time | +0.476 / +0.437 | −0.008 / −0.054 |
| 6bf407… | channel | +0.286 / +0.278 | −0.040 / −0.040 |
| 559a82… | late time | −0.544 / −0.196 | +0.091 / +0.017 |
| 559a82… | channel | +0.106 / +0.384 | +1.627 / +0.997 |

On `6bf407…`, both T1AT comparisons converge and favor the near bank on both folds, while V16 weakly favors the far bank with incomplete convergence. On `559a82…`, T1AT prefers the far bank on reserved late time but the near bank on reserved channels; only the fitted-c channel pair converges fully. V16 favors near, particularly on held channels, but none of those pairs has both fits converged. Observation counts are 859/916 heldout windows for the first scan's time/channel folds and 620/728 for the second.

Position errors remain separate: first-scan near T1AT fits are 3.21–3.41 km from the roof, near V16 fits 11.16–16.75 km, and far fits 281.61–298.53 km. Second-scan near fits are 5.79–6.36 km and far fits 183.16–198.42 km. RF fitting changes heldout likelihood and fitted positions independently; its effect on regional preference is inconsistent across folds. A better frequency score here does not certify a better location.

For example, on the first scan's near V16 bank, fitted c lowers heldout NLL/window by 0.054 on late time and 0.471 on channels compared with zero c, while increasing position error by 4.46 km and 2.58 km respectively. These fits do not converge, so the numbers establish the observed distinction between predictive score and accuracy rather than a stable RF treatment effect. On the second scan's near T1AT bank, fitted c worsens late-time NLL/window by 0.190 and increases position error by 338 m, but improves channel NLL/window by 0.156 and reduces error by 36 m.

This diagnostic reserves observations from the numerical final fit. It does **not** provide independent satellite identification or clean out-of-sample validation: the bank, association proposal, receiver correction, and starting basin were originally selected using all observations. The near comparison proposal was chosen from the archived V16 fitted-c best-within-10-km result, making hypothesis selection evaluation-informed. Roof coordinates are used only for reporting errors during this new fit, never in its objective.

Protocol: two catastrophic scans (`6bf407cfe8158445`, `559a822227a6a71b`), one near and one far archived bank each, T1AT and V16, matched final fitted-c / c=0 arms, two predetermined folds. The time fold reserves the last quarter of the capture's observed time span; the channel fold reserves the most populated channel separately for each receiver. Every original top-one passing GLRT window participates, including unassigned/clutter windows. Receiver/channel folds test a different conditional extrapolation, not independent captures. The original archived TLE is reconstructed, with no RF collection or full regional search.

Each arm starts from the same associated proposal with its residual c coordinate initialized to zero. Position and remaining nuisance coordinates are fitted **only on training rows** in the original saved local disk, with six seconds and 100 iterations per fit. Holdout prediction calls `evaluate` on the frozen training vector and never refits nuisance, correction, labels, or position. Training/holdout rows retain the full original time/RF coordinate frame, avoiding accidental clock/RF recentering. Bank pairs use identical train/test window IDs and hashes. Thirty-two fits run serially under a five-minute guard.

`comparisons.json` reports far minus near holdout data NLL/window, excluding timing and calibration penalties; positive values favor the near bank. Frequency prediction effects must be assessed within each score method, since T1AT and V16 use different likelihoods. Posterior RMS is reported for diagnosis only: changing posterior weights prevents treating it as a universal comparable metric. Position errors are evaluation outcomes and are reported separately. This is a matched final residual-c ablation; the frozen receiver baseline was learned in the original full-data fitted-c pipeline, so zero-c does not remove upstream RF dependence.

The calibration is deliberately retained rather than claiming a train-only calibration: the archived bootstrap/association and correction support already use reserved windows, and reconstructing a new train-only association/calibration would require a different bounded experiment. Heldout preference can flag a poor conditional extrapolation by the saved competing proposals, but cannot certify a NORAD identity or conclusively reject a regional hypothesis. Limited optimizer convergence further restricts negative findings. A clean confirmatory experiment would fix proposal generation, calibration, and nuisance initialization from training data alone and reserve new observations before selection.

Run with the deployed overlay on `PYTHONPATH` and the scientific Python environment, using narrow read access to the published stores; `run.py` uses the shared `../timing/inputs.py` read-only extractor. `test_protocol.py` verifies disjoint exhaustive folds, frozen-vector scoring, and preservation of the original design matrix frame. `report.py` produces the static paired plot and compact comparisons.
