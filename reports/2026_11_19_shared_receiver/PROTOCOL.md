# Shared receiver calibration and coarse pilot acquisition

Freeze the two experiments before scoring positions or the confirmation waveform panel.

The track receiver fits one horizontal position, an offset and drift per receiver and band, an optional RF calibration slope, and one time shift per assigned satellite. The same time shift has to explain different fragments of the same satellite. Use no surveyed coordinates, historical fixes or per-site thresholds. Choose satellite identities and the region using zero calibration. Then solve both calibration arms with the same assignments, integer ambiguities, robust weights, priors and position bounds. Keep a fifth of the tracks, interleaved in time, out of the initial fit. Require agreement between held tracks and the pooled solution before publishing. Report the best unqualified point separately. Evaluate roof16 and the identical-acquisition present/missing pairs in transfer16, then the frozen larger panels without threshold tuning. StarLoc is the independent recorded installation.

For the detector, profile the original two capture-era producers on the first six windows. The development comparison uses visits 0 and 2 of the first segment. For confirmation use local windows 1, 3, 5 and 7 from segments 1, 4, 8 and 12 of each recording, DS26-0036 and DS29-0082. All geometry, thresholds, fine CFO search, two-receiver fallback and capture exclusions come from the verified producer. Alternate which timed implementation runs first. Compare every returned receiver and candidate field and each archived downstream tracking probe. Report CPU and wall time for a full detector call after IQ decoding and include invalid windows in counts. Small waveform substitutions may qualify their own position effect. They do not establish a speed or accuracy result on complete recordings.
