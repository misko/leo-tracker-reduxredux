# Native held CFO export — NO REFIT

This separately labeled visualization reuses the first random time fold (`time_f0`), fitted c, and SD1/SD4 for all five declared scans. DS26-0309 also includes its two frozen receiver-transfer directions when structurally supported and completed. These choices were made before the predictive results were sealed. Missing endpoints are reported rather than fitted again. Complete unqualified endpoints remain visibly labeled as unqualified.

The exporter starts only after predictive workers are joined. It reconstructs each saved training mask, position and phase reference using the unchanged preprocessing adapter and family SD wrapper, then requires the training objective to agree within `1e-5`. It never runs an RF optimizer, chooses a candidate, reads GPS for evaluation, or updates nuisance parameters from held outcomes. Recomputing the coefficient/Gamma posterior at a saved endpoint uses training rows only.

Each endpoint has a held-native-point CSV and compressed NPZ under `arrays/`, plus a JSON replay receipt. They retain native observation IDs, times, receiver, channel, family, fragment, alias integer, original graph CFO and alias-lifted CFO, orbit and full predictions, radio/smooth/fragment contributions, residuals, and conditional covariance components. `prediction_native_branch_hz` subtracts the same fixed integer alias used to lift the observed CFO. It preserves the observed native graph branch rather than selecting a more favorable alias. Graph CFO values can already be unwrapped across the alias period; the first panel is deliberately labeled as the graph branch rather than a canonical modulo-period raw-peak display.

`orbital_at_reference_hz` is the nominal causal orbital frequency at the saved phase reference. `orbital_conditional_hz` also includes the training posterior's linear phase and existing physical-mode corrections, excluding receiver hardware, smooth calibration and fragment constants. `prediction_lifted_hz` adds all those trained components. A good raw orbit overlay or small held residual alone is not a qualification or identity decision.

The NPZ preserves the full shared covariance through low-rank factors:

`Cov = diag(noise_variance_hz2) + nuisance_covariance_factor_hz @ nuisance_covariance_factor_hz.T + position_covariance_factor_hz @ position_covariance_factor_hz.T`.

The nuisance factor comes from the training-only conditional coefficient precision. Noise uses the approved training Gamma inverse-precision moment; wholly absent fragments use the declared prior. Position uses the saved local covariance and a training-only posterior-mean derivative at ±0.02 km. This is the same unbounded local Gaussian approximation underlying the predictive runner; it does not integrate the nonlinear position posterior, bounded box or exact Gamma mixture. The exported within-block coefficient variances omit between-block terms individually; `nuisance_between_block_variance_hz2` restores their sum, and may be negative. Never add block variances as if hardware, phase and orbit were independent.

Figures show target-family held points in native-branch, lifted and residual panels, separated by receiver/channel. Model lines stop at graph fragment boundaries and gaps greater than 5 s. Gray boundaries and gap shading come from the fixed graph. Shaded ±1 standard deviation bands are local model summaries, not calibrated confidence intervals. Conditional graph/alias/mask selection and known development-panel selection remain limitations.

The entire export is capped at 120 s with at most four workers, after fitting has exited. `manifest.json` records caps, missing endpoints, replay deltas and source/result hashes. Four synthetic tests check dense covariance equivalence, between-block covariance, held-precision isolation, and graph/gap boundaries. No production sources or frozen inference sources are edited.

The completed reconstruction took 9.96 s: all 14 declared endpoints and all seven figure pairs were exported without caps or missing endpoints. All training-objective and all-held unbounded predictive-density replay differences were exactly zero. Twelve endpoints were qualified under the frozen predictive checks. SD4 at DS26-0070 `time_f0` was unqualified for failed optimization, regional boundary and local Gaussian probes; SD4 at DS26-0309 `family_to_rx1` was unqualified for failed optimization. Their figures retain these labels. The 5,900 CSV rows include both models and overlapping split inventories; they are not independent observations.

`finalize.py` redraws figures from exported NPZ files only and builds the publication allowlist. A plot-only wording correction changed the first panel from “wrapped” to “native graph CFO branch”; it did not reconstruct endpoints or change arrays. The execution and final renderer hashes are both retained in the manifest.
