## A concrete inherited omission

The [saved-metadata inventory](shape_support_inventory.json) finds 33 of 89 active likelihood families without any of the original three physical-mode coordinates. They contribute 1,147 curved plus 397 predictive native likelihood IDs. None is predictive-only. Counts by case are 10/23, 6/19, 8/21 and 9/26.

For 0010/69148 there are two active curved fragments, 19 and 28 points, plus a nine-point predictive fragment, all RX1 CH5. The original [support rule](source_evidence/dsx01_joint_marginal.py) counts distinct `(receiver, channel)` pairs among the original curved graph, not fragments: two fragments on the same channel still give one owner. The model retains its phase, radio, constants and measurement uncertainty but constructs no three-mode shape coordinates. Both its curved and old-predictive factors therefore omit that shape covariance. The blocked new branch exposes this inherited construction; it does not create the omission.

The exact [geometry builder](source_evidence/ds23_height_problem.py), [predictive adapter](source_evidence/ds23_predictive_linear.py), [event adapter](source_evidence/ds23_frequency_events.py), [mode builder](source_evidence/build_dsx01_orbit_modes.py) and source hashes are archived. Any next study should reuse that control and first measure fixed-state sensitivity, preserving association/support gates and candidate ownership.
