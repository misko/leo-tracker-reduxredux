draws and the generating-identity rule are frozen without their responses.
There is no geographic truth, position fitting, GLRT detection/selection replay,
new RF or operational identity acceptance rule.

and four unresolved trials total. The generating identity is sampled uniformly
without replacement from catalogue indices absent from every original protected,

±15 s, positive width, and visibility at the causal location and operational
