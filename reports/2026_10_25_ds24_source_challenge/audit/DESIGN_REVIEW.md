The coordinator's two bounded branches are scientifically useful with the
following interpretation. No position fit, geographic truth or operational
association decision belongs to either branch.

**Portions.** Use all six metadata-fixed masks plus whole-W control (seven
masks, fourteen matched-arm screens), unchanged native observations,
original CH5 discovery-cavity conditional background and each arm's calibration
at the same saved c0 RF position. Slice every catalogue/noise/time/RF array on
the same mask. Reconstruct per-ID causal-mean visibility once: the archived
whole-source minimum cannot determine visibility on a portion. Preserve the
ordinary nearest-alias Gaussian-phase screen and complete catalogue ordering;
57450 must not be reserved. The whole-W control keeps the alternative of one
different satellite visible before assuming source splitting. Unrestricted
alias and affine phase estimates
are proposals, with operational-range flags, not exact orbit fits. Overlapping
prefix/suffix rows are not independent evidence, and the historical duration
and curvature requirements remain unchanged. A promising ordinary nominee
would justify a later declared joint prediction test, not certify identity now.

**Matched-law control.** One fixed exogenous background per arm must be held
unchanged for every physical candidate and proper unresolved comparator.
There is no response-selected cavity after generation. The six vectors are
generated under c0; fitted-c uses those same vectors as an explicitly off-law
sensitivity. Candidate/phase/shape/alias/visibility construction must match
between generation and exact c0 scoring. The original coarse catalogue screen
is intentionally approximate and is the algorithm being tested; it need not
be an exact marginal of that law. Geometry-only confusable-pair design precedes
new CFO draws, and the screen receives no truth or source-kind fields. A
label-available diagnostic remains separate from the ordinary shortlist.
Save every miss without backfill. A physically centered proper smooth null
can test near-confusion if its mean/covariance are fixed before the draws;
it is a declared synthetic comparator, not empirical clutter.

The matched background is an exogenous target-control law even when its
numerical covariance originates in old calibration. A hypothetical satellite
sharing an old parent's label still has a fresh target-specific prior here;
this does not reproduce the actual shared-family conditional hierarchy,
winner-selected GLRT observations or an end-to-end false-association rate.
One noisy incorrect rank is not itself a bug. Distinguish lost nomination,
model ambiguity and numerical abstention before selecting the next code change.

Both branches need a real input-load smoke before freeze, with exact IDs,
array dimensions, common positions, finite positive covariance and charged
setup time. Independent numerical checks should exercise dense density
normalization and injection closure without repeating corpus propagation.
Preserve original failures and cumulative 180/600-second scientific budgets.
