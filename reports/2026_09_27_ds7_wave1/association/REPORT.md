The RF audit confirms that 11.2 GHz prediction is required by the measurement contract. In the pinned source, `PersistentHopTrajectoryConfig.canonical_rf_hz` is 11.2 GHz; `_lane_tracklets` scales raw CFO by `canonical_rf_hz / actual_rf_hz`; the episode graph stores `normalized_dealiased_cfo_hz` as `measured_cfo_hz`; and `prepare_adaptive_tle_position_inputs` exports that graph field as `measured_hz`. The track values from 10.94 to 11.69 GHz are physical channel provenance, not the scale of the exported measurement. Using per-track RF in prediction without reversing the upstream normalization would be wrong. The gate's training-selection digest therefore excludes physical `rf_hz`, and a regression test verifies that changing this metadata cannot alter selection.
