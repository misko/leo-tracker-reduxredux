# Second field installation

The three evaluation hours contain measured SDR power spectra from Bob Watson’s monitoring installation in Altrincham, UK. The acquisition repository gives a WGS84 reference of 53.39820° N, 2.34860° W at 27 m. This is the author’s documented station reference, with no independently reported survey uncertainty. [Data deposit](https://doi.org/10.5281/zenodo.8400658), [station coordinate in author code](https://github.com/DrWhatson/Starlink_Monitoring_station/blob/a922487260752648a7e4d67984e2568f0ddc59c4/bin/get_Transits#L84), [instrument paper](https://www.cambridge.org/core/services/aop-cambridge-core/content/view/4C58E5B0E5750C51C3FA6777D83A5520/S1743921324001042a.pdf/starlink_in_your_backyard_a_diy_approach_to_satellite_monitoring_and_data_analysis.pdf).

## Recorded windows and catalog

The evaluation windows were selected as the three consecutive complete hours from 03:00 to 06:00 UTC on 11 March 2023 before reading their spectral detections. The 00:00 hour provided a separate frequency-bin baseline and preflight. A subsequent study of the archive analyzed 00:00 to 03:00 UTC and reported a fitted effective time shift for those separate hours. The benchmark preserves recorded UTC seconds, applies no shift, and retains the source timing uncertainty. [Archive format](https://zenodo.org/api/records/8400658/files/readme.txt/content), [earlier use of the archive](https://arxiv.org/html/2611.02377v1#S3.SS1).

Each CSV contains 3,600 consecutive one-second spectra. After discarding the repeated edge value emitted by `rtl_power`, each spectrum has 1,024 power bins. The IF grid starts at 541,780,000 Hz with exact spacing 1,000,000/1,024 Hz. The CSV rounds the step to 976.56 Hz. The author adds a 10,782,720,000 Hz LNB LO, putting the midpoint at 11,325,000,000 Hz. The SDR clock, the LNB LO, and the one-second power integration limit the effective time and frequency precision. [Logger launch](https://github.com/DrWhatson/Starlink_Monitoring_station/blob/a922487260752648a7e4d67984e2568f0ddc59c4/launch_SDRs.py#L27), [RF conversion](https://github.com/DrWhatson/Starlink_Monitoring_station/blob/a922487260752648a7e4d67984e2568f0ddc59c4/bin/Plot_all_10m#L324), [CSV writer](https://github.com/osmocom/rtl-sdr/blob/master/src/rtl_power.c#L721).

The archived `230311/starlink.tle` includes 3,783 Starlink entries. The ZIP extended Unix modification timestamp is 2023-03-10 23:15:03 UTC. The author documents retrieval before midnight for the following day and the acquisition script names CelesTrak as the source. All line-one epochs precede every evaluation hour. The benchmark uses the archived catalog bytes and the ZIP member timestamp. [Archive format](https://zenodo.org/api/records/8400658/files/readme.txt/content), [author retrieval script](https://github.com/DrWhatson/Starlink_Monitoring_station/blob/a922487260752648a7e4d67984e2568f0ddc59c4/bin/get_TLEs#L27).

The retrieval script checked that every response was HTTP 206 with the expected Content-Range and that every decompressed member matched its original ZIP CRC32 and size. The four spectra members total 22.3 MB of compressed transfers. The test retrieved the OneWeb catalog for the provenance check. The evaluation uses only the Starlink catalog and applies the Starlink comb check before the solver sees the data. ## Signal extraction and outcome

`extract_tracks.py` is the initial signal-only extractor frozen for this panel. It subtracts the preflight per-bin temporal median and a 17-bin local median. It scores narrowband excess on nine teeth separated by 45 bins, approximately 43.945 kHz, and links detected centers by frequency continuity. It retains at least 13 measured centers with decreasing frequency, a quadratic residual under one bin, and power support on both extremal teeth and at least six of the nine teeth. It rejects comparable out-of-comb power and groups overlapping candidates consistent with the same physical comb. The extraction receives no station coordinates, catalog, transit annotations, or model output. The signal signature is documented for Starlink downlink tones. [Measured tone spacing](https://link.springer.com/article/10.1186/s43020-025-00163-y), [comb discrimination from OneWeb](https://www.ion.org/plans/abstracts.cfm?paperID=12018).

The preflight hour yielded one 16-point track over 15 elapsed seconds. The three evaluation hours yielded 295, 296 and 385 candidate trajectories, respectively, and zero accepted tracks in all six half-hours. These evaluations establish withholding behavior on recorded inputs under the documented detector. They supply zero position estimates and zero location-error samples. Several evaluation intervals contain short or partial comb candidates whose center-frequency identity and continuity fail this extraction’s acceptance rule.

The public panel consists of six independently shuffled four-region offers on three measured hours. Each hour shares the same measured observation and catalog across a matched pair of offers. One offer includes a 25 km square that contains the documented station reference. The other has four squares outside the reference. The shifts for the containing square were drawn independently on each local axis within ±8 km. Each decoy is displaced at least 34 km on one local axis. The generator checks overlap of squares in each offer. `truth.json` and `source_windows.json` preserve the realization and measured-source hashes.

For reproduction, copy `fetch_archive.py` and `extract_tracks.py` to a scratch directory. Download `monitor-dir.bin` using HTTP Range `bytes=1050817010-1050841170` from the archive member endpoint. Run the retrieval script from the scratch directory and then run `extract_tracks.py 0 3 4 5` with NumPy and SciPy installed. The original extractor script’s SHA-256 is `d30543d73f390a302ab40f1506213b9883ad1dd4ad993c0c81a8853cc705552c`.

## Other source evaluated

Tomáš Heinl’s public thesis annex contains measured Doppler series. A bounded byte-range read recovered one 100,700-byte MAT file with three measurement series and 1,118 stored raw-frequency rows. The released extraction code sets `IDENTIFY_AT_TRUTH = true` before selecting and following the peaks around satellite predictions. The annex inventory contains no raw spectra or causal TLE snapshot. The local historical catalog starts later than those April–May 2026 recordings. These conditions prevent receiver-independent use of the published rows in this blind panel.
