# Next evidence-role test, conditional on the current mechanism result

Preparation memo, 2026-10-25. Written while execution03 runs; no execution03
results, additional propagation, position fits or geographic selection used.

If retaining the declared shape prior reduces 0095's harmful pull, the supported
conclusion would be that deleting an unsupported correction can delete important
uncertainty. It would not establish 65500's identity, an actual orbit error, or
the safety of all predictive additions. Covariance also changes the normalized
log-volume term; more covariance need not monotonically reduce position movement.

## Candidate rule to freeze

Keep three separate evidence roles. Original qualified curved observations may
calibrate parent receiver, phase, noise and supported orbital coordinates. An
otherwise eligible new source may be tested as a predictive factor, carrying
the original causal phase distribution, physical shape prior, source constant
and shared parent covariance. A one-owner source must not become a calibrated
orbit family merely because it fits or pulls position consistently. Preserve
the original support rule for learning orbital corrections.

The predictive target cannot update fixed-position parent calibration or noise.
Its factor must be joint with existing predictive evidence, or the exact
conditional increment given that evidence. A local phase integral and any
latent shape posterior are conditional inference under the assigned identity;
they are not an independently calibrated timing/orbit correction. In particular,
do not replace the original prior by a tighter target-fitted prior on reuse.

Keep nomination, likelihood membership and accepted association as separate
ledger fields. Frozen NORAD/alias choices mean "conditional on this candidate",
not identity truth. Promotion still needs unchanged original association and
owner-support requirements, with no target self-confirmation. If the predictive
role lacks an established association qualification rule, retain it as research
likelihood input and keep the exact operational baseline. Additional points or
an attractive endpoint cannot supply the missing qualification.

## Why earlier selectors do not supply that qualification

[REVIEW.md](../REVIEW.md) records that the fresh DS24 held-family policy accepts
three RX1 replacements: one improves geographically and two worsen; its fitted-c
mean gain is only about 9 m and median/P95 are unchanged. The earlier 0017/0095
separation did not reproduce; 0095's fresh held scores support movement and its
fallback instead follows failed training-phase convergence. Do not reuse that
selector with new seeds or thresholds as an identity or safety certificate.

The published [ALGO04 controlled recovery report](https://github.com/misko/leo-tracker-reduxredux/blob/main/reports/2026_10_23_algo04_controlled_recovery_tests/README.md), verified
from the clean publisher's `HEAD`, also rejects common-family ranking. Its three
largest-family composite uses full-fit positions, Gaussian/Gamma approximations
and a 0.95 signal/0.05 clutter mixture. It selects no replacement, including
useful controls; the 0256 gains 2.20/1.65 fall below the frozen 3.22 penalty and
0404 has a roughly 15.5 kHz uncertainty-width failure. It is neither full
cross-validation nor a calibrated identity probability. A residual-family rank
also omits coupled prior/noise/volume costs and can reverse the complete score's
direction. These negative results argue against adding another score threshold
to the same reused endpoints.

## One bounded next test

After sealing and interpreting this pilot, inventory existing archived proposals
without propagation or new nomination. Freeze the first **four** eligible new
one-owner families in a declared wider fast-scan archive, sorted by recording
UTC, scan ID and source observation ID, excluding 0095/0087. Pin the archive and
eligibility before looking at their geographic effects. One source per scan;
preserve all original parent IDs. If fewer than four exist, use that exact smaller
set and stop: do not fill it with a fresh candidate search or favorable cases.
The same-site, previously exposed corpus is a transfer panel, not unseen accuracy
validation. Independently supported owners or archived causal alternatives are
retained as identity challenges, never assumed to confirm the nomination.

Test one policy candidate: predictive-only with the existing physical prior,
against each exact saved baseline. At fixed baseline positions and +/-10 m axes,
compare zero/prior target scores and derivatives (at most 80 branch evaluations).
Then at most **eight fits**, four scans x matched c0/fitted-c, using the prior
branch only, identical cold starts and original budgets/gates. This next test
assesses transfer of the evidence role; it does not repeat the current causal
zero-versus-prior endpoint comparison. Freeze phase-rule accuracy before fitting,
check final-reference endpoint accuracy, and preserve numerical failures/fallbacks.
Cap the entire test at 20 minutes, two workers, one BLAS thread each and 180 s per
arm; no rescue, variance tuning, added nodes after fitting, or retries. Lack of
a numerically adequate fixed rule is a valid stopping outcome.

Seal RF outputs before geography. Report parent and target densities, complete
score decomposition, accepted/likelihood counts, position movement, c-arm error
changes and every failure separately. Keep the baseline operational until a
distinct target-association rule is qualified; do not count these likelihood
inputs as newly validated associations.

## Falsification and decision

Close the specific 0095 covariance explanation if the current matched branches
do not materially distinguish its influence, or if that distinction disappears
under the required endpoint numerical check. Do not widen the prior to repair it.
On the wider panel, any parent-calibration leakage, changed observation/noise
binding, covariance double-counting or unsupported learned correction invalidates
the role implementation. Failure against an independently held owner/causal
alternative leaves identity unresolved even if the conditional density is good.

A single geographic regression falsifies a claim that this role guarantees safe
movement. Retain and display it; do not create an exception. Preventing a large
regression relative to a shape-zero/support control is not improvement over the
baseline. Require both c-arm means to improve without worse median/tail before
claiming even panel-level performance improvement. Even favorable four-case results justify
only a separately frozen wider assessment, not an algorithm release or new-location
claim. No further selector or campaign is authorized by this memo.
