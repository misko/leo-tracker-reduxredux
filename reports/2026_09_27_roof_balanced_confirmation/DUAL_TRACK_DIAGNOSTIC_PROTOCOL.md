# Fixed-point diagnostic of dual-effect geographic regressions

This is a post-outcome diagnostic, not an additional estimator or confirmation
test. The complete dual geographic result is frozen and negative overall.
Investigate SECOND indices 0 and 1 (339af and 53ce): the former loses earlier
geometry gains under ratio uncertainty, while the latter retains a Sacramento
regression and repairs a Reno regression. Report both priors of both recordings;
do not select only favorable tracks or priors.

For each independent prior, evaluate only that prior's saved Doppler-only,
original-geometry, shared-detection, and dual-effect winning coordinates from
the completed dual replay. Deduplicate coincident coordinates within that prior.
No reference coordinate, extra search points, new fitting, threshold changes,
track exclusion, or candidate borrowing is allowed. Every coordinate builds its
own full-catalogue training-only frequency shortlist and CFO, exactly as in the
frozen replay. Shared orbit propagation is permitted, not shared fitted
identities. Do not use these diagnostics to alter any saved estimator output.

Use the accepted frozen geometry, detection-offset and ratio-offset models,
zero timing, identical reception rows, full reserve denominators and occupied-
second track weights. Bind input/cache/evidence/snapshot/topology, model and
calibration artifacts, completed replay and report, protocol and code. Refuse
overwriting outputs. Reproduce every variant and score arm at every selected
point to absolute 1e-7; require finite outputs and accepted quadrature checks.

Export each track's IDs, training prior, CFO, reserve count and weight; candidate
frequency, detection and ratio log likelihoods (including reversed controls);
per-track score arms; and normalized frequency/joint identity probabilities.
Differentiate the training-only frequency prior from the frequency posterior
that also includes reserve observations. All satellite IDs remain model
associations, not decoded physical truth.

Attribute differences between the saved competing positions using exact
weighted per-track changes in Doppler score, detection increment and conditional
ratio increment. These increments telescope under a shared-candidate marginal
likelihood and are not independent reception marginals. Check reconstruction
against aggregate scores. Count shortlist, frequency-MAP and joint-MAP changes
separately. Stable MAP does not imply unchanged mixture weights or prove a
fixed-identity causal mechanism. Any same-candidate comparison must be explicitly
conditional and report unsupported/missing candidate cases rather than borrow
an identity from another prior.

The purpose is to distinguish evidence for local score bias from association
changes and identify a calibration-grounded next experiment. It cannot establish
better tracking resolution by itself. Keep the unused confirmation recordings
untouched, and do not tune weights or select model variants using known errors.
