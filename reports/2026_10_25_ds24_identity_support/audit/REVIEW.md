**Independent review: what the fast-scan evidence supports**

The strongest verified limitation is weak separation of position from orbital
timing/shape and shared receiver terms, conditional on assumed identities. The
evidence does not identify a unique physical cause of the real errors. The next
useful work is identity/selection closure, followed by testing whether an
additional source supplies a different constraint. Numerical support repair is
an enabling correction to that test, not a localization improvement by itself.
This review uses saved reports and source reads only; it runs no propagation,
fit, simulation, IQ processing or new geographic evaluation.

**Three supported bottlenecks, with their limits**

1. **Nuisance ambiguity and repeated geometry.** Across the 24 DS24 cases, the
   median local curved-data weak-axis SD is 534 m, versus 386 m with receiver
   terms known, 110 m with timing known and 56 m with timing and shape known.
   The median strongest phase/weak-position correlation is .935. Band halves
   share 11 families and their weak axes differ by only 17.7 degrees. These are
   conditional model diagnostics, not achieved accuracy, calibrated intervals
   or a physical error floor. They explain why more CFO samples can repeat the
   same uncertain constraint. The synthetic factorial, with known labels and
   unchanged inference, supports contributions from both generating timing and
   shape; its observation-level B(phi) interaction prevents attributing the
   endpoint interaction solely to inference error.
   [Information evidence](/srv/bulk/leo/research/ds24-bottlenecks-20261024/information/REPORT.md),
   [factorial and later validation](/srv/bulk/leo/research/ds24-phase-shape-validation-r01/README.md).

2. **Identity and discovery exposure remain unresolved.** In the four real
   identity challenges, 0011/58175 beats 63312 by only .384/1.289 units of
   minus-twice-log predictive density. On 0012, 57743 and 66259 exchange rank
   between saved c-arm states, each by about 1.4 units. Those states differ in
   position and calibration, so the reversal is not c alone. The 0013 complete-
   owner tests favor 60108, but all owners are RX1 and were historically found
   under that assignment. Repeated labels and overlapping folds are not
   independent identity confirmations. The old nominee-retention rule did not
   change those four actual top-eight candidate sets; forced retention is not
   established as the cause of these ambiguities.
   [Real competition and owner tests](/srv/bulk/leo/research/ds24-identity-components-r01/identity/FINDINGS.md),
   [ordinary-screen check](/srv/bulk/leo/research/ds24-nomination-information-r01/archive_shortlist_check.json).

3. **Better RF fit and more model flexibility do not reliably select better
   positions.** The frozen RX1 basis gave about 100 m fitted-c development mean
   gain, but only 15.4 m on the later eight, with worse P95 and maximum; c0 mean
   gain was 2.7 m. Retaining missing parent orbit covariance materially changed
   information and gradients, yet all four new candidate pairs failed the
   predeclared conservative research rule. Their raw means worsened 8.4/9.9 m;
   every operational policy stayed at the reference. All full-mean primitive
   residual checks passed while unsupported-owner checks failed; conservative
   residuals failed even with the original support set frozen. Neither gate
   view establishes calibrated physical corrections or justifies a waiver.
   [Later validation](/srv/bulk/leo/research/ds24-phase-shape-validation-r01/README.md),
   [completed parent uncertainty experiment](/srv/bulk/leo/research/ds24-parent-orbit-uncertainty-r01/README.md).

Sample rate is not identified: all 24 channel-control scans are 10 MS/s, and
same-rate receiver differences remain. Geometry matters under the model, but
the 40-degree nominal feed separation is not a measured bearing or two-station
baseline. Identity error is possible but no real nominee here has authenticated
truth. Receiver discrepancy is measured, but its physical origin is unidentified.
Engineering orbit priors, robust/variational approximations and response-selected
membership remain possible contributors. These alternatives cannot be separated
by subtracting synthetic and real mean errors.

**Experiments not worth repeating unchanged**

Thirty continuous restarts already agreed within 2.06 cm for the tested labels;
four transferred label starts gave no qualified better full-data result.
Remeasuring 322 RX2 curves reduced median fixed-degree scatter 78.07 to 22.18 Hz
without qualified cohort accuracy gain. More native-point recovery increased
coverage and worsened the tail. The four chronological predictive-prior additions
gave two geographic gains and two regressions per c arm, with no validated new
identity. Blanket parent uncertainty, arbitrary receiver bases, phase-width
sweeps, unchanged soft-label tests and GPS-selected family deletions have no
current justification as the next broad campaign.
[Accumulated controls](/srv/bulk/leo/research/ds24-phase-shape-validation-r01/README.md),
[four-source transfer](/srv/bulk/leo/research/ds24-fast-identifiability-r01/transfer/FINDINGS.md).

Cross-satellite ephemeris dependence is also **existing prior work**. Neighbor
phase-mean forecasting slightly worsened the 410-case fitted-c mean. Geometry
covariance qualified 0/24 elliptical-Student contexts; a Student-marginal-
preserving Gaussian copula qualified 2/24, with final latent correlation .03.
This is limited positive proxy evidence, not proof of independence or a tested
DS24 localization improvement. A generic publication/plane covariance proposal
would repeat existing work. [Exact scope and saved results](CROSS_FAMILY_PRIOR_ART.md).

**Three discriminating next tests, in order**

1. **Close identity support and selection before moving a position.** First
   replay the same 12 sealed nomination observations with the minimal numerical
   correction: zero likelihood on invisible phases under the original phase
   normalization, certified support intervals, stable integrals and the existing
   absolute 1e-3 objective criterion. Then, only under a separately frozen design,
   use two layouts and a small response-free confusable-pair/null panel (six
   fresh observations is sufficient for falsification, not calibration).
   Ordinary full-catalogue nomination must receive the injected observations;
   no true-label backfill. Generation, nomination and primary exact scoring
   must share a declared conditional background. Keep the old response-dependent
   shortlist cavity as a separate sensitivity: it removes data that generated
   the old draws. For active-parent labels, fresh independent target priors are
   a target-specific model, not the fully shared-family conditional law.
   Invisible-phase masking describes observed/detected evidence; account for
   no-detection mass or explicitly restrict generating eligibility before draws.
   Preserve the same observations in both c arms. Stop at unresolved support,
   numerical qualification or ambiguous observation law; no threshold tuning,
   rerolls, position fits or real identity acceptance follows from a successful
   rank. Keep regression plus fresh work within the proposed 600 s cap.
   [Old measured result and failures](/srv/bulk/leo/research/ds24-nomination-information-r01/nomination/FINDINGS.md).

2. **Compare information-ranked and count-ranked whole sources on the same
   pool, after identity challenge.** The existing 26-owner inventory already
   answers the allocation question conditionally: information chooses a shorter
   source in three of four scans. For 0010-0012, its implied SD improvements are
   only about 5-21 m from baseline; the larger 0013 gain depends on unqualified
   60108. Do not call this achieved precision. Freeze the count and information
   choices on all four scans, with no backfill, then challenge identities and
   predict complete independent owners using shared radio/orbit covariance.
   Where that prerequisite exists, compare one-source additions with matched
   c arms, priors and budgets; at most four scans and 16 fits for two allocation
   rules is a sufficient bounded pilot. Otherwise retain fixed-position
   diagnostics only. Stop a branch if independent ownership is unavailable,
   identity remains unresolved, nonlinear numerical checks fail, or original
   gates fail. Report all chosen sources, failures, likelihood/accepted-ID
   counts and raw versus fallback outcomes; do not adopt from local information
   alone. [Frozen pool, ranks and exact covariance](/srv/bulk/leo/research/ds24-nomination-information-r01/information/FINDINGS.md).

3. **Test whether an actually independent observable can break the ambiguity.**
   Begin with a read-only feasibility check on at most two existing recordings:
   a known-pilot/frame-time relation with a valid transmit/clock reference,
   independently documented orbit information, or a measured directional
   observable. Verify units, reference and capture provenance before processing.
   Project its sensitivity against the existing position/phase/receiver nuisance
   space. A relative cross-RX same-family contrast is a useful hardware control,
   but is not independent data when its CFOs are already included; stable frame
   periodicity alone is not absolute transmit time. Direction-finding literature
   uses an extra measured observable that these uncalibrated feed axes lack.
   Stop if no identifiable reference or distinct constraint exists. If one does,
   predeclare at most eight existing native windows, hold complete families out
   and assess reference closure and held prediction before any position fit.
   Do not repeat generic CFO smoothing or change sample rate without a causal
   control. No new recording is authorized by this review.

The remaining phase-dependent physical-mode integration approximation is a
valid secondary fixed-state question, especially because the factorial changes
B(phi). It must differ from the already tested parent nonlinear-phase slice
(maximum predicted movement 22.795 m with Gamma and B fixed). A stable score/
gradient effect would motivate a bounded experiment, not establish accuracy.
[Prior integration scope](/srv/bulk/leo/research/ds24-next-directions-review-r01/methods/FINDINGS.md).

Keep the reference operational. These cohorts are now exposed development
evidence. Any later validation needs verified unused recording membership,
frozen inclusion/failures, matched c arms and RF decisions sealed before errors;
repartitioning familiar recordings cannot manufacture new validation.
