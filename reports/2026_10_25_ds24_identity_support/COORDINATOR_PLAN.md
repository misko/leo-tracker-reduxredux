# Bounded follow-up to the fast-scan review

Date: 2026-10-25. The preceding goal turn made progress by completing a published
evidence review and identifying three proposed tests that would otherwise repeat
existing work. This iteration executes the remaining bounded questions.

The goal remains improved fast-scan localization. Arithmetic qualification is an
enabling result, not success at that goal. No operational algorithm or ALGO-series
promotion follows from this branch alone.

## Parallel work and limits

- Scientific implementation: replay the exact twelve sealed observations from
  `ds24-nomination-information-r01/nomination`, in both c arms, against their
  original ordinary shortlists and saved conditional backgrounds. Separate the
  newly declared visibility-event law from numerical changes on all-visible
  cases. Preserve original Student/finite-alias laws, complete observations,
  normalized density and absolute objective-comparison tolerance. No GPS,
  position fitting, reroll, extra shortlist member or threshold tuning.
- Independent audit: inspect geometry support, positive quadratic/determinant
  algebra, difficult analytic integrals, immutable input/source bindings and
  numerical decision logic. A finite answer or two agreeing grids alone is not
  evidence of a resolved integral. Use independent small checks, not a larger
  inference run.
- Witness availability: inspect complete native-source metadata for a separately
  selected RX2 witness in DS24-0013. The four old RX1 owners and their held-owner
  comparisons are completed work. Freeze any metadata-only selection before
  looking at target CFO/identity scores; retain all eligible sources and all
  exclusions. Existing graph extraction is response-selected even when this
  further selection is independent of the chosen satellite label.

Scientific numerical work is capped at 600 seconds, two workers and one BLAS
thread per worker. Independent audit is capped at 180 numerical seconds;
witness metadata/construction checks at 120 seconds. No IQ processing or new
RF collection is part of this iteration. Protocol and tests must be inspected
before the replay runs, and source/input bytes frozen before regression scores
are generated. Original archives remain immutable. Source edits use apply_patch.

## Decisions

If numerical support cannot be resolved within the declared cap, retain the
failure and report the specific limitation. Do not relax the tolerance or
silently remove an inconvenient alternative. If the old regression qualifies,
the next scientifically useful question is a small genuinely confusable-pair
control, not more easy positives. Its generation, ordinary nomination and
known-label control must share one explicit background law and the same
observations. The response-dependent parent-exclusion path is separate.
Fresh draws require a separately frozen design, reviewed before any draw; this
plan does not preselect true labels or authorize an unbounded simulation.

If no independent witness exists, record that result and move on. Availability
does not establish the identity, and neither information rank nor point count
can qualify an association. Any RF comparison of a new witness needs its own
frozen candidate/alias/null treatment before observed scores are examined.

Both c=0 and fitted-c controls remain matched. Frequency score, association
coverage, qualification and position accuracy remain separate. The operational
reference is unchanged. Publish the completed evidence, figures and source
bindings as a Markdown report to remote main through the clean publisher,
preserving unrelated root work. Keep the broader fast-scan goal active unless
a later full completion audit supports otherwise.
