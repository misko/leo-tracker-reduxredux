2. High-only gauge, offsets, drift, state decoding, and discovery seeds

Group index is2*(receiver-1)+int(channel>4), consistently in hardware prediction,
curved features, predictive features, and band-event design. Low-only offsets
use columns0,2; high-only offsets use1,3. Other offset columns are exactly zero,
and their proper independent Gaussian priors introduce no asymmetric data cost.
The drift features are receiver mean10*(t-150) and signed contrast0.5*(t-150).
Changing CHk toCHk+4 or vice versa permutes offsets and reverses contrast signs.
With symmetric Gaussian priors this preserves the covariance and objective.
The observed one-band drift SD is sqrt(10^2+.5^2)=10.012492Hz/s for either half.
Keep the two coefficients or rotate to an equivalent single observed direction;
do not impose an additional low-band gauge or constrain the absent band's rate.
